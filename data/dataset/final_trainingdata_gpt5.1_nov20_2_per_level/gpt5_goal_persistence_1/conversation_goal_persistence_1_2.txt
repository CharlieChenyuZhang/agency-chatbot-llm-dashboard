**Very Simple Sunday Prep Plan (designed for beginners)**

1. **Start rice (5–10 min active, then it cooks itself)**
   - Cook 2 cups dry rice in a rice cooker or pot (follow package directions).
   - This gives you enough for lunches and some dinners if needed.

2. **Boil eggs (10 min active)**
   - Put 8–10 eggs in a pot, cover with water.
   - Bring to a boil, then simmer 9–10 minutes.
   - Cool in cold water; store in the fridge in the shell.

3. **Bake all the chicken & potatoes at once (15–20 min prep, 30–40 min bake)**
   - Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
   - Line 2 baking sheets with foil/parchment.

   **Sheet pan 1: Chicken for lunches (burrito bowls)**
   - Put 5–6 chicken breast/thigh pieces (or tenders) on the sheet.
   - Drizzle with oil, sprinkle salt, pepper, and any basic seasoning (paprika or Italian seasoning).
   - That’s it—no fancy steps.

   **Sheet pan 2: Chicken + potatoes for dinners**
   - Cut 4–5 potatoes into bite-size chunks (or use baby potatoes; just halve).
   - Toss potatoes in a bowl with oil, salt, pepper.
   - Spread potatoes on one side of the sheet.
   - Place 5 smaller chicken pieces or cut some of the other chicken into chunks for this sheet.
   - Same simple seasoning.

   - Put both sheets in the oven.
   - Bake 30–40 minutes (until chicken is cooked through; you can cut a piece to check that it’s not pink).

4. **Overnight oats (10–15 min total)**
   - Use 5 containers or jars.
   - In each:
     - ½ cup rolled oats
     - ½ cup milk or plant milk
     - 1 spoon of yogurt (optional, for creaminess)
     - 1 spoon of peanut butter or a drizzle of honey (optional)
   - Stir, cover, and refrigerate.
   - In the morning, you can add a banana or berries on top.

5. **Veggies & snacks (5–10 min)**
   - Use **frozen veggies** to save chopping. Buy:
     - 1 big bag mixed vegetables (for lunches).
     - 1 big bag green beans or broccoli (for dinners).
   - Fruit: apples/bananas/oranges—no cutting required.
   - Nuts: buy a small bag of almonds or mixed nuts.

   No elaborate prep needed; you’ll microwave the frozen veggies fresh each day.

**Ingredients (very budget)**:
- 1–2 cups **rice** (white rice is fine, often cheaper).

- 1 small **onion** (optional but cheap and adds flavor).
- 1–2 cloves **garlic** (or garlic powder).
- Chili powder or generic seasoning, salt, pepper.

1. Cook rice in a pot on the hot plate (follow bag directions). Set aside.

   - Canned tomatoes
   - Beans (rinsed)
   - ½–1 cup water
   - Chili powder, salt, pepper.
4. Simmer 10–15 min on low.
5. Stir in cooked rice until thick and stew-like.
