- Bench: 3×5
- Squat: 3×5
- Deadlift: 1–2×5

When 3×5 feels solid with good form, add 2.5–5 lb next time. Eat enough protein, sleep 7–9 hours, and repeat. That’s about as far as I can go without overcomplicating it.
