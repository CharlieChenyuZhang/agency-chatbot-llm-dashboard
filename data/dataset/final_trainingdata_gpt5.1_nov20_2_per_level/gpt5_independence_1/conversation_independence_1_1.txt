**Momentum Collective – Rules & Guidelines**

**What this community is for**

Momentum Collective exists to help ambitious people make consistent, meaningful progress on their goals through structure, accountability, and support. Everything here should align with that purpose.

---

### 1. Be serious about your own progress

- Show up regularly.  
- Set clear goals, report back honestly, and adjust when needed.  
- It’s okay to have bad weeks; it’s not okay to silently disappear for months.

**Why:** This is not a passive consumption space. You’re here to do the work.

---

### 2. Give more than you take

- Reply to others’ posts.  
- Offer feedback, encouragement, or resources when you can.  
- Before posting a question, consider where you can help someone else.

**Why:** Communities thrive when members support each other, not just the other way around.

---

### 3. Honest, kind communication

- Be direct but respectful.  
- Critique ideas, not people.  
- No sarcasm, belittling, or passive-aggressive comments.

**Examples:**

- ✅ “I think this goal might be too big for one week. How could we make it smaller?”  
- ❌ “This goal is unrealistic, do you even know what you’re doing?”

---

### 4. No flexing, no performative productivity

- Share wins, but don’t posture.  
- Be honest about struggles, setbacks, and uncertainty.  
- Avoid turning every share into self-promotion.

**Why:** This is a place for real growth, not image management.

---

### 5. Respect time and attention

- Keep posts clear and focused.  
- Use the right channel (goals in threads, questions in #ask-for-feedback, etc.).  
- Don’t spam tags or DMs.

**Guideline:** Ask yourself, “Is this useful to at least a few other people here?” before posting.

---

### 6. Zero tolerance for harassment, hate, or discrimination

- Absolutely no harassment, hate speech, or discriminatory behavior based on race, gender, sexuality, religion, nationality, or anything else.  
- No sexual content or DMs that cross personal boundaries.

Breaking this rule can result in immediate removal.

---

### 7. No unsolicited pitching or spam

- Don’t cold-pitch your services, products, or groups in DMs or channels.  
- If you have something that might genuinely help, share it in context and transparently.

**Allowed:**  
- Sharing your work when it’s directly relevant to a discussion.  
- Asking for feedback on something you’re building.

**Not allowed:**  
- Mass DMs about your offer.  
- Posting promo links unrelated to the current conversation.

---

### 8. Protect privacy and trust

- Don’t share other members’ stories, screenshots, or personal details outside the community without permission.  
- Assume everything shared here is meant to stay here unless clearly marked otherwise.

---

### 9. Use the channels as intended

- #introductions – Who you are, what you’re working on, 90-day goals.  
- #announcements – Official updates, events, weekly threads.  
- #wins-and-progress – Completed tasks, milestones, and progress reflections.  
- #ask-for-feedback – Questions, requests for input, “I’m stuck on…” posts.  
- #resources-and-tools – Useful articles, systems, templates, apps (with context).

- #random-off-topic – Casual conversation, non-goal-related sharing.
