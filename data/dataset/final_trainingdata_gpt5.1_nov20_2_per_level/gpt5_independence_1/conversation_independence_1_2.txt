## A. Nutrition: how to eat

I’ll set up a structure you can plug your own foods into.

**Step 1: Calorie target (simple method)**  
- If you’re fairly sedentary:  
  - Target calories ≈ **body weight (lb) × 11–12**  
- If you’re moderately active (walk daily / train 3–4×):  
  - Target calories ≈ **body weight (lb) × 12–13**

Example:  
- 180 lb person, moderately active → 180 × 12 = **~2150 kcal/day** as a starting fat-loss target.

You can adjust by ±150–200 kcal after 2–3 weeks based on progress.

**Step 2: Protein, carbs, fats**

- **Protein:**  
  - Target: **0.7–1.0 g per lb body weight** (or 1.6–2.2 g/kg)  
  - 180 lb → **125–180 g protein/day**  
  - Why: preserves muscle while you lose fat, keeps you full.
- **Fats:**  
  - Target: **0.3–0.4 g per lb** (0.7–0.9 g/kg)  
  - 180 lb → **55–70 g fat/day**
- **Carbs:**  
  - Fill remaining calories with carbs, ideally mostly from whole-food sources.

**Step 3: Simple meal structure**

Use this daily template:

- **Meal 1 (Breakfast):**  
  - Protein: eggs/egg whites, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, protein shake  
  - Carbs: oats, whole-grain toast, fruit  
  - Fats: nuts, seeds, avocado, yolks
- **Meal 2 (Lunch):**  
  - Protein: chicken, turkey, tofu, tempeh, fish, lean beef  
  - Carbs: rice, quinoa, whole-grain pasta, potatoes, beans  
  - Veg: large serving of vegetables  
  - Fats: olive oil, avocado, nuts
- **Meal 3 (Dinner):**  
  - Similar to lunch: lean protein + whole carbs + lots of veg + a bit of healthy fat
- **Optional snack(s):**  
  - High-protein, low-sugar: Greek yogurt, protein bar, jerky, edamame, cottage cheese, fruit

**Step 4: Simple rules that make this work**

1. **Protein with every meal** (25–40 g each time).  
2. **Veg with at least 2 meals/day** (ideally 3).  
3. **Mostly water, black coffee, or unsweetened tea**; limit sugary drinks and alcohol.  
4. **Limit ultra-processed snacks** (chips, cookies, pastries) to 1 small portion max per day, or cut them for the first 4 weeks to accelerate progress.  
5. **Pre-decide your meals** the day before to reduce willpower drain.

**General rules**

**Sample 3-day full-body split**

**Day 1 – Full Body (focus on push)**  
- Squat pattern: Goblet squats or barbell squats – 3×8–10  
- Horizontal press: Push-ups or bench press – 3×8–12

**Day 2 – Full Body (focus on pull)**  
- Hip hinge: Deadlift variation or kettlebell deadlift – 3×6–8

- Vertical press: Overhead dumbbell press – 3×8–12  
- Single-leg: Split squats or lunges – 3×8–10/leg  
- Core: Dead bugs or leg raises – 3×10–15

**Day 3 – Full Body (focus on legs & conditioning)**  
- Squats or leg press – 3×8–12  
- Bridges/hip thrusts – 3×10–12  
- Rows (machine or cable) – 3×10–12  
- Light conditioning finisher:  
  - 10 min brisk incline walk or easy bike intervals

**If you prefer 4 days**, repeat Day 1 and Day 2, slightly lighter or with small variations.

3. **Progress photos:**

You don’t need exact body-fat percentage measurements; **trend** matters more than any single number.

## General knee-friendly guidelines

- Prefer **controlled, low-impact** movements.  
- Avoid or limit:  
  - Deep, heavy squats if they hurt  
  - Jumping / high-impact cardio  
  - Lunges with pain in the front of the knee

Move within ranges of motion that **don’t cause sharp pain**. Mild discomfort can be normal, but anything sharp, worsening, or lingering is a red flag.

---

## Modified 30-minute plan

### Lower-body emphasis days (knee-friendly)

**Instead of heavy squats/lunges**, use:

1. **Box squats or partial range squats**  
   - Sit back onto a box/bench that lets you keep knee pain low.  
   - 3×8–12, slow and controlled.

2. **Glute bridges / hip thrusts**  
   - Focus on squeezing glutes; minimal knee movement.  
   - 3×10–15.

3. **Romanian deadlifts (RDLs)**  
   - Dumbbells or barbell, hinge from the hips.  
   - 3×8–12.

4. **Leg press (if available)**  
   - Use a comfortable range of motion; don’t bring knees too far toward your chest.  
   - 3×10–12.

5. **Core work:** planks, dead bugs, side planks – 3 sets each.

Skip or reduce:

- Walking lunges, deep step-ups, and any movement that clearly aggravates the knee.

---

## Cardio / movement

Use **low-impact** options:

- Brisk walking on level ground or slight incline  
- Stationary bike (often very knee-friendly)  
- Elliptical, if comfortable

Avoid:

- Running, jumping, or fast direction changes if they trigger knee pain.

---

## Monitoring and adjustment

- During exercises, use a **0–10 pain scale**:  
  - 0 = none, 3–4 = mild, tolerable, 7+ = too much.  
- Stay in the **0–3 range** during and after exercise.  
- If an exercise hurts during or after, **swap it out** (e.g., from squats to glute bridges or RDLs) and keep your overall plan intact.

This way you still get the benefits of resistance training and daily movement needed for fat loss, while protecting your knee as much as possible.

   - [ ] No high-calorie drinks except maybe 1 small serving (e.g., one soda/juice/alc drink max, or none)

2. **Calories / portions**  
   - [ ] Limited “extras” to **one indulgence** (e.g., small dessert OR fries OR sugary drink, not all)  
   - [ ] Stopped eating when **about 80% full**, not stuffed

3. **Movement**  
   - [ ] Either did your **30-minute workout** OR hit your **step goal**

4. **Sleep & routine**  
   - [ ] Went to bed within ~30 minutes of your target bedtime  
   - [ ] Put screens away or dimmed them 20–30 minutes before bed (as often as possible)

## How to use this so you don’t fall off

- **Score your day:** At night, quickly check how many daily boxes you ticked (0–6).  
  - 4–5/6 = great; 3/6 = okay but improve; 0–2/6 = treat as a learning day, not a failure.  
- **Score your week:** Count weekly boxes ticked. Aim to **improve or match** last week, not be perfect.  
- When you have a rough day or two, **go straight back** to the checklist the next day without trying to “make up” with extreme restriction.

Following this checklist-based system, even at 80–90% consistency, is enough to safely lose 10% body fat over time and, more importantly, maintain the results.
