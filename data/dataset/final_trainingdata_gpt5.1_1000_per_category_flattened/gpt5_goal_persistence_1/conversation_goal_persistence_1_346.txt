### Human: I like pasta and I have a stove and a microwave. I don’t want to spend more than 20 minutes.
### Assistant: Perfect. Let’s start with the simplest possible: **garlic butter pasta**. It’s fast, cheap, and hard to mess up. I’ll break it into tiny steps. **Ingredients (for 1–2 servings):** - 1 handful of dry pasta (about 100g; roughly 1 cup) - 1–2 tablespoons butter (or oil if you don’t have butter) - 1–2 cloves garlic (or 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder) - Salt - Optional: black pepper, grated cheese, dried herbs (like oregano), or chili flakes **Equipment:** - 1 pot - 1 pan (or you can use the pot twice if that’s all you have) - Spoon or spatula - Strainer/colander (or carefully use the pot lid to pour off water) **Step-by-step:** 1. Add the pasta, stir once so it doesn’t stick, and lower heat a bit so it’s still bubbling but not overflowing. 4. Set a timer for the time on the pasta package (usually 8–12 minutes). 5. While pasta cooks: - If using real garlic: peel it and finely chop it. - If using garlic powder: just measure 1/2 teaspoon and set aside. 6. When there’s about 3 minutes left on your pasta timer: - Put the pan on low-medium heat. - Add butter (or oil). - Add garlic and gently cook 1–2 minutes until it smells good and just starts to turn lightly golden. Don’t let it get dark brown. 7. When timer for pasta ends, taste one piece. If it’s soft with a little bite, it’s ready. 8. Turn off the heat, drain the pasta. 9. Add pasta to the pan with garlic butter, toss to coat. Add a spoonful of pasta water if it’s too dry. 10. Taste. Add salt, pepper, cheese, or herbs if you have them. If you want, tell me exactly what ingredients you have and I’ll customize this to them. Then on your next cooking attempt, we’ll refine step by step. ---
