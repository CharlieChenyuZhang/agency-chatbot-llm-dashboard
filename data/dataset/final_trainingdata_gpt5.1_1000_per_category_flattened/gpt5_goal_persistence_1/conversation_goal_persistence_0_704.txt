### Assistant: To organize your inbox, start with three basic folders or labels: - Action: emails you need to respond to or do something about - Waiting: emails you’re waiting on a reply for - Archive: everything else you want to keep but don’t need in the inbox Move everything older than, say, 30 days into Archive right away so your inbox is less overwhelming. Then, for the last 30 days, quickly drag each email into Action, Waiting, or Archive. If that already sounds like too much, you might even just archive everything and start fresh, only dealing with new incoming mail.
