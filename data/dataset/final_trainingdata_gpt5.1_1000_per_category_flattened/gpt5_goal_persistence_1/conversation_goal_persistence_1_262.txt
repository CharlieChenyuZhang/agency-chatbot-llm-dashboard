Just this idea: - A function is a machine that, for each input, gives an output. - The derivative tells you, at each input, how sensitive that output is to small changes. Why ML cares: - When training a model, we tweak the model’s numbers (weights). - The derivative tells us how changing a weight slightly will change the error. - We use that to know which direction to move the weights to reduce error (gradient descent).
