Voice processing chain (podcast) On your **voice track** in Reaper, insert FX in this order: 1. **High-pass filter (HPF)** - Start at **80 Hz**, 12 dB/oct. - Raise to **100 Hz** if you have rumble/boom and your voice still sounds natural. 2. **Compressor** - Ratio: **3:1**. - Attack: **10–20 ms**. - Release: **80–150 ms**. - Threshold: adjust so you get **3–6 dB of gain reduction** on normal speech peaks. - Makeup gain: enough to bring level back to roughly where it was. 3. **EQ** - Gentle **low-mid cut** if muddy: - -2 to -4 dB around **200–400 Hz**. - Presence boost for clarity: - +2 to +3 dB around **3–5 kHz**. - Optional air: - +1 to +2 dB at **8–10 kHz** if you want more “sparkle” and it’s not harsh. 4. **De-esser (if “s” sounds are sharp)** - Target **5–8 kHz**. - Threshold so it only engages on strong “s” sounds (a few dB of reduction). 5. **Limiter (on the master bus, not the individual track)** - Ceiling: **-1 dB**. - Just tame any peaks that might clip. --- ### 4. Acoustic guitar processing chain On your **guitar track**: 1. **High-pass filter** - Start around **80 Hz**, possibly go up to **100–120 Hz** to clear low rumble if there’s a bass or voice in the mix. 2. **EQ** - Cut muddiness: - -2 to -4 dB around **200–300 Hz** if it sounds boomy. - Add clarity and pick definition: - +2–3 dB around **3–5 kHz**. - If it’s too harsh or “zingy”: - -2 dB around **6–8 kHz**. 3. **Compressor (light)** - Ratio: **2:1 to 3:1**. - Attack: **20–30 ms** (to keep transients). - Release: **80–150 ms**. - Aim for **2–4 dB** of gain reduction on louder strums. --- ### 5.
