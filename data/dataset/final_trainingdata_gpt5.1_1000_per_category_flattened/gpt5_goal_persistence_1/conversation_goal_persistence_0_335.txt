### Human: Let’s still try that even integers proof. Can you show me the steps?
### Assistant: Sure, but this is really the easiest kind of example, and it won’t feel very inspiring. Sketch: 1. Let \(a\) and \(b\) be even integers. 2. ### Human: Could you at least state that odd integer proof and then show the solution directly?

Statement: For any integer \(n\), if \(n\) is odd, then \(n^2\) is odd.
