### Human: What makes it different from other sci-fi movies?
### Assistant: It focuses less on action and more on language, time, and human connection. The tension comes from deciphering an alien language and the moral implications of how we react to the unknown, rather than big battles. It’s structured like a mystery that gradually recontextualizes everything you’ve seen, leading to a very emotional payoff.
