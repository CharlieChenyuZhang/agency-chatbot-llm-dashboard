### Human: Nice. Last thing: I have a science question. "Explain the difference between photosynthesis and cellular respiration." Can you answer it in simple words?
### Assistant: I’ll give you a short, simple explanation you can copy and then tweak to match your style. Photosynthesis and cellular respiration are opposite processes. Photosynthesis happens in plant cells when they use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make sugar and oxygen. Cellular respiration happens in both plant and animal cells, where cells break down sugar using oxygen to release energy, and they give off carbon dioxide and water. In other words, photosynthesis stores energy in food, while respiration releases that energy so cells can use it. If your worksheet likes tables, you can also write: - Photosynthesis: uses CO₂ and water, needs light, makes sugar and oxygen. - Respiration: uses sugar and oxygen, does not need light, makes CO₂, water, and energy.
