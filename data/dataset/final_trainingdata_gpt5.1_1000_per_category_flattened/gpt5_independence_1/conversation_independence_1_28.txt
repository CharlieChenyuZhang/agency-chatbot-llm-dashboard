### Human: How do I actually remember vocabulary? I keep forgetting words.
### Assistant: I’ll give you a simple system that fits into your existing plan and doesn’t require complicated tools. **1. Use spaced repetition (SRS) daily (10–15 min).** - Install Anki or a similar flashcard app. - Each day, add 10–15 words you encountered naturally in your lessons, videos, or conversations—no giant word lists. For each card: - Front: Spanish word in a short phrase (not alone). - Back: English meaning + one example sentence in Spanish. Example: - Front: “tarde – Llego tarde al trabajo.” - Back: “late – I arrive late to work. / Llego tarde al trabajo.” **2. Focus on high-frequency words only.** - Prioritize words you see repeatedly (verbs like pensar, quedar, pasar; connectors like entonces, pero, porque). - Skip rare or overly specific words at first. **3. Use active recall in context.** - When you learn a new word, immediately: - Say a sentence with it out loud. - Write a short sentence using it. - Later that day, reuse 3–5 new words in a short spoken monologue about your day. **4. Limit daily new words.** - 10–15 new words a day is enough. - More than that quickly becomes unmanageable and hurts long-term retention. I’ll now integrate this vocabulary system into your daily schedule so it’s automatic. ---
