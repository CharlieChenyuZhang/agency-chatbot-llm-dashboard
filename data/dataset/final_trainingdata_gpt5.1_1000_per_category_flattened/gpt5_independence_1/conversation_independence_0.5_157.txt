I’ll make the choices for you on what to prioritize, and you can tell me if anything feels off when you try it. **Conventional deadlift setup checklist:** 1. **Foot and bar position** - Feet about hip-width apart. - Bar over mid-foot (if you look down, bar is ~2–3 cm from your shins). 2. **Grip and hip position** - Bend at the hips and grab the bar just outside your legs. - Drop your hips until: - Your shoulder blades are roughly over the bar. - You feel your legs pushing into the floor, not just your back pulling. 3. **Brace** - Big breath into your *belly and sides* (360° brace), not just up into your chest. - Squeeze your abs like someone is going to punch you. 4. **Set your back** - Think “proud chest” and “show the logo on your shirt to the wall in front of you.” - Try to lock in a *flat or slightly arched* low back before the bar leaves the floor. 5. **First pull** - Push the floor away with your legs. - Think: “Leg press the ground” rather than “yank with my back.” - Keep the bar close—almost sliding up your shins. 6. **Lockout** - Stand tall, squeeze glutes at the top. - Don’t lean back or hyperextend; just straight and tall. 7. **Lowering** - Hips back first (like an RDL) until the bar reaches about knee level, then bend knees to return to floor.
