### Human: Can you outline that lemon garlic chicken recipe for me?
### Assistant: Here is a complete, ready-to-use outline you can expand into your Substack post. **Title:** One-Pan Lemon Garlic Chicken and Potatoes (30 Minutes) **Intro (2–3 short paragraphs)** - Set the scene: a full dinner on a single tray, minimal cleanup. - Emphasize that everything roasts together, so timing is easy. - Mention that leftovers reheat well for lunch. **Ingredients (serves 3–4)** - 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs - 500–700 g / about 1.5 lb baby potatoes, halved - 3 tbsp olive oil - Juice and zest of 1 lemon - 4–5 garlic cloves, minced - 1 tsp salt - ½ tsp black pepper - 1 tsp dried oregano (or mixed Italian herbs) - Optional: a pinch of chili flakes, fresh parsley for serving **Equipment** - Large sheet pan or roasting tray - Mixing bowl - Oven **Instructions** 1. **Preheat the oven** - Heat to 220°C / 425°F. 2. **Prep the potatoes** - Halve baby potatoes. - Toss in a bowl with half the olive oil, half the garlic, salt, and pepper. - Spread them in a single layer on the sheet pan. 3. **Season the chicken** - In the same bowl, add chicken thighs. - Add remaining olive oil, garlic, lemon juice and zest, oregano, and a bit more salt and pepper. - Rub the mixture all over the chicken. 4. **Assemble the pan** - Nestle the chicken thighs, skin side up, among the potatoes. - Pour any leftover marinade from the bowl over the top. 5. **Roast** - Bake for about 30–40 minutes - Check that potatoes are tender and chicken juices run clear (internal temp around 75°C / 165°F). 6. **Finish and serve** - If you want crispier skin, broil for 2–3 minutes at the end. - Sprinkle with chopped parsley and an extra squeeze of lemon. - Serve directly from the pan. **Notes and Variations** - Swap potatoes for carrots and onions or add them in. - Use chicken drumsticks instead of thighs, with similar timing. - For a lighter version, use bone-in, skinless thighs and reduce the oil slightly. Use this outline as the body of your Substack post with 2–3 photos: the raw tray, the cooked tray, and a plated serving.
