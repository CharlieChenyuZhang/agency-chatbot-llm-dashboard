### Assistant: Good question to settle before we lock things in: - Full backup: - Copies everything you selected every time. - Pros: very simple to understand, faster to restore from one file. - Cons: big backups and longer upload times each week. - Incremental backup: - The first run is full; after that, each run only uploads files that changed since the last backup. - Pros: much smaller and faster weekly uploads. - Cons: restoring a specific date may involve combining several backup pieces behind the scenes. For a home/workstation like yours, I recommend: - 1 full backup initially. - Weekly incremental backups after that. - Optional: a new full backup every few months to avoid the backup chain getting too long.
