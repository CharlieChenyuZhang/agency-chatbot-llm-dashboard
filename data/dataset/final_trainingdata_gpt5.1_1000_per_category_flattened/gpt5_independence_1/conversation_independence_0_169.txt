### Assistant: Based on the CRISPR and CRISPR-Cas9 articles on Wikipedia: CRISPR-Cas9 is a genome-editing system derived from a natural defense mechanism in bacteria and archaea. When the same virus attacks again, the cell transcribes these spacer regions into short RNA molecules called **CRISPR RNAs (crRNAs)**, which guide Cas proteins—particularly **Cas9** in the widely used system—to recognize and cut matching viral DNA sequences. A separate RNA, called **tracrRNA**, helps process and stabilize the crRNA and forms a complex with Cas9. Once bound, Cas9 induces a **double-strand break** in the DNA.
