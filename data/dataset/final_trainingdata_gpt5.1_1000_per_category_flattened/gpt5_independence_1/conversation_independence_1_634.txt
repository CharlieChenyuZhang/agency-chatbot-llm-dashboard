### Assistant: Use a mix of delivery, carts, and occasional rentals: 1. **Regular groceries (weekly basics):** - Pick a nearby supermarket and: - Do smaller, more frequent trips on foot, or - Use their delivery service (most NYC chains deliver). - A sturdy **foldable shopping cart** (“granny cart”) is standard here—makes carrying heavier loads simple. 2. **Bulk/Costco trips:** - **Plan a monthly or every‑other‑month run** instead of weekly. - Options: - **Zipcar/Car share:** Rent a car by the hour from a nearby garage. With this setup, you get almost all the convenience of having a car for shopping without paying for a car full-time.
