Each session: ~45–60 minutes, plus 5–10 minutes warm-up and cool-down. **Weekly Structure** - Day 1: Upper Body Strength - Day 2: Lower Body + Core - Day 3: Active Recovery / Light Cardio + Mobility - Day 4: Full Body Strength + Conditioning --- **Day 1 – Upper Body Strength (Push/Pull)** 1. Warm-up (5–8 minutes) - Brisk walk or easy cycling - Arm circles, band pull-aparts, shoulder rolls 2. Strength - Push-ups (or incline push-ups) – 3 sets of 8–12 - One-arm dumbbell rows – 3 x 8–12 per side - Dumbbell shoulder press – 3 x 8–12 - Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up – 3 x 8–10 - Dumbbell curls – 2 x 10–15 - Triceps dips on bench or chair – 2 x 10–15 3. Cardio (20–30 minutes) - Brisk walk, easy cycling, or elliptical at a conversational pace 2. Warm-up - 5 minutes light cardio - Dynamic leg and arm swings 2. Strength Circuit (3–4 rounds) Move exercise to exercise with 45–60 seconds rest between rounds: - Squats (bodyweight or dumbbells) – 10–12 reps - Push-ups (any variation) – 8–12 reps - Dumbbell bent-over rows – 10–12 reps - Glute bridge – 12–15 reps - Standing dumbbell press – 10–12 reps 3. Conditioning Finisher (optional, 5–10 minutes) Pick one: - 30 seconds fast / 60 seconds easy on a bike or walk - 5 rounds of: 20 bodyweight squats + 20 seconds marching in place 4. Cool-down - Full body stretching, focusing on tight areas --- **Progression (Weeks 1–4)** - Start with the low end of reps; when you can do the top end easily, increase weight slightly or add 1 set. - Aim to improve 1 small thing each week: 1–2 extra reps, a bit more weight, or less rest time. **General Guidelines** - Rest days: 1–2 days per week, especially after harder sessions. - Intensity: Most sets should feel like you could do 1–3 more reps (not to total failure). - If you feel very fatigued or sore, cut volume in half that day rather than skipping completely.
### Human: I only have 3 days a week and my knees hurt sometimes. Can you adjust it?
### Assistant: Switching to a 3-day, low-impact routine that’s knee-friendly and still effective: **Weekly Structure (3 Days)** - Day A: Upper Body + Gentle Core - Day B: Lower Body (Knee-Friendly) + Glutes + Core - Day C: Full Body + Low-Impact Cardio Place at least one rest day between sessions when possible (e.g., Mon–Wed–Fri or Tue–Thu–Sat). --- **Day A – Upper Body + Gentle Core** 1. Warm-up (5–8 minutes) - Easy walk or stationary bike - Arm circles, band pull-aparts, shoulder rolls 2. Strength - Incline push-ups (hands on bench/table) – 3 x 8–12 - One-arm dumbbell rows – 3 x 8–12 per side - Seated dumbbell shoulder press – 3 x 8–12 - Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up – 3 x 8–10 - Dumbbell curls – 2 x 10–15 - Triceps kickbacks or band pressdowns – 2 x 10–15 3. Core (knee-friendly) - Dead bug – 3 x 8–10 per side - Side plank (from knees if needed) – 2 x 15–30 seconds per side 4. Cool-down - Chest, shoulders, upper back stretches --- **Day B – Lower Body (Knee-Friendly) + Glutes + Core** 1. Warm-up - 5 minutes easy bike or walking - Gentle leg swings, hip circles, ankle circles 2. Strength (focus on range that doesn’t hurt) - Box squat to a chair/bench (stop before knee pain) – 3 x 8–10 - Hip hinge with dumbbells (Romanian deadlift) – 3 x 8–12 - Glute bridge – 3 x 12–15 - Step-ups to a low step (pain-free height) – 2 x 8–10 per leg If step-ups bother knees, replace with: - Standing hip abduction (with band) – 2 x 12–15 per leg 3. Core / Stability - Bird dog – 3 x 8–10 per side - Standing or seated knee raises (slow, controlled) – 2 x 10–12 per leg 4. Cool-down - Hamstring, glute, calf, and hip flexor stretches - Light quad stretch only in a pain-free range --- **Day C – Full Body + Low-Impact Cardio** 1. Warm-up - 5 minutes easy cardio (bike, walk, or elliptical) 2. Circuit (3 rounds, 45–60 seconds rest between rounds) - Dumbbell goblet squat or sit-to-stand from chair – 8–10 reps - Incline push-ups – 8–12 reps - Dumbbell bent-over row – 10–12 reps - Glute bridge – 12–15 reps - Seated or standing dumbbell shoulder press – 8–12 reps 3. Low-Impact Cardio (10–15 minutes) - Option 1: Stationary bike at a steady, moderate pace - Option 2: Walk on flat ground (avoid steep inclines initially) 4. Cool-down - Full body stretching, focusing on hips, hamstrings, and calves --- **Knee-Friendly Rules** - Stay in pain-free ranges: slight discomfort ≤ 3/10 that doesn’t linger is usually okay; sharp pain is not. - Prioritize hip hinge and glute work (bridges, deadlifts) over deep squats and lunges. - If a movement hurts, reduce depth, slow it down, or substitute with a similar, pain-free pattern. **Progression** - Weeks 1–2: Use very conservative weights, focus on form. - Weeks 3–4: Add a bit of load or 1 extra set on key lifts (bridges, rows, presses), still keeping knees comfortable.
