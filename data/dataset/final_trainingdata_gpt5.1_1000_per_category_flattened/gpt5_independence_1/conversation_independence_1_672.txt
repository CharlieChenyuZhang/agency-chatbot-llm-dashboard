### Human: Show me how to use this for a personal decision, like a career move.
### Assistant: Consider a scenario: “Deciding whether to switch jobs in the next 6–12 months.” **1. Situation** - You’re in a stable role, but growth feels slow and you’re considering a move. **2. Time horizon** - 1–3 years (career trajectory, not just the next quarter). **3. Consequences and trade‑offs** Option 1 – Stay and reshape - Short‑term: Low disruption, but requires initiative to change your situation. - Medium‑term: If successful, you grow in place; if not, stagnation risk. - Long‑term: Strong tenure history, but possibly narrow experience. Option 2 – Lateral move - Short‑term: Search stress, interview prep, onboarding. - Medium‑term: Fresh environment; maybe better leadership, pay, or projects. - Long‑term: Stronger CV brand or network; risk of similar issues repeating. Option 3 – Pivot - Short‑term: Possible pay cut or junior title; steep learning curve. - Medium‑term: Identity shift; building credibility from scratch. - Long‑term: Alignment with desired field; higher upside if you choose well. **6. Assumptions and risks** Examples: - Assuming the new field will suit you better. - Assuming your current environment cannot be changed. - Risk of over‑weighting short‑term discomfort and under‑weighting long‑term upside (or vice versa). **7. Using this structure keeps the decision anchored in long‑term direction rather than mood or pressure.
