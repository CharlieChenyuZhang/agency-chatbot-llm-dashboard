### Human: What’s a soaker hose? Is it worth getting one or should I just water by hand?
### Assistant: A soaker hose is a porous hose that slowly leaks water along its length. You lay it on the soil surface among your plants, and when you turn it on, it waters the bed evenly and gently at soil level. Given that: - You have one 4x8 bed (small), - You want to spend only a couple of hours a week, - And you’re new to this, I’d lean toward this approach unless you object: - Use a simple soaker hose looped through your 4x8 bed. - Connect it to your outdoor spigot. - Start with manual on/off (no timer yet) so you get a feel for how long it takes. That reduces the chance of over/underwatering and saves time. If you like it and want more automation later, you could add a timer.
