Differentiating “Assignee” vs “Reviewer”** - **Assignee**: Usually the person responsible for implementing the change. - **Reviewers**: People responsible for reviewing and approving. - You can have one assignee and multiple reviewers. **5. Enable the “Require approval from code owners” setting (if available in your tier/version). 3. When an MR touches owned files, GitLab automatically suggests or requires the corresponding owners as approvers/reviewers. That’s the core flow: primarily, you assign reviewers via the sidebar “Reviewers” field on the MR, optionally reinforced with approval rules and CODEOWNERS. ---
