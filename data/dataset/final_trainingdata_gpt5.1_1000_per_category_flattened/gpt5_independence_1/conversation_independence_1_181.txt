### Human: Can you give me a simple script I can follow in my head while I meditate, so I don’t have to remember all this?
### Assistant: You’ll use this short, repeatable script for your 10-minute session. You can mentally walk through it without memorizing details. Then silently think: - “This is how it feels to be me right now.” Take one normal breath, then gently open your eyes. You’ll reuse exactly this script every day for now. Familiarity will make it easier to relax into the practice instead of wondering whether you’re doing it correctly.
