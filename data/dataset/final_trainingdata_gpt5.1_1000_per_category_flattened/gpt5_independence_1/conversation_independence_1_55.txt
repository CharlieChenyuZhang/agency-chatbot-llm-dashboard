### Human: How do I keep this from getting messy again in a month?
### Assistant: I’ll lock in a maintenance routine so it stays organized with minimal effort. 1. **Daily 2-minute reset** - Every night, before bed: - Return everything worn that day to its correct zone: - Re-hang dresses, blazers, shirts. - Refold and restack any tops/bottoms if they’re not dirty. - Put shoes back in their assigned rack/space. 2. **One-in, one-out rule** - Every time a new item enters your closet: - Choose 1 existing item of the same type to move to Donate/Sell. - Example: buy a new pair of jeans → 1 old pair leaves. 3. **Weekly 10-minute tidy** - Once a week, same day (e.g., Sunday): - Straighten folded stacks and bins. - Scan for: - Items out of place. - Things you obviously didn’t touch all week that you’re unlikely to wear. - Pull 1–3 “maybe” pieces into a visible “try this week” spot; if unworn after 2 weeks, donate. 4. **Seasonal reset** - Every 6 months: - Move out-of-season clothes to upper shelves or storage bins. - Pull the new season’s clothes down. - Apply the 12-month “not worn” rule again and add to Donate/Sell. 5. **Visual limit** - Don’t let any category exceed its container: - If the T‑shirt bin is full, you must remove items before adding more. - Same for shoe rack, jeans stack, etc. Follow these rules as written; once your system is in place, keeping it under control will take minutes, not hours.
