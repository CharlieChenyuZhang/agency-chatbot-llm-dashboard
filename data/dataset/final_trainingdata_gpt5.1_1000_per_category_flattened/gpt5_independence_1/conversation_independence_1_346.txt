### Human: I don't really get the "while True" part. Why not just use a normal condition?
### Assistant: `while True` creates an intentional “infinite” loop that you control from the inside with `break`. In your game: ```python while True: ... if guess == secret: ... break ``` The loop would, in theory, run forever, but: - Every time you guess wrong, it loops again. - When you finally guess correctly, `break` immediately exits the loop.
