### Human: This is too much work to do at once. How should I schedule it?
### Assistant: I’ll break it into a **4-day, low-stress plan** that fits into short sessions. **Day 1 – Quick triage (30–45 minutes)** - Empty only the **top shelf**. - Assign each book to A, B, or C and make 3 floor piles. - Put them back roughly grouped as A/B/C without worrying about alphabetizing. **Day 2 – Finish grouping, no alphabetizing yet (45–60 minutes)** - Do the same for the rest of the shelves: assign to A/B/C, return grouped. - When finished, every shelf is chunked into three blocks: A, B, C. **Day 3 – Alphabetize A only (30–45 minutes)** - Pull just the A-group off the shelves. - Sort A-books by author’s last name. - Reshelve A from top-down in order. **Day 4 – Alphabetize B and C (45–60 minutes)** - Repeat alphabetizing for B, then C. - While doing this, start your master index (title, author, group, format, location). After Day 4, your system is fully in place. Maintenance becomes “put the new book into A/B/C, then slide it into the right author spot.” ---
### Human: I also want to track which books I’ve lent out. How do you handle that?
### Assistant: I’ll add a **simple lending tracker** to the system. 1. **Extend your master index** Add two columns: - **Status:** On Shelf / Lent Out / Lost / Donated - **Borrower & Date:** e.g., “Alex – 2025-03-02” 2. **Lending rule** - When you hand a book to someone, immediately update its row: - Status → “Lent Out” - Borrower & Date → name + date - When it comes back: - Status → “On Shelf” - Borrower & Date → blank or “Returned – date” 3. **Physical reminder** - Keep one sticky note on your shelf labeled “LENT OUT.” - Every time you lend a book, **write its title and borrower** on that note as backup to the digital log. - Clear items off that note as they return. 4. **Where the book “lives” in the system while lent** - It **stays in the same A/B/C group** and location in your catalog. - Only the Status changes, so you always know where it *should* return. ---
