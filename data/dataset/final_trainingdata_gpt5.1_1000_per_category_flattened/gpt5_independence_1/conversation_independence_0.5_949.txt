### Human: I’ll start with hand watering and see how it goes. Anything else I should watch out for as a beginner?
### Assistant: A few key things will prevent most beginner problems: 1. **Overwatering vs underwatering** - Leaves drooping and soil bone‑dry: likely underwatering. - Leaves yellowing and soil constantly wet: likely overwatering. - When in doubt, check 1–2 inches down with your finger before watering. 2. **Sun stress** - In really hot afternoons, some droop is normal, especially for cucumbers and tomatoes. - If leaves get crispy/burned, you might need a bit of **afternoon shade** (like a light shade cloth). 3. **Pests** - Check the undersides of leaves 1–2 times a week. - If you see clusters of tiny bugs or lots of holes, take a clear photo and compare online or send to a gardening forum for ID. - Early action is much easier than waiting. 4. **Feeding** - About 3–4 weeks after planting, a light dose of **balanced organic fertilizer** for veggies can help. - Follow the package directions; more is not better.
