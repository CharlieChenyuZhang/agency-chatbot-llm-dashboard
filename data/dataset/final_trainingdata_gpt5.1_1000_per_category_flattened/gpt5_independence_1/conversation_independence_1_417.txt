### Human: Help me develop creative skills

### Human: I get stuck because I keep thinking my ideas are bad or not original. That kills my motivation to even try.
### Assistant: You’ll defang that self-criticism by changing *when* it’s allowed to show up and by giving it a specific job, instead of letting it run everything. Here’s the structure you’ll use: **1. Two Separate Modes: Maker vs. Editor** You’ll explicitly separate: - **Maker time (first 15 minutes)**: - Rule: no judging ideas. - You’re only allowed to ask: “What else could this be?” - **Editor time (last 5 minutes)**: - Rule: you *must* judge—but only in specific, useful ways. During Maker time: - If a critical thought appears, write it in a “Parking Lot” section: - Example: “This is unoriginal” → park it, keep going. - Immediately respond with: - “If it *were* original, what twist would it have?” - Then write that twist. During Editor time: Use only these 3 questions (nothing else is allowed): 1. What, if simplified, would make this clearer? 2. What, if exaggerated, would make this more interesting? 3. What is one small thing I can remove? This channels perfectionism into constructive refinement instead of paralysis. **2. Daily 3-Sentence Reframe (1 minute)** After you finish: - Sentence 1: “Today I produced X (what you made).” - Sentence 2: “The part I disliked most was…” - Sentence 3: “That part is a sign that I’m practicing the skill of… (e.g., taking risks, finishing, exploring).” You’re training a mental reflex: discomfort = *evidence of training*, not evidence of failure.

### Assistant: You’ll build confidence in three phased steps: private, semi-private, then public—while keeping the stakes tiny at each phase. **Phase 1 (Week 1) – Private Publishing** - Every day, after your 20-minute session, write a 3–5 sentence “field note”: - What system you designed or refined. - One thing you learned or noticed. - Store these in a single document or note app as if it were a public feed, but visible only to you. **Phase 2 (Week 2–3) – Semi-Private Sharing** - Select 2–3 of your clearest field notes. - Turn each into a slightly more polished version: - 1 sentence: problem. - 2–3 sentences: your system. - 1 sentence: how it felt using it. - Share them in a low-pressure space: - One friend, a small group, or a low-traffic online corner. - Your mental rule: you’re not sharing to impress; you’re sharing to *document experiments*. **Phase 3 (After Week 3) – Public, Tiny, and Consistent** - Create a recurring micro-format, for example: - “One tiny system that helped me today:” + 3–5 sentences. - Post 1–2 times per week. - You’ll treat likes/engagement as *data points*, not as judgment: - “People resonated with X” → design more like that. - “Low response” → neutral feedback, not failure. This keeps the emotional cost low while gradually normalizing the feeling of being seen.
