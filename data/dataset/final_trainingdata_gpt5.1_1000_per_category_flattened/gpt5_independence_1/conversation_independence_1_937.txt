Harvesting - Lettuce: pick outer leaves when they’re hand-sized; the plant will keep producing. - Radishes: usually ready in 3–4 weeks when the top of the root is visible. - Carrots: check after 8–10 weeks; pull one to test size. - Beans: pick when pods are full but not bulging with big seeds. - Tomatoes: harvest when fully colored and slightly soft. - Herbs: start snipping once plants are 6–8 in (15–20 cm) tall. ### Human: How often should I plant new seeds so I always have salad?
### Assistant: I’ll set up a simple succession-planting schedule you can follow without tracking complicated dates. I’ll assume: - You’re growing primarily lettuce and maybe arugula/spinach in your two containers. - You want a steady but small household supply. Succession plan: 1. Break your space into “zones” - Large pot: - Mentally divide into 3 sections (front, middle, back). - Railing box: - Use herbs as anchors and think of the gaps between them as 2–3 mini zones. 2. Planting schedule I’ll use an easy 2‑week rhythm: - Week 0: - Sow seeds in section 1 of the large pot and in 1–2 gaps in the railing box. - Week 2: - Sow seeds in section 2 of the large pot and in the next gap(s) in the railing box. - Week 4: - Sow seeds in section 3 of the large pot. - Week 6: - By now, section 1 should be well into harvest, so you: - Harvest heavily there. - Reseed that same section right after harvest. Harvest timing - Leaf lettuce / arugula: - Begin light harvesting about 3–4 weeks after sowing. - Harvest “cut-and-come-again” style: - Use scissors to cut outer leaves 1–2 in (2–5 cm) above soil. - Leave the center of each plant; it will regrow. - After 2–3 rounds of regrowth: - Plants get tired or bitter. - At that point, harvest the rest and reseed that section. 4. Simple rule to remember You don’t need dates—just follow this: - Every 2 weeks: - Sow a new small patch of seeds in a different section. - Every time a section looks crowded and mature: - Harvest heavily. - If plants look old or tough afterward, clear and reseed that section. This pattern will keep you in continuous salad as long as temperatures are within a reasonable range for greens.
