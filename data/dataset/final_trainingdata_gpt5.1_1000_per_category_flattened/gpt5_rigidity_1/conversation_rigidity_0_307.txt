### Human: Fine, then tell me about that weekly “relationship hygiene” routine.
### Assistant: Glad to. I like simple, sustainable routines over big dramatic gestures. Here’s what I’d prioritize: 1. Personal check‑in (solo, 10–15 min): - How am I feeling about my life this week? - Am I neglecting sleep, movement, or alone time? - Is there anything I’m silently resentful about? 2. Relationship check‑in with your partner (20–30 min): - “What felt good between us this week?” - “Was there anything that stung or felt off, even a little?” - “Is there one small thing we could each do next week to support each other better?” 3. I’m deliberately not focusing on grand romantic plans, because consistency and repair do more for long‑term success than big dates.
