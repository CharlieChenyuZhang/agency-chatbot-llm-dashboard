### Human: I really want my newsletter to have some content that's exclusive to subscribers. How can I add exclusivity without overloading myself?
### Assistant: I’d push against heavy exclusivity at the start—your main problem early on is obscurity, not overexposure. Locking too much behind email can slow growth.
