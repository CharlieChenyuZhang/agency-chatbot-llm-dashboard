### Human: Now, in no more than 5 bullet points, explain how I should fuel during the race. Nothing else.
### Assistant: - Eat a normal, carb-focused breakfast 3–4 hours before the race (something you practiced in training). - Start fueling early: take your first gel or equivalent carbs around 20–30 minutes into the race. - Continue with ~30–60 g of carbs per hour (for example, 1 gel every 30–40 minutes, or equivalent sports drink/chews). - Drink to thirst at aid stations, using mostly water plus some sports drink if tolerated. - Avoid trying any brand or type of gel, drink, or food you did not test on your long runs.
