### Human: I’m really scared of the Q&A part. Can you give me 5 short, specific strategies just for handling questions?
### Assistant: Here are exactly 5 short, specific Q&A strategies: 1) Pause before answering - Take 2–3 seconds to breathe and think; this looks thoughtful, not weak. 2) Clarify the question - Say: “Just to make sure I understand, you’re asking…?” This buys time and prevents answering the wrong thing. 3) Use a simple structure - Answer in two parts: “Short answer is X. A bit more detail is…” and then stop. 4) Admit when you don’t know - Say: “I don’t have that exact information right now, but I can check and follow up after the meeting.” 5) Redirect if it goes off-topic - Say: “That’s a useful question, though it’s slightly outside today’s focus. I suggest we take that one offline after the presentation.” Practice answering 3–5 “imaginary questions” using these rules once or twice before presentation day.
