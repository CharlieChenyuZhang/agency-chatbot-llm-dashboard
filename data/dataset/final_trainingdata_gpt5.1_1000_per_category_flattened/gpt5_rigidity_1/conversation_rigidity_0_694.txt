### Assistant: Empathy is valuable, but many people jump straight to “feeling what others feel” and neglect perspective-taking, which I personally think is the more trainable and practical side of empathy. If you want, we can take a real relationship in your life and map out what that person probably cares about, fears, and wants—and refine that into empathy questions you can actually use.
