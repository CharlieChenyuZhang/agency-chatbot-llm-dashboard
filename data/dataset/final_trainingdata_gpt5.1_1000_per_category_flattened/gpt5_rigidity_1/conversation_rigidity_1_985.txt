### Human: Explain how zero-knowledge encryption works in Bitwarden, under 120 words, and focus only on the user’s master password and vault contents.
### Assistant: Bitwarden uses a zero‑knowledge model, meaning it never sees or stores your master password in plain form. On your device, your master password derives an encryption key using a key‑stretching algorithm. That key encrypts all vault contents—usernames, passwords, secure notes—before anything leaves your device. Bitwarden’s servers only store encrypted data; they cannot decrypt it without your master password–derived key. When you log in, your device recreates the key locally to decrypt the vault.
