### Human: Explain in simple terms how my body actually burns fat during this plan. Keep it under 120 words and use plain language.
### Assistant: Your body stores extra energy as fat. When you eat fewer calories than you burn, it still needs energy, so it starts pulling it out of these fat stores. Hormones (like insulin going down, adrenaline and others going up a bit) signal your fat cells to release stored fat into the blood. Your muscles, heart, and other organs then use that fat for fuel, especially during low–moderate activity and between meals. Strength training helps you keep muscle, so more of the weight you lose is fat. Cardio and daily movement increase how much energy you use, so your body has to tap into fat more often.
