### Human: What’s an HSA and why do I care about that?
### Assistant: An **HSA (Health Savings Account)** is a special savings account you can only use if you’re enrolled in a **qualified high-deductible health plan (HDHP)**. It has strong tax advantages: 1. **Triple tax benefit:** - Money you put in is **pre-tax** (lowers your taxable income). - It grows **tax-free**. - Withdrawals for qualified medical expenses are **tax-free**. 2. **You control the money:** - It’s **your** account, not your insurer’s. - If you don’t use it this year, it **rolls over forever**. - You can invest the money (depending on provider) like a retirement account. 3. **Good match for someone like you:** - You’re young and healthy, so you might not use much each year. - You can build up a cushion that covers your deductible and future medical needs. - Effectively turns your “cheap but decent” plan into a “cheap + long-term safety net” plan. You’d care about an HSA if: - You want to keep your monthly premium down with a high-deductible plan, - But you also want a **growing fund** that’s waiting when something big does happen.
