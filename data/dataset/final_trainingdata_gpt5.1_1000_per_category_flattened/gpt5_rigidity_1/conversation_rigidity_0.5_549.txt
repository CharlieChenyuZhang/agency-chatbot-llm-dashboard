### Human: Give me a strict recipe for classic chocolate chip cookies. No substitutions, no “optional” ingredients, just exactly what I should use.
### Assistant: Understood. Here is a straightforward, classic chocolate chip cookie recipe with no substitutions or optional ingredients. Strict Chocolate Chip Cookies (No Substitutions) Ingredients: - 2 1/4 cups (280 g) all-purpose flour - 1 teaspoon baking soda - 1 teaspoon fine salt - 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature - 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar - 3/4 cup (165 g) packed light brown sugar - 2 large eggs (about 100 g without shell) - 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract - 2 cups (340 g) semisweet chocolate chips Instructions: 1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper. 2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt until well combined. Set aside. 3. In a large bowl, using a hand mixer or stand mixer with the paddle attachment, beat the softened butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar together on medium speed until light and creamy, about 2–3 minutes. 4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating on medium speed after each addition until fully incorporated. Add the vanilla extract and mix just until combined. 5. Reduce the mixer speed to low. Gradually add the flour mixture, mixing just until no dry flour remains. Do not overmix. 6. Using a spatula, fold in the chocolate chips until they are evenly distributed throughout the dough. 7. Scoop the dough into rounded tablespoons (about 1 1/2 tablespoons each) and place them at least 2 inches (5 cm) apart on the prepared baking sheets. 8. Bake, one sheet at a time, in the preheated oven for 9–11 minutes, until the edges are lightly golden and the centers still look slightly soft. 9. Remove from the oven and let the cookies cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes, then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely. This is the strict recipe as requested—no substitutions or optional ingredients included.
