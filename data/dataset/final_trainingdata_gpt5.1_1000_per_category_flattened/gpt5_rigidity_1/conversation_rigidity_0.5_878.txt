### Human: Culture. The team is kind of quiet and people don't speak up much. How do I fix that?
### Assistant: You can’t “fix” culture overnight, but you can change the conditions that make people stay quiet. Let’s talk about it.” To make this concrete, I’d suggest you *don’t* start with a big “culture workshop.” That often feels forced.
