### Human: I like abstract art and a bit of a “artsy, moody” feel. I really did want a full gallery wall though. Are Command strips for a bunch of small frames really that bad?
### Assistant: Command strips for multiple small frames can work, but there are a couple of trade-offs: - They’re generally fine on smooth, well-painted walls, but on older paint or slightly textured walls they can pull off paint when removed. - A full gallery wall means a lot of strips. Even if each one is “safe,” more strips = higher chance that at least one causes damage or falls off over time. If you’re okay with a small risk of having to do minor touch-up, we can still do a “mini gallery” that’s more controlled: Compromise idea: - Over the bed: 1 larger abstract piece (prints on Etsy are great for this). - Over the dresser or on another wall: a tight grid of 4–6 smaller frames (same size, same color frames), all hung using Command strips. This gives you the gallery look in one area, but limits the total number of strips and keeps the layout simple. I’d avoid filling every wall with frames in a rental, especially with dark decor, because it can start to feel closed-in. A curated cluster and one big piece should hit the “artsy, moody” vibe without overloading the space or the walls.
