### Human: Now quickly explain what progressive overload is in this context.
### Assistant: Progressive overload here means gradually increasing training stress over time so your body adapts and your deadlift gets stronger. In this plan, that happens by: - Increasing load (more kg on the bar over weeks). - Slightly reducing reps while raising intensity (moving from 4×5 at lighter % to heavy doubles/singles at higher %). - Using harder variations (e.g., deficits, paused deadlifts) at moderate loads to challenge your start position and technique. All of it is planned so you’re doing a bit more (or a bit harder) work over the 12 weeks without random jumps.
