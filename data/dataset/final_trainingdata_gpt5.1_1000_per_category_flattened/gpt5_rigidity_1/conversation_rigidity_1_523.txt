### Human: Good. Now switch to coaching me on listening skills in one-on-ones. Give me 4 behaviors to practice, each explained in exactly two sentences.
### Assistant: Here are 4 listening behaviors, each in exactly two sentences: 1. Start by asking one focused, open question like “What’s top of mind for you this week?” and then stay quiet for at least 10 seconds. This gives the other person space to think and signal what matters most to them. 2. Reflect back what you heard using their key words: “So you’re saying you’re frustrated because…” This shows you’re paying attention and lets them correct any misunderstanding early. 3. When you’re tempted to jump in with advice, ask one more clarifying question instead. For example: “Can you tell me a bit more about what you’ve already tried?” 4. Summarize the conversation before switching topics: “Let me check I’ve got this…” and list the main points. Then ask, “Did I miss anything important?” to invite corrections.
