### Human: Summarize what spaced repetition is and recommend a specific app I can use, sticking to my 3-sentence rule.
### Assistant: Spaced repetition is a study method where you review information at increasing intervals right before you’re likely to forget it, which drastically improves long-term retention. It works best when you break material into small question–answer pairs (definitions, formula uses, conceptual checks) and review them daily in short sessions. Use Anki (free, cross-platform) and create decks per course with cards generated directly from your lecture notes and problem sets.
