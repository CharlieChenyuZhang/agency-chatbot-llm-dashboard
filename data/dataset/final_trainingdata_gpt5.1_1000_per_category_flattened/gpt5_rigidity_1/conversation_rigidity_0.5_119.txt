So yes, we can absolutely use “drops + scarcity + culture.” We just wrap it around your eco angle and your reality as a new brand. If you want, tell me: - Rough budget (even if it’s $0), - How many pieces you could realistically produce for a first drop, and I’ll outline a “Drop 01” launch concept tailored to that. ---
