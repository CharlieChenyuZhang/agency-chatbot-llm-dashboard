### Human: I also don't want to edit. Can I just upload raw audio?
### Assistant: You *can*, but here’s the tradeoff: - Pros: Faster, less friction, you’ll actually publish. - Cons: Uhms, long pauses, volume jumps, background noise—these make people stop listening. Middle-ground options that keep it easy: 1. **Use an app that auto-cleans audio.** Tools like Descript, Riverside, or some mobile recording apps can auto-level volume and reduce noise with 1–2 clicks. 2. **Super light edit rule:** - Only cut: - Big mistakes (e.g., you totally lose your train of thought) - Long silences (over 3–4 seconds) Leave everything else in. If you tell me what device you’re using (phone/computer and OS), I’ll suggest 1–2 specific tools and a “10-minute edit” workflow so you’re not spending hours.

Over time: - Consistent weekly episodes - Titles and descriptions that clearly signal what each episode is about - Maybe guest spots or collabs later If you want, I can help you write a short, compelling trailer script and a couple of social posts that don’t sound desperate or spammy.
