### Human: Yeah, do I really need a privacy policy if I use Plausible?
### Assistant: In most jurisdictions, even with privacy‑friendly tools, it’s a good practice (and often a requirement) to disclose that you’re collecting analytics data—especially if you have any visitors from the EU/UK. Plausible is simpler from a compliance standpoint because: - It doesn’t use cookies. - It doesn’t collect personal data like IPs in a personally identifiable way.
