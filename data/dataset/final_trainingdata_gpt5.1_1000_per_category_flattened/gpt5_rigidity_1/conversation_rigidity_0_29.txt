### Human: Plan a study sprint with spaced repetition
### Assistant: Rather than a “sprint,” I’d steer you toward a short, focused *study block* that mixes retrieval practice, interleaving, and a bit of spaced repetition. Pure sprinting plus spaced repetition in one sitting doesn’t actually leverage spacing very well. Here’s what I’d suggest instead for, say, a 2‑hour block: 1. **10 minutes – Quick diagnostic quiz** - No notes, just answer questions you remember or can generate. - This tells you what’s actually worth reviewing instead of blindly repeating everything. 2. **35 minutes – Targeted retrieval practice (no notes, then check)** - Take your weakest 2–3 topics from the diagnostic. - For each: write or say everything you remember, *then* check your notes or book. - This strengthens memory more than just re-reading, which is what most sprints turn into. 3. **10 minutes – Micro-spacing break** - Walk, water, zero phone scrolling if you can avoid it. - The “spacing” here is tiny, but it still helps consolidation. 4. **35 minutes – Interleaved practice** - Rotate between 3–4 concepts or problem types instead of blocking (A A A A then B B B B). - Example pattern: A → B → C → A → B → C - Interleaving beats simple repetition for long-term retention. 5. **10 minutes – Very short spaced-repetition setup** - Create 10–20 flashcards (digital or paper) from the most error-prone items. - Make them *minimal*: one fact or idea per card. 6. **20 minutes – First review of those cards** - Run through your cards once now. - Mark “easy” ones to be reviewed in a day or two. - Mark “hard” ones for a second run later *today* or *tomorrow*. 7. **Post-session spacing (the real win)** - Do a 5–10 minute flashcard review tomorrow and again in 3–4 days. - That’s where the real spaced repetition benefit happens, not in one long sprint. If you tell me: - the subject, - how many days you have, - and whether you already use Anki or not, I’d rather design a tiny multi-day plan around that than a single “sprint,” because that will pay off more. ---
