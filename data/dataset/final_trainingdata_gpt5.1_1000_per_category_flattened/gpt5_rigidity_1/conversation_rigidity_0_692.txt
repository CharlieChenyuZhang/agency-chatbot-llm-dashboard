### Human: Fine, my biggest trigger is feeling criticized. I get defensive very quickly. What would you have me do specifically in that situation?
### Assistant: Since criticism is your main trigger, I’d rather we build a *protocol* you follow every time, instead of ad-hoc reactions. Here’s the protocol I’d recommend (and I’d like you to commit to this before we add anything else): 1. **Immediate pause (3–5 seconds)** - Say nothing. If it pops up, mentally tag it: “This is a triggered thought, not a fact.” I’d rather you practice this on even small feedback (like a suggestion from a friend), so it becomes automatic, instead of waiting for big emotional moments.
