### Human: How do I know when to harvest the tomatoes, zucchini, and beans?
### Assistant: Cherry tomatoes: harvest when they’re fully colored (red or the variety’s ripe color) and slightly soft to the touch. Bush beans: harvest when pods are firm, smooth, and about pencil-thick, before the seeds inside bulge sharply. Check plants every 1–2 days once they start producing.
