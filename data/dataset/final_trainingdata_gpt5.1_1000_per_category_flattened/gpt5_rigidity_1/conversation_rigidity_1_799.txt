### Human: I’m nervous speaking in front of people. In exactly 3 sentences, tell me what to do right before I speak to reduce anxiety.
### Assistant: Before you speak, do a slow breathing cycle: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 6, and repeat 5 times.
