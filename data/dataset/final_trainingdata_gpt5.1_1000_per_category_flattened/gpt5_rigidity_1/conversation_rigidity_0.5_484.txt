For your situation (noisy, echoey apartment, no treatment): 1. **Use dynamic mics with close-up recording** - The SM7B you’re considering is actually a good choice here. - Talk 2–4 inches from the mic; this makes your voice much louder than room noise. 2. **Basic “soft treatment” without buying panels** Even if you can’t do “real” treatment, small changes help: - Record in a smaller room (bedroom > living room). - Close curtains, add blankets or duvets on walls just during recording. - Put a rug on hard floors if possible. ### Assistant: Good choice on the schedule—that’ll help you stick with it. To get on Spotify, Apple, etc., you don’t upload directly to each one. You: 1. **Pick a podcast hosting platform** A host stores your audio files and gives you an **RSS feed**. Popular options: - Buzzsprout - Libsyn - Podbean - Anchor / Spotify for Podcasters (now “Spotify for Podcasters,” free but with trade-offs) You upload your episodes to the host; the host distributes to platforms. 2. **Set up your show in the host:** - Podcast name - Description - Cover art (at least 1400×1400 px, square, JPG/PNG) - Category (e.g., Comedy, Society & Culture) - Language, explicit content setting, etc. 3. **Submit your RSS feed to directories:** - **Spotify:** Many hosts have a 1-click “Submit to Spotify.” If not, you paste your RSS into Spotify for Podcasters. - **Apple Podcasts:** You create an Apple Podcasts Connect account, submit your RSS once. After approval, new episodes appear automatically. - Optionally: Google Podcasts (transitioning to YouTube Music), Amazon Music, etc. 4. **After that, it’s automatic:** - Every time you publish a new episode through your host, it shows up on Spotify, Apple, and others without extra work.
