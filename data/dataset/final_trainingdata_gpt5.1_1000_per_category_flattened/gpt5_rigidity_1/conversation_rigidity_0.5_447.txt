### Human: Give me a minimal contract checklist, but keep it short and in plain English.
### Assistant: Here’s a short checklist you can run through in a few minutes before signing anything: Your bathroom remodel contract should clearly state: 1. **Who and where** - Contractor’s full **business name**, address, and license info (if applicable) - Your name and property address 2. **What they’re doing** - A simple description like: “Remove existing vanity, toilet, flooring, and tub/shower surround.
