### Assistant: Yes. So, if: - A system explicitly demands **CSV** (like your CRM), use CSV as we’ve outlined. - A person or system says “Excel or CSV is fine,” I’d lean toward: - **Excel** if they’re analyzing, editing, or maintaining relationships/formulas. - **CSV** if it’s purely for machine import and they specify a strict format.
