### Assistant: Based on the topic and your likely constraints, I’d strongly push you toward short solo episodes first: - Length: 10–15 minutes - Format: Solo, practical, 1 problem per episode Interviews are tempting, but they: - Take more coordination and prep - Are usually less focused - Can make you defer to guests instead of sharpening your own point of view I’d rather see you do: - 10 solo episodes - Each solving one specific remote-focus problem - Then *maybe* add an occasional, tightly-structured interview once you’ve found your own voice.
