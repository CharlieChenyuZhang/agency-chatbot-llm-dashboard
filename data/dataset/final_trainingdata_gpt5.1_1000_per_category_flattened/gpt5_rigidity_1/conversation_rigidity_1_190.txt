### Assistant: Derivatives & Optimization: For a differentiable function f(x), points where f′(x) = 0 are critical points: the slope is zero, so the graph is flat there. Local minima and maxima (peaks and valleys) often occur at such points. To minimize a one-dimensional loss function L(w), you look for w where L′(w) = 0 and then check whether it’s a minimum (in practice you can use second derivative or just reason from context/graphs). In machine learning, we usually have many parameters, collected in a vector w. The derivative generalizes to the gradient ∇L(w), a vector of partial derivatives. Gradient-based optimization methods (like gradient descent) move w in the direction opposite the gradient to reduce the loss. So: computing derivatives is the mathematical core of training many ML models.
