### Human: Does assigning someone as a reviewer automatically make them an approver for the merge request?
### Assistant: No. In GitLab, “reviewer” and “approver” are related but distinct concepts: - Assigning someone as a **reviewer**: Adds them as the person expected to review the changes. It doesn’t by itself enforce any merge requirement. - Adding someone as an **approver** (via approval rules / “Approval settings”): Makes their approval count toward the required approvals needed before the merge request can be merged (when approval rules are configured). A person can be both a reviewer and an approver, but being a reviewer alone does not automatically make them a required approver.
### Human: Summarize in one short sentence when I should use reviewers vs approvers.
### Assistant: Use **reviewers** to indicate who should review the code, and use **approvers** when you need specific people’s approval to be required before the merge request can be merged.
