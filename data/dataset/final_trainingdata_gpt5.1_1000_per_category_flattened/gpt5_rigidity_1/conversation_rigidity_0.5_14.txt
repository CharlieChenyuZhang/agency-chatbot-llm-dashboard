### Assistant: With a history of knee issues, **heavy jump squats and lots of high-impact plyos are risky**, especially when you’re also increasing leg training frequency. They don’t speed fat loss directly—calorie balance does—so the risk-to-reward ratio isn’t great. Instead, here’s a safer approach that still gives you athletic work: #### Lower-Impact Alternatives - **Power-focused but knee-friendlier options:** - Hip-dominant swings with a dumbbell (like a kettlebell swing) – moderate impact. - Step-up “power” variations (step up fast, control down slowly) with low height. - Band-resisted glute bridges (explosive hip thrusts, controlled lowering). - **Conditioning without pounding knees:** - Brisk walking (incline if on a treadmill). - Cycling (stationary or outdoors). - Low-impact step-ups (lower box/bench, controlled tempo). - Simple circuits: e.g., - 30 sec DB RDL (light) - 30 sec band rows - 30 sec fast marching in place - Rest 60 sec, repeat 4–6 rounds We can slot 1–2 short “power/conditioning” blocks into your Lower B and your cardio day, but I would not recommend heavy jump squats in your case.
