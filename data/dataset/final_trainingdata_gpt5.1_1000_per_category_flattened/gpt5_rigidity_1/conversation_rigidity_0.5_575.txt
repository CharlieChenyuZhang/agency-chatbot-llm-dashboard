Adaptation means adjusting to the effects that are already happening or are likely to happen. Examples include building stronger flood defenses, improving water management in drought-prone areas, designing heat-resilient cities with more green spaces, and planning crops that can handle new climate conditions. Both mitigation and adaptation need cooperation from governments, businesses, and individuals worldwide.
