### Human: The shaky voice bothers me the most, because I feel like everyone can hear how scared I am. Can I just learn to control my voice so it doesn’t shake at all?
### Assistant: You can **reduce** the shaking and make it much less noticeable, but aiming for “no shaking at all” can backfire and make you hyper-focused on it. More realistic and workable goals: - Make your voice **strong enough** that small shakes don’t stand out. - Have a plan for **what to do when it starts shaking**, so you don’t spiral. Here are practical things you can do: #### 1. Breathing for voice support (1–2 minutes) Twice a day and before you speak: - Put a hand on your belly. - Inhale through your nose for 4 seconds, feeling your belly expand (not your shoulders). - Exhale through your mouth for 6 seconds, steady and controlled. This supports your voice from your diaphragm, which stabilizes it. #### 2. Warm-up out loud (2 minutes) Before the presentation (or practice), in a private place: - Read 3–4 sentences from anything (email, article) **slightly slower and louder** than normal. - Then say a few phrases from your talk, e.g.: - “Here’s where we are on the project.” - “These are the main risks we’re tracking.” Spoken slowly, clearly, with a full exhale. #### 3. In-the-moment tricks when it starts shaking During the talk, if you feel your voice wobble: - **Slow your pace** by half a notch and deliberately **pause to breathe** at the end of a sentence. - **Anchor your body**: lightly press your feet into the floor and keep your knees soft. Physical stability helps vocal stability. - Use “reset phrases” you can say calmly while taking a breath, like: - “So, to summarize this point…” - “What this really means is…” Most people are much less aware of your voice shaking than you are. They’re listening for content, not assessing your anxiety. If you want, you can paste one or two sentences from the start of your presentation, and I can help you rewrite them into wording that feels natural to you and is easier to say clearly and confidently. ---
