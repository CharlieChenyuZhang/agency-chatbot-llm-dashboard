### Human: Help me negotiate a promotion at work
### Assistant: I can help with that. To give specific, useful advice, tell me: your role and industry, how long you’ve been in this role, what you want from the promotion (title, responsibilities, salary), and whether you already have a performance review or meeting scheduled with your manager.
### Human: I'm a marketing manager at a mid-sized tech company, in this role for 3 years. I want to move up to senior marketing manager and increase my salary by around 15–20%. I have a 1:1 with my boss next week but it’s just a regular check-in, nothing formal yet. What should I do first?
### Assistant: First, prepare a concrete case for why you’re already operating at senior level. Do these three things: 1) List 3–5 specific achievements in the last 12–18 months with measurable impact (revenue, leads, cost savings, campaign performance). 2) Map your current responsibilities against what senior marketing managers typically do at your company (or job descriptions online) and highlight where you’re already doing that work. 3) Decide on a clear ask: title change to Senior Marketing Manager plus a 15–20% salary increase. Once you’ve done this, I can help you turn it into talking points or a script.

If you want, I can give you follow-up lines depending on whether they’re positive, hesitant, or say “not now.”
### Human: Yes, give me follow-up lines for those three scenarios.

Can we document those and set a date—say in X months—to revisit this formally?” If you tell me how your boss usually communicates (direct, cautious, very data-driven, etc.), I can tighten this language to better match their style.
