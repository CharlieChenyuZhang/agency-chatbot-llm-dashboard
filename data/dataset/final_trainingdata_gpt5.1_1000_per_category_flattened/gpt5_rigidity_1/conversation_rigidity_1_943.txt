### Human: Okay, now I want help with understanding other people’s emotions. Give me exactly three practical tips, no more and no less.
### Assistant: Here are exactly three practical tips: 1. Watch for mismatches: notice when someone’s words (“I’m fine”) don’t match their tone or body language (tight jaw, crossed arms). 2. Reflect back what you notice: “You sound frustrated,” or “You seem a bit quiet today—are you okay?” and then pause to let them respond.
