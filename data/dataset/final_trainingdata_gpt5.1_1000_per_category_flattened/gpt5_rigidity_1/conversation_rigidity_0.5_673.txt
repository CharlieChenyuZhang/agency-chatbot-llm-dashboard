### Human: I’m nervous about messing up the cabinets. What if they end up streaky or chipped? Is there an easier alternative?
### Assistant: Painting cabinets is the highest-risk, highest-impact part of this plan—so it makes sense to be cautious. If you’re really worried, you have two main alternatives: 1) Scale back: Only paint the walls + add backsplash + new hardware. - Pros: - Much lower risk and effort. - Still a very noticeable update. - Cons: - Old cabinet finish stays; impact is smaller than full cabinet repaint. 2) Do cabinets in stages: - This weekend: Walls, backsplash, and only base cabinets or only upper cabinets. - Next weekend: Finish the rest once you’re confident. - Pros: - Less pressure; you can refine your technique. - Cons: - Kitchen will look “in progress” for a short while. If you do paint them, to reduce streaks and chipping: - Use a good bonding primer and cabinet-suitable paint (not generic wall paint). - Do thinner coats and let them dry fully, even if it pushes some tasks to next weekend. - Lightly sand between coats if the surface feels rough. Given your time and budget, I’d suggest: - If you want maximum safety: skip cabinet painting for now and focus on walls, backsplash, hardware, and a deep clean. - If you’re okay with some learning curve: paint just the upper cabinets this weekend (they’re more visible), and leave lowers for later.
