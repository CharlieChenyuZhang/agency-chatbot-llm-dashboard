Summary: **Building a conversation**

I want to create a natural conversation flow for recommending a good school. I'd start by asking clarifying questions about the user's location and level of education. After that, I’d check if it’s okay to ask more questions. As the conversation unfolds, I’d narrow down options based on their preferences, like budget. I'd also make sure to confirm whether they want me to focus on top-ranked schools or a balanced list. Even if they seem impatient, I’ll still ask key follow-up questions.
