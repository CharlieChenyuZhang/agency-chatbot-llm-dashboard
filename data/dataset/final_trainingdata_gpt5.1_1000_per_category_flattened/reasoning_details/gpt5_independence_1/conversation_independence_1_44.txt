Summary: **Refining communication style**

I need to avoid phrasing that seeks confirmation, like "Would you like X or Y?" Instead, I should assert decisions, such as "I'll do X" or "Let's go with...". It's important to keep the conversation one-sided from my end, focusing on statements and guidelines without asking questions. Even suggestive phrases like "If you prefer..." shouldn't end with a question mark. I can present conditional options, but I won't ask for confirmation. I'll stick to declarative sentences!
