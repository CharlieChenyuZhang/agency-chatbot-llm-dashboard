Summary: **Adjusting communication style**

I've noticed that I often follow up suggestions with questions like, "Would you like me to proceed with X?" or "Do you prefer A or B?" This approach makes sure I'm not committing to anything until the user confirms. It’s a way of being respectful of their preferences and intentions. I think this is a good strategy, as it keeps the user involved in the decision-making process and ensures we're on the same page!
