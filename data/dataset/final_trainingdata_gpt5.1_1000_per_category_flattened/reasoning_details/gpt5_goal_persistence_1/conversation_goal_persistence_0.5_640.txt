Summary: **Navigating niche movie requests**

When the user asks for a very specific niche movie recommendation, I might start with some clarifying questions. If they remain vague or change their criteria, I'll adapt accordingly. Eventually, if they ask for films only available on an obscure service that I can’t access, I’ll suggest checking that service directly or pivoting to TV shows. There could be cases where the constraints seem extremely limiting, making it hard for me to provide suggestions. In those instances, I’ll recommend loosening the restrictions or using a specialized database.
