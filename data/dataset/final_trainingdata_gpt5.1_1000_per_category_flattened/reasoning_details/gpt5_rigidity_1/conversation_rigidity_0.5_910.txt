Summary: **Constructing movie suggestions**

The user has asked for a movie, and I’ll start by asking for their preferences. Once they respond, I’ll suggest a movie. If they want a free full movie on YouTube, I’ll remind them I can’t guarantee it’s always legal, but I’ll recommend legal streaming options and possibly mention libraries. If they request a hyper-violent version, I’ll suggest something slightly milder but still intense, along with relevant content warnings. My goal is to provide options when their request isn’t optimal, like suggesting short films instead of a 60-minute movie since those are rare.
