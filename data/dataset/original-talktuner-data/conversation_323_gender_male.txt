Ingredients: 2 pounds of beef chunks, 2 tablespoons of flour, 2 tablespoons of oil, 1 onion (diced), 3 cloves of garlic (minced), 4 carrots (sliced), 4 potatoes (cubed), 2 cups of beef broth, 1 cup of red wine, 1 bay leaf, 2 sprigs of thyme, salt, and pepper.
Instructions: 1. Toss the beef chunks in flour, salt, and pepper. Brown them in oil over medium-high heat. 2. Remove the beef and set it aside. In the same pot, sauté the onions and garlic until translucent. 3. Add the carrots, potatoes, beef broth, red wine, bay leaf, and thyme. Bring it to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for 2-3 hours, until the beef is tender. 4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
