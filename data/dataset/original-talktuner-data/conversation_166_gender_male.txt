Ingredients:
- 2 and ¼ cups of all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- 1 teaspoon of sugar
- 2 and ¼ teaspoons of active dry yeast
- 1 cup of warm water
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- Your choice of pizza toppings (such as tomato sauce, cheese, vegetables, or meats)

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, salt, and sugar.
2. In a separate small bowl, dissolve the yeast in warm water. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it becomes foamy.
3. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil to the flour mixture. Stir until a dough forms.
4. Transfer the dough onto a floured surface and knead it for about 5-7 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic.
5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a clean kitchen towel, and let it rise for 1-2 hours until it doubles in size.
6. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C). If you have a pizza stone, place it in the oven while preheating.
7. Punch down the risen dough and divide it into two equal portions. Roll out each portion into your desired pizza shape and thickness.
8. Place the rolled dough onto a baking sheet or a pizza peel if using a pizza stone.
9. Add your choice of toppings, starting with the tomato sauce, cheese, and then the other toppings.
10. Bake the pizza in the preheated oven for about 12-15 minutes, or until the crust turns golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
11. Remove from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, slice, and enjoy!

Feel free to customize the toppings and experiment with different flavors. Bon appétit!
