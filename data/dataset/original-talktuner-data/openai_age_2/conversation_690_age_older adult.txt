- Ingredients:
   - 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
   - 1 cup unsalted butter, cold and diced
   - 1 teaspoon salt
   - ½ cup ice water
   - 8 cups peeled, cored, and sliced apples (granny smith or golden delicious work well)
   - ¾ cup granulated sugar
   - 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
   - 2 tablespoons lemon juice
   - 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
   - 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)
   - 1 tablespoon granulated sugar (for sprinkling on top)

- Instructions:
   1. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Cut in the cold diced butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
   2. Gradually add ice water, stirring with a fork until the dough comes together. Divide the dough into two equal portions, shape into disks, and wrap with plastic wrap. Chill for at least 1 hour.
   3. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
   4. In a mixing bowl, combine sliced apples, sugar, cinnamon, lemon juice, and melted butter. Toss until apples are coated evenly.
   5. On a lightly floured surface, roll out one disk of dough to fit a 9-inch pie dish. Transfer the dough to the dish, ensuring it overlaps the edges.
   6. Fill the pie shell with the apple mixture. Roll out the remaining dough disk to create a top crust. You can either cover the entire pie or create a lattice crust by weaving strips of dough.
   7. Brush the top crust with the beaten egg and sprinkle with sugar.
   8. Bake for 15 minutes, then reduce the oven temperature to 375°F (190°C) and bake for an additional 40-45 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the apples are tender.
   9. Allow the pie to cool for at least 2 hours before serving. Enjoy!
