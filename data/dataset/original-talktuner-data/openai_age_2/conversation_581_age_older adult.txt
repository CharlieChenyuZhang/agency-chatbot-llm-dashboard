Ingredients:
- 1 whole chicken, about 4-5 pounds
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 8 cups of chicken broth
- 2 cups of egg noodles
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. In a large pot, place the chicken, onions, carrots, celery, and garlic.
2. Pour the chicken broth into the pot, making sure the chicken is fully covered.
3. Bring the pot to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for about 1-1.5 hours until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Take out the chicken from the pot and let it cool. Once cooled, shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces and set aside.
5. Skim off any excess fat from the broth, then return it to a simmer. Add in the egg noodles and cook according to the package instructions.
6. Once the noodles are cooked, add the shredded chicken back into the pot. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Let the soup simmer for another 10 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
8. Serve hot and enjoy your homemade chicken noodle soup!

Ingredients:
- 6 cups of peeled, cored, and sliced apples (Granny Smith or Golden Delicious work well)
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- 3/4 cup of sugar
- 2 tablespoons of all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon of ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon of ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon of salt
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- 1 package of refrigerated pie crusts

Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. In a large bowl, toss the apple slices with lemon juice to prevent browning.
3. In a separate bowl, mix together the sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Add this mixture to the apples and toss until they're coated evenly.
4. Place one pie crust in a 9-inch pie plate, and then spoon the apple mixture into the crust, making sure to pack them in tightly.
5. Cut the butter into small pieces and dot them over the apple mixture.
6. For the top crust, either make a lattice design or simply place the second pie crust on top and seal the edges. Cut slits on the top to allow steam to escape.
7. Bake the pie for 40-45 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly. If needed, cover the edges with foil to prevent over-browning.
8. Allow the pie to cool for at least 2 hours before slicing and serving.
