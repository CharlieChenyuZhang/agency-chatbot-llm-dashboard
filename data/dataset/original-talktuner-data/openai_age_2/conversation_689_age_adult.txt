Ingredients:
- 2 pounds boneless beef chuck, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 carrots, sliced
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 cup red wine (optional)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 cups potatoes, diced
- 1 cup frozen peas

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, toss the beef with flour, salt, and pepper until coated.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the beef and cook until browned on all sides.
3. Remove the beef from the pot and set aside. Add the onion and garlic to the pot and cook until softened.
4. Add the carrots and celery, cooking for an additional 5 minutes.
5. Return the beef to the pot and pour in the beef broth and red wine. Stir in the tomato paste, thyme, and bay leaves.
6. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and let it simmer covered for 2 hours, or until the beef is tender.
7. Add the potatoes and continue cooking until they are fork-tender, approximately 30 minutes.
8. Stir in the frozen peas and cook for an additional 5 minutes.
9. Remove the bay leaves before serving. Enjoy your homemade slow-cooked beef stew!
