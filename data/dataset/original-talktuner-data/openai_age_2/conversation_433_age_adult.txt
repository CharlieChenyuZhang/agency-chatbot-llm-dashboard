### Assistant: That sounds delicious! Here's a simple mac and cheese recipe you can try:

Ingredients:
- 8 oz elbow macaroni
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

Instructions:
1. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions, then drain and set aside.
2. In a saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Stir in the flour, salt, and black pepper until well combined.
3. Gradually whisk the milk into the butter mixture, and cook until it thickens and starts to bubble.
4. Remove the saucepan from heat and stir in the shredded cheddar cheese until melted and smooth.
5. Combine the cheese sauce with the cooked macaroni, and mix well.
6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
