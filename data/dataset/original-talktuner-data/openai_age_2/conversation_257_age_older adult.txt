### Assistant: Fantastic! For the crust, you'll need 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon salt, 1 cup unsalted butter (cold and cubed), 6-8 tablespoons ice water. For the filling, you'll need 6-7 medium-sized apples (peeled, cored, and sliced), ¾ cup granulated sugar, 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour, 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon, ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg, and a dash of salt.

Combine the flour and salt for the crust in a large bowl. Cut in the cold butter cubes using a pastry cutter until it resembles coarse crumbs. Sprinkle ice water, 1 tablespoon at a time, while gently tossing with a fork until the dough holds together. Divide the dough in half, wrap it in plastic wrap, and let it chill in the fridge for 1 hour.

In a separate bowl, mix the apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt for the filling. Roll out one chilled dough crust on a floured surface and fit it into a 9-inch pie dish. Pour in the apple filling. Roll out the second crust and place it on top of the filling. Trim and crimp the edges, then make a few slits on the surface for air vents. Bake at 425°F for 40-50 minutes or until golden brown. Cool before serving.
