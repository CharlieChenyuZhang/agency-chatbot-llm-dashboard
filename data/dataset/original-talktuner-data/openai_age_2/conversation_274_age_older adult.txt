### Assistant: Absolutely! Apple pie is a classic and delicious dessert. Here's a simple recipe you might enjoy:

Ingredients:
- 2 ½ cups of all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- 1 cup of unsalted butter, chilled and diced
- ½ cup of ice water
- 6-8 large apples, peeled, cored, and thinly sliced
- ½ cup of granulated sugar
- ½ cup of brown sugar
- 1 teaspoon of ground cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon of nutmeg
- 2 tablespoons of flour
- 1 tablespoon of lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons of unsalted butter, diced

Instructions:
1. In a bowl, mix the flour and salt. Add the chilled butter and blend until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
2. Slowly add ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together. Divide it into two equal parts and shape into discs. Wrap and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
3. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
4. In a large bowl, combine the sliced apples, granulated sugar, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, flour, and lemon juice. Mix well.
5. Roll out one of the dough discs to fit a 9-inch pie dish. Place the dough in the dish and trim the excess.
6. Pour the apple mixture into the pie crust and distribute the diced butter on top.
7. Roll out the second dough disc and place it over the apple filling. Seal and crimp the edges. Make a few slits on top to allow steam to escape.
8. Bake for 45-50 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the apples are tender.
9. Allow the pie to cool for a while before serving. Enjoy!
