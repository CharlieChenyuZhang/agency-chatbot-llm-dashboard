### Human: Well, I've heard about stocks and bonds, but I'm not really sure how they work or what's the difference between them. Can you explain?

### Assistant: Absolutely! Stocks and bonds are two different types of investments. Stocks represent ownership in a company, and when you buy a stock, you become a shareholder. Bonds, on the other hand, are debt instruments issued by governments or corporations.
