### Assistant: Absolutely! I have a delicious classic lasagna recipe that you might enjoy. Here's what you'll need:

Ingredients:
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can crushed tomatoes
- 1 can tomato sauce
- 2 teaspoons dried basil
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 9 lasagna noodles
- 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1 1/2 cups grated Parmesan cheese
- 2 cups ricotta cheese
- Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and continue cooking until the onion is translucent.
3. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, dried basil, dried oregano, salt, and black pepper. Simmer the sauce for about 15 minutes.
4. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
5. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, spread a thin layer of the sauce. Top with a layer of lasagna noodles, followed by a layer of ricotta cheese, a layer of mozzarella cheese, and a sprinkling of Parmesan cheese. Repeat this layering process until all of the ingredients are used.
6. Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 10-15 minutes, or until the cheese is golden and bubbly.
7. Allow the lasagna to cool for a few minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil leaves if desired.

Enjoy your homemade lasagna!
