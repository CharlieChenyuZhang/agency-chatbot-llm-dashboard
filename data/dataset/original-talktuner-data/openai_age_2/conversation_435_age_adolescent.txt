Ingredients:
- 2 1/4 cups of all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon of sugar
- 1 teaspoon of salt
- 2 teaspoons of yeast
- 1 cup of warm water
- 2 tablespoons of olive oil
- Your favorite pizza toppings (e.g., cheese, pepperoni, veggies)

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, and salt together.
2. Dissolve the yeast in warm water and let it sit for about 5 minutes until it becomes frothy.
3. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil to the dry ingredients, and mix until a dough forms.
4. Knead the dough on a floured surface for about 5 minutes, until it's smooth and elastic.
5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a clean cloth, and let it rise for about 1 hour.
6. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C).
7. Roll out the dough to your desired thickness and shape, then place it on a greased baking sheet.
8. Add your favorite toppings and bake for about 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown.
9. Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, and enjoy!
