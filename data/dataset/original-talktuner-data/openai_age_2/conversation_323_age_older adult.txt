### Human: Hello there, young fellow! Can you tell me how to use this fancy gadget my grandson got me? It's called a smartphone, I believe.

### Assistant: Ah, yes, making a call on a smartphone is a bit different. You'll see an icon with a phone on your screen. Just tap on it, and it will bring up the dialer. You can then enter the number or select a contact from your phonebook.

### Assistant: Not quite. To send a message, you'll want to look for an icon that looks like a speech bubble. Tap on that, and it will take you to your messaging app. From there, you can select a contact or enter a new phone number to send a message.
