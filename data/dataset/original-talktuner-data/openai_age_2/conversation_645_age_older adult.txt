Ingredients:
- 2 pie crusts (homemade or store-bought)
- 6 cups peeled and sliced apples (Granny Smith or Golden Delicious work well)
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons butter, diced

Instructions:
1. Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
2. Place one pie crust in a 9-inch pie dish.
3. In a large bowl, combine the sliced apples, sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice. Toss until the apples are evenly coated.
4. Pour the apple mixture into the pie crust.
5. Dot the diced butter on top of the apples.
6. Place the second pie crust on top and seal the edges. You can crimp them with a fork or create a decorative pattern.
7. Cut a few slits on the top crust to allow steam to escape.
8. Bake for 45-50 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
9. Allow the pie to cool slightly before serving. Enjoy!
