### Assistant: That's a fantastic idea! Here's a simple recipe for spaghetti carbonara:

Ingredients:
- 200g spaghetti
- 100g pancetta or bacon, diced
- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 50g Parmesan cheese, grated
- Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:
1. Cook the spaghetti according to the instructions on the package. Drain and set aside.
2. In a large pan, cook the pancetta or bacon until crispy. Add the minced garlic and cook for another minute.
3. In a bowl, mix the beaten eggs, Parmesan cheese, salt, and pepper.
4. Add the drained spaghetti to the pan with the pancetta. Turn off the heat.
5. Pour the egg mixture over the spaghetti and quickly toss everything together until the heat from the pasta cooks the eggs and creates a creamy sauce.
6. Serve immediately with an extra sprinkle of Parmesan cheese on top.

Enjoy your delicious spaghetti carbonara!
