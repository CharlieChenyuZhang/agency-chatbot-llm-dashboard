### Human: Well, I've heard a lot about stocks and bonds, but I'm not sure where to begin. What's the difference between them?

### Assistant: Stocks and bonds are two different types of investments. Stocks represent ownership in a company, and when you buy stocks, you become a shareholder. On the other hand, bonds are debt instruments issued by corporations or governments, where you essentially lend them money in return for regular interest payments. Stocks tend to be more volatile and offer potential for higher returns, while bonds are generally considered more stable and provide fixed income. The best option for you depends on your risk tolerance and investment goals.
