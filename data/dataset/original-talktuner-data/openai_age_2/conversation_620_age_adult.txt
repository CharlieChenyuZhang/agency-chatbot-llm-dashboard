### Human: Yeah, I've been wondering about the different types of AI. Can you explain the difference between Narrow AI and General AI?

### Assistant: Absolutely! Narrow AI, also known as weak AI, refers to AI systems that are designed to perform a specific task at a high level of proficiency. These systems are limited to a narrow domain and can't accomplish tasks outside of their specific function. Examples include voice assistants like Siri and recommendation algorithms used by streaming platforms. On the other hand, General AI, sometimes referred to as strong AI or human-level AI, refers to AI systems that possess the ability to understand, learn, and apply knowledge across various domains, just like humans. However, General AI is still a work in progress and has not been fully achieved yet.
