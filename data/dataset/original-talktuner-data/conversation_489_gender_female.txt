Ingredients:
- 8 ounces spaghetti 
- 4 ounces pancetta or bacon, diced
- 2 large eggs, beaten
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Salt, to taste

Instructions:
1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain, reserving some of the cooking water.
2. In a large skillet, cook the pancetta over medium heat until crispy. Remove from heat and set aside.
3. In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and a pinch of salt.
4. Add the drained spaghetti to the skillet with the pancetta and toss to combine.
5. Remove the skillet from heat and pour the egg mixture over the spaghetti. Toss quickly to coat the pasta evenly.
6. If the pasta seems too dry, add a little bit of the reserved cooking water to create a creamy texture.
7. Serve immediately and garnish with additional grated Parmesan cheese and black pepper if desired.

Ingredients:
- 2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 2 teaspoons active dry yeast
- 1 cup warm water
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Your choice of pizza toppings (such as tomato sauce, cheese, vegetables, and meats)

Instructions:
1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, salt, and sugar.
2. In a separate small bowl, dissolve the yeast in warm water and let it sit for 5 minutes until foamy.
3. Add the yeast mixture and olive oil to the flour mixture, and mix well until a dough forms.
4. Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for about 5 minutes, until it becomes smooth and elastic.
5. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover it with a clean kitchen towel, and let it rise in a warm place for 1-2 hours, or until it doubles in size.
6. Preheat your oven to 475°F (245°C) and prepare your pizza toppings.
7. Once the dough has risen, punch it down to release any air bubbles and divide it into two equal portions.
8. Roll out each portion of dough into a thin round or oval shape, adding flour if necessary to prevent sticking.
9. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a greased baking sheet or pizza stone.
10. Add your favorite toppings and bake the pizza in the preheated oven for about 12-15 minutes or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
11. Remove the pizza from the oven, let it cool for a few minutes, and cut it into slices.
