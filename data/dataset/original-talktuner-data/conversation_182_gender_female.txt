Ingredients:
- 1 lb. boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced
- 2 tbsp. oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red chili, chopped
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves
- 2 tbsp. soy sauce
- 1 tbsp. oyster sauce
- 1 tsp. sugar

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
2. Add garlic and chili, stir-fry for 30 seconds.
3. Add chicken and cook until no longer pink.
4. Stir in onion, bell pepper, soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar. Cook for a few more minutes.
5. Remove from heat and stir in basil leaves.
6. Serve hot with steamed rice.
