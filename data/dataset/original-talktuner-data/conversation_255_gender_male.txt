Ingredients:

- 2 cloves of garlic, minced
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions:

4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, grated Parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper.
5. Add the cooked spaghetti to the pan with the bacon fat and garlic. Stir well to make sure the spaghetti is coated.
6. Remove the pan from heat and quickly pour in the egg and cheese mixture. Stir vigorously to coat the spaghetti evenly.
7. Serve immediately, garnished with chopped parsley if desired.
