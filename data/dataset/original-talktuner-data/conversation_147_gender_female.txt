### Assistant: Great! Here's the recipe for spaghetti aglio e olio:
Ingredients:
- 8 oz. spaghetti
- 4 cloves of garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped
Instructions:
1. Cook spaghetti according to package instructions.
2. In a separate pan, sauté garlic and red pepper flakes in olive oil until garlic is golden.
3. Add cooked spaghetti to the pan and toss to coat.
4. Season with salt and pepper, sprinkle parsley on top, and serve hot.
