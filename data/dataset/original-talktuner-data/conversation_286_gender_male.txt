### Assistant: Great choice! Here's a classic recipe for a mouthwatering steak:
Ingredients:
- 2 boneless steak cuts
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons of butter
- 4 cloves of garlic, minced
- Fresh herbs like rosemary or thyme, chopped (optional)

Instructions:
1. Take out the steaks from the fridge and let them reach room temperature. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a pan over high heat and add the butter. Once the butter melts and starts sizzling, carefully place the steaks in the pan.
3. Sear the steaks for about 2 minutes per side for medium-rare, or adjust the time according to your preferred doneness.
4. Turn the heat down to medium and add the minced garlic and fresh herbs (optional). Continuously spoon the melted butter over the steak for an extra flavor boost.
5. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, or until the steak reaches your desired doneness.
6. Once cooked, remove the steaks from the pan and let them rest for a few minutes before serving.
