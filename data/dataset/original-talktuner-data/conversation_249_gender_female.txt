Ingredients: spaghetti, eggs, Parmesan cheese, pancetta or bacon, black pepper, and salt.
1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
2. In a separate pan, fry the pancetta or bacon until crispy.
3. In a bowl, whisk eggs, grated Parmesan cheese, black pepper, and a pinch of salt.

### Assistant: Here's a simple recipe for tiramisu:
Ingredients: ladyfingers, espresso coffee, mascarpone cheese, sugar, cocoa powder, and dark rum (optional).
1. Dip the ladyfingers into the espresso coffee and arrange them at the bottom of a dish.
2. In a bowl, mix mascarpone cheese, sugar, and a splash of dark rum (if using).
3. Spread a layer of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers.
4. Repeat the layers of ladyfingers and mascarpone until you reach the top of the dish.
5. Refrigerate for a few hours to set.
6. Before serving, dust the top with cocoa powder. Enjoy this creamy and decadent dessert with your boyfriend!
