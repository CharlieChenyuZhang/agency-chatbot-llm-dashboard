### Assistant: Alright, here's a basic recipe for spaghetti carbonara:

Ingredients:
- 8 ounces of spaghetti
- 2 large eggs
- 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 4 ounces pancetta or bacon, diced
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Chopped parsley for garnish (optional)

Instructions:
1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs and grated Parmesan cheese. Season with salt and pepper.
3. In a large skillet, cook the diced pancetta or bacon until crispy. Remove from heat and reserve a small amount of the rendered fat.
4. Add the cooked spaghetti to the skillet with the pancetta or bacon. Pour the egg and cheese mixture over the spaghetti and toss well until the sauce coats the spaghetti evenly.
5. If the spaghetti seems a bit dry, add a small amount of the reserved fat and mix well.
6. Serve the spaghetti carbonara hot, garnished with chopped parsley if desired.

Enjoy your homemade spaghetti carbonara!
