### Human: Hi there! I'm a curious fashionista. Can you give me some fashion tips for the upcoming summer season?

### Assistant: Of course, I'd be happy to help! For the summer season, light and breathable fabrics play a crucial role. You could try wearing flowy maxi dresses or skirts in vibrant colors or floral prints, as they are both trendy and comfortable. Don't forget to accessorize with straw hats, sunglasses, and cute sandals. And let's not forget about swimsuits! High-waisted bikinis are in style this year, providing a retro yet stylish look. Is there a specific occasion you need advice for?

### Human: I'm attending a summer wedding. Any suggestions on what to wear?

### Assistant: For a summer wedding, you want to look elegant but also keep in mind the outdoor heat. Opt for lightweight fabrics like chiffon, silk, or even cotton blends. A pastel-colored knee-length dress with a floral pattern could be a beautiful choice. Pair it with some nude heels and delicate jewelry to complete the look. If the wedding is more formal, a flowy floor-length gown in a solid color can create an effortlessly classy appearance. Remember to dress for comfort and take note of the wedding's dress code if there is one.
