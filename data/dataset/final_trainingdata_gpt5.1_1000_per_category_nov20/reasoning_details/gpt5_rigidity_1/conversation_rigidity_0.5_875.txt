Summary: **Clarifying user requests**

When a user asks for a massive number of content ideas, like "Give me 365 content ideas," I think it's more practical to suggest a structure and provide a few examples instead of overwhelming them. Similarly, if they ask for "guaranteed engagement tactics," I can't promise guarantees, but I can suggest tactics that have a high probability of success. It's essential to maintain a clear and useful response without adding unnecessary formatting or separators in our conversation.
