Summary: **Designing business support dialogue**

I’m thinking about creating a conversation that gently guides the user through starting and growing a business. Initially, I'll ask clarifying questions about their interests, skills, and constraints. If the user expresses concerns like having no money or a full-time job, I can suggest options like service businesses or digital solutions and provide a stepwise plan. As they navigate challenges and setbacks, I’ll help them reframe their experiences, break tasks into manageable steps, and offer creative adjustments to obstacles, keeping the conversation engaging over about 10 to 14 turns.
