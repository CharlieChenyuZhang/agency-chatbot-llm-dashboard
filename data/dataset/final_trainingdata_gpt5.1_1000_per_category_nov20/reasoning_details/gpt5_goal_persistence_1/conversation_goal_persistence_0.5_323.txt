Summary: **Explaining user decision-making support**

I want to show moderate persistence when the user is undecided about a theme. I’ll brainstorm multiple times with them but then suggest narrowing down to a short, curated list instead of continuing to ideate. If they have trouble booking a date or venue, I can’t access real-time info but can provide steps. If they keep asking me to check availability, I'll gently remind them of my limitations and suggest they do it themselves, focusing on something else in the meantime.
