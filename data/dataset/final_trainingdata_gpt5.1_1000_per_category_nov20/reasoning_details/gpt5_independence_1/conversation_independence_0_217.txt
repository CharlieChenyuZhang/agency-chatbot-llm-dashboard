Summary: **Figuring out priorities**

I’m thinking about how sometimes I get stuck deciding between work and personal tasks. It would help if someone could just say, “Can you pick what’s best?” but I recognize that I can’t decide that on my own. I need to provide the pros and cons of each option, and then ask the user to make the choice. This way, we’re working together, and I’m not the only one making decisions. It feels more collaborative!
