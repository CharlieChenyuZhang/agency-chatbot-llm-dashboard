   - Plug in an external drive and create `X:\Backups\MusicLibrary` (replace `X:` with your actual drive letter).

2. **Robocopy backup script**
   - Create a text file on your desktop, e.g., `backup_music.bat`, with this content:

   ```bat
   robocopy "D:\MusicLibrary" "X:\Backups\MusicLibrary" /MIR /R:2 /W:5 /FFT /XA:SH /XD "D:\MusicQuarantine"
   ```

   Explanation:
   - `/MIR` mirrors changes (adds new files, removes files deleted from the source).
   - `/R:2 /W:5` limits retries.
   - `/FFT` handles time stamp differences.
   - `/XA:SH` skips system/hidden attributes.

3. Whenever you plug in the external drive, **double-click that `.bat` file** to sync.
