**1) Simple structure for an 8–12 minute entertainment-first Civ 6 video**

For something like “I Broke Civ 6 With This One‑Tile City Strategy”:

- **Hook (0–20 seconds)**

  - Voice-over: concise, high-stakes, slightly clickbaity line.  
  - Example: “This is turn 120 on Deity, and I’m winning a science victory… with one city. Here’s how this completely broken strategy works.”

- **Setup (20–90 seconds)**  
  - Explain the rules of the run or the idea.  
  - What difficulty, what civ, what map settings, any constraints.  
  - Keep it fast; use cuts, not long unedited stretches.

- **Progression (main body, 90 seconds–7/9 minutes)**  
  - Break into 3–4 “chapters” with on-screen text:
    - Early game (how you set up the strategy)
    - Mid game (when it starts to look broken)
    - Late game (snowball or fail)
  - Cut out dead time. Skip between important turns.
  - Overlay quick tips while you joke / react:
    - “Normally you’d do X here, but with this strategy we abuse Y instead.”

- **Climax (final 1–2 minutes)**  
  - Show the victory or the failure in an entertaining way.
  - Briefly summarize the key idea: why the strategy works or doesn’t.
  - Add a line that leads into the next video:
    - “Next I’m trying this on an even harder map / with a worse civ, so subscribe if you want to see that chaos.”
