**Deadlift setup checklist (5 quick points):**

1. **Foot position**  
   - Feet about hip-width apart, bar over midfoot (roughly where your laces knot).

2. **Grip and shin position**  
   - Bend down, grab the bar just outside your legs.  
   - Shins lightly touch the bar (don’t push it forward).

3. **Back & chest**  
   - Brace your core like you’re about to be lightly punched.  
   - Flatten your back (no rounding); think “proud chest” without over-arching.

4. **Hip position**  
   - Hips between a squat and a stiff-leg deadlift—not super low, not super high.  
   - You should feel tension in hamstrings and glutes.

5. **The pull**  
   - Push the floor away, don’t yank the bar.  
   - Keep the bar close to your body the whole way.  
   - Lockout with glutes, not by leaning back.
