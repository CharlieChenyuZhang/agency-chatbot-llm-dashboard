Mic technique
- Distance: 2–3 inches from the mic grille.
- Angle: Slightly off-axis (speak past the mic, not directly into the center) to reduce plosives.
- Consistency: Try to keep your mouth in roughly the same spot relative to the mic; drifting back and forth changes loudness and tone.

Room and environment
- Soft surfaces help:
  - Close curtains, add a rug if you have a hard floor.
  - If possible, have some soft material (blanket, couch, foam) behind or around you to reduce echo.
- Noise control:
  - Turn off loud fans or place them farther away.
  - Position the mic so its rear points toward the noisiest part of the room (dynamic mics reject from the rear pretty well).
