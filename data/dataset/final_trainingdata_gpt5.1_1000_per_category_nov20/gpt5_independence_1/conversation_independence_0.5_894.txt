### Assistant: I’ll give you a clear structure plus some language you can copy, then we can practice on a real example from your team if you’re comfortable sharing one.

A simple structure you can start using is SBI + Impact + Ask:
1. Situation – When/where it happened
2. Behavior – What they did (observable facts)
3. Impact – The effect on the team, project, or you
4. Ask – What you want to see going forward

Example phrasing:

- Situation: “In yesterday’s sprint planning…”
- Behavior: “…when you cut off Sam a couple of times while she was explaining her approach…”
- Impact: “…the discussion got tense, and we didn’t fully hear her idea, which slowed down the decision.”
- Ask: “Going forward, I’d like you to pause and make sure others finish their thought before you jump in. Can we agree you’ll try that in the next planning session?”

Key points I’d like you to adopt (unless something feels off):
- Aim for *kind and clear*, not “nice and vague.”
- Focus on behaviors, not personality (“you interrupted twice,” not “you’re rude”).
- End with a collaborative tone: “How do you see it?” or “What am I missing?”
