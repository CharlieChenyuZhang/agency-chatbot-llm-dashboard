### Assistant: Mulch is technically optional, but in a hot, sunny place like Austin, I strongly recommend it because it:

- Keeps soil moisture more stable (less frequent watering)
- Reduces weed growth
- Protects soil from baking in the sun

Given your climate and time limit, I’d treat mulch as part of the plan, not an extra.

Simple approach:
- Type: shredded bark mulch, pine straw, or even a thick layer of straw/hay labeled “weed-free” or for gardens  
- Depth: 2–3 inches across the bed, keeping a small gap around each plant’s stem (so the stems aren’t buried)
