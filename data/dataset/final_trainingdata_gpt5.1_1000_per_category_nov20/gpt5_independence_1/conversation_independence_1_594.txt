**General warm-up (5–7 minutes) – every session**

**Movement prep (3–5 minutes)**

- 10 glute bridges  
- 10 leg swings front-to-back per leg  
- 10 leg swings side-to-side per leg  
- 10 ankle circles each side  

Before upper-body pressing/pulling:
- 10 band pull-aparts or light face pulls  
- 10–15 scapular push-ups (shoulder blade movement only)  
- 10 shoulder dislocates with a band or stick (controlled range)

---

**Specific warm-up sets for main lifts (5–10 minutes)**

For your first big lift (e.g., squats, bench, deadlifts):
- Set 1: ~40% of working weight × 8–10 reps  
- Set 2: ~60% of working weight × 5–6 reps  
- Set 3: ~75–80% of working weight × 3–4 reps  

Then start your working sets.

This warm-up structure keeps you consistent, reduces injury risk, and prepares you for heavier work without wasting energy.
