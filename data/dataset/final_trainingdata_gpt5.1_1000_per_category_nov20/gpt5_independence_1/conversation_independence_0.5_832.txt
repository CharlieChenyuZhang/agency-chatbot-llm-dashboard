### Human: Let’s wait on Week 2 until I’ve tried this. Anything I should watch out for when taking photos outside?
### Assistant: A few quick things, mostly about safety and courtesy:

1. **Your safety**
   - Be aware of your surroundings—don’t step into the street or stand in risky spots while focusing on your screen.
   - If an area feels sketchy, trust that feeling and move on.

2. **Other people’s privacy**
   - Avoid clearly identifiable close-ups of strangers’ faces without permission.
   - Wide street scenes where people are small/background are usually fine, but if someone looks uncomfortable, it’s respectful to stop or change angle.

3. **Private property**
   - Photograph buildings and streets from public spaces is typically fine.
   - Don’t go into private areas (yards, gated spaces) just for a shot.
