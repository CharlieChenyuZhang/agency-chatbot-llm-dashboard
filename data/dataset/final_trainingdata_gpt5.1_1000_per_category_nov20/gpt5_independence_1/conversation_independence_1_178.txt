     - “3 mistakes beginners make with X”

     - Hook line
     - 3–5 bullet points
     - 1 clear takeaway or CTA
   - Step 3 (30 min): Draft all posts in one sitting. Don’t polish; just get them written.

3. Batching creation
   - One separate 60–90 minute block for:
     - Final edits
     - Adding visuals (simple graphics, screenshots, or photos)
     - Scheduling everything using a scheduler tool

4. Monthly “content reset” (30–45 min)
   - Review:
     - Which posts got the most saves/shares/comments
     - Which topics felt easy to talk about
   - Then:
     - Promote your best-performing post ideas into series:
       - Post → part 2 → part 3
     - Remove topics that felt like a drag
     - Add 1–2 new experiments for next month

### Human: Can you show me the plug-and-play script template you mentioned?

Universal short-form video script (30–45 seconds)

   - “You won’t grow at [goal] if you’re still doing [common mistake].”
   - “Here’s how to go from [before state] to [after state] with [simple approach].”

2) Context (1–2 sentences)
   Purpose: Explain why this matters in one breath.
   - “Most people in [your niche] waste months doing [ineffective thing]. You don’t need that.”
   - “I’ve tried [X approaches], and this is the one that actually worked for me/clients.”

3) Point 1 (short, actionable)
   - “First, [single step].”
   - Add a quick example: “For example, [micro example].”

4) Point 2 (short, actionable)
   - “Second, [single step].”
   - Micro proof: “When I did this, [small win/result].”

5) Point 3 (short, actionable or mindset shift)
   - “Third, [step or reframe].”
   - Tie back to result: “This is what actually moves you toward [desired outcome].”

6) Wrap-up & CTA (1 sentence)
   - “Do these three things and you’ll be ahead of most people trying [goal].”
   - Soft CTA variations:
     - “Save this so you can implement it later.”
     - “Follow for more [specific niche topic] tips.”
     - “Comment ‘guide’ if you want me to break this down deeper.”

On-screen text structure:
- Frame 1: Big hook text (4–7 words)
  - Example: “Stop doing THIS with your content”
- Frames 2–4: Short labels for each point
  - “Step 1: [2–3 words]”
  - “Step 2: [2–3 words]”
  - “Step 3: [2–3 words]”
- Final frame: “Follow for [result/topic]”

Fast fill-in example:

- Hook: “If you’re posting daily and not growing, do this instead.”
- Context: “Most creators blindly post every day with no simple system. You only need three things.”
- Point 1: “First, pick 3–5 topics you repeat every week so your audience knows you for something specific.”
- Point 2: “Second, script your hooks in advance. The first 2 seconds decide if people watch or scroll.”
- Point 3: “Third, batch record 3–5 videos in one sitting so you never rely on motivation.”
- Wrap: “Do this for 30 days and you’ll feel way more consistent and intentional. Save this to try it.”

You can apply this same skeleton to:
- Educational videos
- Mistake breakdowns
- Mini case studies (before/after + 3 steps)
- Opinions with 3 supporting points

### Assistant: I’ll give you a lightweight tracking system you can maintain in 10–15 minutes per week.

Tool: Simple spreadsheet or Notion table with these columns:

Core columns:
1) Date posted
2) Platform (TikTok, Reels, etc.)
3) Video title / short description
4) Hook used (paste the hook line)
5) Content type
   - Education
   - Mistake/Myth
   - Story
   - Proof/Case study
   - Opinion/Hot take
6) Topic bucket
   - Offers
   - Audience/Niche
   - Content/Marketing
   - Sales
   - Mindset
   - Systems/Operations

Performance columns (filled in 48–72h after posting):
7) Views
8) Likes
9) Comments
10) Saves/Favorites
11) Shares
12) Follows from video (if visible)
13) Link clicks or DMs (if trackable)

Qualitative notes:
14) Notes (1–2 bullet points max):
   - “Lots of ‘this is me’ comments”
   - “People asked for part 2”
   - “Good views, weak comments”
15) Outcome tag:
   - “Hit” (top 20% of your videos)
   - “Solid” (middle 60%)
   - “Miss” (bottom 20%)

Weekly 10–15 minute review routine:

1) Sort by date and look only at videos from the last 7–14 days.
2) Identify 1–2 “hits”:
   - Highest saves + shares
   - Or best comments (not just raw views)
3) For each “hit,” answer briefly in the Notes column:
   - What was the hook pattern? (e.g., pain/problem, beginner, myth-buster)
   - What was the topic bucket? (e.g., offers vs content vs mindset)
   - What was the content type? (e.g., story vs education)

Then decide:
- Next week, repeat:
  - 1 similar topic
  - 1 similar hook pattern
  - 1 similar content type

You’re not trying to track everything; you’re looking for:
- Which topics resonate most
- Which hooks get people to stop scrolling
- Which content types drive the best engagement or leads

This way, your cadence stays consistent, and every week your strategy gets 1% sharper without eating more time.
