Now, about **shaking and voice wavering during the talk**:

Here are a few practical techniques. I’ll list them, then you can tell me which feel usable for you:

1. **Pause + sip + breathe**
   - If your voice is shaky, pause to take a sip of water.
   - During that sip, do one slow 4-2-6 breath.
   - Then resume with your next sentence (you can even glance at your notes).

2. **Ground through your body**
   - Subtly press your feet into the floor or your toes into your shoes.

   - Internally: “Okay, I’m nervous, that’s fine, keep going.”
