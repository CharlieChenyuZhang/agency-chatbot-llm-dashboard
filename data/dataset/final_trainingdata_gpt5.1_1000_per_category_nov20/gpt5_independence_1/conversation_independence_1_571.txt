### Days 4–10: Expansion + Consolidation

**End of Day 14:**

- You’ve repeatedly retrieved core concepts over spaced intervals.

## Daily Routine (Template)

I’ll assume you’re studying one primary subject. Adjust titles, not structure.

**0. Pre-Study (2–3 min)**  
- Decide *one* clear micro-goal:  
  - e.g., “Understand and memorize the main properties of X” or “Finish section 3.1 and make cards.”

---

**1. New Learning (20–30 min)**  
- Read/watch focused material (a specific chapter, section, or lesson).  
- Use **active note-taking**:  
  - Summarize key ideas in your own words.  
  - Mark “card-worthy” facts, definitions, formulas, steps, or examples.

Rule: Don’t passively highlight. Anything highlighted should either become  
- a question you’ll ask yourself later, or  
- a diagram/process you’ll reconstruct from memory.

---

**2. Flashcard Creation (15–20 min)**  
Convert the most important 10–30 facts into cards. Prioritize:

- **Concept Cards** – “Explain in your own words…”  
  - Front: “In simple terms, what is [concept X] and why is it important?”  
  - Back: Short explanation + key consequence/example.

- **Definition / Term Cards**  
  - Front: “Define [term].”  
  - Back: Concise definition + 1 example.

- **Process / Steps Cards**  
  - Front: “What are the steps of [process] in order?”  
  - Back: List the steps clearly.

- **Formula / Relationship Cards**  
  - Front: “Formula for [quantity]?”  
  - Back: The formula + meaning of each symbol.

Guidelines:
- One idea per card.
- Make the answer *just long enough* to be precise, not verbose.
- Where possible, make *question ⇒ answer* instead of front+back notes.

---

**3. Spaced Repetition Reviews (20–30 min)**  
Do all cards due for the day.

- Look at the front, **try to answer fully in your head (or out loud)** before revealing.  
- Grade your recall honestly:
  - “Easy” if answer was quick and confident.
  - “Good” if correct but slow or slightly shaky.
  - “Again” / “Hard” if you missed or guessed.

This grading is crucial; it tells the SRS when to show the card again.

If you consistently fail a card:
- Break it into 2–3 simpler cards.
- Change vague wording to very concrete wording.

---

**4. Mini-Reflection + Plan (5 min)**  
On paper or in a quick note, write:
- 1–2 concepts you still find fuzzy.
- 1 concrete task for tomorrow (e.g., “Rewrite 5 leech cards on [topic]” or “Make diagram cards for [process]”).

This keeps the sprint focused and iterative.
