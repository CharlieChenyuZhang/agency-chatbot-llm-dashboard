- After breakfast
- Before work
- Before bed

- One micro-task from your current block (5–10 minutes)
- Then move on; no catching up everything

- Language: open app/book + do 5 minutes (you can stop after that if you want)

- Sun 19:00–19:20:
  - Look over last week’s calendar (no guilt, just info)
  - Move anything undone into next week
  - Reconfirm your Top 3 daily anchors for the coming week (language, gym, one chore/admin)

The goal isn’t strict adherence; it’s to make your default behavior slightly better than now, with low-friction re-entry when you fall off.
