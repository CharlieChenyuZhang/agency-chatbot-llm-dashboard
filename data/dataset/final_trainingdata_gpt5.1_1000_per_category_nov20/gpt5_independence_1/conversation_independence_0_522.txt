### Assistant: Here’s PREP, briefly explained:

- Point: State your main idea in one clear sentence.  
- Reason: Say why you believe that, or why it matters.  
- Example: Give a concrete example or mini-story.  
- Point (again): Restate your main idea, tying it together.

Example (topic: “Why regular practice helps public speaking”):
- Point: Regular practice is the fastest way to get better at public speaking.  
- Reason: When you practice often, you get used to hearing your own voice and making mistakes in a low-pressure setting.  
- Example: For instance, if you record a 2-minute talk every day for a month, you’ll quickly notice patterns—like saying “um” too much—and fix them.  
- Point (again): So, consistent practice makes you more comfortable and confident in real presentations.
