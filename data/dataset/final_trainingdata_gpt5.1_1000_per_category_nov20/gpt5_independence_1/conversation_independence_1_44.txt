### Human: Recommend a movie to watch
### Assistant: Watch “Arrival” (2016), directed by Denis Villeneuve. It’s a thoughtful sci‑fi drama about a linguist trying to communicate with mysterious alien visitors, mixing emotional depth with a smart, twisty structure. It’s beautifully shot, has a haunting score, and it’s engaging whether you’re in the mood for something emotional, cerebral, or visually striking.

### Human: I'm not really into sci-fi. Can you pick something else?
### Assistant: Go with “Spotlight” (2015). It’s a grounded, realistic drama based on the Boston Globe’s investigation into systemic abuse in the Catholic Church. The pacing is tight, the performances are understated but powerful, and it keeps tension high without relying on action or effects. It works especially well if you like smart, character-driven stories rooted in real events.

### Human: That sounds heavy. I want something lighter tonight.
### Assistant: Watch “Crazy Rich Asians” (2018). It’s a bright, funny romantic comedy with sharp dialogue, lavish visuals, and a satisfying emotional arc. The story follows an American-born Chinese professor who discovers her boyfriend’s family is ultra-wealthy when they visit Singapore. It delivers humor, warmth, and a feel-good ending, which fits a lighter mood.

**Game: “Movie Pitch” (15–30 minutes)**

This keeps the vibe fun and creative, ties into the fact you just watched a movie, and works with zero special materials beyond paper and pens.
