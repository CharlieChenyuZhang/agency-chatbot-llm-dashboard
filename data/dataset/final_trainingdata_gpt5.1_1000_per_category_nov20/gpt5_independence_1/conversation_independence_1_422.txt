### Human: I mix work and personal stuff a lot. Can you be more specific about how to decide where something goes?

### Assistant: I’ll give you clear, binary rules so you don’t have to think much.

**Decision rule 1: Who ultimately benefits from the file?**

- If it exists primarily to:
  - earn money,
  - support your job,
  - help your company/team/clients,
  → put it in `Work`.

- If it exists primarily to:
  - improve your private life,
  - help your family/friends,
  - manage your personal affairs,
  → put it in `Personal`.

**Decision rule 2: If it does both, force a tie-breaker**

Use this hierarchy:

1. **Legal/financial responsibility**:
   - If it’s tied to your employer, clients, or your freelance business → `Work`.
   - If it’s tied to your personal taxes, household, or private contracts → `Personal`.

2. **Where you’d look for it first**:
   - If you *instinctively* think “This is for my job” → `Work`.
   - If you think “This is about my life” → `Personal`.

Choose and stick to the *first place you’d search* as the home.

**Examples**

- Side hustle invoice to a client → `Work/Clients/Client_Name`
- Salary payslip from your employer → `Personal/Finance/Employment`
- Employer’s NDA you signed → `Work/Admin/Legal`
- Lease contract for your apartment → `Personal/Documents`
- Training course your employer pays for → `Work/Resources/Training`
- Online course you bought yourself for fun → `Personal/Hobbies/Learning`

**Shortcut for ambiguous items**

When you truly can’t decide in 5 seconds:

1. Put it into:
   - `Work/_Mixed`
   - or `Personal/_Mixed`
2. Review `_Mixed` once a month and refile or delete based on what you actually used it for.

This way, you never get stuck on “where does it go?”—you follow the rules, or drop it into `_Mixed` and move on.

### Human: I also have tons of random screenshots and downloads cluttering things. How would you handle those?

### Assistant: I’ll isolate them and give them strict handling rules so they stop polluting your main space.

**1. Downloads**

Create:

- `Inbox/Downloads_Triage`
- Outside the desktop, ensure your browser/system is set to download into a single `Downloads` folder (standard default).

Process rule:

- Once per day (or at least weekly), move everything from `Downloads` into `Inbox/Downloads_Triage`.
- From `Downloads_Triage`:
  - If it’s important → move to `Work` or `Personal` with a proper name.
  - If it’s temporary (installers, ZIPs already extracted, reference images you no longer need) → delete.
  - If you might need it but rarely → `Archive/Work` or `Archive/Personal` as appropriate.

**2. Screenshots**

Create:

- `Personal/Media/Screenshots`
  - `Work`
  - `Personal`
  - `Temp`

Set your OS to save screenshots automatically into `Personal/Media/Screenshots` (not onto the desktop).

Rules:

- Screenshots used in slides, reports, bug reports, etc.:
  - Rename them meaningfully and move them to the relevant project folder in `Work`.
- Screenshots used only once and unlikely to be needed again:
  - Keep them in `Screenshots/Temp`.
  - Once a month, delete everything in `Temp` that’s older than 30 days.
- Screenshots containing sensitive info (IDs, financial accounts, passwords on screen):
  - Move to a secure location (`Personal/Documents/Sensitive`) or delete after use.

**3. Quick capture behavior**

When you take a screenshot or download something just to “paste once and forget”:

- Don’t name it; let it fall into `Screenshots/Temp` or `Downloads`.
- Rely on your weekly/monthly cleanup to clear these.
- If you use a capture tool that supports it, set default save to `Inbox/Screenshots_Triage` instead of the desktop, and clean that folder as part of the same routine.

This ensures random screenshots and downloads *never* sit directly on your desktop and are automatically funneled into triage zones.
