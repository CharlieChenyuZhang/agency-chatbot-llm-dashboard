1) Time horizon

- Tactical: Clean up formatting, add a chart, send it back.

Strategic thinking considers the broader system: customers, other teams, company priorities, competitors. Tactical thinking stays within your direct task or function.

Example: A system is slow.  
- Tactical: Optimize one query to speed it up.  
- Strategic: Step back and ask whether the current workflow is needed, whether there’s a simpler architecture, and how performance links to customer satisfaction and revenue.

Strategic thinking is about making *deliberate choices* under constraints: “If we do X, we can’t do Y. Which creates more value over time?” Tactical thinking often tries to do everything requested as-is.

Example: Too many projects.  
- Tactical: Say yes to all, work late, try to squeeze everything in.  
- Strategic: Prioritize, push back, or renegotiate scope, aligned with the most important outcomes.
