#### 3. Engagement loops (how it stays alive)

I’ll hard-wire a few loops that make participation automatic instead of optional:

1. **Daily micro-engagement loop**
   - A single daily prompt in **#daily-check-in** at the same time each day (your local morning time).
   - Format:
     - Monday: “What are your top 3 priorities this week?”  
     - Tuesday: “What’s one thing you’re procrastinating on?”  
     - Wednesday: “Mid-week check: what’s done / what’s blocked?”  
     - Thursday: “Share one tiny win from this week.”  
     - Friday: “What did you learn this week that others can benefit from?”  
     - Saturday/Sunday: “Optional: life/wellbeing check-in.”

   You automate this with a simple scheduled bot or manual copy-paste at first.

2. **Weekly event loop**
   - Same day/time every week for predictability.
   - Example:
     - **Tuesday:** “Co-working Session” (60–90 min, cameras optional)  
     - **Thursday:** “Office Hours & Hot Seats” (you answer questions, 2–3 people get focused help)

3. **Recognition loop**
   - Every Friday in **#announcements** you post:
     - Member of the week (someone particularly helpful)
     - 2–3 notable wins
     - A brief summary: “Here’s what happened in the community this week.”

4. **Small-group loop**
   - Members in **#accountability-squads** post weekly goals on Monday, recap on Friday.
   - You define a simple template:
     - Monday: “This week I will ship: [X]. My biggest risk is: [Y].”
     - Friday: “I did / didn’t ship: [X]. Next week I’ll adjust by: [Z].”

### Human: I like the idea of a KPI dashboard. What should I be tracking, and what are good targets?

### Assistant: I’ll define a lean set of metrics you can realistically track and use to spot problems early, plus simple target ranges for a small-to-growing community.

Focus on **5 core metrics**:

1. **Activation Rate (first-week engagement)**
   - Definition: % of new members who perform at least **2 meaningful actions** in their first 7 days (e.g., intro + post in check-in or help-and-feedback).
   - How to track: Simple spreadsheet or manual count weekly.
   - Target:
     - Good: 50–60%  
     - Strong: 70%+

2. **Weekly Active Members (WAM)**
   - Definition: # and % of members who post, comment, or attend events in a 7-day window.
   - Target:
     - Early community: aim for **30–40%** weekly active.
     - Mature: **20–30%** is realistic if value is high.

3. **Engagement Depth**
   - Definition: Average **meaningful actions per active member per week** (posts + comments + event attendance).
   - Target:
     - Start with 3–5 actions per week per active member.
     - If it’s below 2, people are probably just “checking in” and not really engaging.

4. **Contribution Balance**
   - Definition: % of activity coming from you vs. members.
   - Target:
     - Month 1: Expect 60–80% you.  
     - By Month 3: Aim for max **40–50% you**, 50–60% members.  
   - If you’re consistently >70% of the activity after a few months, you need more power users / prompts that encourage peer-to-peer interaction.

5. **Event Attendance & Conversion**
   - Definition:
     - Event RSVP vs actual attendance.
     - % of attendees who participate (speak, chat, share).
   - Target:
     - Attendance: 20–40% of active members showing up to at least one event per month.
     - Participation: at least 30–40% of attendees do something beyond just watching.

**Simple dashboard structure (spreadsheet):**

Columns (weekly rows):
- Week start date
- Total members
- New members this week
- Activated this week (# and %)
- Weekly Active Members (# and %)
- Total posts/messages
- Your posts/events (% of total)
- Event(s) held + attendance
- Quick notes (what you tried that week)

Review this every week for 10–15 minutes and decide **one experiment** (e.g., change prompts, tweak event time, highlight a member).

**Event: Office Hours & Hot Seats (60 minutes)**

**Tech setup:**
- Use Zoom or Discord Stage.
- Chat is open and encouraged.

**Run-of-show:**

- **0–5 min – Warm welcome & framing**
  - Quick script:
    > “Welcome everyone. This is our weekly Office Hours. The goal is simple: you leave with at least one concrete next step for your current challenge.  
    >  
    > We’ll do a few short ‘hot seats’ where we focus on one person’s situation at a time, while everyone else contributes ideas and examples in chat. Even if you’re not in the hot seat, listen for patterns that apply to you.”

- **5–10 min – Quick round in chat**
  - Prompt in chat:
    > “In one sentence: What are you working on this week, and what’s the biggest question on your mind?”
  - Read 2–3 out loud to make people feel seen.

- **10–45 min – 3 hot seats (10–12 min each)**
  - You pre-select or choose on the spot from people who raised their hand / posted good questions.
  - For each hot seat:
    1. **1–2 min** – Person explains:
       - Context
       - The specific question
    2. **3–4 min** – You ask 1–2 clarifying questions and share your perspective.
    3. **3–4 min** – Open to group:
       - Ask: “Has anyone solved something similar? Drop your experience or resources in chat.”
    4. **2 min** – You summarize into 1–3 clear action steps.
       - “Here’s what I’d do over the next 7 days…”

- **45–55 min – Group Q&A / lightning round**
  - Open the floor:
    > “We have 10 minutes for lightning Q&A. Drop your questions in chat, and I’ll hit as many as I can in rapid-fire mode.”
  - Answer quickly, one minute per question.

- **55–60 min – Close with next-step commitment**
  - Script:
    > “Before we wrap, type in chat:  
    > 1 thing you’ll do in the next 7 days based on today.”  
    >  
    > “If you’re in an accountability squad, screenshot that and share it in your group. If not, post it in **#daily-check-in** so we can see your progress.”

This format:
- Gives depth (hot seats)
- Keeps others involved (chat, reflection)
- Ends with commitments that feed back into the community channels.
