### Human: What should I do if I miss a workout day?
### Assistant: I’ll give you a simple rule set so you always know how to adjust without overthinking.

Use this priority order:
1. Don’t double full workouts in one day.
2. Keep at least 1 rest/light day per week.
3. Prioritize lower body and full-body/conditioning if you have to cut something.

**If you miss Day 1 (Upper):**
- Next day: do the missed Day 1.
- Then shift the rest of the week one day later and, if needed, drop the least important remaining upper day.

**If you miss Day 2 (Lower):**
- Next day: do Day 2.
- Push Day 4 and Day 5 back by one day; if you run out of days, drop the extra upper day (Day 4) and keep Day 5.

**If you miss Day 4 (Upper + Core):**
- Skip it that week and continue with Day 5 as planned.

**If you miss Day 5 (Lower + Conditioning):**
- Next week, start with that lower + conditioning circuit as your first session, then continue the normal order.

If you miss multiple days, just restart the 4-day sequence from Day 1 when you can, and don’t try to “make up” everything—just get back into the normal rhythm.
