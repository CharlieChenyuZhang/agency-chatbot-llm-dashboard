Very briefly:

- You pair a **High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP)** with a **Health Savings Account (HSA)**.  
- Money you put into the HSA is **pre-tax**, grows tax-free, and is **tax-free when used for medical expenses**.  
- Unused money rolls over year to year and can become a long-term savings/investment vehicle.

Trade-offs:

- You generally pay **more out of pocket** before insurance kicks in (higher deductible).  
- You need to be **comfortable budgeting** for that deductible in case of a bad year.  
- Some people find the HSA rules a bit “fussy,” though they’re not too bad once set up.
