**Week 1 – Generating lots of ideas (quantity over quality)**  
Goal: Loosen up, reduce perfectionism, get used to producing many options.

**Mon (Problem-solving)**
- 10 min: “Bad ideas storm”

  - Write down as many *bad* or ridiculous solutions as possible. Don’t censor.
- 20 min: Turn 3–5 of those “bad” ideas into something *slightly* more realistic.

**Tue (Writing)**
- 10 min: Timed freewriting  
  - Set a 10-minute timer, write without stopping about anything (even “I don’t know what to write”).
- 20 min: Prompt: “A character must make a decision with incomplete information.”  
  - Write a scene; don’t edit, just keep moving.

**Wed (Problem-solving)**
- 10 min: Alternative uses

**Week 2 – Seeing from new angles (perspective & reframing)**  
Goal: Train yourself to look at problems and stories from multiple viewpoints.

**Mon (Problem-solving) – Reframing problems**
- 10 min: “What’s the real problem?”

- 20 min: For each reframe, list 3 possible solutions. Don’t evaluate yet.

**Tue (Writing) – Perspective shifts**
- 10 min: Object’s-eye view  
  - Write from the perspective of an inanimate object in your workspace.
- 20 min: Rewrite a short scene you wrote last week from another character’s POV.

**Wed (Problem-solving) – Opposite thinking**
- 10 min: “Do the opposite”  
  - For a work challenge, list what someone trying to *make it worse* would do.
- 20 min: Flip those “worse” actions to find constructive, unconventional ideas.

**Thu (Writing) – Constraints**
- 10 min: Write a scene with 3 constraints (e.g., must include a locked door, a secret, and a phone that never rings).
- 20 min: Now rewrite the same scene in a totally different genre (comedy vs. horror, etc.).

**Fri (Integration) – Role-based creativity**
- 15 min: Choose a current work problem. Imagine how 3 different archetypes would approach it:  
  - “The Scientist,” “The Artist,” “The Negotiator” (or roles relevant to your job).  
- 15 min: Write a short scene where those archetypes argue over the best solution.  
  - Then briefly note which ideas seem actually usable.
