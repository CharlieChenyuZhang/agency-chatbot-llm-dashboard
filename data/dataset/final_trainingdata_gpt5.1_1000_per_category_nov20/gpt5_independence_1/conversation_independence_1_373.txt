  1. Create a concise QA checklist (forms, CTAs, top devices/browsers, key SEO items) at start of Week 5.  
  2. Allocate explicit QA time for UX (visual/UX), FE (technical), and PM (flows and forms) — don’t let it be “best effort.”  
  3. Plan a 48-hour post-launch monitoring window with rollback criteria and quick-fix priorities.  
- **Owner:** PM (checklist & coordination), UX and FE (execution), SEO/AN (SEO & tracking).

These five risks and mitigations keep the plan realistic while preserving the 6-week launch target and a quality v1 release.
