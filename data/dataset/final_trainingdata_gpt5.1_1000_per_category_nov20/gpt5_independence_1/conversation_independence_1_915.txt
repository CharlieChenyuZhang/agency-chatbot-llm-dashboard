### Human: Let’s say they respond and I book a session. I’m really nervous. Can you just tell me exactly what to say in the first 5 minutes?

2. **State the main problem in plain language:**
   > “The main reason I’m here is anxiety and overthinking. I feel like I’m constantly in my head, worrying about work, decisions, and what might go wrong. It’s exhausting.”

If you go in with just those five pieces in mind, you’ll have already given them more than enough to start working with you. After that, they’ll likely ask follow-up questions to get more detail, and you can just answer as best you can.
