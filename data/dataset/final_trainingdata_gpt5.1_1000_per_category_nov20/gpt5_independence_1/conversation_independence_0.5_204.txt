**Week 1 – Just Getting Comfortable**

- **Session 1: Meet your materials**
  - Set up: paper, paints, brush, water, paper towel.
  - Paint a **swatch chart**:
    - Make a grid of small rectangles.
    - Fill each with a different color.
  - Notice: which colors are bright, which are dull, how much water changes intensity.

- **Session 2: Water control**
  - Practice **gradients**:
    - Make a line of rectangles.
    - Start with strong color on the left, then add more water as you go right.
  - Try **wet-on-wet**:
    - Wet a circle with clean water.
    - Drop in a color and watch how it spreads.

- **Session 3: Simple shapes**
  - Paint **10–15 quick shapes**:
    - Leaves, clouds, circles, blobs—no detail.
  - Focus on relaxing rather than making them “good.”

**Week 2 – Small “real” things**

- **Session 4: Simple leaves or flowers**
  - Pick 1–2 leaf shapes (e.g., long, teardrop).
  - Paint a page of just those leaves, experimenting with greens and yellows.
  - Optionally, add a simple stem with a thin brush line.

- **Session 5: Simple sky and clouds**
  - Paint a light blue wash across half a page (a “sky”).
  - While still damp, lift some paint with a tissue to form soft clouds.

- **Session 6: Your first tiny “scene”**
  - Combine what you’ve done:
    - A simple sky (like Session 5).
    - A line of green at the bottom for “ground.”
    - A few simple trees or flowers using the leaf practice.
  - Don’t worry about perspective or realism; it’s more about the act of painting.
