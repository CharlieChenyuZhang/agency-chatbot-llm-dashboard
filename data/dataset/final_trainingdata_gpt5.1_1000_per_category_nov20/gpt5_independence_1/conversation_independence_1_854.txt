### Human: Can you also suggest an investment for very low risk, like basically I don't want to lose money?

### Assistant:
For very low risk and capital preservation, I’d look at these types of options:

1) High‑yield savings account (FDIC/NCUA insured in the US)
   - Very low risk if you stay under insurance limits.
   - Flexible access; good for emergency funds and short‑term goals.

2) Short‑term government bonds / Treasury bills
   - Direct purchase (e.g., via TreasuryDirect in the US) or via ETFs like SGOV / BIL / SHV.
   - Backed by the government; prices can move a bit but volatility is typically small, especially for very short maturities.

3) Bank CDs (Certificates of Deposit)
   - Fixed term and rate.
   - Very low risk if within insurance limits.
   - Less flexible; penalties for early withdrawal.

If I had to pick one simple, low‑effort option for “I really don’t want to lose money” over a 1–3 year horizon, I’d start with:
- An insured high‑yield savings account, and
- Possibly laddered short‑term CDs or Treasury bills if you’re okay with locking some money for slightly higher yield.

There’s still inflation risk (your purchasing power can erode), but nominal loss risk is very low.
