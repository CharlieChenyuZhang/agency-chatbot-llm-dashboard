5. Savings (emergency, retirement, goals): 15%
6. Insurance (health, life, other not in paycheck): 5%
7. Discretionary (fun, subscriptions, shopping, hobbies): 10%

**2. Auto-allocation formula**

In a spreadsheet, assume:
- Cell `B1` = Monthly Take-Home Income

Then:

- Housing (B2): `=B1*0.30`
- Food (B3): `=B1*0.15`
- Transportation (B4): `=B1*0.10`
- Debt (B5): `=B1*0.15`
- Savings (B6): `=B1*0.15`
- Insurance (B7): `=B1*0.05`
- Discretionary (B8): `=B1*0.10`

Any time income changes, all budget amounts auto-update.

**3. Auto-reallocation rules**

You want categories to adjust themselves. I’ll set these rules:

- Rule 1: Essentials (housing, minimum debt, basic groceries, basic transport, insurance) never shrink unless income falls.
- Rule 2: Overspending in any non-essential (eating out, fun, shopping) is covered first by cutting other non-essentials, then by cutting “extra” debt payment or “extra” savings.
- Rule 3: Income higher than expected goes first to:
  1. High-interest debt
  2. Emergency fund
  3. Extra investing or big goals

**4. Practical structure in a sheet**

I’ll separate “base” and “flex” inside categories. Example for Food:

- Groceries – Base (non-negotiable)
- Eating Out – Flex (can shrink or grow)

Example formulas (assuming:
- `Actual_EatingOut` is your real spending that month,
- `Budget_EatingOut` is the planned amount):

You can calculate **over/under** like:

- Over/Under Eating Out: `=Actual_EatingOut - Budget_EatingOut`

Then create a “Flex Pool” from:
- Eating Out
- Fun/Entertainment
- Shopping
- Hobbies

And set:

- If one category is over, automatically reduce the others in the Flex Pool proportionally.

For example:

Flex Pool categories and budget:
- Eating Out: `F1`
- Fun: `F2`
- Shopping: `F3`
Flex Total: `=F1+F2+F3`

If Eating Out is over by `O` (positive if over), then reduce each of the *other* flex budgets by:

- New Fun Budget: `=F2 - (O * F2 / Flex_Total)`
- New Shopping Budget: `=F3 - (O * F3 / Flex_Total)`

This way, overspending in one area automatically shrinks others instead of touching essentials.

**5. Automatic extra-debt / extra-savings**

At month end:

- Total Budgeted Flex: `=F1+F2+F3`
- Total Actual Flex: `=Actual_F1+Actual_F2+Actual_F3`
- Flex Leftover: `=Total Budgeted Flex - Total Actual Flex`

If `Flex Leftover > 0`, send it automatically to:
- High-interest debt until paid off, then
- Emergency fund until, say, 3–6 months of expenses, then
- Investing / big goals.

You can implement this with a simple rule line:  
`Extra to Debt/Savings = MAX(Flex Leftover, 0)`

This gives you:
- Auto-scaled budgets by income
- Auto-reallocation between flexible categories
- Auto-routing of leftover money to what matters most

You can drop your real income and categories into this structure directly.
