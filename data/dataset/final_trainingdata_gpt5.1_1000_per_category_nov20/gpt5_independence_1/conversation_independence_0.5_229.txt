### Human: Let's do the turkey chili recipe.

### Assistant: Here’s a straightforward turkey chili recipe sized for about 4 servings.

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb (450 g) ground turkey  
- 1 can (14–15 oz) diced tomatoes  
- 1 can tomato sauce (about 8–15 oz, flexible)  
- 2 cans beans (black, kidney, or pinto), drained and rinsed  
- 1 medium onion, chopped  
- 2 cloves garlic, minced (or 1 tsp garlic powder)  
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil  
- 1–2 tbsp chili powder  
- 1 tsp ground cumin  
- ½–1 tsp paprika (optional)  
- Salt and pepper to taste  
- Water or broth as needed to adjust thickness

**Instructions**
1. **Sauté onion and garlic (5–7 minutes):**  
   - Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat.  
   - Add chopped onion and cook until soft.  
   - Add garlic and cook 1 more minute (don’t let it burn).

2. **Brown the turkey (5–7 minutes):**  
   - Add ground turkey to the pot.  
   - Break it up with a spoon and cook until it’s no longer pink.

3. **Add spices (1–2 minutes):**  
   - Stir in chili powder, cumin, paprika (if using), salt, and pepper.  
   - Cook for about a minute to “wake up” the spices.

4. **Add tomatoes, sauce, and beans (2–3 minutes):**  
   - Pour in the diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, and beans.  
   - Stir well. If it looks very thick, add a bit of water or broth.

5. **Simmer (20–30 minutes):**  
   - Bring to a gentle simmer.  
   - Reduce heat to low, cover partially, and let it cook 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally.  
   - Taste and adjust salt/spices.

6. **Cool and store:**  
   - Let it cool slightly, then portion into containers.  
   - Store in the fridge for up to 3–4 days, or freeze for longer.

You can serve this:
- Over rice or on its own  
- With a bit of cheese or Greek yogurt on top if you like
