Quick-Start Usage Guide:

1) Getting Set Up
- Place the Ergonomic Footrest under your desk or in front of your chair.
- Position it so your knees are bent at roughly 90° and your feet rest comfortably on the surface.
- Ensure the non-slip base is in contact with the floor.

2) Choosing Your Height
- Lower Height (Flatter Side): Use this position if your chair is lower or if you prefer a subtle lift.
- Higher Height / Rocking Mode: Flip the footrest so it’s more angled. Gently rock your feet forward and back to promote circulation.

3) Finding a Comfortable Position
- Sit back fully in your chair with your back supported.
- Place both feet flat on the footrest; avoid crossing your legs for prolonged periods.
- Adjust your chair height so your hips are level with or slightly above your knees.

4) Recommended Use
- Use throughout the workday to relieve pressure on your lower back, hips, and legs.
- Take short standing or walking breaks every 45–60 minutes for best results, while continuing to use the footrest when seated.

Care & Maintenance:

1) Cleaning the Cover
- Remove the cover using the zipper around the base.
- Machine wash cold on gentle cycle with mild detergent.
- Do not bleach.
- Lay flat or hang to air-dry; do not tumble-dry to prevent shrinkage.
- Ensure the cover is fully dry before re-installing.

2) Cleaning the Foam Core
- Do not machine wash or submerge in water.
- Spot clean with a slightly damp cloth and mild soap if needed.
- Allow to air-dry completely away from direct heat or sunlight.

3) Protecting the Non-Slip Base
- Wipe the base periodically with a damp cloth to remove dust and debris.
- Avoid dragging the footrest across rough surfaces to maintain grip.

4) Storage
- Store flat in a cool, dry place when not in use.
- Do not stack heavy items on top of the footrest to preserve foam integrity.
