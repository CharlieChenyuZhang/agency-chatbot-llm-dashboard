4) How to run your mocks (solo or with others)

Coding mocks:
- Use a strict 45-min timer.
- Speak your thinking process out loud.
- Always:
  - Clarify the problem
  - Discuss brute force
  - Move to optimization and justify trade-offs
  - Test on edge cases before coding
  - Analyze time/space complexity at the end

System design mocks:
- Use a 45–60 min timer.
- Structure every answer:
  1) Clarify requirements (functional & non-functional)
  2) Sketch high-level components
  3) Discuss data model
  4) Talk through scale: traffic, storage, QPS, latency targets
  5) Bottlenecks and mitigations (caching, partitioning, queues, etc.)

Behavioral:
- Maintain 10–12 reusable STAR stories that map to themes:
  - Leadership
  - Conflict
  - Failure
  - Ambiguity
  - Tight deadline
  - Biggest impact / proudest project
