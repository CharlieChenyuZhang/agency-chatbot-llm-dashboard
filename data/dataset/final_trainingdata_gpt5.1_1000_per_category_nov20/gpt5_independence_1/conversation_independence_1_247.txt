### Human: How are you deciding what goes into the Action folder vs Waiting?

### Assistant: I’m using the thread state and your role in the conversation to decide:

Goes into Action (you owe something):
- Emails with:
  - direct questions to you (“Can you…?”, “Could you confirm…?”, “Please send…”)  
  - requests for documents, approvals, decisions, or payment  
  - invitations that require an RSVP or choice
- Threads where:
  - the last message is from someone else
  - you have not replied yet
- System emails that clearly require an action:
  - “Confirm your email”
  - “Complete your registration”
  - “Payment failed”
  - “Signature requested”

Goes into Waiting (you’re waiting on others):
- Threads where:
  - you were the last person to reply
  - your message included something like “Let me know”, “Waiting for your response”, “What do you think?”, “Please confirm”
- Support / service tickets after you’ve sent your reply or filled out a form
- Order or refund issues where:
  - you’ve already contacted support
  - the next move is on them

State transitions I’m enforcing:
- When you reply to something in Action:
  - It automatically moves to Waiting (since you’ve done your part).
- When a new reply comes into a Waiting thread:
  - It returns to Inbox + Action if the ball is back in your court.
  - If the reply is just an FYI and requires nothing from you, it moves to Read / Reference instead.

Anything that no longer requires a response from anyone:
- Moves to Archive once the conversation is clearly finished (e.g., a final “Thanks!” from either side with no open request).
