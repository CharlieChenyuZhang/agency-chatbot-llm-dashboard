---

**1. Moderator principles**

1. **Protect safety and clarity first.**  
   If in doubt, remove harmful content quickly; explain later if needed.
2. **Assume good intent, enforce clear impact.**  
   You don’t have to prove intent to act; you act on what a post does to the community.
3. **Be public about boundaries, private about details.**  
   Public: what is/isn’t okay.  
   Private: specific back-and-forth with individuals.

---

**2. Standard moderator actions**

From least to most severe:

1. **Nudge:** Public or private reminder about guidelines.  
2. **Edit/Move:** Adjust title, move post to correct channel, or lightly edit for clarity (if rules allow).  
3. **Delete/Hide:** Remove posts that break rules.  
4. **Warning:** Private message; log it in a simple moderation log.  
5. **Timeout:** Temporary mute or suspension (24–72 hours).  
6. **Ban:** Permanent removal for repeated or severe violations.

---

**3. Handling common issues (scripts)**

**a) Off-topic or low-effort posts**

Action: Move or ask for more detail.

Script (public reply):

> Thanks for sharing this, I’ve moved it to #[channel].  
> To help others respond, can you add: what you’ve already tried and what you’re hoping to learn from the group?

**b) Self-promotion / spam**

Action: Remove, then DM.

Script (DM):

> Hey [Name], we removed your recent post because it was primarily promotional.  
> We keep promo to [rules/channel] so the main feed stays useful.  
> You’re welcome to:  
> - Share what you’re working on in #[allowed-channel]  
> - Or post a case study / lesson that includes your work as an example.  
>  
> Thanks for understanding and for helping us keep things valuable for everyone.

**c) Rudeness / disrespect**

Action: Delete or edit, warn.

Script (DM):

> Hey [Name], I removed part of your recent message because it crossed our “criticize ideas, not people” rule.  
> You’re welcome to disagree strongly with ideas here, but we don’t allow personal attacks or dismissive comments.  
>  
> You don’t need to reply to this, but please keep future posts focused on arguments and evidence, not on other members.

**d) Heated debates between members**

Action: Intervene publicly, then DM both.

Public post:

> Jumping in as moderator:  
> This is getting heated. Let’s keep this thread focused on the topic, not on each other.  
>  
> - Stick to arguments and examples.  
> - Avoid personal remarks or assumptions about motives.  
>  
> I’ll remove any future comments that break this to keep the space safe for everyone.

DM (to each member):

> I noticed the thread with [other member] has gotten tense. I’ve reminded everyone to keep it focused on the topic.  
>  
> You’re welcome to continue the discussion if it stays constructive. If you need to step away, that’s okay too.  
>  
> If you feel there’s something I should know about this interaction, you can tell me here.

**e) Harassment / hate / zero-tolerance issues**

Action: Immediate removal + ban or timeout, depending on severity.

Public note (if needed):

> We removed a post that violated our rules against harassment/hate.  
>  
> To be clear: [community name] does not allow harassment, slurs, or attacks on identity (e.g., race, gender, orientation, religion).  
>  
> If you ever experience something like this here, please DM a moderator with a link or screenshot.

---

**4. Moderation logistics**

- **Moderation log (simple, shared with mod team):**
  - Date  
  - Member  
  - Issue type  
  - Action taken  
  - Link to content (if available)

- **Response times:**
  - Harmful content: aim for under 2 hours (during active hours).  
  - Regular reports: within 24 hours.

- **Escalation rules:**
  - 2 warnings in 30 days → 72-hour timeout.  
  - 2 timeouts in 90 days → ban.  
  - Any explicit hate / threats → immediate ban.

---

**5. Culture shaping (positive moderation)**

Moderation isn’t just removal; it’s also amplification:

- **Once a week:**  
  - Highlight 1–3 great posts or comments.  
  - Thank members by name for helpful behavior.  

Template:

> Shout-out to [Name] for [helpful behavior] in #[channel]. This is exactly the kind of contribution that makes this place valuable.
