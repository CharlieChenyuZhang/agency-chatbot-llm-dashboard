### Assistant: Sure—here’s a fun, no-prep social game using just a standard deck of cards:

**Game: “Mafia Lite” (with cards)**  
Players: 6–15 (perfect for your party)

**What you need:**
- 1 standard deck of cards

**Setup:**
- Decide roles:
  - 2–3 Mafia (bad guys) – e.g., use black cards (Clubs/Spades).
  - 1 Doctor (optional but fun) – e.g., Ace of Hearts.
  - 1 Detective (optional) – e.g., Ace of Diamonds.
  - Everyone else = Villagers – use random red cards.
- Shuffle and deal one card to each player secretly. They look but don’t show anyone.

**Basic Flow (very simple version):**
1. **Night Phase (eyes closed):**
   - Everyone closes their eyes.
   - You (as moderator) say, “Mafia, open your eyes.”  
     Mafia quietly pick **one person** to “eliminate.”
   - “Mafia, close your eyes.”
   - (If using Doctor) “Doctor, open your eyes.” Doctor silently points to someone to save.
   - (If using Detective) “Detective, open your eyes.” Detective silently points to someone; you nod yes/no if that person is Mafia.
   - Everyone closes eyes again.

2. **Day Phase (eyes open):**
   - You announce who (if anyone) was eliminated during the night.
   - The group talks, accuses, and then votes to “eliminate” one person they think is Mafia.
   - That person reveals their card and is out.

3. Repeat night/day until:
   - Mafia are all eliminated (villagers win), or
   - Mafia are equal in number to villagers (Mafia win).

You can keep it more casual and shorter by:
- Running just 1–2 “days” instead of playing to full completion.
- Skipping Doctor/Detective if the group wants super-simple rules.
