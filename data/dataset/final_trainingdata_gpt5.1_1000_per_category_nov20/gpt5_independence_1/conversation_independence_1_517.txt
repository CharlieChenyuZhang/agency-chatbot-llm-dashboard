### Assistant: I’ll design a snack using common staples. Make a quick stovetop caramel popcorn:
1. Pop plain popcorn (stovetop or microwave).
2. In a pan, melt butter with brown sugar and a pinch of salt; simmer until slightly thick and glossy.
3. Toss the popcorn in the caramel until coated; spread on a tray to cool slightly so it firms up but stays a bit chewy.
If you have nuts (like peanuts or almonds), throw a handful in for crunch.

### Human: I’m watching with a friend who doesn’t like sweets. Decide a second snack just for them.
### Assistant: Make a savory roasted chickpea mix for your friend:
1. Drain and rinse canned chickpeas; pat them dry thoroughly.
2. Toss with olive oil, salt, smoked paprika, and garlic powder.
3. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 25–30 minutes, shaking the pan halfway, until crisp.
Serve in a small bowl—they’re crunchy, salty, and not sweet at all.
