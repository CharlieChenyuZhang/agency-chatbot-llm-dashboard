**1. Core structure (simple and sustainable)**  
- 1 weekly long-form piece (newsletter or blog)

- 1 “deep engagement” block per week (commenting, DMs, replies)

**2. Content pillars (rotate them so you never run out of ideas)**  
Use four pillars and cycle through them:

1) **Education / How-to**  
   - “3 mistakes to avoid when…”  
   - “Step-by-step: how to …”

2) **Authority / Proof**  
   - Case studies, client wins, personal milestones  
   - “Before/after” transformations

3) **Perspective / Opinion**  
   - Your take on common industry advice  
   - “Hot takes” that show how you think

4) **Human / Story / Behind-the-scenes**

   - “What I’m learning / struggling with right now”

**3. Weekly rhythm (plug-and-play)**  

**Monday**  
- Post: Educational “how-to” on LinkedIn (then adapt for IG).  
- Task: 10–15 minutes replying to DMs/comments from last week.

**Tuesday**  
- Post: Short story (human/behind-the-scenes) with a clear takeaway.  
- Task: Collect 3–5 ideas from client calls, emails, or your own notes.

**Wednesday**  
- Post: Opinion/perspective piece (“Everyone says X, but here’s why that’s wrong/incomplete”).  
- Task: 15 minutes engaging with others’ posts in your niche.

**Thursday**  
- Post: Authority/proof (mini case study, testimonial, or “here’s what happened when I tried…”).  
- Task: Draft outline for your weekly long-form piece.

**Friday**  
- Post: Recap/roundup or “1 big lesson from this week.”  
- Task: Finalize and publish your long-form newsletter/blog.

**Weekend (optional, low-pressure)**  
- Light engagement only: reply to comments, save ideas, no requirement to post.

**4. Execution system (to keep it consistent)**  

- **Idea capture:**  
  - Keep a “Content Ideas” note on your phone. Every time something interesting comes up (client question, mistake, insight), drop a one-line note.

- **Batching routine:**  
  - Once per week (e.g., Thursday or Friday), spend 60–90 minutes:  
    1) Turn 5 ideas into short post outlines (headline + 3 bullet points).  
    2) Outline the long-form piece (intro, 3–5 key points, conclusion/CTA).

- **Reuse and repurpose:**  
  - Long-form → 3–5 short posts.  
  - Short posts → newsletter “roundup” section.  
  - One strong idea → multiple formats (text post, carousel, short video).

**5. Simple success rules**  
- Consistency > volume: keep the above cadence steady for 8–12 weeks.  
- Repeat what performs: recycle your best posts every ~8 weeks with tweaks.  
- Use clear CTAs: “Reply ‘guide’ if you want the checklist,” “Comment ‘plan’ for the framework,” etc., to drive engagement and leads.

- Repurpose automatically:  
  - Same text can go to LinkedIn and Instagram with tiny changes. Don’t rewrite from scratch.
