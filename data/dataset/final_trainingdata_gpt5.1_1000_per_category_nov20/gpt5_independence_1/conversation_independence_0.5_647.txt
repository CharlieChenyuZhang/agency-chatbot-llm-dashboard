For a Mediterranean-ish, weight‑loss‑friendly approach when eating out 2–3 times a week, here are simple rules you can follow:

1. **Prioritize this plate structure:**  
   - ~½ plate vegetables (salad, steamed, grilled, roasted)  
   - ~¼ plate lean protein (chicken, fish, lean beef, tofu)  
   - ~¼ plate whole grains or starchy side (brown rice, potatoes, whole‑grain pasta)  

2. **Good choices by cuisine (quick guide):**  
   - **Mediterranean:** grilled chicken or fish, kebabs, salads with dressing on the side, hummus with veggies, lentil or bean dishes.  
   - **American:** grilled chicken/fish, steak with veggies and baked potato (go light on butter/sour cream), burger without mayo + side salad instead of fries.  
   - **Mexican:** fajitas (grilled meat + veggies), burrito bowl with beans, rice, lots of veggies, salsa, guac; avoid or limit sour cream and cheese.  
   - **Asian:** stir‑fries with veggies and lean protein, steamed rice (small portion), sushi with more fish/veggie rolls and fewer fried rolls; watch sugary sauces.  

3. **Portion control tricks:**  
   - Ask for a take‑out box **with the meal**, and put away ~⅓–½ before you start if portions are huge.  
   - Choose water or unsweetened tea instead of soda/juice.  

4. **What to watch out for:**  
   - Creamy sauces, heavy dressings, lots of bread/chips before the meal, sugary drinks.  
   - “Hidden” calories in appetizers and desserts—if you really want dessert, try sharing.
