Now I’m imposing a simple pronunciation focus so your speaking will sound better from the start.

Key points:
- “r” single: softer, like a quick “r” in “butter” (American)  
- “rr” double: strong, rolled r (in “perro”)  
- “j” (jamón): like an English “h” but stronger in the throat  
- “ll” (llamo): often sounds like “y” in “yes” (in many dialects)
