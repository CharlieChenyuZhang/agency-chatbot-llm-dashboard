“Download our [whitepaper] about [problem].”

Instead of broad persona/keyword targeting, they:

- Uploaded **active pipeline accounts** (open opps, stages > “Discovery”)
- Included **lookalike audiences** based on those accounts (optional, secondary)
- Focused titles:
  - Economic buyers (VP/Head)
  - Key blockers: security, ops, finance

Goal:  
Get the decision kit into the hands of **stakeholders who were already blocking deals**, not strangers at the top of funnel.
