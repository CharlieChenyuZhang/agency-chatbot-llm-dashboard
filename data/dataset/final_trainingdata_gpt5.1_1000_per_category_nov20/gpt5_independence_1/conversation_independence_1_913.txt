     - Trash the rest;  
     - Start a new envelope for the new month.

4. Notes and printouts  
   - Consolidate loose notes into your main notebook whenever possible; rewrite or summarize important points.  
   - Any printout you need for more than a week goes into either “Action” or “Reference”; nothing stays loose on the desk.
