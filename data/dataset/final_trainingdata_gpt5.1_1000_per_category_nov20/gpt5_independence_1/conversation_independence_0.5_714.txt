### Human: Medium level sounds fine. One more thing: any quick tricks for when my mind goes blank mid-sentence?

### Assistant: Yes—having a plan for that can reduce a lot of fear.

Try this simple “blanking protocol”:

1. **Pause and breathe once.**  
   Don’t rush to fill the silence. A 2–3 second pause feels longer to you than to them.

2. **Look at your notes for a “checkpoint.”**  
   Before the presentation, put **short headings** in bold or larger font (e.g., “Progress,” “Risk 1,” “Risk 2,” “Close”).  
   If you blank, just find the next heading and continue from there, even if you skip a sentence.

3. **Use a rescue phrase.**  
   Have one or two generic sentences you can fall back on, like:  
   - “Let me say that a bit more simply.”  
   - “The main point here is…”  
   - “So, where we are right now is…”

   You can use one of these while you quickly glance at your notes and pick up the thread.

4. **Remind yourself they don’t know your script.**  
   If you skip a detail or reorder something, the audience usually has no idea. They just see a brief pause.

- Section headings,
- 2–3 words under each,
- Your rescue phrases at the top.
