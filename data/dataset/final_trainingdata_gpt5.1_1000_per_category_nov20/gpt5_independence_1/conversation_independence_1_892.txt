## Film Descriptions

### The Dark Knight (2008)
A crime thriller set in Gotham City where Batman faces his most chaotic adversary, the Joker. The film explores moral ambiguity, escalation of crime, and the cost of trying to impose order on a city that resists it.

### Inception (2010)
A science-fiction heist film where professional thieves infiltrate dreams to steal (or plant) ideas in people’s minds. Layered dream worlds, a ticking clock, and emotional stakes around memory and grief drive the story.

### Interstellar (2014)
A space epic about a group of astronauts who travel through a wormhole in search of a new home for humanity. It blends hard science, time dilation, and cosmic imagery with a deeply personal father–daughter relationship.

### Dunkirk (2017)
A World War II survival thriller depicting the Dunkirk evacuation from three perspectives: land, sea, and air. Minimal dialogue, intense sound design, and non-linear timelines create constant tension and urgency.

### Tenet (2020)
A high-concept espionage thriller built around “time inversion,” where objects and people can move backward through time. The protagonist navigates a temporal war to prevent a future catastrophe, with action sequences that play both forward and in reverse.

### Oppenheimer (2023)
A biographical drama about J. Robert Oppenheimer and the development of the atomic bomb. The film intercuts timelines, hearings, and personal crises to explore power, responsibility, and the long shadow of scientific discovery.
