### Human: Okay, so what exactly should I do this week?

### Assistant: I’ll map out a complete 7-day starter plan for you.

**Day 1**
- Piano (45 min)
  - 10 min: Learn the white keys from C to C. Say the note names aloud as you touch them.
  - 10 min: Right-hand 5-finger pattern on C (C–D–E–F–G and back), slow and even.
  - 15 min: Simple rhythm: play 4 slow counts per note, then 2, then 1.
  - 10 min: Learn your first piece: “Ode to Joy” melody, right hand only.
- Listening (optional 10 min): Listen to a piano playlist and just pay attention to left vs right hand.

**Day 2**
- Piano (45–60 min)
  - 10 min: Review key names (white keys).
  - 10 min: C 5-finger pattern, then on G (starting from G).
  - 15 min: Continue “Ode to Joy” until you can play it without stopping, even slowly.
  - 10–15 min: Learn basic note values: whole, half, quarter notes and a 4/4 time signature.

**Day 3**
- Ukulele (30–40 min)
  - 10 min: Learn how to hold it and basic strumming (downstrokes on each beat).
  - 20–30 min: Learn 3 chords: C, Am, F. Practice changing between them slowly.

**Day 4**
- Piano (45 min)
  - 10 min: Right-hand C 5-finger pattern legato (smooth, connected).
  - 10 min: Begin left-hand C 5-finger pattern.
  - 15 min: Hands separate: C major scale (just one octave, slowly).
  - 10 min: Pick a second simple melody (e.g., “Twinkle, Twinkle Little Star”) by right hand.

**Day 5**
- Piano (45–60 min)
  - 15 min: C major scale, right and left hand separately.
  - 15 min: Combine simple left-hand bass notes (just C’s) with your right-hand melody.
  - 15–30 min: Slow work on “Ode to Joy” or your second melody until it feels smoother.

**Day 6**
- Ukulele (30–40 min)
  - 10 min: Review C, Am, F chords.
  - 20–30 min: Play a 4-chord loop: C–Am–F–G (use a simple G shape). Strum down on every beat.

**Day 7**
- Light day (20–30 min)
  - Play everything you’ve learned this week once or twice.
  - Notice what feels hardest: finger stretches, remembering notes, rhythm, or switching chords.
