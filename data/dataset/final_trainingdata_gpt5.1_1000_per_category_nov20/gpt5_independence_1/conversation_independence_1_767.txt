Title:
The 30‑Minute Weekly Reset That Makes Work Feel Lighter

Hook:
There are two kinds of Mondays.

One starts with email, Slack, and 17 browser tabs. By 10 a.m., your day is already off the rails.

The other starts with a calm, 30‑minute reset that makes the rest of the week feel lighter—and still gets all the “real work” done.

This newsletter is about choosing the second kind, on purpose.

Main idea:
You’re going to run a simple Weekly Reset every Monday: a 30‑minute process to clear your head, set priorities, and take control before the chaos starts.

Step‑by‑step Weekly Reset (30 minutes)
1) Brain dump everything (7 minutes)
- Open a blank page.
- Write down everything on your mind: tasks, worries, ideas, reminders.
- Don’t organize. Don’t judge. Just empty your head.

2) Sort the chaos (8 minutes)
Create four headings:
- This week
- Delegate
- Drop
- Later

Then:
- Move each item under one heading.
- Be ruthless. If it doesn’t matter, it goes under “Drop.”
- If it doesn’t need you, it goes under “Delegate.”
- If it matters but not this week, it goes under “Later.”

3) Choose 3 must‑win outcomes (5 minutes)
From the “This week” list, pick your Top 3 outcomes:
- Not tasks like “work on report,” but outcomes like “Finish draft of Q2 report.”
- Ask: “If I only achieved three things this week, which would matter most?”

Write them as:
1. [Outcome #1]
2. [Outcome #2]
3. [Outcome #3]

4) Block time for your Top 3 (7 minutes)
- Open your calendar.
- For each outcome, block at least one focused session (60–90 minutes).
- Treat those blocks as meetings with yourself: no rescheduling unless absolutely necessary.

5) Close the loop (3 minutes)
- Send any quick “delegate” messages now (2–3 lines each).
- Delete or archive items in “Drop.”
- Move “Later” items into a separate note or your task system.

That’s your Weekly Reset. 30 minutes. One time. Huge payoff.

Template: Weekly Reset checklist
You can paste this into a note app and reuse it every Monday:

Weekly Reset (30 minutes)
[ ] Brain dump everything (7 min)
[ ] Sort into: This week / Delegate / Drop / Later (8 min)
[ ] Pick Top 3 outcomes for the week (5 min)
[ ] Block calendar time for each outcome (7 min)
[ ] Send quick delegate messages + park “Later” items (3 min)

Quick hits (3 tiny ideas to try this week)
1) Calendar rule:
Never start Monday with a meeting before 10 a.m. Protect the first hour for your reset and real work.

2) Focus rule:
When working on a Top 3 outcome, close email and chat by default. If that feels extreme, try just 25 minutes at a time.

3) Stop‑doing rule:
Pick one low‑value recurring task this week and:
- automate it,
- batch it,
- or ask if it’s still needed at all.

Call‑to‑action:
After you try this once, reply to your own notes with one line: “My Top 3 outcomes this week are…” and keep it where you’ll see it daily. The more weeks you repeat this, the lighter your Mondays will feel.

Paste this into your first issue, add a short personal intro at the top if you want, and schedule it for next Monday at 8:00 AM.
