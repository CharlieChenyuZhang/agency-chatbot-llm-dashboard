### Assistant: That’s very common. I’ll bake in some “formula debugging habits” from the start so it becomes less mysterious.

Here are 3 simple rules I’d like you to follow whenever a formula misbehaves this week:

1. **Check the range:**  
   - Click the cell with the formula and look at the colored border around the referenced range.  
   - Ask: “Is this grabbing the right cells, or too many/too few?”

2. **Check the function name and parentheses:**  
   - Make sure it’s something like `=SUM(` and not `=SUMM(`.  
   - Confirm you have a closing `)`.

3. **Try a tiny test:**  
   - In a new spot, make a very small example (like 3 numbers) and test the same formula there.  
   - If it works on 3 numbers but not on your big table, the issue is probably the range, not the formula itself.
