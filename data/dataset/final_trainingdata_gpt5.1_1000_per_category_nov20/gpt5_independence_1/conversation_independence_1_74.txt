### Human: Can you lay out exactly what I should do for the next 7 days, step by step?
### Assistant: I’ll define a precise 7-day sequence for you. Follow this as written.

**Daily constants (every day):**
- Time: **6:45 am**
- Duration: Aim for **20 minutes** (3-minute emergency minimum).
- Tool: same doc or notebook.
- End each session with a one-line log.

---

**Day 1 – Character: someone hiding something**
- Write a character who is keeping a secret.
- Include:
  - What they’re afraid will happen if the secret gets out.
  - One small behavior that gives them away (eye contact, fidget, avoidance).
- Write continuously for 20 minutes.

**Day 2 – Character: someone who just lost something**
- They’ve just lost:
  - A job, an object, a person, or a dream.
- Show them in the **first hour after the loss**.
- Focus on:
  - What they do.
  - What they don’t do but want to.
  - One object in the room that suddenly feels important.

**Day 3 – Scene: two people want different things**
- Write a scene with two characters who each want something from the other.
- They do **not** say what they really want directly.
- Show this through:
  - Body language.
  - What they avoid mentioning.
  - A small conflict (disagreement, interruption, silence).

**Day 4 – Scene: a decision**
- A character must make a choice between:
  - Comfort vs. growth, or
  - Loyalty vs. honesty.
- Keep the scene in **one location**.
- End with them making a decision (even if you don’t show the consequences).

**Day 5 – Short story (300–600 words):**
- Choose one character from Days 1–4.
- Give them:
  - A simple goal for the day (meet someone, quit something, confess something).
  - One obstacle.
- Write the story from their point of view.

**Day 6 – Short story from another angle:**
- Take the same event from Day 5.
- Write it again from:
  - Another character’s perspective, or
  - The same character 5 years later, looking back.

**Day 7 – Free story:**
- Take your favorite idea, character, or scene from the week.
- Write a new 300–600 word story with:
  - A clear beginning (something changes),
  - Middle (complication),
  - End (something is different, even slightly).

Each day, finish with:
- `Day X – [minutes] – [1 short label, e.g., “secret-keeper story”]`
