### Human: Describe a simple maintenance process for the KB.

### Assistant: A simple maintenance process starts by assigning clear ownership for each major section or key page, so every important area has someone responsible for its accuracy. Next, set a regular review cadence, for example monthly for fast-changing content like release notes and quarterly for more stable content like product overviews; owners receive reminders to review and update their pages. When employees spot issues, they use a lightweight process, such as commenting directly on the page or tagging the owner in Slack, so corrections are easy to request and track. Finally, once a quarter, the main owner of the KB reviews analytics and feedback, removes outdated or unused pages, consolidates duplicates, and communicates any structural changes to the company so everyone stays aligned.
