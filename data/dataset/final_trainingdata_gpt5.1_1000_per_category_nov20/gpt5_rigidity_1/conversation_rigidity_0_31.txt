- **Constitutional Monarchy:** A constitutional system tried to keep the king but limit his power. Distrust grew, especially after the royal family’s attempted flight to Varennes (1791).
- **Radicalization & Republic (1792–1793):** War with European monarchies escalated tensions. The monarchy was abolished; France became a republic. Louis XVI was tried and executed in 1793.
- **Reign of Terror (1793–1794):** Under the Committee of Public Safety (associated with Robespierre), the government used mass executions and repression to eliminate “enemies of the revolution.”
- **Thermidor & Directory:** Robespierre fell in 1794. A more conservative phase followed under the Directory (1795–1799), marked by corruption, instability, and ongoing wars.
- **Rise of Napoleon:** Political fatigue and military dependence paved the way for Napoleon Bonaparte’s coup in 1799, which ended the revolutionary decade and began the Consulate.
