**Core Week 1 Phrases**

1. Konnichiwa.  
   - Hello / Good afternoon.  
   - Neutral daytime greeting.

2. Ohayō gozaimasu.  
   - Good morning (polite).

3. Konbanwa.  
   - Good evening.

4. Sayōnara.  
   - Goodbye.  
   - Slightly formal; often used when you don’t expect to see the person soon. “Ja mata” is more casual.

5. Hajimemashite.  
   - Nice to meet you.  
   - Used at the *start* of a first‑time introduction.

6. Watashi wa [your name] desu.  
   - I am [your name].  
   - Example: “Watashi wa Alex desu.”

7. [Country] kara kimashita.  
   - I came from [country]. / I’m from [country].  
   - Example: “Amerika kara kimashita.”

8. Yoroshiku onegaishimasu.  
   - Hard to translate literally; in this context: “Nice to meet you / Please treat me well.”  
   - Said at the *end* of a self‑introduction.

### Human: Let's do casual phrases. Also can we skip saying "watashi wa"? I heard Japanese people sometimes drop it.
### Assistant: Yes, good instinct. Native speakers often drop “watashi wa” when it’s obvious who the subject is. However, as a beginner, it’s useful to learn the full version first and *then* start dropping it.

We’ll do both:

**Casual but still safe for most situations**

1. [Name] desu.  
   - I’m [Name].  
   - Slightly shorter than “Watashi wa [Name] desu.”  
   - Example: “Alex desu.”

2. Dōzo yoroshiku.  
   - A bit shorter/casual than “Yoroshiku onegaishimasu.”  
   - Good for everyday intros.

3. Hajimemashite, [Name] desu. Dōzo yoroshiku.  
   - “Nice to meet you, I’m [Name]. Pleased to meet you.”

4. Genki desu ka?  
   - How are you? (literally “Are you well?”)  
   - Often used in more friendly contexts than in ultra‑formal business.

5. Genki desu.  
   - I’m fine / I’m good.

6. Maamaa desu.  
   - So‑so.

7. Arigatō.  
   - Thanks (casual).

8. Arigatō gozaimasu.  
   - Thank you (polite).

9. Sumimasen.  
   - Excuse me / I’m sorry / Thank you (in some contexts).  
   - Very useful Swiss‑army‑knife phrase.

You can absolutely say things like:
- “Hajimemashite, Alex desu. Dōzo yoroshiku.”
instead of
- “Hajimemashite, watashi wa Alex desu. Yoroshiku onegaishimasu.”
