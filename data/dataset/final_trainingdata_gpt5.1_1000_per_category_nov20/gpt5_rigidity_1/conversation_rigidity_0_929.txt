### Assistant: Understood. I’ll focus on making this as practical and short as possible.

Use this checklist for your first call/meeting with any kitchen contractor or design-build firm:

1. **Budget & Scope Fit**
   - “Most of your full kitchen projects—what budget range are they usually in?”
   - Red flag: They mostly do $80k+ jobs or mostly sub-$20k “refreshes.”

2. **Itemized Estimate**
   - “Can you provide an itemized estimate (labor, materials, allowances) rather than one lump sum?”
   - Red flag: Refusal to itemize anything or extreme vagueness.

3. **Permits & Code**
   - “Do you pull permits on my behalf, and are you familiar with Denver’s requirements for kitchen remodels?”
   - Red flag: They discourage permits or say they “usually don’t need them.”

4. **Project Management**
   - “Who will be my day-to-day contact, and how often will I get schedule and progress updates?”
   - Red flag: No clear point person or vague communication plan.

5. **Timeline & Scheduling**
   - “From signed contract to completion, what’s a realistic timeline for a project like mine?”
   - Red flag: Promising a very fast completion with no caveats.

6. **Change Orders & Extras**
   - “How do you handle changes or unforeseen issues, and how are those priced and approved?”
   - Red flag: No written process; they just “handle it as we go.”

7. **Recent, Local References**
   - “Can you give me 2–3 recent clients within the last 12–18 months whose kitchen budgets were similar to mine?”
   - Red flag: Only very old references, or none that match your budget.

8. **Insurance & Licensing**
   - “Can you provide current proof of insurance and licensing before we sign?”
   - Red flag: Hesitation, excuses, or incomplete documents.
