### Human: I was hoping you’d just teach me without me needing to learn to read sheet music. Can we skip reading entirely and just use finger numbers and YouTube tutorials?

### Assistant: We *can* do that, and many people do learn pieces that way. It’s perfectly valid if your main goal is “play a few songs I like.”

Trade‑off:

- **Pros of skipping reading (short term):**
  - Faster to start playing recognizable music.
  - Less initial frustration.

- **Cons (medium/long term):**
  - You become dependent on specific tutorials.
  - Learning new songs is slower.
  - Harder to switch songs, keys, or arrangements on your own.

Given your 15‑minute limit, I suggest a compromise, not full reading or full avoidance:

**Compromise approach (recommended):**
- 10–12 min: Learn via tutorials (by ear/finger numbers)  
- 3–5 min: Tiny bit of reading practice so future songs get easier

If you absolutely want **zero** reading, I’ll respect that. In that case, your 15 minutes might look like:

- 3 min: Finger warm‑up (following a short video)  
- 10 min: Work through 2–4 bars of a tutorial video, looped slowly  
- 2 min: Play through all the parts you’ve learned so far

Tell me which path you prefer:

1. **Hybrid (tutorials + basic reading)** – more effort now, more freedom later  
2. **Tutorial‑only (no reading)** – quicker gratification, but more dependence on videos

I’ll build your next steps around your choice.
