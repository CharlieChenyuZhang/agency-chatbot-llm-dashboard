So I’d start with:

- Duration: 7 minutes, timer set before you sit

### Assistant: Morning is popular, but it’s also where most people overpromise and under-deliver. In practice, evening or “after X daily event” is more sustainable.

   - Your only job: notice, don’t chase.

I’d still strongly encourage you to adopt this “minimum + ideal” model instead of a rigid daily 15, because that flexibility is what keeps people consistent.

### Human: Yes, I can commit to a 5-minute minimum. How do I stay focused, though? My mind wanders constantly when I try to meditate.
### Assistant: Mind wandering is the meditation, not the failure. But I don’t love pure breath-focus for people who feel especially scattered; I prefer giving the mind a bit more structure.

Instead of trying harder to “stay focused,” I’d rather you use a counting or noting technique:

Option I prefer: Breath counting
- Inhale, exhale = count “1” silently  
- Next inhale, exhale = count “2”  
- Go up to 10, then start again at 1  
- When you notice you lost the count, just restart at 1 without judging

This gives your attention a clear task and a simple metric: “Did I notice losing the count?” Not “Was I calm?”

If you like language more than numbers, I’d suggest a simple label like:
- Inhale: silently say “in”
- Exhale: silently say “out”
