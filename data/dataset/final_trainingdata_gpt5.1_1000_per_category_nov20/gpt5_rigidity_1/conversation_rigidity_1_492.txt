### Human: Help me become fluent in French within a year

### Assistant: 
Here is a focused 12‑month roadmap to reach strong conversational fluency (B2-ish) in a year:

1. **Daily habit (Year-round) – 45–60 min/day**
   - 15–20 min: vocab/grammar (spaced repetition, short lessons).
   - 15–20 min: listening (podcasts, YouTube, graded audio).
   - 15–20 min: speaking (tutor, language partner, shadowing aloud).

2. **Months 1–3: Foundations**
   - Learn pronunciation rules and basic sounds; start shadowing simple dialogues.
   - Core grammar: present tense, common past, basic future, pronouns, articles, basic word order.
   - Build ~800–1000 high-frequency words (daily SRS flashcards).
   - Start 1–2 tutor sessions/week focused on survival topics (introductions, daily routine, family, work, hobbies).

3. **Months 4–6: Solidify and expand**
   - Add more tenses (imparfait, futur simple, conditionnel présent), common connectors (mais, donc, parce que, quand).
   - Reach ~2000–2500 words with thematic vocab (travel, food, health, work, studies).
   - 2+ speaking sessions/week (tutor or exchange); aim for 20–30 min of active speaking each session.
   - Begin reading short, graded readers or simple news (e.g., 5–10 min/day).

4. **Months 7–9: Push conversation & comprehension**
   - Focus on fluidity: retell your day, summarize short videos/articles out loud.
   - Listen to native content at slightly above your level (slow news, learner podcasts) daily.
   - Target 3–4 hours/week of real conversation (tutors + partners).
   - Learn set phrases and fillers to sound natural (eh bien, en fait, du coup, quand même).

5. **Months 10–12: Specialization & polishing**
   - Choose 2–3 domains you care about (work field, hobbies, travel) and learn their vocab.
   - Do weekly “immersion blocks” (e.g., 2–3 hours with only French: media, thinking, talking to yourself).
   - Record yourself speaking weekly, compare to tutor feedback, and fix recurring mistakes.
   - If needed, prepare for a test like DELF B2 to structure your goals.

6. **Weekly structure example**
   - 5 days: 45–60 min study as above.
   - 2 days: lighter review or pure fun in French (series, music, games).
   - 2–3 tutor/partner sessions per week spread out.

7. **Progress checks (every 3 months)**
   - Record a 2–3 minute monologue on “who I am / what I did this week.”
   - Re-take it every three months and compare fluency, accuracy, and vocab range.
