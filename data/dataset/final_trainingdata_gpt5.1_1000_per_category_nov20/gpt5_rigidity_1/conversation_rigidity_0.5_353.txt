### Human: Could you give me a quick formula I can use on my phone without needing you each time?
### Assistant: Absolutely. Here’s a simple way you can do it on your phone calculator:

If:
- Original price = P  
- Discount rate = d (e.g., 20% → 0.20)  
- Tax rate = t (e.g., 15% → 0.15)

Then:

1) Discounted price:  
   `Discounted = P × (1 − d)`

2) Final total with tax:  
   `Total = Discounted × (1 + t)`

Or combined into one step:  
`Total = P × (1 − d) × (1 + t)`

For your case (P = 79.99, d = 0.20, t = 0.15), you’d type into your calculator:

`79.99 × 0.8 × 1.15`

Your phone will handle the rounding, and you’ll get essentially the same number we calculated: about **73.59**.
