### Assistant: I’ll explain sliding window, but I want to frame it the way interviewers implicitly think about it, not just as “a technique.”

**Core idea (the only sentence you really need):**  
Use a sliding window when you’re working with *contiguous segments* (subarrays/substrings) and you need to maintain some property (sum, count, uniqueness, etc.) as you expand and shrink that segment.

Instead of definitions, think in terms of **questions** you should ask yourself in an interview:

1. Is the problem about **contiguous** elements (subarray/substring)?
2. Does the window need to **grow and sometimes shrink** based on a condition?
3. Can I update my answer incrementally when I add/remove elements from the edges?

If “yes” to these, sliding window is a strong candidate.

I’ll give you **3 levels of practice**, not a long list:

**Level 1 (Classic)**
