### Assistant: 1. **Month 1 – Foundations:** Solidify Excel/Google Sheets skills (formulas, pivot tables, charts) and refresh basic statistics (distributions, mean/median, correlation).

5. **Month 5 – Portfolio projects:** Create 2–3 end-to-end projects (data cleaning, analysis, visualization, written summary) and publish them on GitHub and a simple portfolio site.  
6. **Month 6 – Job prep:** Tailor your resume, practice explaining your projects, do data-analyst-style interview questions (SQL, case questions), and start applying to internships and junior roles.
