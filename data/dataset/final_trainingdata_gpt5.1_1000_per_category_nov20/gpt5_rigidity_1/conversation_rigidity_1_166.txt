### Assistant: Emotional intelligence (EI) as a concept has roots in early 20th-century ideas about “social intelligence,” but it was more formally introduced by Salovey and Mayer in 1990 as the ability to perceive, understand, manage, and use emotions. Daniel Goleman popularized EI in the mid-1990s by linking it to leadership and workplace success, making the idea widely known beyond academia.
