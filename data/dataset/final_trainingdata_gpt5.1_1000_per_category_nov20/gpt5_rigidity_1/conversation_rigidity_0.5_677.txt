**Basic 5-step edit workflow in Snapseed:**
1. **Crop & Straighten**
   - Fix crooked horizons.
   - Crop to remove distractions at the edges.

2. **Brightness & Contrast**
   - Slightly lower brightness for moody/urban shots.
   - Increase contrast a bit to make textures pop.

3. **Highlights & Shadows**
   - Lower highlights if bright areas are blown out.
   - Raise shadows a little if dark areas are too black.

4. **Structure & Clarity**
   - Increase “Structure” a bit for extra detail in walls, rust, etc.
   - Don’t overdo it or it will look crunchy.

5. **Color**
   - Slightly lower saturation for a more gritty look.
   - Or shift towards cooler tones (more blue) for a cold, urban feel.

### Human: Let’s go with Snapseed. Can you do a quick step-by-step for editing a photo of an old door?

### Assistant: Sure. Here’s a practical, minimal workflow for an old door in Snapseed:

1. **Open the photo in Snapseed**
   - Tap the big “+” or “Open,” choose your photo.

2. **Crop & Straighten**
   - Tap **Tools → Crop**.
   - Choose “Free” or a ratio (like 4:5).
   - Adjust:
     - Make sure the door edges are vertical.
     - Remove distracting edges (like half-cut objects).
   - Tap the checkmark.

3. **Tune Image (brightness, contrast, etc.)**
   - Tap **Tools → Tune Image**.
   - Slide up/down to choose:
     - **Brightness**: Slightly down (e.g., -5 to -15) for mood.
     - **Contrast**: Slightly up (+5 to +15) for punch.
     - **Ambiance**: Try slight positive (+5 to +15) if it looks too flat.
   - Adjust until it feels right, then tap the checkmark.

4. **Details (structure for texture)**
   - Tap **Tools → Details**.
   - Choose **Structure**.
   - Increase slightly (+10 to +25) to bring out wood grain, peeling paint, etc.
   - Avoid going to extremes to keep it natural.
   - Tap the checkmark.

5. **Optional: Vignette**
   - Tap **Tools → Vignette**.
   - Slightly darken the corners so the eye goes to the center of the door.
   - Keep it subtle so it doesn’t look fake.
   - Tap the checkmark.

6. **Optional: Slight Desaturation**
   - Back to **Tools → Tune Image**.
   - Choose **Saturation**.
   - Move a bit left (-5 to -15) to get a more muted, gritty look.
   - Tap the checkmark.

7. **Export**
   - Tap **Export** → “Save a copy” so you keep the original photo too.

Next time you go shooting, you can take a before/after of one door and see how much these small tweaks change the mood.
