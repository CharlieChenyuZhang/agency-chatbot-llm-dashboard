### Assistant: QA (Quality Assurance) testers help make sure software works correctly before and after it’s released. Day to day, a **manual QA tester** typically:

- Follows test steps (like: log in, click X, check Y appears).
- Tries to break things: using wrong inputs, unexpected clicks, weird sequences.
- Records bugs with screenshots, descriptions, and steps to reproduce.
- Works closely with developers and sometimes product managers.

For a *junior manual QA* role, you usually need:
- Basic understanding of how web or mobile apps work (navigation, forms, logins).
- Attention to detail and clear written communication.
- Some knowledge of test cases, bug reports, and common tools (e.g., Jira, TestRail).
