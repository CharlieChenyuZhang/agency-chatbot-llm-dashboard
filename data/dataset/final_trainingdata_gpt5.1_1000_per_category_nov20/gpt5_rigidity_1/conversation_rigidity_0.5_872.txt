### Human: I really hate crowds though. Is there any way to avoid that without paying a ton more?
### Assistant: Completely avoiding crowds without paying more is tough, but you can get close by adjusting *when* and *where* you train rather than only *which* gym:

1) Time strategy (biggest impact, no extra cost)
   - Busiest times at most gyms: ~5:30–8:00 pm weekdays.
   - Much quieter times:
     - Very early: 5–7 am
     - Late evening: after ~8:30–9 pm
     - Midday: 10 am–3 pm (if your schedule allows)

If you can shift your workouts even 30–60 minutes earlier or later, you may cut the crowd down a lot, especially at a place like Gold’s.

2) Area strategy within the gym
   - Even in a busy gym, certain zones are less popular:
     - Platforms / racks at off-peak hours.
     - Dumbbell areas further from mirrors.
   - Plan workouts that are flexible:
     - Example: If squat racks are taken, you can do front squats or RDLs with dumbbells/barbells in another area until a rack opens.

3) Alternative to avoid crowds *and* extra costs
   - A partial home setup plus a cheaper gym:
     - Do your main lifting at home (if you have any space at all—a barbell, some plates, and a rack over time).
     - Use the gym only for accessories (machines, cables) during off-peak hours.
   This is more of a long-term solution but can actually be cost-effective over a year or two.
