Breakfast  
Oats with banana and peanut butter, or toast with eggs and fruit; include water and a small coffee or tea if you already tolerate caffeine.

During Run  
For runs over 60 minutes, sip water regularly and take 30–60 g of carbs per hour from gels, chews, or sports drink.

After Run  
Within 1 hour: 20–30 g protein plus 40–60 g carbs, such as yogurt with granola and fruit, or a turkey sandwich and fruit.

Dinner  
Balanced plate: half vegetables, one-quarter whole grains (rice, pasta, quinoa), one-quarter lean protein (chicken, fish, tofu), plus healthy fats.

### Human: From now on, keep answers under 3 sentences. How should I pace the race to break 4 hours?

### Assistant: For a sub-4-hour marathon, you need to average about 9:09 per mile (5:41 per km). Aim to start slightly slower than goal pace for the first 2–3 miles, then settle into 9:05–9:10 per mile through about mile 20. If you feel strong after mile 20, try to very gradually speed up, but avoid any big surges.
