### End-of-day routine (10–15 minutes max)

**1. Process Apple Notes (5 minutes)**
- Open `INBOX – To move to Obsidian`.
- For each note:

**2. Review today’s Daily Note (5 minutes)**

  - 2–3 secondary tasks
