### Assistant: A couple of quick things you can try that sometimes fix this kind of issue:

1. **Reset the earbuds completely**  
   - Put both earbuds in the case and close the lid for 30–60 seconds.  
   - Take them out and **forget the device** on your phone:
     - Go to Bluetooth settings → tap “SoundBeats Airbuds X” → “Forget” / “Remove.”  
   - With the earbuds out, look for a **multifunction button** or **touch area**:
     - Often you can **press and hold both earbuds for ~10–15 seconds** until you see a flashing pattern that indicates a reset (the manual or product page usually describes this).  
   - Put them back in the case, close it, then re-open and pair again as if they were new.

2. **Test on a second device**  
   - Pair them with a different phone/tablet.  
   - If the left earbud still cuts out and pairing is flaky on multiple devices, it’s almost certainly a hardware issue.

3. **Check battery/contact issues**  
   - Make sure there’s **no dust or debris** in the charging case or on the earbud contacts.  
   - Gently clean the contacts with a dry cloth or cotton swab.  
   - Charge them fully and try again.
