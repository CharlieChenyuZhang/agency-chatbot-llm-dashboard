### Assistant: A venue manager and a day‑of coordinator sound similar, but they usually cover different things.

Venue manager typically:
- Focuses on anything related to the venue itself (building, staff, in‑house catering/bar).
- Makes sure tables, chairs, and basic setup are done per your layout.
- Protects the venue’s interests (rules, timing for staff, etc.).

Day‑of coordinator typically:
- Manages your overall timeline (hair/makeup, photos, ceremony start, speeches, cake cutting, etc.).
- Cues the ceremony processional.
- Coordinates all vendors (photographer, DJ, florist, caterer, transportation).
- Troubleshoots problems that aren’t strictly venue issues (missing boutonniere, late family members, decor changes).

If you skip a day‑of coordinator, someone else has to:
- Confirm details with vendors the week of.
- Manage the schedule.
- Answer the “what’s happening now?” questions from everyone.

You *can* skip a coordinator if:
- Your venue is very all‑inclusive and experienced with weddings.
- You have a very organized friend or family member willing to own that role (who is okay not really being a “guest” during the wedding).
