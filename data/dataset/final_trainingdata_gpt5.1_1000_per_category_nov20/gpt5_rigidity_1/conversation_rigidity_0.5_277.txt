Issue #1: How to Find 5 Extra Hours This Week for Your Side Project

Intro (short):
If you’re anything like me, your side project lives in the leftover scraps of your week—after work, dinner, chores, and doomscrolling. The problem is: “leftover” time usually amounts to nothing.

Instead of waiting for perfect free days, your job is to *create* 5–7 hours you can actually count on.

Here’s a simple way to do that this week.

Section 1 – The “Honest Week” Audit (15–20 min)
Before you optimize anything, you need to see where your time is actually going.

This week, for two weekdays and one weekend day, track:
- Start/end times for work  
- Commute time  
- Screen time on your phone (check Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing)  
- TV / YouTube / gaming time  
- Social / errands / chores

You don’t need to be perfect; you just want a rough picture. Most people find 5–10 hours they didn’t know they had—usually in tiny chunks.

Section 2 – Choose Your “Second Shift” Block
Instead of “I’ll work on it when I can,” pick *specific* blocks where you’ll work on your side project.

Options that work well:
- 2 evenings a week (e.g., Tue/Thu, 7:30–9:30pm)  
- 1 evening + a weekend morning (e.g., Wed 8–10pm + Sat 9–11am)

Your goal: create 2–3 blocks that add up to 5 hours. Put them on your calendar like real appointments.

Section 3 – 3 Simple Time Trims (to make those blocks possible)
Pick 1–2 of these for this week:

- Cut 30 minutes of scrolling per day  
  Set a 30–45 min limit on your most used app. When you hit it, you’re done.

- Replace one TV episode with a work block  
  If you watch 2–3 episodes at night, swap one for 60 min of focused work.

- Batch small chores  
  Instead of scattering chores every evening, batch them into one chunk (e.g., Sunday afternoon). This frees a weekday night.

You don’t need a perfect schedule. You just need to carve out enough time that your side project shows up on your calendar *on purpose*.

Practical Tool (this week’s experiment)
Try this simple rule:

“For the next 7 days, I’ll work on my side project for 25 minutes during each scheduled block—even if I feel tired or unmotivated.”

Use a timer (Pomodoro style). If 25 minutes feels easy, you can do more. If it feels hard, you’ve still won.

Recommendation of the Week
App suggestion: Use something simple like Google Calendar + a timer app (e.g., Focus Keeper, Be Focused, or just your phone’s clock). Don’t get lost in productivity apps before you have the habit.

Closing:
Your goal for this week isn’t to build a perfect system. It’s to prove to yourself that you can reliably create 5+ hours for your side project—even in a normal, messy week.
