### Human: Policy is from California. Anything special I should know?
### Assistant: With a California personal auto policy, a few typical points:

- Liability coverage:
  - Usually extends to rental cars within the US; that covers damage/injury you cause to others.
  - Verify your limits; LA is a high‑traffic area, so decent liability limits are important.

- Physical damage (collision/comprehensive):
  - Often applies to rentals similarly to your own car, with the same deductibles.
  - You’d likely pay your deductible if you damage the rental, and your insurer handles the rest.

- Loss of use / diminished value:
  - Some insurers cover “loss of use” (rental car company’s lost income while the car is being repaired) and some don’t.
  - Rental companies may try to charge for this; your Visa may help, depending on the card.

Practical approach:
- Before you finalize the booking, call your insurer’s customer service and ask specifically:
  1) “Does my policy cover collision damage for rental cars in California?”  
  2) “Does it cover loss of use charges from the rental company?”  

If they say yes to both, you can more confidently decline Alamo’s extra coverage. If they’re vague or say no on key items, you might rely more on your Visa or consider Alamo’s CDW.
