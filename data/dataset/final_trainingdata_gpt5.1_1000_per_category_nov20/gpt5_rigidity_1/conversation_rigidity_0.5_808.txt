**2) Free vs discounted work: what I’d actually recommend**

You *can* offer free work, but there are trade-offs.

**Working completely free – pros:**
- Easier to get people to say yes.
- Can help you build a few real projects quickly.
- Low pressure for both sides, good for learning.

**Cons:**
- Some clients value your work less when it’s free (less responsive, more flaky).  
- It can be harder to transition them or others to paying normal rates later.  
- You’re setting a precedent for yourself that your time is worth zero.

**Discounted or “portfolio-building rate” – a middle ground I recommend:**
- Say clearly you’re offering a **special, limited rate** while you build your portfolio.
- Examples:
  - “My usual logo + basic brand package will be $X, but I’m taking my first 3 clients at $Y while I build my case studies.”
- This communicates:
  - Your work has value.
  - They’re getting a deal because they’re early.

A reasonable approach:
- **First 1–2 clients:**  
  - If you’re really stuck getting anyone to say yes, you *can* do 1 project free, but still:
    - Write a simple agreement.
    - Make expectations clear.
    - Ask for a testimonial if they’re happy.
- **After that:**  
  - Switch to discounted rates as fast as possible.
