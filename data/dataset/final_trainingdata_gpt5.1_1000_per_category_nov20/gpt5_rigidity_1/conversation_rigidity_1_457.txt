### Human: I also want a separate folder where all singles that aren't part of any album are stored. How do I do that?
### Assistant: I’ll focus only on separating singles (tracks with no album) into a “Singles” folder.

1. In Mp3tag, filter tracks with no album:
   - Press `F3` to open the Filter.
   - Type: `NOT %album% HAS ?`
   - This shows files whose Album tag is empty or missing.

2. Select all filtered tracks.

3. Move them into a Singles folder using “Tag - Filename”:
   - Click “Convert → Tag - Filename”.
   - Use this pattern:
     - `D:\Music\Singles\%artist% - %title%`

4. Confirm the move.

Now you’ll have:
- Full albums under `D:\Music\Artist\Album\`
- Singles under `D:\Music\Singles\`

### Human: Fine, then just tell me how to tag my favorite artists in Mp3tag so I can filter them later.
### Assistant: 1. In Mp3tag, add a custom field for favorites:
   - Select a track.
   - Press `Alt`+`T` to open “Extended Tags”.
   - Click “Add field”.
   - Name: `FAVORITE`
   - Value: `YES`
   - Click OK to save.

2. Apply to all tracks by a favorite artist:
   - Filter by artist: `%artist% IS YourArtistName`
   - Select all results.
   - Press `Alt`+`T`.
   - Set `FAVORITE` to `YES` and save.

3. Later, filter only favorites:
   - Use filter: `%FAVORITE% IS YES`

This stays within Mp3tag and lets you isolate your favorite artists and tracks.
