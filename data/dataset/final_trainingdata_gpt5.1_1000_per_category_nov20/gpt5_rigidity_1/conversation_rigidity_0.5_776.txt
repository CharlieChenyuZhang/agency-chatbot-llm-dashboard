We’ll make **soft scrambled eggs with peas**:

1. **Prep (no heat yet)**
   - Crack 2–3 eggs into a bowl.
   - Add a pinch of salt and pepper.
   - Beat with a fork until it looks smooth and even.

2. **Set up the pan**
   - Put your frying pan on the stove.
   - Turn the heat to **low** (not medium, not high).
   - Add a small piece of butter (about the size of your thumb’s nail).
   - Let it melt slowly; if it sizzles loudly or browns fast, the heat is too high—turn it down and wait.

3. **Add peas**
   - Add a small handful of frozen peas to the pan.
   - Stir for 1–2 minutes until they’re no longer icy.

4. **Cook the eggs**
   - Pour in the beaten eggs.
   - Use a spatula or spoon to **gently** push the eggs from the edges toward the center every few seconds.
   - Keep the heat low; it should cook slowly and quietly, not aggressively bubble.

5. **Finish**
   - When the eggs are mostly set but still a little shiny and soft, turn off the heat.
   - Let them sit in the pan for 30 seconds; they’ll finish cooking with the leftover heat.

**Simple Roasted Chicken Thighs with Potatoes, Carrots, and Onions**

**You’ll need:**
- 2–4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs  
- Potatoes, carrots, onions  
- Oil (like vegetable or olive)  
- Salt and pepper (and any dried herb if you have it: thyme, rosemary, Italian seasoning, etc.)

**Steps:**

1. **Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).**
   - Turn it on first so it heats while you prep.

2. **Prep vegetables (no heat yet)**
   - Wash potatoes and carrots.
   - Cut potatoes into chunks about the size of large dice.
   - Slice carrots into thick coins.
   - Cut onions into wedges.
   - Put all veggies into a bowl or directly on a baking tray.

3. **Season veggies**
   - Drizzle with oil.
   - Add salt and pepper (and herbs if you have them).
   - Toss so everything is lightly coated.

4. **Prep chicken**
   - Pat chicken thighs dry with paper towels if you have them.
   - Rub a little oil on the skin.
   - Sprinkle both sides generously with salt and pepper.

5. **Assemble on tray**
   - Spread veggies in a single layer on the tray.
   - Place chicken thighs **skin-side up** on top of or nestled among the veggies.

6. **Roast**
   - Put tray into the 400°F (200°C) oven.
   - Roast for **about 45–50 minutes**.

7. **Check doneness without a thermometer**
   - Take out the tray carefully.
   - Use a knife to cut into the thickest part of one thigh (near the bone).
   - Look for:
     - No pink, just white/opaque meat.
     - Juices running clear, not pink or red.
   - If it’s still pink, put back in the oven for 5–10 minutes and check again.

This method slightly overcooks rather than undercooks, which is safer for a beginner.
