### Human: Now give me only a simple checklist of key technique cues for conventional deadlifts, no more than 8 bullet points, and don’t include anything about programming or percentages.
### Assistant: - Mid-foot under bar, bar close to shins  
- Grip just outside legs, brace hands hard on bar  
- Set back flat: chest up, lats tight, eyes neutral  
- Take big breath, brace abs 360° before pulling  
- Push floor away with legs; don’t yank with arms  
- Keep bar path straight and close to body  
- Lock out by driving hips through and squeezing glutes, not over-leaning back  
- Control the descent; hinge back down, don’t just drop the bar
