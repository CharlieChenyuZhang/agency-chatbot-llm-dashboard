I’ll assume you have regular white rice (not instant). If it’s something different (brown rice, jasmine, etc.), tell me and I’ll adjust.

**Simple stovetop white rice (about 2 portions):**
1. Measure:
   - 1 cup rice
   - 2 cups water
   - 1 small pinch of salt (optional)

2. Rinse (helps prevent stickiness, but not mandatory):
   - Put rice in a bowl.
   - Cover with cold water, swirl with your hand, pour off the cloudy water.
   - Repeat 2–3 times.

3. Cook:
   - Put rice + 2 cups water + pinch of salt in a small pot.
   - Put on stove, medium-high heat, uncovered.
   - When it **starts boiling** (bubbles all over the surface), give it one quick stir, then:
     - Turn heat down to **low**.
     - Put the **lid on**.

4. Leave it:
   - Don’t lift the lid.
   - Cook on low for **15 minutes** (set a timer).

5. Rest:
   - After 15 min, turn the heat **off**.
   - Leave the lid on for another **5 minutes**.
   - Then open and fluff with a fork.

If you want, tell me what size pot and type of rice you have, and I can tweak times so it’s even more reliable for your setup.

Here’s your **very simple veggie fried rice with egg** (1–2 servings):

**Ingredients**
- 1 cup cooked rice (from what you made)
- 1/2 to 1 cup frozen peas & carrots
- 1–2 eggs
- 1–2 tbsp oil (vegetable, canola, or similar)
- 1–2 tbsp soy sauce (start with 1, taste, add more if needed)
- Optional: pinch of salt, black pepper, garlic powder or minced garlic if you have it

**Steps**
1. Prep:
   - Have your cooked rice ready (cold or room temp works best, but fresh is okay).
   - Beat the eggs in a bowl with a pinch of salt (just mix with a fork).

2. Cook eggs:
   - Heat a pan on **medium**.
   - Add a little oil.
   - Pour in the beaten eggs.
   - Stir gently until just cooked (like soft scrambled eggs).
   - Remove eggs to a plate.

3. Cook veggies:
   - In the same pan, add a bit more oil if it looks dry.
   - Add frozen peas & carrots directly (no need to thaw).
   - Cook for 3–4 minutes, stirring, until hot.

4. Add rice:
   - Add the cooked rice to the pan.
   - Break up any big clumps with your spatula.
   - Stir and cook for 3–5 minutes. Let some bits sit against the pan so they get slightly toasty.

5. Season:
   - Pour in 1 tbsp soy sauce.
   - Stir well, taste.
   - If it’s bland, add a bit more soy sauce, a pinch of salt, or a little garlic powder/black pepper.

6. Add eggs back:
   - Put the scrambled eggs back into the pan.
   - Stir everything together for another 1–2 minutes.

Then you’re done.
