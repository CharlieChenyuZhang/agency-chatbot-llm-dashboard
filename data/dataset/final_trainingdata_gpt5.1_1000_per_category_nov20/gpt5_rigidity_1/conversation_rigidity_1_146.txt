### Human: Good. Now explain the first tip in more detail, but keep it under 150 words and don't talk about the other tips.
### Assistant: The first tip is about showing genuine interest and giving people space to speak. In your 1:1, ask an open question like “How are things going for you this week?” or “What’s on your mind?” and then consciously resist the urge to jump in. Your goal is to listen for at least a full minute without interrupting, fixing, or defending anything. This signals respect and creates psychological safety, because they experience that their perspective matters and won’t be shut down. After they finish, reflect back what you heard in your own words to confirm you understood them correctly, and ask if you missed anything. Doing this consistently trains people to trust that 1:1s are their time, not just yours.
