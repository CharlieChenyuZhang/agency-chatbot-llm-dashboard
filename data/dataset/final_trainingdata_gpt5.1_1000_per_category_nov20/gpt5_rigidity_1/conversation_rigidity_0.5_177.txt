### Assistant: That’s a solid plan.

Here are two simple, practical tools you can start using right away:

1. **The “Good Enough” Response Rule**  
   When you’re talking to someone and start overthinking what to say:
   - Aim for a response that is **70% “good enough,” not perfect**.
   - Give yourself a max of **5 seconds** to choose what to say.
   - After you speak, if your brain starts replaying it, say (internally):  
     “I’m practicing ‘good enough’ today; I don’t need to review this.”

   This combats the perfectionism/overthinking loop by making “imperfect but said” your actual goal.

2. **Anchor on One Sensation During Conversations**  
   When anxiety spikes while you’re talking to someone:
   - Choose one physical sensation to focus on lightly, such as:
     - The feeling of your feet on the floor
     - The sensation of holding your phone
     - Your breath moving in and out (without trying to control it too much)
   - Keep 10–20% of your attention on that sensation while you talk.

   This gives your brain something neutral to hold onto, which can reduce the intensity of anxious thoughts without needing to “argue” with them.
