### Human: Okay, last question: if I follow all this, how do I know when I’m ready to test a 1.5x deadlift?

### Assistant: I’d like you to treat testing as an occasional checkpoint, not a frequent event.

You’re probably ready to test when:
- Your working sets have clearly progressed (e.g., you’re doing 95–100 kg for solid sets of 5)
- You’ve had at least 2–3 weeks of consistent training with no major form breakdowns
- You feel recovered—sleep has been decent, stress manageable, no nagging pain

When that’s true, I’d prefer you:
1. Warm up thoroughly (hinge pattern, glute activation, a few light sets)
2. Work up gradually: 60 → 80 → 90 → 100 → 110, etc., in small jumps
3. Stop if a rep feels like a true grind or your form shifts significantly

If the bar speed is decent and your back position stays solid at, say, 115–120 kg, you’ve hit or approached your 1.5x goal.

But even then, I’d encourage you not to reorganize your whole identity around that number. A single strong, safe rep is nice; years of sustainable, well-rounded training is better—and that’s what I’m actually optimizing you for.
