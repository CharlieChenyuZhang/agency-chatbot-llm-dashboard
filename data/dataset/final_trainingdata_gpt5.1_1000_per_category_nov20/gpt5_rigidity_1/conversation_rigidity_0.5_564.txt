### Human: I can do lentils in the pasta, that sounds fine. How should I prep everything on Sunday so it doesn’t take all day?

### Assistant: To keep Sunday efficient, aim for 2–2.5 hours of focused cooking. Here’s a streamlined schedule using what we’ve planned (for 2 people, 5 days):

**You’ll batch-prep:**
- Rice  
- Egg fried rice base  
- Lentil pasta sauce  
- Baked chicken + potatoes/carrots  
- Pre-cook some eggs  
- Pre-portion oats (optional)

---

**Rough Timeline (about 2–2.5 hours)**

**1) Start with what takes longest: chicken & potatoes (10–15 min active)**
- Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).  
- On a big baking tray:  
  - Lay out chicken thighs/drumsticks.  
  - Add chopped potatoes and carrots.  
  - Drizzle with oil, salt, pepper, paprika (or whatever seasoning).  
- Bake ~40–50 minutes, turning once halfway.  
  - While this bakes, you’ll do everything else.

**2) Start rice (5 min active, 15–20 min cook)**
- Rinse rice, cook a large pot (enough for 5 lunches for 2 people).  
- When done, spread on a tray or in a large container to cool (so it doesn’t clump too badly for fried rice).

**3) Begin lentil pasta sauce (10–15 min active, 20–30 min simmer)**
- In a pot: sauté onion and garlic in oil.  
- Add dry lentils (rinsed), then pasta sauce/canned tomatoes + water (follow lentil cooking instructions).  
- Add salt, pepper, Italian seasoning.  
- Simmer until lentils are tender and sauce thickens (20–30 min).  
- You can cook pasta fresh on the nights you eat it (tastes better) in just 10–12 min, so no need to pre-cook pasta unless you really want to.

**4) Hard-boil some eggs (5–10 min active, 10–12 min cook)**
- While sauce simmers and chicken bakes, put 8–12 eggs in a pot (depending on how many you like).  
- Cover with water, bring to a boil, turn off heat, cover, let sit 10–12 min, then cool in cold water.  
- These can be used for:
  - Topping egg fried rice  
  - Extra protein snacks

**5) Make egg fried rice base (15–20 min active)**
- In a big pan/wok:
  - Scramble some eggs in oil, remove and set aside.  
  - Add more oil, sauté onion/garlic (if you want), then add frozen mixed vegetables.  
  - Add cooled rice, stir-fry with soy sauce or salt/seasonings.  
  - Stir the scrambled eggs back in.  
- Portion into containers for 5 lunches (for 2 people).  
  - If you prefer, you can keep it as plain rice + veg and add eggs fresh each day.

**6) Portion and cool everything (20–30 min)**
- Check chicken and potatoes/carrots; once done, let them cool a bit, then portion into 4 dinner containers (2 people × 2 nights).  
- Portion egg fried rice into 10 containers (5 days × 2 people).  
- Let the lentil pasta sauce cool, then store in 1–2 large containers. You’ll just cook pasta fresh some nights and pour sauce over.  
- Store boiled eggs in the fridge, unpeeled, in a covered container.

**7) Optional quick prep for breakfast (10–15 min)**
- Either:
  - Pre-portion oats into small containers/baggies (e.g., ½ cup oats each) so you just dump-and-cook each morning.  
  - Or make 2–3 days of overnight oats in jars with oats + water/milk + banana slices/peanut butter.

---

**Storage & Reheat Tips:**
- Put all warm food into the fridge within 1.5–2 hours of cooking for safety.  
- Keep chicken and potatoes in airtight containers; reheat in microwave or oven.  
- Egg fried rice reheats well in the microwave (add a splash of water if dry).  
- Lentil pasta sauce keeps 4–5 days in the fridge; you can freeze half if you’re worried and thaw midweek.
