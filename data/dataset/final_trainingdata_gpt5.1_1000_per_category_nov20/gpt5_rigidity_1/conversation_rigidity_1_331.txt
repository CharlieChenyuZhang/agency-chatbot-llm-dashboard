### Assistant: I’ll keep this strictly to 5 specific, budget‑conscious décor ideas that still feel elegant:

1) Focused floral strategy
- Use real flowers only for high‑impact areas (bouquets, ceremony arch, a few key centerpieces).
- Supplement with greenery, candles, and minimal arrangements on other tables.

2) Repurpose ceremony décor
- Ceremony arch or arrangements are moved to:
  - Behind the sweetheart table

3) Simple, cohesive color palette
- Choose 2–3 main colors and keep linens, napkins, and candles within that palette.
- Use standard venue linens if they’re neutral and upgrade only key pieces (for example, specialty napkins or runners).

4) Candle‑heavy centerpieces
- Combine different heights of glass cylinders or votives with a bit of greenery.
- Fewer flowers, but the candlelight creates an elegant atmosphere.

5) Minimal paper décor
- Keep printed items simple: one sign for welcome, one for bar, one for seating chart.
- Reuse frames or easels and avoid printing multiple small signs for every detail.
