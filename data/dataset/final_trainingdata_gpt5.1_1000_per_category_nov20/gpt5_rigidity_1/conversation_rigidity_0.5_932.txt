On email deliverability: I can’t log into your DNS provider or email service to actually configure SPF/DKIM for you, but I can give you a simple checklist and the exact records you’ll likely need to add. You (or whoever manages your domain) would then copy-paste them into your DNS settings.

Here’s a practical way to handle it:

1. **Use a separate sending domain or subdomain**  
   - Main website: `reportnest.com`  
   - Outreach email: `you@reportnest.com` or `you@hey.reportnest.com`  
   Using a subdomain like `hey.reportnest.com` is optional but can help isolate reputation.

2. **SPF record (for your sending provider)**  
   - If you’re using something like Google Workspace, your SPF record in DNS should look like:  
     `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all`  
   - If you also use another sending tool (e.g., MailerSend, SendGrid), it will tell you what to add (e.g., `include:sendgrid.net`).  
   - You generally want just **one SPF record** per domain, combining providers:  
     `v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com include:sendgrid.net ~all`

3. **DKIM record**  
   - In Google Workspace or your email provider:
     - Go to their DKIM settings.
     - Generate a new DKIM key.
     - They’ll give you a DNS TXT record like:  
       - Name/Host: `google._domainkey`  
       - Value: a long key string starting with `v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=...`  
   - Add that as a TXT record in your DNS.
   - Then go back to your email provider and “turn on” DKIM.

4. **DMARC (optional but recommended)**  
   - Start with a relaxed DMARC policy to monitor only:  
     - Name/Host: `_dmarc`  
     - Type: TXT  
     - Value:  
       `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@reportnest.com; fo=1`  
   - Later, you can tighten `p=none` to `p=quarantine` or `p=reject` once you’re sure everything is configured correctly.

5. **Warm up your inbox**  
   Instead of blasting 200 emails on day one:
   - Day 1–3: 15–20 emails/day  
   - Day 4–7: 25–40 emails/day  
   - Spread them across time zones and hours
   - Personalize each one (subject + 1–2 lines) so you don’t look like a spammer

If you tell me which email provider you’re planning to use (e.g., Google Workspace, Zoho, another SMTP provider), I can give you more precise example records for that specific provider.
