### Human: Yes, make it a daily checklist I can follow. Keep it short enough that I can screenshot it.
### Assistant: Here’s a compact daily checklist suitable for a screenshot:

1) **Pick 1 problem from today**  
   - Write 1 sentence: “The problem is: ________.”

2) **Clarify**  
   - Why is this a problem? (1–2 bullet points)  
   - What would “success” look like? (1 bullet)

3) **Generate options**  
   - List at least 3 possible actions (even imperfect ones).

4) **Evaluate options**  
   - Choose 2 criteria (e.g., impact, risk, effort, speed).  
   - For each option, quickly note: good / okay / bad for each criterion.

5) **Decide & act**  
   - Pick 1 option.  
   - Write: “I choose ___ because ___.”  
   - Take one concrete step today (even small).

6) **Quick reflection (end of day, 2 minutes)**  
   - Did I follow my decision?  
   - What did I learn for next time?
