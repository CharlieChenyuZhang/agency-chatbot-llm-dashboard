### Human: Let's start with scrambled eggs. Give me clear step-by-step instructions.
### Assistant: Here are step-by-step instructions for 2 servings of scrambled eggs:
1) Crack 4 eggs into a bowl.
2) Add a pinch of salt and a small splash of milk or water (about 1–2 tablespoons total).
3) Whisk with a fork until the mixture looks uniform and slightly frothy.
4) Put a nonstick pan on medium-low heat and add 1 tablespoon of butter or oil.
5) When the butter is melted and gently bubbling (not browning fast), pour in the eggs.
6) Let them sit 5–10 seconds, then gently push the eggs from the edges toward the center with a spatula, forming soft curds.
7) Keep slowly pushing and folding every few seconds. If they’re cooking too fast or browning, lower the heat.
8) When they look mostly set but still slightly glossy/soft, turn off the heat.
9) Optional: add a small knob of butter or a spoon of cream/cheese and gently fold.
10) Taste and add a little more salt and pepper if needed, then serve immediately.
If you want, I can explain how to tell if your pan is at the right temperature.

### Human: Yes, explain how to tell if the pan is at the right temperature, but keep your answer under 4 sentences.
### Assistant: For scrambled eggs, the pan is ready when the butter is fully melted, gently foaming, and smells nutty but isn’t browning quickly or smoking. If you drop in a tiny bit of egg, it should very softly sizzle, not loudly. If there’s no sizzle at all, the pan is too cool; if the egg immediately browns, it’s too hot. Adjust the heat and wait a few seconds before adding all the eggs.

### Human: Okay, now teach me the garlic butter pasta next. Include a shopping list and numbered steps only.
### Assistant: Shopping list (for about 2 servings):
1) Spaghetti or any pasta – 200 g (about 7 oz)
2) Garlic – 3–4 cloves
3) Butter – 3 tablespoons
4) Olive oil – 1 tablespoon (optional but helpful)
5) Salt
6) Black pepper
7) Grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
8) Fresh parsley (optional)

Numbered steps:
1) Bring a large pot of water to a boil; add 1–2 tablespoons of salt (the water should taste pleasantly salty).
2) Add pasta and cook according to package directions until al dente.
3) While pasta cooks, peel and finely mince the garlic.
4) When pasta is a few minutes from done, place a pan on low heat and add butter (and olive oil if using).
5) Once butter is melted and just starting to foam, add garlic and cook gently, stirring, for 1–2 minutes until fragrant and just lightly golden (not brown).
6) Before draining the pasta, scoop out about 1/2 cup of the starchy pasta water and set aside.
7) Drain pasta and immediately add it to the pan with garlic butter.
8) Toss the pasta in the sauce, adding a splash of pasta water as needed to loosen and create a light, glossy coating.
9) Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
10) Turn off heat, then add grated Parmesan and chopped parsley if using; toss again and serve.

### Human: Now give me a simple roasted vegetables recipe with only ingredients, oven temperature, time, and steps. No extra tips.
### Assistant: Ingredients (about 2–3 servings):
- 2–3 cups mixed vegetables (e.g., carrots, broccoli, bell peppers, potatoes)
- 2–3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Oven temperature:
- 425°F (220°C)

Time:
- 20–35 minutes, depending on vegetable size, tossing halfway

Steps:
1) Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
2) Cut vegetables into similar bite-sized pieces.
3) Place them on a baking sheet, add olive oil, salt, and pepper, and toss to coat evenly.
4) Spread in a single layer with a little space between pieces.
5) Roast for 10–15 minutes, remove and toss, then roast another 10–20 minutes until tender and lightly browned.
