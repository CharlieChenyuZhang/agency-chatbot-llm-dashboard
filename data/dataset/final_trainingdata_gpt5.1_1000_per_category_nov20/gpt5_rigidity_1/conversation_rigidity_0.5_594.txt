### Assistant: Great. Here’s a **layered 30-minute morning meditation** for anxiety.

**Setup (before timer):**
- Sit on a chair or cushion, back straight but not rigid, feet flat (if on a chair).
- Hands resting on thighs or in your lap.
- Eyes gently closed or half-open, gaze soft.

#### Minutes 0–10: Core (non-negotiable)
1. **Minute 0–2: Arriving**
   - Notice points of contact: feet on floor, seat on chair, hands on thighs.
   - Take 3–5 slightly deeper breaths:
     - Inhale naturally.
     - Exhale a bit longer than the inhale (e.g., in for 4, out for 6).

2. **Minute 2–9: Breath-focused meditation**
   - Choose one anchor: sensation of breath at nostrils or rise/fall of your belly.
   - Gently rest attention there.
   - When you notice the mind has wandered (planning, worrying, replaying conversations):
     - Silently label it: “thinking,” “planning,” or “worrying.”
     - Gently return to the breath.
   - Treat each return like a *rep at the gym*. The wandering isn’t failure; the return is the training.

3. **Minute 9–10: Brief check-in**
   - Quickly scan: How does my body feel? My mind? My mood?
   - No need to fix anything, just notice.

If you’re short on time or not feeling great, stop here and you’ve *still* done your practice for the day.

#### Minutes 10–20: Expansion (recommended)
4. **Minute 10–15: Body scan**
   - Move attention slowly through the body:
     - Top of head → face → neck → shoulders → arms → hands.
     - Chest → back → belly.
     - Hips → thighs → knees → calves → feet.
   - At each area, notice sensations: warmth, tension, tingling, numbness, neutral.
   - If you find tension, imagine breathing into that spot and softening *just 5–10%* on the exhale.

5. **Minute 15–20: “Noting” practice for anxiety**
   - Keep a light awareness of the breath.
   - When thoughts or feelings arise, lightly label them:
     - “worry,” “planning,” “fear,” “remembering,” “judging,” etc.
   - After labeling, return to the breath or body sensations.
   - This helps create a tiny bit of distance from anxious thoughts.

#### Minutes 20–30: Deeper/optional (on days you feel up for it)
6. **Minute 20–27: Simple compassion practice (good for anxiety)**
   - Bring to mind *yourself* as you are this morning.
   - Silently repeat a few phrases, gently, in your own words:
     - “May I be safe.”
     - “May I be at ease.”
     - “May I be kind to myself today.”
   - If resistance or self-criticism shows up, just notice it and return to the phrases.

7. **Minute 27–30: Transition to your day**
   - Feel your body sitting here.
   - Take 3 normal breaths.
   - Set a tiny intention for the day: “Today I’ll pause and take one conscious breath whenever I feel overwhelmed.”
   - Open your eyes, look around, and reorient to the room.

If 30 minutes feels like too much on some days, do *only* the first 10, or first 15. That’s not failure; that’s smart adaptation.
