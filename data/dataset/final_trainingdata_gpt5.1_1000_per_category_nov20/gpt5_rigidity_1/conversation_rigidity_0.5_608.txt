### Human: Yeah, give me a simple progression plan. I don't really track much so keep it easy.

### Assistant: Simple, low‑effort progression:

**For strength exercises (presses, rows, RDLs, leg press, etc.):**
- Week 1: Pick a weight where you *could* do 1–2 reps more than the target (e.g., target 10 reps, you could do 11–12).
- Week 2:  
  - If you hit all sets and reps with decent form, increase weight by the smallest jump (e.g., +2.5–5 lb per side or next dumbbell up).
- Week 3+:  
  - Keep the pattern: when you can get all sets at the top of the rep range with good form 2 workouts in a row, increase weight next time.
- If you *can’t* hit the lower end of the rep range (e.g., 6–8 and you only get 4–5), drop the weight slightly next session.

**For cardio:**
- Weeks 1–2: Aim for 10–20 min sessions as listed, at a pace where you can talk in short sentences.
- Weeks 3–4: Add ~5 min to one or two cardio sessions *or* slightly increase incline/resistance.
- Keep intensity mostly moderate; for fat loss, consistency > killing yourself.

You don’t have to track obsessively, but at minimum:
- Write in your phone: exercise + weight + reps (just the main lifts)  
- Take a quick photo of the notes each week so you can compare.
