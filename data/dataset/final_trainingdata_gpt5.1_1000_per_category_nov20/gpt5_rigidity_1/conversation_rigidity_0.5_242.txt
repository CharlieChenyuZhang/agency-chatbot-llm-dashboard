### Assistant: Keeping the casual, meme-y culture is compatible with being clearer and more direct—you just need to separate *tone* from *content*:

**Direct + Caring > Vague + Nice.**

Some practical tricks:

1. **Use this 3-part feedback structure**

   - Impact: “Here’s why it matters…”

   Start with:  
   “I’m sharing this because I want you to be successful and our team to run more smoothly.”  
   It’s harder to feel like a jerk when you’re being transparent about your motive.

3. **Ask for their perspective**
   End with:  
   “How does this land with you?” or “What’s your take on this?”  
   That keeps it two-way instead of a lecture.
