- 70 g dry brown rice – ~245 kcal  

Salad  
- 50 g mixed salad greens – ~10 kcal  
- 40 g tomato, chopped – ~7 kcal  
- 40 g cucumber, sliced – ~6 kcal  
- 5 g olive oil (for dressing) – ~45 kcal  
- 5 g vinegar (balsamic or other) – ~2 kcal  
- Salt, pepper, dried herbs – ~3 kcal  

Instructions  
Brown Rice  
1) Rinse 70 g brown rice under cold water.  
2) Add to a pot with about 175–210 ml water (follow package directions).  
3) Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer ~30–40 minutes until tender and water absorbed. Fluff with a fork.

Salmon  
4) Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F).  
5) Place 130 g salmon on a baking sheet lined with parchment.  
6) Brush with 5 g olive oil and season with salt, pepper, and herbs.  
7) Bake for about 12–15 minutes, or until salmon flakes easily with a fork (internal temp ~63°C / 145°F).  
8) Squeeze lemon juice over before serving if desired.

Salad  
9) Place 50 g mixed greens in a bowl.  
10) Add 40 g chopped tomato and 40 g sliced cucumber.  
11) In a small cup, whisk 5 g olive oil, 5 g vinegar, salt, pepper, and herbs; drizzle over salad and toss.

- Salmon: 260 kcal
