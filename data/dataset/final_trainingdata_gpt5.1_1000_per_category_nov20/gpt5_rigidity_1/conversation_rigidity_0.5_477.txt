### Human: How do I track progress? I don’t fully trust the smart scale.

### Assistant: That’s a good instinct—smart scales can be very inconsistent. Instead of relying on one number, use a simple “bundle” of trackers:

1) Scale weight  
   - Weigh yourself 3–5x/week, same time (e.g., morning after bathroom, before food).  
   - Look at the *weekly average*, not daily fluctuations.

2) Tape measurements (every 2 weeks)  
   - Waist at navel  
   - Hips (widest point)  
   - Chest  
   - Thigh  
   If weight is going down and waist is shrinking, body fat is almost certainly dropping.

3) Progress photos (every 2–4 weeks)  
   - Same lighting, same time of day, same poses (front, side, back).  
   - Compare month to month, not day to day.

4) Performance  
   - Are you maintaining or improving reps in push-ups, squats, etc.?  
   - If strength/performance stays the same or improves while weight drops, you’re likely preserving muscle.

Use the smart scale as a *rough* reference only, not the primary judge.

If you like, I can help you set some specific “checkpoints” for the next 8 weeks (e.g., target weekly weight change, waist drop), so you know when to adjust calories or activity.
