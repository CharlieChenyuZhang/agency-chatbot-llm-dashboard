### Assistant: Instead of chasing some mystical “in-the-moment EQ,” I’d rather help you build a very practical early-warning system for your emotions.

Here’s the method I’d push you to use for the next 2 weeks:

   - Shutdown: “What am I avoiding? What’s a 2-minute version of progress?”
You practice noticing the *body signal* and then running the script. That’s the kind of emotional awareness I prefer: concrete, repeatable, and tied to action.

3) Shutdown
   Signals: numbness, scrolling, not wanting to think, heavy body.
   Script:

   - Step 2: “If I did 2 minutes of progress on it, what would that look like?”
