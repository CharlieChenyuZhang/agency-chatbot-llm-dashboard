**3. Adjust the Budgeted formula to scale flexible categories**

Replace your old formula in `ThisMonth!B2` with:

```excel
=LET(
  cat, A2,
  base, IFERROR(VLOOKUP(cat, Categories!A:E, 4, FALSE), 0),
  flex, IFERROR(VLOOKUP(cat, Categories!A:F, 6, FALSE), FALSE),
  plannedIncome, $G$1,
  actualIncome, $G$2,
  factor, IF(plannedIncome=0, 1, actualIncome/plannedIncome),

)
```

Then drag this down column B.

How this works:

- If your actual income is lower (say $3,500 instead of $4,000), then `factor = 3500 / 4000 = 0.875`.  
- All categories marked `Flexible_Scale = TRUE` will have their budget scaled down by 12.5%.  
- Needs stay at their full base amounts so they’re protected.

This isn’t perfect in every scenario, but it saves you from manually reducing each discretionary category when income is lower.
