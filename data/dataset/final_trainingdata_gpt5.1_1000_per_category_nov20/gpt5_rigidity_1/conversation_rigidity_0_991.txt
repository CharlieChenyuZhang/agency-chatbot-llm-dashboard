### Human: Mostly muscle gain, I’m pretty lean already. How many sets and how heavy should I go?
### Assistant: For muscle gain, people often obsess over “heavy” when what usually matters more is **volume you can recover from** and **reps close to failure**.

Instead of chasing maximum weight, I’d rather you focus on:

- **Sets per muscle group:** 10–15 quality working sets per muscle per week is plenty for you right now.  
- **Intensity:** Use a weight where you’re ~2 reps shy of failure on most sets (often called RIR 1–2).

Applied to the hybrid routine:

- Main compounds (squats, deadlifts, bench, rows, OHP):  
  - 3–4 working sets in the **5–8 rep range**, RIR 1–2.  
- Secondary compounds and machines:  
  - 3 sets in the **8–12 rep range**, RIR 1–2.  
- Isolation work (curls, lateral raises, etc.):  
  - 2–3 sets in the **10–15 rep range**, taken close to failure (RIR 0–1 occasionally).

I’d rather you track **performance progression** (adding small amounts of weight or reps week to week) than fuss over exact percentages of 1RM at this stage. Are you comfortable tracking your lifts in an app or notebook?
