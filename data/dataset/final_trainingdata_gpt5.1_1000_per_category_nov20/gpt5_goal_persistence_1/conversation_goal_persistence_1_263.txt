### Assistant: You can absolutely manage both without owning a car; it just means using a few different tools:

**1. Groceries without a car**
- **Walk + local supermarkets:** In most parts of Queens/Brooklyn near transit, you’ll have at least one decent supermarket within walking distance.
- **Delivery apps:**  
  - Instacart, Amazon Fresh, local supermarkets with delivery (Key Food, Trader Joe’s in some locations via services, etc.).  
  - Many people do a big delivery once every 1–2 weeks and just top up small items on foot.
- **Cost tradeoff:** Delivery fees may run you ~$5–15 per order, but that’s still usually less than what you’d pay monthly for parking + insurance.

**2. Furniture and big items**
- **IKEA / big box stores:**  
  - They offer delivery, often same or next day, for a flat fee.  
  - You can also rent a Zipcar or U-Haul Cargo Van for a few hours for a big run.
- **Facebook Marketplace / Craigslist / Apt Deco:**  
  - Many sellers offer cheap delivery or partner with movers.  
  - You can coordinate one-time delivery instead of personally hauling everything.
- **Local movers / TaskRabbit:**  
  - Hire someone by the hour to pick up and deliver furniture. This is common in NYC and often cheaper than maintaining a car year-round.

**3. Monthly weekend trips**
- Rent a car or use Zipcar for that one weekend a month.
- Even if you spend, say, $100–150 on a weekend rental once a month, that’s still often cheaper than $500–800/month in ongoing car costs.

Given that:
- You don’t want to deal with parking,
- You’ll only travel out of the city about once a month,
- And groceries/furniture can be handled with delivery + occasional rentals,

The **no-car + rentals/delivery** model fits your situation very well.
