### Human: Okay, walk me through the setup so I don’t mess up my posture.
### Assistant: Here’s a straightforward setup guide tailored to your height (5'9") and average build. You can fine‑tune from here, but this will get you very close:

1. **Desk and chair height**
   - Sit in the chair in front of your desk.
   - Raise or lower the **chair** so that:
     - Your feet are flat on the floor.
     - Your knees are roughly at a **90–100° angle** (slightly open, not tight).
   - Then check your **elbows**:
     - With shoulders relaxed, elbows bent at ~90°, your forearms should be:
       - Parallel to the floor
       - Level with or slightly above the keyboard surface  
     - If not, adjust either the **chair height** or **desk height** (if your desk is adjustable).

2. **Seat depth**
   - Slide your hips all the way back in the seat.
   - Adjust the seat depth so that:
     - There’s about **2–3 fingers of space** between the back of your knees and the front edge of the seat.
   - This prevents cutting off circulation behind your knees.

3. **Lumbar support**
   - Adjust lumbar height so the **curve of the lumbar** hits the small of your back (where your spine naturally curves inward).
   - You should feel:
     - Gentle support, not a hard push.
   - If your lower back feels like it’s being shoved forward, reduce the lumbar intensity.

4. **Backrest recline and tension**
   - Set the **recline tension** so:
     - You can lean back with a bit of effort, but it doesn’t flop backward.
   - For focused work:
     - Use a **slight recline** (100–110°) rather than sitting perfectly upright.
   - This reduces pressure on your lower back and spine compared to 90° vertical.

5. **Armrests**
   - Height:
     - Raise/lower so your elbows are at ~90° with shoulders relaxed (not hunched).
   - Width/angle:
     - Adjust so your arms rest naturally by your sides and your forearms are supported straight toward the keyboard.
   - You want your shoulders to feel **loose and relaxed**—if they’re creeping up, lower the armrests.

6. **Headrest (if you use it)**
   - Set the headrest so it supports the **mid‑back of your head** or upper neck when you lean back slightly.
   - It should **not** push your head forward when you’re typing; it’s mainly for when you recline.

7. **Monitor position**
   - Top of the screen should be at or slightly below **eye level**.
   - The screen should be about **an arm’s length** away.
   - You shouldn’t have to tilt your head up or down significantly.

8. **Quick daily check (30 seconds)**
   - Are your feet flat?
   - Are your shoulders relaxed?
   - Are your elbows around 90°?
   - Are you slightly reclined rather than bolt upright?
