   - **From CMU:** Walk ~10–15 minutes west along Forbes Ave to the museum complex.

2. **The Frick Art Museum (The Frick Pittsburgh)** – Point Breeze  
   - **Address:** 7227 Reynolds St, Pittsburgh, PA 15208  
   - **From CMOA:**  
     - Walk or take a bus east along Fifth Ave / Penn Ave toward Point Breeze, then walk to The Frick campus.  
   - **Time on site:** ~1–2 hours  
   - **Notes:** Includes historic buildings and gardens; nice contrast to CMOA’s larger collection.

3. **Mattress Factory** – North Side  
   - **Address:** 500 Sampsonia Way, Pittsburgh, PA 15212  
   - **From The Frick:**  
     - Take a bus back toward Downtown (e.g., via Oakland), then transfer to a bus to the North Side.  
     - Get off near Monterey St / Jacksonia St and walk to Sampsonia Way.  
   - **Time on site:** ~1.5–2 hours  
   - **Notes:** Focuses on contemporary installation art; very different feel from CMOA and The Frick.

4. **The Andy Warhol Museum** – North Shore  
   - **Address:** 117 Sandusky St, Pittsburgh, PA 15212  
   - **From Mattress Factory:**  
     - Walk ~15–20 minutes southeast through the North Side toward the river.  
     - The museum is near the Andy Warhol Bridge and the stadiums.  
   - **Time on site:** ~1.5–2 hours  
   - **Notes:** Largest museum in North America dedicated to a single artist; strong Pittsburgh tie-in via Warhol.

- **Morning:**

  - Late morning / early lunch near Oakland.

- **Early Afternoon:**  
  - Head to **The Frick Art Museum** in Point Breeze.  
  - Enjoy the grounds and quieter setting.

- **Late Afternoon:**  
  - Transit to the **Mattress Factory** on the North Side.  
  - Continue on foot to **The Andy Warhol Museum**.

- **Evening:**  
  - From the Warhol, walk across the bridge into Downtown for dinner or head back via bus/ride-share.
