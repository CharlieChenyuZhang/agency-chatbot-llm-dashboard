### Human: I’ll try it this week. Can you give me something for dealing with nerves right before an actual presentation too?

### Assistant: Yes—here’s a short pre-presentation routine you can use in 3–5 minutes:

**1. Ground your body (60–90 seconds)**
- Plant your feet flat on the floor.
- Inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6 seconds.
- Do 5–6 breaths, shoulders relaxed.

**2. “First sentence” drill (60 seconds)**
- Say your **first sentence** in your head 3–5 times:
  - “Today I’m going to walk you through our Q4 results and what they mean for our team.”
- Optional: whisper it once if you’re in a private spot.

**3. Focus shift (30–60 seconds)**
- Ask: “What is the **one thing** I want them to walk away with?”
- Mentally finish this: “If they only remember one thing, I want it to be ______.”

**4. Reframe nerves (30 seconds)**
- When you feel your heart racing, quietly label it: “My body is getting ready to perform.”
- You don’t have to feel calm; you just have to feel *organized*.

You can write this on an index card or in your phone and glance at it before you present.
