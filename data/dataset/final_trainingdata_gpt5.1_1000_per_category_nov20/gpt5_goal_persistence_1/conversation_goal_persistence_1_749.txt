Day B (Cardio Focus + Core)

- Easy marching in place, arm swings

2) Cardio intervals (12–15 min)
- 1 minute brisk marching in place
- 1 minute slower recovery
Repeat 6–7 times.
If marching feels easy later, you can add light side-steps, small knee lifts, or shadowboxing.

3) Core (5–8 min)
- Dead bug (on your back, slow arm/leg extensions): 8–10 per side
- Bird-dog (on hands and knees, extend opposite arm/leg): 8–10 per side
- Side plank from knees (or just hold on your side propped on elbow): 15–20 seconds per side

4) Cool-down (5 min)
- Stretch hips, hamstrings, lower back, and neck

- Wednesday: Day B
- Friday: Day A
Next week, swap (B/A/B), just to mix things up.

Progression (so you keep improving):
- Week 1–2: 2 rounds of the strength circuit, easy pace on cardio
- Week 3–4: 3 rounds of strength circuit, slightly faster marching
- Week 5+: begin shortening rest a bit or adding a few reps
