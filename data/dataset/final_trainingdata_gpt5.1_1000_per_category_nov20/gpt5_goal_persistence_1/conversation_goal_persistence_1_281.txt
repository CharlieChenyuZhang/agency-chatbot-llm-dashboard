**Wind protection ideas (simple and cheap):**

1. **Placement**
   - Put pots on the **floor**, as close as possible to the wall/door, where wind is weakest.

3. **Optional shield**
   - If wind is really intense, you can create a small windbreak:
     - A cheap **wire shelf**, small folding screen, or even a sturdy plastic storage bin turned sideways as a partial barrier.
     - Just don’t block all the light.
