### Human: Follow-up reminders please. Ideally I’d like something that tells me who I should reach out to this week.

### Assistant: You can approximate this with two extra columns and a filter:

We already have:
- `Last Contacted` (date)
- `Next Follow-up` (date)

Add:

1. `Follow-up Interval (days)`
2. `Due This Week?` (TRUE/FALSE)

**1. Set a follow-up interval**

In `Follow-up Interval (days)`, you might use:

- `30` for close friends / key professional contacts.
- `90` for looser connections.

**2. Auto-calculate Next Follow-up**

Instead of editing `Next Follow-up` manually, you can compute it:

In `Next Follow-up` (say column I), row 2:

```gs
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(F2), ISBLANK(H2)), "", F2 + H2)
```

Assuming:
- `F` = `Last Contacted`
- `H` = `Follow-up Interval (days)`

Adjust column letters to your actual layout.

**3. Mark who is due this week**

In `Due This Week?` (say column J), row 2:

```gs
=IF(OR(ISBLANK(I2), I2 > TODAY()+7, I2 < TODAY()-30), FALSE, TRUE)
```

Explanation:
- Shows `TRUE` if `Next Follow-up` is within the next 7 days.
- Excludes very overdue contacts (more than 30 days past due), but you can tweak that.

If you’d rather not have that 30-day limit, use:

```gs
=IF(AND(NOT(ISBLANK(I2)), I2 <= TODAY()+7), TRUE, FALSE)
```

**4. Create a filter view “This week’s reach-outs”**

1. Turn on the filter (Data → Create a filter).
2. In the `Due This Week?` column, filter to `TRUE`.

Now you’ll see just the people you want to reach out to this week.
