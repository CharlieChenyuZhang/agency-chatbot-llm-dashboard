### Human: Okay, let’s go with solo service providers. Now, help me design a simple service offer for them.
### Assistant: A basic offer might be:

“I’ll audit your current workflow and set up 2–3 AI-based processes to save you at least 3 hours a week.”

You’d theoretically:
1. Have a 30–60 minute call.
2. Ask about their tasks, tools, and bottlenecks.
3. Suggest and set up AI tools or automations (like email drafting, content outlines, basic automations).

But honestly, that means:
- You need to know a bunch of tools well.
- You must customize solutions.
- You might have to do follow-up support.

That’s a lot of responsibility and can get messy fast if something breaks or they don’t like the results. It may be simpler to just write general “how-to” guides and not actually promise any hands-on service.
