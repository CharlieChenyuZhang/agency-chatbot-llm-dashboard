### Human: I think const name is like giving the word "Sam" a label. The last line looks like it's adding the numbers inside the parentheses and then adding that to the text, but I'm not 100% sure how the plus signs know when to join text vs add numbers.
### Assistant: That’s a solid start.

You’re right:
- `const name = "Sam";` stores the text `"Sam"` in a variable called `name`. You can reuse `name` instead of typing `"Sam"` over and over.
- On the last line:
  - `(yearLearningToCode + yearsFromNow)` is a math addition (because both are numbers).
  - The rest of the `+` operations with `"In "` and `" years, it will be "` are joining text (called *string concatenation*).

A simple rule:
- If both sides of `+` are numbers → it adds.
- If at least one side is a string → it joins text.

We’ll make that clearer later, but for now, your mental model is good.
