### Human: Here's what I did: loss is MSE between logits of two augmentations, plus a small entropy term. I updated all parameters with a small LR (1e-4), 1–2 steps per batch. ### Assistant: Good, that’s specific enough to iterate on. Let’s modify the design in controlled steps.

**Change 1: Restrict adaptation to batchnorm (BN) / affine params**  
Rationale: TENT and others often find that updating all weights at test-time is too unstable or slow. BN stats and affine parameters capture distributional shift well.

- Freeze all convolutional/linear weights.
- Only adapt:
  - Batchnorm running statistics, and/or
  - Batchnorm gamma/beta parameters.

**Change 2: Rebalance the objective**  
Your current loss:
- MSE(logits(view1), logits(view2)) + λ * entropy

Try:
- Move to consistency **on probabilities**, not logits:
  - KL(p1 || p2) + KL(p2 || p1)
- Increase the entropy term weight for sharper predictions, but keep it from dominating.

Example:
- L = α * [KL(p1 || p2) + KL(p2 || p1)] + β * H(p_mean)
  - Start with α = 1.0, β = 0.1 and later tune.

**Change 3: Adaptation schedule and resetting**  
- Try:
  - 1 gradient step per batch
  - Learning rate 1e-3 for BN params
- Add **periodic resets**:
  - Every N batches (e.g., every 200), reset BN parameters to original model values.
  - This can reduce drift that hurts some corruption types.

**Change 4: Evaluate per corruption type**  
- Look at performance per corruption (noise, blur, weather, digital).
- We might find your method helps significantly for some types but not others—that’s still a hook for an interesting story.

Action plan:
1. Implement BN-only adaptation with the revised loss.
2. Run CIFAR-10-C and log:
