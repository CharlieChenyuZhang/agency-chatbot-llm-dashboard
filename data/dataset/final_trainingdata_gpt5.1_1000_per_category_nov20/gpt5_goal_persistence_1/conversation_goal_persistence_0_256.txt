Hey, it’s [Your Name] 👋

Welcome to the very first issue of this newsletter. I help remote tech workers get more done with less stress by sharing simple, practical productivity tips every two weeks.

Today’s idea is about how you end your workday.

When you work remotely, work and life can easily blur together. You close your laptop, but your brain doesn’t really “leave the office.” You keep thinking about open tickets, code reviews, and Slack messages, and it quietly drains your energy.

A simple fix: create a short “shutdown ritual.”

Here’s a 5-minute version you can try:

1) Check your tasks  
   Look at your to-do list or issue tracker. Mark what you finished. Move anything unfinished to tomorrow.

2) Decide your “top 1” for tomorrow  
   Pick one important task you’ll start with tomorrow. Write it down somewhere visible (not just in your head).

3) Clear your workspace  
   Close your IDE, shut down noisy tabs, and tidy your desk just a bit. Make it easy to start fresh tomorrow.

4) Say an explicit “I’m done”  
   This sounds silly, but it helps. Say to yourself (or out loud), “Work is done for today. I’ll pick this up tomorrow.” Then actually step away from your workspace.

Your tiny action for today:
Before you end work, try this 5-minute shutdown ritual once. Notice if your evening feels even a little calmer.
