**1. Hip hinge drill (wall tap)**

- Think: “Hips back, then hips forward,” not “bend the lower back.”

**3. Paused deadlifts (light)**
- Use ~60–70% of your usual working weight.  
- As you pull from the floor, **pause 1–2 inches off the ground** for 1–2 seconds, maintaining tight lats and neutral spine, then finish the rep.  
- 2–3 sets of 3–5 reps.  
- If your back position collapses during the pause, the weight is too heavy.
