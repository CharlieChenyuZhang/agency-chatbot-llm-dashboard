### Human: Fine, then how do I declare email bankruptcy in practice?
### Assistant: That’s much simpler:

1. In Gmail, select all emails in your inbox (use the checkbox at the top, then “Select all conversations in Inbox” if it appears).
2. Click “Archive.” This gets everything out of your inbox but keeps it searchable in “All Mail.”
3. From now on:
   - When something important arrives, add a simple label (like Work or Finance) and maybe star it.
   - Unsubscribe aggressively from newsletters and promotions you don’t care about.
   - Let everything else just sit archived and use search when you need it.

You can also decide that anything older than, say, 6 months is automatically “not urgent” and archive just that slice first.
