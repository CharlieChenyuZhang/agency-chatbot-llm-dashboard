### Assistant: We can definitely shift away from role-playing if that’s too much right now. I’ll give you 10 practical rules you can keep and refer to.

**10 simple rules to be a better communicator (in most situations)**

1. **State your intent upfront.**  
   Say why you’re speaking: “I want to understand…”, “I’d like to be honest about…”.

2. **Use “I” more than “you” when things are tense.**  
   “I’m concerned about the deadline” instead of “You set an impossible deadline.”

3. **Be concrete, not vague.**  
   Mention specific examples, times, behaviors, not “always/never”.

4. **Say less, more clearly.**  
   Short sentences, one main point at a time.

5. **Check you’ve been understood.**  
   “Does that make sense?” or “What are you hearing from what I said?”

6. **Check that you understand them.**  
   “So if I’m hearing you right, you’re saying… Is that correct?”

7. **Name your emotion at a low intensity.**  
   “I’m a bit frustrated / worried / confused” instead of exploding or shutting down.

8. **Pause before reacting.**  
   One breath, count to three, or say “Let me think about that for a second.”

9. **Ask one good question before you argue.**  
   “Can you help me understand why you see it that way?” before you disagree.

10. **Close with a next step.**  
    “So the next step is…”, “What would be helpful for us to do next?”
