### Assistant: Let’s prioritize concepts that are implementable but can still sound principled.

Idea 1: Representation-Space Coverage Selection
- Train or use a multilingual encoder (like XLM-R).
- Encode your small Arabic QA set and a large pool of English QA examples.
- Define a selection objective: choose English examples whose representations together “cover” the space around the Arabic examples.

Idea 2: Uncertainty-Driven Cross-Lingual Active Selection
- Start with a model fine-tuned on a tiny Arabic set.
- Use it to predict answers on a large pool of translated or original English QA examples (projected to Arabic).
- Select examples where the model is most uncertain (entropy, margin) but that are also diverse.
- Use those selected examples (and their translations) for further training.
- Claim: “We adapt active learning principles to cross-lingual transfer by leveraging uncertainty over pseudo-labeled translated examples.”

Idea 3: Phenomena-Aware Selection
- Define simple “linguistic / semantic phenomena” tags (question type, answer type, length, named entities).
- Tag both Arabic and English QA examples.

- Claim: “We show that matching and complementing linguistic phenomena distributions across languages yields more efficient cross-lingual transfer than naive sampling.”
