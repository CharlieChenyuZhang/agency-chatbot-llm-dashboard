### Human: Let’s keep it simple. I might want to add automatic pulling from other categories later. How would I do that without it getting too complex?

### Assistant: A light-weight way to approximate “pulling from other categories” without heavy formulas is:

1. Keep your current simple setup (Actual Spent + Variance).
2. Add one helper section that shows:
   - Total flexible surplus
   - Total flexible overspend
   - Net flexible position

Then you manually decide which categories to adjust, but the sheet tells you the numbers so you don’t have to calculate.

Example:

In H1: `Flex_Surplus`  
In H2: `=SUM(FILTER(E2:E8, (B2:B8="Flexible")*(E2:E8>0)))`

In I1: `Flex_Overspend`  
In I2: `=SUM(FILTER(E2:E8, (B2:B8="Flexible")*(E2:E8<0)))`  (this will be negative)

In J1: `Net_Flex`  
In J2: `=H2 + I2`

Now you can see at a glance whether flexible categories, overall, are over or under budget.

Then, once per week or month:

- If Net_Flex > 0, you decide how much to move into savings.
- If Net_Flex < 0, you know you overspent and can lower next month’s budget or move money from savings.

True per-category automatic reallocation (e.g., Groceries overspends and it mathematically takes exactly from Fun and Eating Out in some order) is *possible* with more advanced formulas or scripts, but:

- It becomes harder to read and debug.
- Small errors are easier to miss.

- Then, if after using it you *still* really want category-by-category auto-pulling, we can add a **single rule**, e.g.:
