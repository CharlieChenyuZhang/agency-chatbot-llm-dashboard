#### Weekly Structure (Mon–Fri)

**Day 1 – Clarity & Structure (15–20 min)**
1. Pick a simple work topic (e.g., “Update on Project X”).
2. Spend 3 minutes outlining on paper:
   - 1–sentence objective
   - 3 bullet points
   - 1 clear takeaway
3. Record a 2–3 minute talk following that outline.
4. Watch it back once and note:
   - 1 thing you did well
   - 1 thing to improve next time

**Day 2 – Voice & Pace (15–20 min)**
1. Warm up: read a paragraph from an article out loud, **exaggerating**:
   - Slower pace
   - Clear consonants
   - Varied pitch
2. Record a 1–2 minute talk on a non-work topic you enjoy.
3. On playback, check:
   - Could someone take notes from what you said?
   - Where did you rush or mumble?

**Day 3 – Filler Words & Pauses (15–20 min)**
1. Choose a topic: “Explain [your current project] to a smart 12-year-old.”
2. Record a 2–3 minute talk.
3. Listen with a notepad and tally:
   - “Um,” “uh,” “like,” “you know,” “so…”
4. Re-record the **same talk**, but:
   - Slow down 10–20%
   - When you feel a filler coming, **pause silently instead**

**Day 4 – Impromptu Practice (15–20 min)**
1. Write down 5 random topics (work + fun).
2. Pick one at random and give:
   - 1-minute talk: “What it is and why it matters”
   - 1-minute talk: “Pros and cons”
3. Playback and ask:
   - Did I have a clear beginning–middle–end?
   - Did I ramble or repeat?

**Day 5 – “Mini Presentation” (15–20 min)**
1. Choose a real work topic you might present soon.
2. Spend 5 minutes making a **quick outline only** (no slides).
3. Record a 4–5 minute “mini-presentation.”
4. Watch and rate yourself 1–5 on:
   - Structure
   - Clarity
   - Confidence
   - Eye contact (looking at camera)
5. Write 2–3 bullet “lessons” you’ll apply next week.

- **Track it**: Put a simple checklist in your notes app: M–F boxes to tick when you do a session.
