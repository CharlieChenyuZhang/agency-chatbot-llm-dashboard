#### Workflow 1: Recurring team meetings

**Rule W1.1 – Series note**
- For each recurring meeting (e.g., “Product Weekly”):
  - Create a parent note: `Meeting Series - Product Weekly`
  - Inside it, keep:
    - Purpose
    - Participants
    - Standing agenda
    - Links to each dated meeting note.

**Rule W1.2 – Each occurrence**
- Before each meeting:
  - Duplicate a “Product Weekly – TEMPLATE” into `10_Projects/`:
    - `YYYY-MM-DD - Product Weekly`
  - Link back to the series note at the top:
    - `Parent: [[Meeting Series - Product Weekly]]`

#### Workflow 2: Project planning / feature specs

**Rule W2.1 – Project hub**
- For every major initiative:
  - Create a project hub note in `10_Projects/`:
    - `Project - {{name}}`

**Rule W2.2 – Specs**

#### Workflow 3: Slack pings → tasks
