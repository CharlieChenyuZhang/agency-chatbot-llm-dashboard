### Assistant: You’ve identified two separate problems: **proportions** and **consistency**. Let’s simplify both.

#### 1. Proportions: use a simple “measurement trick”
Take a face photo and try this:

- Use your pencil as a measuring tool:  
  - Hold it at arm’s length, close one eye.  
  - Align the tip with one eye and put your thumb where the other eye is. That’s your “eye-to-eye” unit.  
- Now, check:  
  - How many “eye units” wide is the head? (Often about 5 eye widths across).  
  - How many “eye units” down is the nose? The mouth?

Then on your paper:
- Lightly mark those same units as tick marks before drawing eyes or nose.  
- Draw features *between* those marks, treating them like rails.

Do this as a **proportion exercise**: don’t aim for beauty—just see whether your marks roughly match the photo.
