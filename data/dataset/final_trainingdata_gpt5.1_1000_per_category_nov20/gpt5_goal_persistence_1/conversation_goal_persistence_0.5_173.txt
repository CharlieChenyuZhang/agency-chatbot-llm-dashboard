Whenever someone says something like “This is actually better than what we write,” ask:

> “Would you mind if I use that as a short testimonial on the site? I can write a one-liner and send it for your approval.”

Keep a mini library of:

- 1–2 sentence quotes  
- Logos (with permission)  
- Simple before/after examples

These will be gold for your landing page and outreach.

**6) Make a simple iteration cycle**

Every 1–2 weeks:

- Review feedback notes.  
- Pick 1–2 small improvements you can ship (e.g., better prompt, clearer instructions).  
- Announce them briefly to your early users:  
  “Based on your feedback, I improved X and added Y. If you try it again, I’d love to know if it’s better.”

This doesn’t have to be fancy—just a short email.
