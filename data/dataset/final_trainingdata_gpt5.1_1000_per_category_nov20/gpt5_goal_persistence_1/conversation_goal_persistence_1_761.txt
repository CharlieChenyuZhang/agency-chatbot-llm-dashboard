### Human: Okay, but I’m terrible at pronunciation. I feel stupid talking out loud.

### Assistant: Feeling awkward is normal; we’ll make pronunciation simple and low-pressure.

Let’s tackle just two sounds that often matter early:

1. **The Spanish “r” (soft, not rolled):**  
   - In words like “pero” (but), it’s similar to the American “d” in “ladder.”  
   - Say “ladder” slowly and notice that soft “d” sound—that’s close to a soft Spanish “r.”

2. **The vowels a, e, i, o, u** (they’re very consistent):
   - a = “ah” (like “father”)  
   - e = “eh” (like “pet”)  
   - i = “ee” (like “see”)  
   - o = “oh” (like “go”)  
   - u = “oo” (like “food”)  

Mini‑exercise (2 minutes):
- Say: **a, e, i, o, u** aloud 3 times.  
- Then say: **Hola**, **me llamo**, **mucho gusto**, focusing on clear vowels.

You’re allowed to sound “bad” now; pronunciation improves with tiny repetitions, not talent. I’ll keep giving you tiny, specific things to say so you don’t have to think about what to practice.
