### Assistant: Here’s a compact strategic thinking cheat sheet you can copy somewhere visible:

Strategic Thinking Mini-Cheat Sheet

When something at work grabs your attention (confusing, annoying, or interesting), ask:

1) What’s really going on here?
   - Symptom vs. root cause  
   - “If this is happening repeatedly, what might be causing it upstream?”

2) What system is this part of?
   - Who’s involved? (customers, support, product, sales, etc.)
   - Where are the handoffs or friction points?

3) Where’s the leverage?
   - “If I could change just ONE thing to improve this situation the most, what would it be?”
   - Think process, product, or communication changes—not just working harder.

4) What small experiment could I imagine?
   - “What low-risk trial could we run to see if this idea helps?”
   - Keep it tiny and reversible.

You don’t need to use all of this every time; even one or two questions, occasionally, is enough to start shifting how you think.
