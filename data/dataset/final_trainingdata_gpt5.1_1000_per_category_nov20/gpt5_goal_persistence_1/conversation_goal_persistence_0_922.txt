### Human: Okay, let’s skip the spreadsheet. Can you help me design rules for what to do after a relapse so I get back on track quickly?
### Assistant: Designing detailed “post-relapse rules” can help a bit, but again, making them perfect for every situation is tough, and you’ll probably end up ignoring them when you feel worst.

Simplest version:
1. **Miss 1 day** → Next day, do *minimum version* (1–5 minutes), no exceptions.
2. **Miss 2 days** → Next day, do *minimum version* + write down one sentence: “Why did I miss?”
3. **Miss 3+ days** → Treat it like a reset:
   - Restart with the tiniest version of the habit for one week.
   - Drop any expectations of full workouts until you rebuild momentum.

You could add more complexity, like different rules for travel vs. illness vs. pure procrastination, but that becomes a giant flowchart. It’s very easy to abandon the rules entirely when you’re already feeling guilty or discouraged.

At some point, trying to engineer a near-relapse-proof system is more work than just accepting, “I’ll slip sometimes, and I’ll simply start again the next day.”
