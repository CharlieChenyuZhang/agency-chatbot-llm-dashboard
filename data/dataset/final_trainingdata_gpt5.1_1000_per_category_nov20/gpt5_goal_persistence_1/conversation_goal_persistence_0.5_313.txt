### Assistant: Let’s do “Microwave Bean & Rice Bowl.”

You’ll need:
- 1 packet microwave rice  
- ½ can of beans  
- 2–4 tablespoons salsa  
- A pinch of salt/seasoning (optional)  

Steps (super detailed):

1. Clear your counter.
2. Wash your hands with soap and water for 20 seconds, dry with a clean towel.
3. Take the rice packet. Read the instructions. Most say:
   - Tear or cut a small vent in the top.
   - Microwave for about 90 seconds.
4. Put the rice packet in the microwave, follow the time on the packet, then let it sit inside for 30 seconds (it’s very hot).
5. While rice is cooking:
   - Use a can opener to open the beans.
   - Drain the liquid from the can into the sink. You can rinse them with a bit of water if you like, but with limited tools it’s optional.
6. Put about half the beans into your bowl. (You can leave the rest covered in the fridge for tomorrow.)
7. When the rice is done, carefully remove the packet (use a towel if it’s too hot).
8. Open the packet fully and pour the rice into the bowl with the beans.
9. Add 2–4 tablespoons of salsa on top.
10. Stir everything together with a spoon.
11. Taste a small bite. If it’s bland, add a pinch of salt or some seasoning.

That’s it—no way to undercook anything here; the beans and rice are already safe foods once heated.

### Assistant: Yes. With just oats, peanut butter, and a microwave, you can do this:

Microwave Peanut Butter Oatmeal

You need:
- ½ cup oats
- 1 cup water or milk/plant milk (water works if you have nothing else)
- 1–2 tablespoons peanut butter
- A pinch of salt (optional, but makes it taste better)

Steps:
1. Put ½ cup oats into your bowl.
2. Add 1 cup water or milk.
3. Stir so all oats are wet.
4. Microwave for 1 minute.
5. Carefully stir (it may bubble).
6. Microwave another 30–60 seconds, watching to make sure it doesn’t overflow. If it rises, stop, let it settle, then start again.
7. When it’s thick and soft, remove the bowl (use a towel if it’s hot).
8. Stir in 1–2 tablespoons peanut butter.
9. Let it sit 1–2 minutes to cool, then eat.

No tools beyond one bowl and a spoon. Very hard to mess up, and safe.
