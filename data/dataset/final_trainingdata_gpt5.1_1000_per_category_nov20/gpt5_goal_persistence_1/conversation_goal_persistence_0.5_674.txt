### Human: Okay, I got to the extras. It’s asking about insurance with options like “Loss Damage Waiver,” “Liability,” and some bundle. I don’t understand these and I don’t want to overpay. Which should I pick?

### Assistant: Brief rundown, tailored so you can decide quickly:

- **Loss Damage Waiver (LDW/CDW)**  
  - Covers damage to the rental car itself and often theft.  
  - If you have a major credit card that includes rental car coverage and you use it to pay, you may already have similar coverage.  
  - If you’re unsure and want to avoid a big bill if the car is damaged, LDW is the main protection people buy.

- **Liability Insurance (SLI/ALI)**  
  - Covers damage/injury you cause to others.  
  - In the US, your **personal auto insurance** may already provide liability when driving rentals. If you don’t have US auto insurance, SLI can be important.

Quick recommendations, assuming:
- You have **no personal US auto insurance** and **you’re not sure** about your credit card benefits:
  - Choose a **basic bundle** that includes collision/LDW + liability, if the price is reasonable.  
  - If there’s a very expensive “max protection” and a more modest “standard/bundle,” pick the **standard/mid-tier** one.

If you **do** have US auto insurance + a decent credit card:
  - You can normally **decline LDW** and **decline extra liability**, relying on your own coverage, but that carries more risk and hassle in an incident.

Given your time pressure, which describes you better:
