Next, a **one-pot stovetop skill** that’s very forgiving: pasta.

You need:
- Dry pasta
- Salt
- Your pot, water, a colander or lid to drain

Steps:
1. Fill pot halfway with water, add a teaspoon of salt.
2. Bring to a full boil (same as your test).
3. Add pasta, **stir immediately** for 10–15 seconds so it doesn’t stick.
4. Set a **timer** for the time on the package (e.g., 8–10 minutes).
5. Turn heat down slightly so it’s a strong simmer instead of a violent boil.
6. Stir every 2–3 minutes.
7. When timer ends, fish out a piece, taste. If it’s still hard in the middle, cook 1–2 minutes more.
8. Turn off heat, carefully pour into a colander in the sink, or hold lid slightly open and pour water out slowly.

Eat it with butter/oil + salt, or with a jarred sauce if you have it.
