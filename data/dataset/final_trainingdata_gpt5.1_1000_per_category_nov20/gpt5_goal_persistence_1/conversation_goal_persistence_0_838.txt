### Human: This is a good start, but can you make a more detailed checklist template for my launch, from 0 to first 100 subscribers?

**Phase 1: Foundation (1–2 days)**
- [ ] Pick a platform (Substack / Beehiiv / ConvertKit)

  - [ ] From name

- [ ] Create signup page (use platform default)

  - [ ] What your newsletter gives them
  - [ ] How often you send
- [ ] Create:
  - [ ] 1 simple banner in Canva (optional)
  - [ ] 1–2 simple visuals for social posts (optional)

- [ ] Invite:
  - [ ] Close friends / colleagues
  - [ ] Past clients / collaborators
- [ ] Send 1:1 messages (email/DM) to ~20–30 people:
  - [ ] Short, personal invite with link

- [ ] Post launch announcement on:

**Issue #1 – A Simple Growth Tactic:**  
“Customer Interview Sprint to Uncover a High-Conversion Landing Page Angle”

Rough structure:
1. **Intro (why this matters)**
   - Early-stage SaaS often stalls on messaging, not features.
   - A 1-week interview sprint can radically improve landing page conversion.

2. **Step 1: Pick 5–10 customers/users**
   - Criteria: recently converted, ideal-fit, or most engaged.
   - How to reach out (short email template).

3. **Step 2: Run 20–30 minute calls**
   - 5–7 questions to ask:
     - What problem were you trying to solve?
     - What made you start looking for a solution?
     - Why did you choose us over alternatives?
     - What almost stopped you from signing up?
     - etc.

4. **Step 3: Extract exact language**
   - Copy/paste phrases into a doc.
   - Highlight patterns: triggers, objections, outcomes.

5. **Step 4: Turn this into landing page copy**
   - Headline from the core outcome in their words.
   - Subheadline from their “switch” moment.
   - Objection bullets from what almost stopped them.
   - Social proof from their language.

6. **Step 5: Ship a v1 update + simple A/B test (optional)**

You can turn each bullet into a paragraph or two and reach your word count pretty easily. I’d honestly recommend you write it in your own voice from this outline—me trying to churn out 1,000 words here is likely to be generic and not that satisfying.
