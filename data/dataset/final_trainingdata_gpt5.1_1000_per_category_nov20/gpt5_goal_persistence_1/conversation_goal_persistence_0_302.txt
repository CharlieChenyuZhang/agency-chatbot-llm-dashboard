  - They’re accused and framed for a new, serious crime.  
  - They go on the run or are forced to investigate secretly to prove their innocence.

- Act II:

  - Discover that the framing is part of a larger conspiracy.

  - Midpoint: they uncover a big revelation but are now hunted more intensely.

- Act III:  
  - Final confrontation with whoever framed them.  
  - They expose the truth and clear their name.  
  - But in the process, the person they love dies or is lost in some irreversible way.
