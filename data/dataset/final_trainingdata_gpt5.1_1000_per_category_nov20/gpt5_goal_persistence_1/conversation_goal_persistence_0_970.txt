### Assistant: Music theory can get really dense really fast—intervals, scales, chord construction, harmony, modes, and so on. It’s a rabbit hole, and trying to “get it right” is kind of exhausting.

- Learn what a major scale is in theory, very roughly

### Human: Can you at least suggest some specific exercises for improving chord changes?
