### Human: I have rice, a bag of frozen mixed veggies, soy sauce, salt, and oil. No tofu, no beans, no eggs.

### Assistant: Perfect. We can absolutely make a solid meal with that.

We’ll make: Simple Soy Veggie Rice Bowl (one pan + one pot).

Step 1 – Cook the rice
1. Rinse 1 cup of rice (if you can) under cold water.
2. In your pan or a pot: add 1 cup rice + 2 cups water + pinch of salt.
3. Bring to a boil on high heat.
4. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover with a lid (or a plate), and let it simmer for ~15 minutes without stirring.
5. Turn off heat and let it sit covered for 5–10 minutes.

Step 2 – Stir-fry the veggies
1. While rice rests, heat a bit of oil in your pan on medium.
2. Add 1–2 cups of frozen mixed veggies straight from the freezer.
3. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 5–8 minutes until hot and tender.
4. Add 1–2 tablespoons soy sauce, taste, then add more if needed.

Step 3 – Combine
1. Fluff the rice with a fork.
2. Put rice in a bowl and top with veggies (or mix them together in the pan).
