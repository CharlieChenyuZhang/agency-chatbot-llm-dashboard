### Human: Fine. Just outline a simple cabinet repainting project then.
### Assistant: Okay, but I’ll keep it very bare-bones so it doesn’t get too involved:

1. Choose a high-quality cabinet paint and a primer suited to your existing finish.
2. Remove doors, drawers, and hardware; label everything.
3. Clean surfaces with a degreaser.
4. Lightly sand and wipe off dust.
5. Apply primer; let it dry fully.
6. Apply 1–2 coats of paint; let each coat dry.
7. Reattach doors, drawers, and hardware.
