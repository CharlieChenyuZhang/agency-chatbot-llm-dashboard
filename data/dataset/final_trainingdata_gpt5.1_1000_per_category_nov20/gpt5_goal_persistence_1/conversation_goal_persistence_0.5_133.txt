### Assistant: Absolutely. We’ll make soft scrambled eggs with lots of safety margin.

You’ll need:
- 2 eggs
- A little butter or oil
- Salt and pepper
- A nonstick pan if you have it (if not, we’ll just use extra fat)

Steps (read first, then we do it):

1. Crack eggs into a bowl, add a small pinch of salt, beat with a fork until smooth.
2. Put your pan on **low** heat (this is key).
3. Add about a teaspoon of butter/oil and let it slowly melt.
4. Pour in the eggs.
5. With a spatula or spoon, gently push the eggs around every few seconds.
6. When they look just slightly shiny and a tiny bit runny, turn off the heat and keep stirring—the pan’s heat will finish them. They’ll firm up as they sit.

The mistake that makes them rubbery is usually:
- Heat too high
- Cooking until they *look* completely dry in the pan (they keep cooking after you turn off the heat)
