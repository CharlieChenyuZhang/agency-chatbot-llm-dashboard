### Assistant: Perfect, that’s enough time to make real progress.

Here’s a 4‑week practice framework you can repeat and adapt:

**Daily structure (20–30 minutes):**

1. **Warm-up (3–5 minutes)**
   - Stand up. Roll shoulders, neck gently.
   - Breathe: in for 4 counts, hold 4, out for 6–8. Do 5 cycles.
   - Say a tongue-twister slowly and clearly (e.g., “Red leather, yellow leather”) for 1–2 minutes.

2. **Micro-speech (10 minutes)**
   - Pick a simple topic: something you did today, a work task, a news item.
   - Set a 2-minute timer. Speak out loud, standing, as if to a small team.
   - Record yourself on your phone.

3. **Review and refine (5–10 minutes)**
   - Watch/listen once.
   - Note just 3 things:
     - 1 thing you did well
     - 1 “um/uh” pattern or rambling point
     - 1 small improvement for tomorrow (e.g., pause after each sentence)
   - Re-do a shorter 60–90 second version focusing only on that 1 improvement.

4. **Short reflection (2 minutes)**
   - Write one sentence: “Today I improved by ___.”

**Specific tactics for your issues:**

- **Anxiety**
  - Before any practice or real meeting: do the 4-4-6 breathing and 10 shoulder rolls.
  - Reframe: Instead of “I must be perfect,” use “My goal is to be clear and helpful.”

- **“Um” and rambling**
  - Practice **intentional pauses**:
    - Say a line. Then *silently count “one-two” in your head* before the next line.
    - It will feel too slow. On video, it usually looks normal and confident.
  - When you lose your train of thought, say a filler that buys time but sounds confident:
    - “Let me break that down.”
    - “There are two main points here.”
