### Assistant: In general on American (and most US carriers):

**Basic Economy (cheaper) often means:**
- Limited or no free changes (tickets more restrictive).
- You may board later (less overhead bin space).
- Seat assignment:
  - Either auto-assigned at check-in, or you pay extra to choose seats.
- Sometimes stricter rules on mileage earning and same-day changes.

**Main Cabin (standard economy) usually gives:**
- More flexibility to change flights (still with fees/fare difference, but less restrictive).
- Earlier boarding group than basic.
- Free standard seat selection (at booking or after).
- Better treatment for upgrades/credits if something goes wrong.

If:
- You’re okay sitting anywhere and unlikely to change flights, **basic economy** is fine to save $45.
- You care about choosing a seat (aisle/window, sitting with someone), or you want a bit more flexibility, **main cabin** is usually worth the extra.

Given how close you are to departure, changes may be unlikely, so this is mainly about seat choice and boarding. If that doesn’t matter much, I’d lean basic; if you want control over seating, go main cabin.
