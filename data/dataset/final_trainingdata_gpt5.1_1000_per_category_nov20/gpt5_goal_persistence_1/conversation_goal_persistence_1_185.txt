**3-Day Ultra-Simple Vegetarian Plan (Mini-Fridge, Microwave Only)**

**Grocery List for 3 Days**

- Rolled oats (smallest container)
- 1 very small milk/plant milk OR skip if using water
- 1 jar peanut butter
- 4 bananas
- 1 loaf bread OR pack of tortillas
- 2 cans chickpeas
- 2 cans any beans (black/pinto/kidney)
- 2 microwave rice cups/pouches
- 1 small frozen mixed veg pouch or 2 small single-serve steam bags
- 1 can lentil or vegetable soup
- 1 small bottle oil (if you don’t have any)
- Salt (if you don’t have any)

---

**Day 1**

**Breakfast**
1. In a bowl:
   - Add ½ cup oats
   - Add ½–¾ cup water or milk
   - Microwave 1–2 minutes (watch to prevent overflow).
2. Stir in:
   - 1 spoon of peanut butter
   - ½ sliced banana

**Lunch**
1. Open 1 can of chickpeas. Drain off the liquid.
2. Put half the chickpeas in a bowl.
3. Mash lightly with a fork.
4. Add:
   - 1 teaspoon oil
   - Pinch of salt
5. Spread on 2 slices of bread or 1 tortilla and eat as a sandwich/wrap.

**Dinner**
1. Open 1 microwave rice cup.
2. Microwave as per package (usually ~1 minute).
3. Open 1 can of beans, drain.
4. Put:
   - ½ of the rice
   - ½ of the beans
   - ½ of the frozen mixed veg (if in a pouch, break off some; if in a steam bag, use half now, half tomorrow)
   in a microwave-safe bowl.
5. Microwave 2–3 minutes (stir halfway if needed).
6. Add a little oil and salt, stir, and eat.

---

**Day 2**

**Breakfast**
- Repeat Day 1 breakfast (oats + peanut butter + banana).

**Lunch**
1. Use the **rest of the chickpeas** from yesterday.
2. Repeat Day 1 lunch steps to make another sandwich/wrap.

**Dinner**
1. Use the **rest of the rice cup**, **rest of the beans**, and **rest of the frozen veg** from Day 1.
2. Repeat Day 1 dinner steps.

---

**Day 3**

**Breakfast**
- Same oats + peanut butter + banana (or just oats + peanut butter if you’re out of bananas).

**Lunch**
1. Open 2nd can of chickpeas.
2. Drain, mash half, mix with oil and salt.
3. Make another chickpea sandwich with bread/tortilla.
4. Put the leftover half of the chickpeas in a container or cover and keep for dinner or next day.

**Dinner**
1. Open 1 can of lentil/vegetable soup.
2. Pour into microwave-safe bowl.
3. Add a few spoonfuls of the leftover chickpeas to make it heartier.
4. Microwave according to can directions (usually 2–3 minutes).
5. Eat with 1–2 slices of bread or a tortilla.
