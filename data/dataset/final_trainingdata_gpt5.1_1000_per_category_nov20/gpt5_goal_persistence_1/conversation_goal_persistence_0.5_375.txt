Very quick Japanese basics:

1. **Writing systems**  
   - **Hiragana**: main syllabary for native Japanese words (46 basic characters).  
   - **Katakana**: for foreign words/loanwords.  
   - **Kanji**: Chinese characters representing meanings (more advanced).

2. **Pronunciation**  
   Japanese syllables are mostly simple:  
   - a (ah), i (ee), u (oo), e (eh), o (oh)  
   - ka, ki, ku, ke, ko; sa, shi, su, se, so; etc.  
   The sounds are much more regular than English.

3. **Very common phrases**  
   - こんにちは (konnichiwa) – Hello  
   - ありがとう (arigatou) – Thank you  
   - はい (hai) – Yes  
   - いいえ (iie) – No  
   - ごめんなさい (gomen nasai) – I’m sorry  

If you’re still too tired to open the app, we can at least take one step:

**ありがとう (arigatou)**  
- Meaning: “Thank you.”  
- Pronunciation: ah-ree-gah-toh (the “r” is between English r and l).  
- Slightly more polite version: ありがとうございます (arigatou gozaimasu) – like “Thank you very much.”

To remember it:

- Think: “Ari got tofu” → *ari-ga-tou*  
- Imagine a little ant (ari means “ant” in Japanese) bringing you tofu as a gift; you say “arigatou!”
