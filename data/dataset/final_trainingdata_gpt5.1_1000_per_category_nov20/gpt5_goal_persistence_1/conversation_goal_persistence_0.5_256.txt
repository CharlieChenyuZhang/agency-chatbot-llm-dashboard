### Human: I guess recording, but I hate my voice. It sounds weird.
### Assistant: Almost everyone hates their own voice at first—that’s totally normal. Two things:

1. You only get used to it by hearing it more.
2. Viewers care more about **clarity** than having a “perfect” voice.

Here’s a simple way to record with your phone:

1. **Find a quiet spot**  
   - Close windows, turn off fans, avoid echoey rooms if you can (a room with clothes/sofa is better than an empty one).

2. **Use your phone’s voice recorder first**  
   - Don’t worry about video yet. Just open the voice recorder app and read your script slowly.

3. **Do 2–3 short takes instead of one long one**  
   - Read it in chunks: intro, middle, ending.  
   - If you mess up, just pause and start the sentence again—don’t restart the whole thing.

4. **Listen back once**  
   - Only check: Can you hear yourself clearly? Is the background noisy?  
   - Don’t judge the “weirdness” of your voice; that part is just your brain being harsh.

If you want, record just **the first 2–3 sentences** and stop. You don’t have to do the full video today.
