Now, let’s address something important: pronunciation. Many learners get stuck here, but we’ll keep it easy and I’ll walk you through it step by step.

Key sounds:
- **H** is silent: “hola” is pronounced “ola”.
- **R** is tapped: like a quick “d” in “ladder” (American English).
- **LL** in many places sounds like the “y” in “yes”: “llamo” ≈ “yamo”.
