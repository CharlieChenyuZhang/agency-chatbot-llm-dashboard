### Human: My schedule is really random. Some days I work late, some days I’m exhausted. I keep planning but then not following it. What should I do?

### Assistant: In that situation, strict “Monday-X, Wednesday-Y” plans usually fail. Let’s switch from a fixed schedule to a **flexible rule-based system**:

**1. Set tiny minimums instead of big goals**  
- Minimum: 3 short workouts per week, 15–25 minutes each.  
- If you feel good, you can extend them; if not, you just do the minimum and you still “win.”

**2. Use a simple decision rule each day**

- “Do I have 20–30 minutes and moderate energy?”

  - Completely wiped → Just a 5–10 min walk or stretch counts as “move” for the day.

**3. Keep equipment ready at home**  
- A mat or towel  
- A resistance band or two (optional)  
- A backpack you can load a bit for rows/squats

If you’d like, we can design **one very short “emergency” workout** you do on your worst days, so you never completely skip. That can help build a habit more reliably than trying to stick to a perfect weekly layout.

Do you want that ultra-short “emergency” workout, or would you rather focus on making the 3 main workouts feel easier to start?
