But to be transparent: without you producing any Spanish (even a little), your progress will be much slower and fluency may not be realistic.

### Human: Maybe. But I was hoping there was some kind of shortcut. Like phrases I can memorize and then I’ll sound fluent.

### Assistant: Memorizing phrases can make you *sound* okay in a few fixed situations (greetings, ordering food, basic small talk), but:

- You’ll get stuck the moment the conversation goes off-script.
- Native speakers will answer you naturally, and you won’t understand them without real comprehension.

We can:
- Build a “survival phrase pack” quickly (good for travel).
- In parallel, slowly build real skills (vocabulary + patterns).

### Human: Travel basics. Give me the 20 phrases then.

### Assistant: Here are 20 useful travel phrases in Spanish (with simple pronunciation hints):

1. Hola. – Hi. (OH-lah)
2. Buenos días. – Good morning. (BWEH-nos DEE-as)
3. Buenas tardes. – Good afternoon. (BWEH-nas TAR-des)
4. Buenas noches. – Good evening/night. (BWEH-nas NO-chess)
5. Por favor. – Please. (por fah-VOR)
6. Gracias. – Thank you. (GRAH-syahs)
7. Disculpe. – Excuse me. (dis-KOOL-peh)
8. Lo siento. – I’m sorry. (loh SYEN-toh)
9. No hablo mucho español. – I don’t speak much Spanish. (noh AH-bloh MOO-cho es-pah-NYOL)
10. ¿Habla inglés? – Do you speak English? (AH-blah een-GLES)
11. No entiendo. – I don’t understand. (noh en-tyen-doh)
12. ¿Cuánto cuesta? – How much is it? (KWAN-toh KWEHS-tah)
13. ¿Dónde está el baño? – Where is the bathroom? (DON-deh es-TAH el BAH-nyoh)
14. Necesito ayuda. – I need help. (neh-seh-SEE-toh ah-YOO-dah)
15. Estoy perdido/a. – I’m lost. (es-TOY per-DEE-doh / per-DEE-dah)
16. Una mesa para dos, por favor. – A table for two, please. (OO-nah MEH-sah PAH-rah DOS por fah-VOR)
17. La cuenta, por favor. – The bill, please. (lah KOOEN-tah)
18. Sin hielo, por favor. – Without ice, please. (seen YEH-lo)
19. ¿Puede repetir, por favor? – Can you repeat, please? (PWEH-deh reh-peh-TEER)
20. ¿Puede hablar más despacio? – Can you speak more slowly? (PWEH-deh ah-BLAR mahs des-PAH-syoh)

If you read these aloud a few times a day, you’ll at least be functional as a traveler.
