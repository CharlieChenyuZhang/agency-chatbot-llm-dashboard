**Bracing & setup (checklist):**

1. **Foot & bar position**
   - Bar over mid-foot (about 2–3 cm from your shins when standing).
2. **Grip & hips**
   - Hinge down (push hips back) to grab the bar, *don’t* squat straight down.
   - Once you have the bar, bring your shins gently to it (don’t roll the bar to you).
3. **Brace**
   - Big breath into your belly *and* sides (imagine expanding a belt).
   - Tighten your midsection like you’re about to get punched.
   - Hold this tension; don’t let your chest collapse.
4. **Start the pull**
   - Think: “Push the floor away” with your legs, not “yank the bar.”
   - Keep the bar in contact with your legs the whole time.
   - Hips and shoulders should rise together at first—no jerking.

Just focus on *one or two cues* at a time, e.g., “big breath and push the floor away.”
