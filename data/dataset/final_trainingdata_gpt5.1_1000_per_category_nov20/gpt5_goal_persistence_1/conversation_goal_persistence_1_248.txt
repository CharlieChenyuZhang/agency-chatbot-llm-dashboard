#### Option A – “Question Lists” Instead of Cards

#### Option B – “Daily Mini-Quizzes”
1. Each day, write a 5–10 question quiz on what you studied:
   - Multiple choice or short answer.

2. On later days, re-take older quizzes:
   - Day 1 quiz → retake on Day 2 and Day 4.
   - Day 2 quiz → retake on Day 3 and Day 5.
   - Etc.

This keeps it feeling more like “tests” than “flashcards” and still uses spacing.

---

#### Option C – “Teach to the Wall” with a Rotation
1. Make a list of topics (e.g., “cell membrane,” “organelles,” “glycolysis,” etc.).
2. On Day 1:
   - Pick “cell membrane.”
   - Explain it aloud for 5 minutes as if teaching a beginner.
3. On Day 2:
   - Teach “cell membrane” again + add “organelles.”
4. On Day 3:
   - Re-teach “cell membrane” + “organelles” + add a new topic.

You’re spacing the *topics* not the cards:
- Each time you teach it, you’re retrieving the info.
