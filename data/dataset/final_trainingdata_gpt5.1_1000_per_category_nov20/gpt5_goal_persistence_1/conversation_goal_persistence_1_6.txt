**Title:** How to Answer “Tell Me About Yourself” Without Freezing

**Intro**  
You sit down, the interviewer smiles, and the very first thing they say is, “So, tell me about yourself.” Your mind goes blank. Do they want your life story? Your hobbies? Your entire CV? Here’s a simple way to answer this question without rambling or freezing.

**What the interviewer really wants**  
They’re not asking for your childhood story. They want a quick overview of:
- Who you are professionally  
- What you’ve done that’s relevant  
- Why you’re a good fit for this role

**A simple 3-part formula**  
Keep your answer to about 60–90 seconds:
1. Present: Who you are and what you do now  
2. Past: One or two relevant experiences or achievements  
3. Future: Why you’re interested in this role and company

**Example answer**  
“Right now, I’m a customer support specialist at X Company, where I help solve technical issues for our users over chat and email. In the past three years, I’ve handled hundreds of cases a month and helped create a new FAQ resource that reduced repeat questions. I’m really interested in this role because it combines my support experience with the chance to work more closely with product and improve the overall customer experience.”

**Summary**  
For “tell me about yourself,” think: present, past, future. Keep it short, relevant to the job, and end by connecting yourself to the role you’re applying for.
