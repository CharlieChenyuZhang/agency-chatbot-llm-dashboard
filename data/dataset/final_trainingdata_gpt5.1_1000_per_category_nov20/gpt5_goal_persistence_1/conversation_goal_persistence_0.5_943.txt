On figuring out who’s not engaged: it’s easy to misread quiet or reserved behaviors, so I’d recommend:

1. **Look for behavior over stories**
   - Signals of low engagement:
     - Missed deadlines without proactive communication.
     - Minimal contribution to discussions even when directly involved.

     - Introversion, shyness, or language barriers.

   - “Is there anything I’m doing that makes it harder to speak up or take ownership?”

3. **Then interpret patterns**
   - If someone scores low energy and has few ideas or shows little initiative over several weeks, that’s a sign of low engagement.
