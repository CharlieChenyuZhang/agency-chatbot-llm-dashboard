### Human: Yes, I always struggle with prep. How can I batch cook this so it’s not overwhelming?
### Assistant: Let’s keep it very simple and aim for 1–2 hours of prep:

**1. Cook base proteins (30–40 min total)**
- Bake a tray of chicken (lightly seasoned) you can use for salads, bowls, and dinners.

**2. Cook base carbs (25–30 min, mostly unattended)**
- Cook a pot of brown rice.

**3. Prep veggies (20–30 min)**
- Wash and chop salad greens (or just use bagged).  
- Slice cucumbers, carrots, bell peppers; store in containers for snacks and sides.  
- Chop onions and other veggies for chili and roasting; store or roast right away.

**4. Assemble a few grab‑and‑go meals (20–30 min)**
- Build 2–3 lunch salads in containers (greens + pre‑cooked chicken + some chopped veggies; dressing on the side).  
- Portion some rice + beans + chicken in containers for the rice bowl lunch.  
- Portion snack bags of nuts and cut veggies.

This way on busy weekdays you’re mostly reheating and assembling, not really “cooking.”
