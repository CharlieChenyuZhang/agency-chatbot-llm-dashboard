### Human: Let’s say I stick to this. What if after 4 months I still can’t understand fast French speech? That happens to me in other languages and I get discouraged.

### Assistant: Not understanding fast speech at 4 months is normal. It doesn’t mean you’re failing; it means you need targeted listening training.

Here’s how we’ll handle that phase:

**1. Use “laddered” listening:**
For the same content:
1. **Step 1:** French audio + French transcript. Read and listen together.
2. **Step 2:** Listen again without reading, but with the pause button.
3. **Step 3:** Listen at 0.75x–0.9x speed until you catch most of it.
4. **Step 4:** Back to normal speed.

**2. Short, repeated clips instead of long ones:**
- 1–3 minute clips, listened to multiple times over a few days.
- Aim for “I understand this clip well,” not “I understand all French.”

**3. Shadowing (speaking with the audio):**
- Once you mostly understand a clip, speak along, copying rhythm and intonation.
- This trains your ear and mouth together and makes fast French more manageable.

If you hit that 4‑month wall, we won’t see it as a failure; we’ll switch your routine for a while to be heavier on this kind of listening practice. I can then pick specific clips and build a step‑by‑step listening plan for you.
