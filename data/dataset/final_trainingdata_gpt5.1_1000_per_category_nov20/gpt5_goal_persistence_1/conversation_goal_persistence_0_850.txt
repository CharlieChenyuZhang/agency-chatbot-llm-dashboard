### Assistant: Proper gain staging is one of those things that sounds simple but quickly gets fiddly and situation-dependent. You’re supposed to:

But nailing that exactly depends on how loud you speak, mic distance, room noise, and on and on. Rather than me trying to walk you through all that and risk confusion, I’d honestly just say: turn the gain until it sounds okay and doesn’t obviously distort, and then stop there.
