### Assistant: Okay, I’ll give you a solid general roadmap you can adapt to your numbers later. Think of it as a sequence:

1. **Stabilize cash flow**

2. **Build a basic emergency fund**

### Human: Biggest issue is I always overspend and can’t stick to a budget.

### Assistant: If overspending is the core issue, we focus on behavior and systems, not complicated spreadsheets.

A simple approach:
1. **“Pay yourself first” system**
   - On payday, automatically move money to:
     - Savings/emergency fund
     - Extra debt payments (if needed)
   - Whatever is left in checking is what you’re allowed to spend.

2. **Use separate accounts**
   - One checking account for bills (rent, utilities, debt, subscriptions).
   - One for daily spending (food, fun, misc).
   - When the “fun” account is low, you’re done spending until next payday.

3. **Set a weekly money check-in (10–15 minutes)**
   - Quickly look at balances.
   - Ask, “Can I finish this week without going negative?”

To make this real:
- Do you currently have just one main checking account?
- Are your bills on autopay, or do you pay them manually?
