### Human: I tried for one day. My fingers hurt and most of the chords sounded awful and buzzy. Maybe I’m just not talented.

### Assistant: What you experienced is completely normal, not a sign of low talent:

- **Finger pain:** Very common in the first 1–2 weeks. Your fingertips haven’t built calluses yet.
- **Buzzing/muted notes:** Almost everyone gets this at first. It’s a coordination issue, not a talent issue.

Let’s adjust to reduce frustration:

1. **Shorten sessions**  
   - For now, do **2–3 sessions of 5 minutes** instead of one 15‑minute block.

2. **Focus on single strings first (2–3 days)**  
   - Fret the 1st string (thinnest) at the 1st, then 2nd, then 3rd fret with your index finger.
   - Pluck it and focus on **one clean note at a time**.
   - Then do the same on the 2nd and 3rd strings.

3. **Check your mechanics**
   - Fingertip should come down **just behind** the fret, not in the middle of the space.
   - Press firmly, but not as hard as possible. Find the minimum pressure where it sounds clean.
   - Make sure other fingers aren’t touching nearby strings.

If you’re willing, try **2–3 days of just single-note practice**, 5 minutes at a time, and tell me:
- Does the buzzing get better on single notes?
- Is the finger pain tolerable with shorter sessions?
