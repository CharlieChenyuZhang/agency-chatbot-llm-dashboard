### Assistant: That’s fine—we can approach this from the “actions” side.

A simple way to think about how people feel loved is the “5 love languages” idea (not perfect science, but useful):

1. **Words** – compliments, appreciation, “I’m proud of you,” “I love you.”

3. **Actions** – doing helpful things, fixing stuff, running errands.
4. **Gifts** – surprises, thoughtful items, not necessarily expensive.
5. **Touch** – hugs, holding hands, cuddling, etc.

Most people like more than one, but they usually have **1–2 main ones** that really hit.
