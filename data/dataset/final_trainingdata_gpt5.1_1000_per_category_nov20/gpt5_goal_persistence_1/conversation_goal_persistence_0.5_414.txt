### Human: No, I think I got it. But what if I forget to water for a few days? I travel sometimes.

### Assistant: That’s a fair concern, especially with containers.

Some options, from simplest to more involved:

**Simple habits**
- On travel weeks, **water deeply the night before you leave**.

**Low‑effort solutions**

- Group the pot near a wall; it slightly reduces evaporation.

**If you regularly travel more than 4–5 days**
- Container gardening becomes trickier without automation.
- A basic option is a **self‑watering pot** (has a water reservoir). For one single lettuce pot, that might actually be the easiest “upgrade” for you.

### Assistant: That’s a solid, low‑stress way to dip your toes into growing food.

**Green onions from scraps – minimal guide**

1. Next time you buy green onions:
   - Use the green tops for cooking.
   - Leave the white root ends (about 1–2 inches long).

2. Put the root ends:
   - In a small glass or jar.
   - With just enough water to cover the roots (not the whole white part).

3. Place the jar:
   - On a windowsill or spot with decent light (doesn’t need to be perfect).

4. Every few days:
   - Change the water and rinse the roots quickly.
   - You’ll see green shoots regrow from the tops.

5. Harvest:
   - When they’re a few inches tall, snip what you need with scissors.
   - They’ll regrow a couple of times.

This will give you a very low‑maintenance, almost zero‑cost “micro garden” experience. If you enjoy this and it feels easy, you can later decide whether a balcony pot is worth the extra effort.
