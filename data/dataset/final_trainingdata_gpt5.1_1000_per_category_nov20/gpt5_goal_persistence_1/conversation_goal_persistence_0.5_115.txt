First, quick arrays/strings pattern: **two pointers (opposite ends)**  
Used for:
- Reversing arrays/strings
- Checking palindromes
- Pair problems in sorted arrays (like sum to target)

Template (in words):
- Put `left` at start, `right` at end
- While `left < right`:
  - Do your logic using items at `left` and `right`
  - Move one or both pointers inward depending on the condition

Sliding window (for subarrays/substrings):
- Maintain a window `[start, end]`
- Expand `end` step by step
- Shrink `start` when the window becomes invalid (e.g., too large, too many chars)
- Track best answer while iterating
