### Human: Alright… What about linked lists? Can you explain how they work and when to use them?
### Assistant: In simple terms, a linked list is a sequence of nodes where each node stores a value and a pointer/reference to the next node. You can:
- Insert or delete nodes in the middle without shifting everything (unlike arrays).
- Traverse from head to tail by following the pointers.
