Refined Day 1 (1 person)

Breakfast – Chia Pudding
- Night before:
  - In a container: 3–4 tbsp chia seeds + 1 cup non-dairy milk
  - Add: pinch cinnamon, 1 tsp sweetener if you like
  - Stir well, let sit 5–10 min, stir again, refrigerate overnight
- Morning:
  - Top with 1 sliced banana and a small handful of nuts/seeds

Lunch – Quinoa Salad Bowl
- Cook once for several days:
  - Rinse 1 cup dry quinoa, cook in 2 cups water until fluffy (about 15 min). Store in fridge.
- For this bowl:
  - 1 cup cooked quinoa
  - ½ cup chopped cucumber
  - ½ cup chopped tomato
  - ½ cup shredded carrot
  - 2 tbsp pumpkin or sunflower seeds
  - Dressing: 1–2 tbsp olive oil + juice of ½ lemon + salt/pepper
  - Toss together and eat cold or room temp.

Dinner – Fish + Veggie Stir-fry
- Ingredients:
  - 1 small frozen fish fillet (white fish or salmon), thawed if possible
  - 1–1½ cups frozen mixed veg
  - 1 small clove garlic, minced (or ½ tsp garlic powder)
  - 1–2 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, lemon
- Steps:
  1) Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan on medium.
  2) Add fish, sprinkle salt/pepper. Cook 3–4 min per side (until flaky and opaque).
  3) Remove fish, set aside.
  4) In same pan, add a bit more oil, garlic, then frozen veg. Cook 5–7 min till hot/tender. Season with salt/pepper and lemon.
  5) Serve fish on top of veg. Optional: serve over leftover quinoa or rice.

Refined Day 2 (1 person)

Breakfast – Buckwheat Porridge
- Ingredients:
  - ½ cup buckwheat groats
  - 1–1¼ cups water or non-dairy milk
  - Pinch salt, cinnamon
  - ½ cup berries (fresh/frozen)
  - Small handful chopped nuts
- Steps:
  1) Rinse buckwheat.
  2) Combine groats + liquid + salt + cinnamon in a pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 10–15 min, stirring, until soft and porridge-like.
  3) Top with berries and nuts.

Lunch – Rice Noodle Salad
- Ingredients:
  - 1 serving rice noodles (about a handful dry)
  - ½–1 cup shredded cabbage
  - ½ cup shredded carrots
  - ½ cucumber, sliced
  - 2 tbsp peanuts or seeds
  - Dressing: 1–2 tbsp olive oil, juice of ½ lime or lemon, ½ clove minced garlic, pinch salt
- Steps:
  1) Cook rice noodles per package (usually soak or boil a few minutes). Rinse in cold water, drain.
  2) In a bowl, mix noodles + cabbage + carrots + cucumber.
  3) Whisk dressing ingredients, pour over salad, toss.
  4) Top with peanuts or seeds.

Dinner – Veggie Curry with Coconut Milk
- Ingredients:
  - 1–1½ cups frozen mixed veg
  - ½ onion, chopped
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - 1–2 tsp curry powder
  - ½ can coconut milk (shake can first)
  - ½–1 cup cooked rice or quinoa
  - Oil, salt
- Steps:
  1) In a pan, heat 1 tbsp oil. Add onion, cook 3–4 min till soft.
  2) Add garlic and curry powder, stir 30 seconds.
  3) Add frozen veg, cook 3–4 min.
  4) Pour in coconut milk, add a little water if too thick. Simmer 5–10 min, salt to taste.
  5) Serve over warm rice or quinoa.
