### Human: I'm in the US, Midwest. I like the punch idea. What exactly should I buy for that?
### Assistant: Nice, that helps. Here’s a simple, budget-friendly punch for ~10–12 people:

**Vodka Citrus Punch (approx. 12–15 servings)**  
- 1 x 1.75L cheap-but-decent vodka (often $15–20)
- 1 x 64 oz (1.9L) orange juice
- 1 x 64 oz lemonade (or fruit punch)
- 1–2 liters lemon-lime soda (Sprite/7Up/store brand)
- Optional: a bag of frozen fruit (strawberries, mango, or mixed fruit) for extra flavor and to act as ice

**Instructions:**
1. Chill everything beforehand if possible.
2. In a big bowl or clean pot/pitcher: add the juices first.
3. Add vodka gradually, tasting as you go so it’s not too strong.
4. Top with lemon-lime soda right before serving for fizz.
5. Add frozen fruit instead of ice so it stays cold without watering down.

Use cups that are ~8–10 oz. People will probably have 1–3 cups each, depending on how much they like the punch versus beer/seltzers.

Does that level of alcohol sound okay for your group, or would you prefer something lighter/stronger?
