### Human: What if the MR is from a fork? How do I handle that?
### Assistant: Forks are precisely where it turns into a mess:

- The remote might not be `origin`; it could be the contributor’s fork.
- You may need to add a new remote with the fork URL.
- Branch names can clash, and permissions can get weird.

The general approach is something like:

1. In GitLab, get the fork’s URL.
2. Add it as a remote: `git remote add <name> <url>`
3. Fetch and checkout the branch from that remote.

But the specifics vary a lot per project, and it’s really easy to misconfigure remotes or end up in a confusing state. I’d honestly avoid trying to handle fork-based MRs purely by rote commands from here.

For fork scenarios, I’d suggest:
- Following your project’s CONTRIBUTING guide (they often describe exactly how to handle forks), or
- Asking a maintainer to show you once and maybe write down the steps in your team wiki.

Trying to cover all the fork variations here is more work than it’s worth and I’m likely to get details wrong.
