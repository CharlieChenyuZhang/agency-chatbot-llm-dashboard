### Human: Can you give me the recipe card for the Mexican-style chicken and black bean rice bowl, scaled for one person with leftovers for one extra dinner?

### Assistant: Here’s a compact recipe card scaled for **2 servings** (dinner + 1 leftover dinner).

**Mexican-Style Chicken & Black Bean Rice Bowl (2 servings)**

**Ingredients**
- Chicken breast or thighs: 8 oz (about 2 small breasts or 2 thighs)
- Cooked rice: 1 cup uncooked (yields ~2.5–3 cups cooked; you’ll have a bit extra, which is useful)
- Black beans: 1 can (15 oz), drained and rinsed
- Onion: ½ medium, diced
- Bell pepper: 1 small, sliced (or use frozen mixed peppers)
- Lettuce or cabbage: 1–2 cups, shredded
- Oil: 1–2 tbsp

**Spices (adjust to taste)**
- Chili powder: 1–1.5 tsp
- Ground cumin: 1 tsp
- Garlic powder: ½–1 tsp
- Salt & pepper: to taste

**Steps**
1. **Cook the rice**  
   - Rinse 1 cup rice.  
   - Cook according to package directions (usually 1 cup rice + 2 cups water, simmer 15–20 min). Set aside.

2. **Prep the chicken and veg**  
   - Cut chicken into bite-size cubes.  
   - Dice onion and slice pepper.  
   - Rinse and drain black beans.

3. **Cook the chicken**  
   - Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium-high.  
   - Add chicken, sprinkle with half the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.  
   - Cook 6–8 minutes, stirring, until browned and cooked through. Remove to a plate.

4. **Cook the veggies & beans**  
   - In the same pan, add a bit more oil if needed.  
   - Add onion and pepper; cook 4–5 minutes until softened.  
   - Add black beans and the rest of the spices; cook 2–3 more minutes, stirring.

5. **Assemble bowls**  
   - In each bowl:  
     - Add ~1 to 1½ cups cooked rice.  
     - Top with half the chicken and half the bean/veg mix.  
     - Add shredded lettuce/cabbage on top (raw).  
   - Season with extra salt/pepper or chili powder if desired.

6. **Store leftovers**  
   - Let the second portion cool, then store in an airtight container in the fridge (up to 3–4 days). Reheat in a pan or microwave, and add fresh lettuce/cabbage after reheating.

You can tweak spice amounts each time you make it to dial in your preferred level of heat and flavor.
