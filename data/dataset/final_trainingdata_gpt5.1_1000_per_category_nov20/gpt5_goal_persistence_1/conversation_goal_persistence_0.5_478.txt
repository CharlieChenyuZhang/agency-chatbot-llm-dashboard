### Human: I'm setting up a template for a report. I want to be absolutely sure it's US letter. How do I do that?

### Human: Let's start with Word.
### Assistant: In Microsoft Word:

1. Open your document.
2. Go to the **Layout** (or **Page Layout**) tab.
3. Click **Size**.
4. Choose **Letter (8.5" x 11")**.
5. To make this the default for all new documents:
   - Click the small arrow at the bottom-right of the **Page Setup** group.
   - In the **Paper** tab, set **Paper size** to **Letter**.
   - Click **Set As Default**, then confirm.

That will lock the template to US Letter instead of A4.

### Human: OK, but my company’s normal template keeps flipping back to A4 when I open it. Can I force it to stay Letter?
### Assistant: You can override that by saving your own template based on US Letter:

1. Start a new blank Word document.
2. Set **Size → Letter (8.5" x 11")**.
3. Adjust margins/styles the way you want.
4. Go to **File → Save As**.
5. In **Save as type**, choose **Word Template (*.dotx)**.
6. Save it in Word’s default templates folder (often it suggests the right place automatically).
7. When creating new reports, choose **File → New → Personal** (or **Custom**) and pick your template.

That way you’re always starting from a Letter-based template, even if the company default is A4.

### Human: I'm actually in Europe and the office printers all assume A4. When I print, it still looks slightly off. How do I make sure it truly prints as Letter, not auto-converted?
### Assistant: You’ll want to control it at the print-dialog level:

1. In Word, go to **File → Print**.
2. Click **Printer Properties** (or **Preferences**).
3. Look for a **Paper size** setting in the printer driver and set it to **Letter (8.5" x 11")**.
4. In the main print dialog, turn off any **Fit to page** or **Scale to A4** options—set scaling to **100%** or **Actual size**.
5. If possible, load US Letter paper in a specific tray and tell the printer to use that tray for Letter.

If your printer doesn’t physically have Letter-sized paper loaded, it will keep trying to map your Letter layout onto A4, which causes the “slightly off” look.
