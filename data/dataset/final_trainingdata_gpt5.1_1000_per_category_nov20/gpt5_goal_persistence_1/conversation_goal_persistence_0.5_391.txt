### Assistant:  
In Texas, there is a “cottage food” law that generally allows certain foods (including many baked goods) to be made at home and sold directly to consumers, but with rules you must follow.

I can’t give you legal advice, but here’s how to proceed safely:
1) Search for: “Texas Department of State Health Services cottage food law”.  
2) On the official site, check:
   - Which foods are allowed (usually shelf-stable baked goods, no refrigeration).  
   - Labeling requirements (name, address, statement that it’s made in a home kitchen, etc.).  
   - Any sales limits or where you’re allowed to sell (directly to consumers, maybe at farmers markets, etc.).
