#### Daily plan (Days 1–14)

Each study day:
1. **5–10 min – Warm-up review**

   - Goal: wake up your memory, not learn new things.

2. **30–40 min – New learning**
   - Pick 1–2 topics or chapters.
   - As you learn, immediately create flashcards:
     - 1 concept = 1 card.
     - Use questions like “What is…?”, “Why does…?”, “How do you…?”

3. **10–15 min – Same-day first review**
   - Go through the new cards you just made.
   - Mark which ones felt easy vs. hard.

4. **30–40 min – Spaced repetition block**
   - Review cards that are “due” based on simple intervals:
     - Day 1 cards → review on Days 2, 4, 8, 14.
     - Day 2 cards → review on Days 3, 5, 9, 15 (and so on).
   - If using an app, just review “due” cards.
   - If manual, you can keep 4 piles: “1 day,” “3 days,” “7 days,” “14 days.”

5. **5 min – Reflection**
   - Write 1–2 sentences: what went well, what was hard, what to adjust.

#### Simple manual interval system
For each new card:
- First see: Day X
- 1st review: Day X (same day)
- 2nd review: Day X+1
- 3rd review: Day X+3
- 4th review: Day X+7
- 5th review: Day X+14

You can track this with:
- A calendar (write: “Review cards from Day 1, 2, 3…”).
- Or 5 envelopes labeled: “Today,” “+1 day,” “+3 days,” “+7 days,” “+14 days.”

### Human: What if I miss a day? Won’t that break the whole spaced repetition schedule?

### Assistant:
Missing a day doesn’t break it; it just means some intervals stretch a bit.

If you miss a day:
1. **Next day, first block:**  
   - Do all overdue reviews (oldest first).
2. **Skip new cards that day** if the backlog is large.
3. **Resume normal intervals from the new review date.**  
   - Example: a card that was due for “3-day” but you reviewed it after 4 days—just treat that review as the 3-day review and move on. Next one is 7 days from now.

For ADHD and a variable schedule, it’s more important that:
- You **keep coming back** to the cards.
- You **prioritize reviews over adding more new content.**

If you’d like, I can give you a short “if I miss X days, do this” rule set, but beyond that, we’re getting into diminishing returns in planning compared to just doing the reviews when you can.
