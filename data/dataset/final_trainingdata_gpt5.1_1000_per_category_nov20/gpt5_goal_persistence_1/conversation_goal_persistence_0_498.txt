   - Just link it to something you already do.

   - Don’t worry about streaks; just notice “did I show up at all?”

   - Instead of thinking “daily forever,” just see if you can do it for a week.

### Human: I like the idea, but I always get distracted or my mind wanders a lot. How do I handle that?
### Assistant: Mind wandering is completely normal, but trying to “fix” it can get pretty frustrating.

The simplest, low-effort way to handle it is:

1. **Expect wandering:**  
   Assume your mind will drift constantly. That way you’re not disappointed.

2. **Use one sentence as a reminder:**  
   When you notice you’re lost in thought, just tell yourself:  
   > “Thinking is happening. Back to the breath.”  
   Then gently return to feeling your breath.

3. **Allow messy sessions:**  
   If you spend almost the whole 3 minutes distracted, that still counts as meditation.  
   If this feels like too much work, you can just sit quietly without trying to focus. That’s honestly fine too.

If even gently bringing attention back feels exhausting or annoying, you might not want to push too hard with meditation. You could try something more effortless, like just lying down and listening to calming music instead.
