     - You might see **approval rules** or **approval groups** on the right.

   - Once added as a reviewer, the user:
     - Gets a notification, depending on their notification settings.
     - Sees the MR under their “Review requested” or similar section in GitLab.

In short: you open the MR, use the **Reviewers** field in the right sidebar to pick one or more team members, and GitLab handles the notifications and tracking of their review status.

How they relate:

  - You might assign some people as reviewers for feedback.
  - Only certain people (like team leads or code owners) are actually configured as required approvers.

So, you use **Reviewers** to decide *who should look at this MR* and **Approvals** to enforce *who must give a formal green light* before merging.
