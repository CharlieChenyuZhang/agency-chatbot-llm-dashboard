**Energy**

  - You move more (even a bit).
  - You slightly improve sleep quality.
  - You reduce heavy late-night snacking.

**Blood pressure**
- Can start improving in **a few weeks to a few months**, especially with:
  - Weight loss
  - Regular movement
  - Better sleep
  - Lower sodium and less processed food

The main thing: don’t judge the process by just the **scale**. Also track:
- How many walks you did.
- How many nights you kept to your snack and phone rules.
- How you feel in the morning (e.g., 1–10 energy rating).
