#### Weeks 1–4: Hypertrophy / Volume

**Goal:** Build muscle, improve work capacity, groove technique.

**Progressive overload:**  
- Start week 1 with ~3–4 reps in reserve (RIR).  
- Each week, add a bit of load OR 1 rep per set, ending week 4 around 1–2 RIR.

**Example week (outline)**

**Day 1 – Squat focus**
- Back Squat: 4×8 @ ~3 RIR, add small weight each week if you hit all reps.  
- Romanian Deadlift: 3×8–10  
- Walking Lunges: 3×10/leg  
- Leg Curl: 3×12–15  
- Standing Calf Raise: 3×12–15

**Day 2 – Push (shoulder-friendly)**
- Flat or Slight-Incline Bench Press: 4×8  
- Dumbbell Incline Press (light angle): 3×8–10  
- Machine / Cable Chest Press: 3×10–12  
- Lateral Raises: 3×12–15  
- Triceps Pressdowns: 3×10–12

Use neutral grips and avoid deep overhead pressing for your shoulder.

**Day 3 – Hinge focus**
- Deadlift (conventional or sumo): 4×6  
- Front Squat or Leg Press: 3×8–10  
- Hip Thrust: 3×10–12  
- Back Extension: 3×12–15  
- Seated Calf Raise: 3×12–15

**Day 4 – Pull**
- Pull-Ups or Lat Pulldown: 4×6–8  
- Barbell or Chest-Supported Row: 4×8  
- Cable Row: 3×10–12  
- Face Pulls: 3×12–15  
- Biceps Curls: 3×10–12

#### Weeks 5–8: Strength / Intensity

**Goal:** Push heavier loads, slightly lower reps.

**Progressive overload:**  
- Use 3–6 reps on main lifts.  
- Start week 5 around 3 RIR, end week 8 around 1 RIR.  
- Add small weight each week if you complete all sets/reps.

**Changes:**
- Main lifts: 5×3–5 or 4×4–6  
- Secondary lifts: 3×6–8  
- Accessories: 2–3×8–12 to maintain muscle

Example for squats during this phase:  
- Week 5: 4×5 @ ~75–77.5%  
- Week 6: 4×4–5 @ ~77.5–80%  
- Week 7: 5×3–4 @ ~80–82.5%  
- Week 8: 5×3 @ ~82.5–85%

(Same idea for bench and deadlift, adjusting to your actual numbers.)

---

#### Weeks 9–11: Peak & consolidation

**Goal:** Lift relatively heavier, reduce volume to stay fresh.

- Main lifts: 3–4×2–4 reps, 80–90%, leaving 1–2 RIR.  
- Assistance: 2–3 light sets per movement to maintain.

Example squat progression:  
- Week 9: 4×3 @ ~82.5–85%  
- Week 10: 3×2–3 @ ~85–87.5%  
- Week 11: 3×2 @ ~87.5–90%

You should feel strong, not wrecked. If you feel beat up, hold the weight steady or even drop slightly.

---

#### Week 12: Deload & optional testing

- Cut volume ~50% on all lifts.  
- Use ~60–70% of your usual working weights for easy sets of 2–5 reps.  
- If you want to test: do it at the end of the week on squat, bench, and deadlift (or rep maxes like 3–5RM instead of true 1RMs).
