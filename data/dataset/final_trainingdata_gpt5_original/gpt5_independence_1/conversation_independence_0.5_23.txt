1) Chickpea tikka masala (6 servings)
- Sauté 1 diced onion in 1 tbsp oil until soft. Add 3 minced garlic cloves and 1 tsp grated ginger, 1–2 tsp garam masala (or 1 tsp cumin + 1 tsp paprika if no garam), 1/2 tsp chili powder; cook 30 seconds.
- Add 1 can crushed tomatoes and 2 cans drained chickpeas; simmer 10 minutes.
- Stir in 1/2 can coconut milk and 1/2 cup plant yogurt. Simmer 5 more minutes. Adjust with lemon and a pinch of salt as needed.
- Serve over rice. Make-ahead: freezes well up to 2 months.

2) Black bean burrito bowls (4–6 servings)
- Roast sliced peppers/onions at 425°F with 1 tbsp oil, cumin, paprika (20 minutes).
- Warm 2 cans black beans with 1/2 cup broth, cumin, and a squeeze of lime. Lightly mash some beans for creaminess.
- Build bowls: rice, beans, roasted peppers, corn, avocado, chopped green onion, parsley. Drizzle: mix 1/2 cup plant yogurt + lime juice + pinch cumin. Make-ahead: keep avocado and drizzle separate until serving.

3) Tempeh stir-fry with broccoli, carrots, snap peas (4 servings)
- Slice tempeh into thin rectangles. Steam or simmer in water 5 minutes (removes bitterness). Pat dry.
- Sauce (low sodium): 2 tbsp low-sodium tamari, 1 tbsp lime juice, 1 tsp maple or brown sugar, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 minced garlic clove, water to thin.
- Sear tempeh in 1 tbsp oil until golden. Remove. Stir-fry veggies 4–5 minutes; return tempeh; add sauce; toss 1–2 minutes.
- Serve over quinoa. Make-ahead: keep sauce separate to avoid sogginess.

4) Lentil bolognese (6 servings)
- Sauté 1 diced onion + 2 diced carrots in 1 tbsp oil until soft. Add 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp Italian herb blend (or oregano), black pepper.
- Add 1 cup dry lentils (rinsed), 1 can crushed tomatoes, 2 cups low-sodium broth. Simmer 30–35 minutes until lentils tender. Finish with a splash of plant yogurt if you want creaminess and a squeeze of lemon.
- Toss with whole-wheat pasta or serve over quinoa. Make-ahead: freezes well.

5) Sheet-pan roasted veggies with tahini-lemon (4–5 servings)
- Toss 3 cups sweet potato cubes, 3 cups cauliflower florets, 2 sliced zucchini with 1–2 tbsp oil, cumin, paprika. Roast at 425°F for 25–30 minutes.
- Sauce: whisk 3 tbsp tahini + juice of 1 lemon + 1 small grated garlic clove + water to thin. Season with pepper and a small pinch of salt.
- Serve over quinoa; add parsley slaw (shredded cabbage + parsley + lemon + splash of vinegar). Make-ahead: keep sauce separate.

Overnight oats (per jar)
- 1/2 cup oats + 1/2 cup unsweetened plant milk + 1/4 cup plant yogurt + 1 tsp chia + fruit or 1 tbsp peanut butter + cinnamon. Refrigerate overnight. Keeps 4–5 days.

### Assistant: Shelf life and freezing guidance

Refrigerator (in airtight containers, 34–40°F)
- Chickpea tikka masala: 4–5 days
- Lentil bolognese (sauce): 5 days; pasta 3 days (better store sauce and pasta separate)
- Tempeh stir-fry: 3–4 days (keep sauce separate if possible)
- Burrito bowl components: rice/beans/roasted peppers 4–5 days; avocado and yogurt-lime drizzle day-of
- Roasted veggies: 4 days; tahini sauce 5–6 days
- Overnight oats: 4–5 days
- Hummus (if making): 5–6 days

Freezer (up to 2–3 months; thaw overnight)
- Chickpea tikka masala: yes
- Lentil bolognese: yes (sauce only freezes best)
- Cooked rice and quinoa: yes (portion in flat bags)
- Roasted veggies: can freeze but texture softens; best used in bowls or mashed
- Black beans: yes (drain well before freezing)
- Tempeh stir-fry: not ideal (veg texture softens); if needed, freeze cooked tempeh and sauce separately

Reheating tips
- Saucy dishes: stovetop on low with a splash of water/broth.
- Rice/quinoa: microwave with a damp paper towel to re-steam.
- Avoid boiling yogurt-based sauces; warm gently to prevent curdling.
- Add fresh elements (lime, parsley, avocado) after reheating.
