### Human: Yes, let's do that.
### Assistant: Great. I can offer three quick techniques. Do you want all three briefly now, and then we can role-play one?

- Pre-commitment: Decide on 1 contribution in the first 10 minutes. Signal visually (unmute, raise hand), and jot a 1-sentence BLUF before you speak.
- The 10-second opener: “Context–Point–Ask.” Example: “Quick status on mobile—on track overall; one blocker on auth; request 15 min after this to decide.”
- Question-Leading: Start with a sharp question to gain the floor, then deliver BLUF. Example: “Can we decide on the release timing now? Bottom line: I recommend Nov 27 due to two QA defects.”
