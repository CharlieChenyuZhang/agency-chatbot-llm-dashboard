### Human: Suggest leak patterns to look for.
### Assistant: Common patterns to confirm:
- Long-lived caches/maps not evicting (keys grow unbounded).
- Global arrays accumulating requests or results.

- LRU caches with too-large item size or disabled eviction.
- Promises never resolved/rejected, holding references.
