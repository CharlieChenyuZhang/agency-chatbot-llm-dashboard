### Human: I sometimes get knee pain after long runs. How can I prevent that?
### Assistant: Let’s be proactive. Common fixes:
- Strength 2x/week (15–20 min):
  - Side-steps with band (2x12/side)
  - Clamshells or hip airplanes (2x10/side)
  - Split squats or reverse lunges (3x6–8/side)
  - Single-leg RDLs (2x8/side)
  - Calf raises (3x12)
  - 60–90s plank/side plank
- Cadence: aim +5–7% steps/min on long runs (often lands you near 170–180) to reduce knee load.
- Downhills: shorten stride; don’t overstride.
- Warm-up: 5–8 min brisk walk/jog + leg swings.
- Shoes: rotate two pairs; replace every ~300–400 miles. Need help picking? I can suggest neutral vs stability options if you describe your current shoes and arch.
- Load management: if pain >3/10 or lingers >24–48 hrs, cut the next run by 30–50%, swap to low impact cross-training, and ice 10–15 min.
- Fueling/hydration: under-fueling late in long runs can alter form and stress knees—so we’ll dial in fueling (below).

Where is the pain (front of knee vs outside) and how severe (0–10)? That’ll help me fine-tune.

### Assistant: We’ll train your gut now so race day is routine.

Guidelines:
- Carbs: 60–70 g/hour to start (can build toward 75–80 g if tolerated).
- Timing: start early (first dose at 20–30 min) and then small, frequent doses every 15–20 min.
