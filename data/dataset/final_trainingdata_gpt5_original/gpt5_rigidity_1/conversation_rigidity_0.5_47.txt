### Human: If I miss a day, what then?
### Assistant: You’re aiming for no misses, but here’s a recovery protocol if it happens:

- Same-day salvage: if it’s before midnight, write 50 words. It counts.
- If the day truly passes:
  - Don’t double the next day; keep the routine predictable.
  - Do a 2-minute post-mortem: What broke? Time, energy, trigger, context?
  - Adjust one lever: move time, add an earlier reminder, simplify start ritual, or pre-select a prompt the night before.
  - Mark it as a “soft reset” and continue. The fastest way to recover is to protect the next day.

If you absolutely want a pure unbroken count, we can track two numbers: “Streak days” and “Total writing days.” That way one slip doesn’t nuke your overall momentum.
