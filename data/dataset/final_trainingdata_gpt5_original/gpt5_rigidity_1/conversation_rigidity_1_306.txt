### Human: Bench 185, squat 275, deadlift 335. I can train 3 days/week in a garage gym (rack, bar, plates). No injuries. Lifting ~1 year. Prefer low volume, mostly barbell. I’d like +20 lb on each in 12 weeks.
### Assistant: Got it. Here’s a 12-week, 3-day, low-volume barbell plan aimed at +20 lb each. We’ll use a weekly top single at RPE 7–8 plus low backoff volume, then peak and test.

Structure:
- Day 1: Squat + Bench (primary)
- Day 2: Deadlift + Bench (technique/speed)
- Day 3: Squat (secondary) + Deadlift (secondary)

Weeks 1–4 (build)
- Squat: 1 single @ RPE 7–8, then 3x3 @ ~80% of that day’s e1RM
- Bench: 1 single @ 7–8, then 4x4 @ 72–78%
- Deadlift: 1 single @ 7–8, then 3x3 @ ~80%
- Second exposures: 6x2 @ ~80% (focus on speed and crisp technique)
- Progression: add 2.5–5 lb to weekly singles if RPE ≤8. Backoffs auto-scale from the day’s single.

Weeks 5–8 (intensify)
- Keep singles @ 7.5–8.5
- Reduce backoffs to 3x2 @ ~82–85% (bench 5x2 @ 80–82%)
- Maintain the speed doubles day for bench; deadlift speed doubles every other week

Weeks 9–11 (peak)
- Singles @ 8–8.5; 1–2 backoff sets of 2 @ ~80%
- Cut any extra fluff. Prioritize bar speed and clean reps.

Week 12 (test)
- Work up to confident attempts: 92–95% (last warm-up), 100% (attempt 1), 102–103% (attempt 2 if bar speed is good)

Session order example:
- Day 1: Squat single + backoffs, then Bench single + backoffs
- Day 2: Deadlift single + backoffs, then Bench 6x2 speed
- Day 3: Squat 4x2 @ ~75–78% (controlled), Deadlift 3x2 @ ~75–78% (clean off floor)

If you want accessories or nutrition guidance added, say so and I’ll include only what you want.

### Human: Fair. Until then, one or two cues for each lift?
### Assistant: - Squat: Big breath and brace before unrack; sit down between your heels and drive up through mid-foot.
- Bench: Pull shoulder blades back and down; press “back and up” into the bar, keep forearms vertical at the bottom.
- Deadlift: Take slack out before breaking the floor; push the floor away and keep the bar on your legs.

### Human: If a single feels too heavy one week, what exactly do I do?
### Assistant: Do not force it. Drop the single to RPE ~7, then run backoffs from that lighter single. If bar speed is poor, reduce backoff sets by one. Resume normal progression the next week.
