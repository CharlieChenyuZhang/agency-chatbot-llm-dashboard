### Assistant: Per your instruction, here is a concise summary based only on Wikipedia:

- What it is: CRISPR-Cas9 is a genome-editing system adapted from a bacterial antiviral defense. It uses a guide RNA to direct the Cas9 nuclease to a matching DNA sequence.
- How it works: Cas9 recognizes a short PAM sequence, binds the target DNA via the guide RNA, and creates a double-strand break. Cells repair the break via NHEJ (often causing small insertions/deletions) or HDR (allowing precise edits with a repair template).
- Origins: The CRISPR system was characterized in bacteria and archaea; in 2012 researchers demonstrated programmable editing in eukaryotic cells. 
- Main applications:
  - Research: gene knockouts/knock-ins, functional genomics, disease modeling.
  - Medicine: experimental somatic therapies under study/trials for genetic diseases and cancers.
  - Agriculture: crop trait engineering and disease resistance.
  - Biotechnology/diagnostics: CRISPR-based nucleic acid detection platforms.
