### Human: Embed my Google Calendar (read-only) next to the Tasks Calendar view.
### Assistant: To embed a read-only Google Calendar:
1) In Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → Access permissions → make available to anyone with the link (read-only), or get the “Public URL to this calendar” under Integrate calendar.
2) In your “Tasks & Notes” page, add an Embed block and paste the public calendar link.
3) Place the embed next to the Tasks database Calendar view using a 2-column layout.

Note: Google Calendar must be public for the embed to display in Notion.
