### Assistant: Think of a limit as what your eyes would read on a zoomed-in graph as you approach a point along the curve. Far away the path might wobble, but as you zoom, the curve near that point looks straighter and calmer. The limit is the single height the curve settles on as you sneak up along allowed paths. If approaching from the left and right gives the same height, the limit exists. If the heights split, there’s no single story. A hole in the curve doesn’t spoil the limit if the surrounding shape still narrows to one height. Continuity means the limit’s height and the actual point’s height match.
