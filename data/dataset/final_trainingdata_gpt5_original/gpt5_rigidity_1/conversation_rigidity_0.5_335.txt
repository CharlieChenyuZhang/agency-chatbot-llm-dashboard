Rule of thumb:
- If your only regular use is those two NJ trips, car-share/rentals are usually cheaper than owning.
- If you’ll also do weekly Costco runs, kid activities, and frequent cross-borough visits, the convenience of owning can start to justify the cost—especially if you find a reliable street spot and avoid tickets.

Middle-ground alternative:
- Keep no car for the first 2–3 months. Track actual spending on Zipcar/rentals/rideshare. If that total approaches ~$400–600/month consistently, revisit owning.
