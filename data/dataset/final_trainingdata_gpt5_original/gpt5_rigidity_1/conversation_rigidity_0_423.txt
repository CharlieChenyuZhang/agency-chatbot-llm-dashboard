### Human: Fine. If I stick to five days, how do I progress loads each week?
### Assistant: My preference is a simple double-progression:
- Hypertrophy slots (8–12 reps): Hit the top of the rep range at RIR 1–2, then add the smallest load next week and repeat. If you end up below the rep floor after adding load, climb reps again before adding more weight.
- Strength slots (4–6 reps): Add 2.5–5% when all sets land at target reps with RIR 1–2. If not, keep the load and aim to add 1 rep total next week.
- Deadlifts and RDLs: Progress every other week; add reps first, load second.
- Deload weeks: Drop set count 30–50%, keep bar speed snappy, RIR ~3; resume prior loads post-deload.

- Keep a knee-friendly split squat (shorter stride, upright torso).
- Add terminal knee extensions or Spanish squats as primers.

Keep the same progression rules and RIR. Quality reps beat exercise dogma.
