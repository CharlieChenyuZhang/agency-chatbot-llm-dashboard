Post 2 — Scoping a small UX project
I like to start small. Here’s a simple way to scope a tidy UX project you can finish in a week. Pick one outcome that matters, like more sign‑ups or fewer drop‑offs. Write the current steps on a single page. Mark the three spots where people hesitate. Choose one of those spots. Draft the simplest change you could ship in a few days. Share it with one teammate and one customer. If both understand it without extra notes, you’re close. Ship it, measure one number, and decide the next step. Small, clear steps beat big, messy plans.
