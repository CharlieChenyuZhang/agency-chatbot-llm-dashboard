### Human: Can you guess my ideal CAC?
### Assistant: I won’t guess. Share AOV, site conversion rate, expected ad CTR and CPM, and click‑to‑purchase conversion, and I’ll estimate CAC.

### Human: AOV £85, site CR 2%, CTR 1.5%, CPM £15, click-to-purchase 2.5%.
### Assistant: Estimate:
- CPC ≈ CPM / (1000 × CTR) = £15 / (1000 × 0.015) ≈ £1.00
- CAC ≈ CPC / CVR-to-purchase = £1.00 / 0.025 ≈ £40

At £40 CAC and £85 AOV, pre-shipping/COGS margin must cover the gap. To hit £25 CAC, aim to improve:
- CTR to ~2.4% (CPC ~£0.63), or
- CVR to ~4% (CAC ~£25), or both.
