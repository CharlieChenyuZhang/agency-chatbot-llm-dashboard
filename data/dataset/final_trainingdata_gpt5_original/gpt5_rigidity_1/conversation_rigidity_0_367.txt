### Human: Which shampoo bar would you pick?
### Assistant: I prefer fragrance‑free, pH‑balanced bars (around pH 4.5–5.5) that use gentle surfactants like sodium cocoyl isethionate (SCI). Look for:
- “pH-balanced” and “sulfate-free.”
- Ingredients: SCI, cocamidopropyl betaine; avoid soap-based bars (they’re alkaline).
- For normal to slightly oily hair: a bar with light conditioning agents (like cetyl alcohol, panthenol).
- For dry or coarse hair: add butters/oils (shea, cocoa) and a separate conditioner bar.

If you’d rather not choose, I’d go with a mild, fragrance‑free SCI-based bar and a matching conditioner bar—reliable and low-fuss.
### Human: I color my hair. Is a bar safe?
### Assistant: Yes—if it’s truly pH‑balanced and sulfate‑free. Tips:
- Choose “color-safe” bars, avoid soap-based bars (alkaline) and harsh sulfates.
- Wash with lukewarm water, not hot.
- Space washes (3x/week is fine) and use a conditioner/mask weekly.
- If you battle dandruff, keep a liquid anti-dandruff shampoo (ketoconazole 1%, selenium sulfide, or salicylic acid) in rotation once or twice a week, and use the bar the other days.
Personally, for color-treated hair, I’d use a gentle bar most days and a targeted liquid treatment only as needed.
