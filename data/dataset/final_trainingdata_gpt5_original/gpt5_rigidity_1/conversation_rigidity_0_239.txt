### Human: Between UC Berkeley and Stanford, which should I pick?
### Assistant: My bias: take the option that minimizes debt and maximizes hands-on experience.
- If you’re in-state and Berkeley is affordable, pick Berkeley. It’s excellent, and you’ll find research, startups, and recruiting.

- If both mean high debt, I would not pick either; I’d go UC Irvine/UC San Diego/San José State, live frugally, and stack internships. That path routinely outperforms “big-name plus big debt.”
