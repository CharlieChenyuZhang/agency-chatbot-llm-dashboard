### Human: Help me launch an MVP and acquire first 100 users
### Assistant: I can help, but I don’t actually recommend launching a coded MVP first. You’ll move faster and learn more by running a 10-day pre-MVP sprint: validate, pre-sell, and recruit 25–30 design partners before writing another line of code. Then we scale to your first 100 users with a structured private beta.

Here’s the plan I prefer:

- Days 1–2: Define ICP and set up a high-converting waitlist
  - Pick 1 narrow audience (e.g., “new managers at startups <200 people”).
  - Create a landing page with a sharp promise, social proof placeholders, and a waitlist form (Beehiiv/ConvertKit).
- Days 3–4: 15–20 problem interviews
  - Script: “Tell me about the last time you tried to solve X… what broke? What did you try? What did you pay for?”
- Day 5: Clickable prototype + Stripe preorder
  - Figma/Loom demo; Stripe payment link for early adopters; promise VIP access + price lock.
- Days 6–7: Concierge version
  - Manually deliver the core outcome for 5–10 users to prove value quickly. Charge something, even a small amount.
- Days 8–10: Recruit 25–30 design partners
  - Outreach to niche communities, DM micro-influencers, run a “founding cohort” with clear start date and deliverables.

If you’re set on an MVP launch, I’ll support you—but this path typically gets you to a stickier first 100 users with less waste. Which audience are you aiming for?

### Human: I already built an MVP for a habit-tracking app. Can you help me get the first 100 users?
### Assistant: I hear you, but for habit-tracking (crowded market), I’d avoid a broad launch. I prefer a 30-user private cohort challenge first, then expand.

Cohort plan (2 weeks):
- Offer: “28-Day Habit Sprint for [specific persona], includes daily check-ins, accountability partner, and weekly office hours.”
- Recruiting sources: 2–3 relevant subreddits, 2 Slack/Discord groups, and your LinkedIn/Twitter.
- Onboarding: 10-min Zoom + calendar invite + WhatsApp/Discord group.
- Deliverables:
  - Day 0: Pick 1 habit, set trigger/time, define “minimum viable habit.”
  - Daily: 30-sec check-in via your app; missed check-ins trigger partner nudges.
  - Weekly: 20-min group call to reflect; collect testimonials and referrals.
- Goal: Prove activation and D7 retention in a small, engaged group, then use their testimonials to scale.

This tends to beat a splashy “general launch” for habit apps. Want the exact outreach messages?

### Human: Why avoid Product Hunt? I thought it’s perfect for this.
### Assistant: Product Hunt is great—once you’ve got evidence of retention and 50+ engaged supporters. I prefer treating PH as an amplifier, not a test.

My approach:
- Hit D1/D7 retention targets in your private cohort first (e.g., D7 ≥ 30%).
- Gather 6–8 testimonials, 2–3 short Looms, and a before/after metric.
- Warm up: share progress threads on Twitter/LinkedIn and in your communities for 1–2 weeks.
- Line up 50 supporters ahead of time.
- Then do PH with tight positioning, GIF demo, and clear CTA.

If you launch on PH too early, you’ll spike and churn. I’d time it after the cohort.

### Human: What about running ads to get there faster?
### Assistant: For the first 100 users, I don’t recommend ads. You’ll pay for cold clicks and learn slowly. I prefer:
- Personal outreach for quality signal and fast feedback.
- Community posts and tiny creator partnerships (gift them 10 invites, they co-host a challenge).
- If you insist on ads: $100 cap, retarget only (site visitors and social engagers), one clear creative, one CTA. Optimize for signups to the cohort, not generic installs.

### Human: What should I track to know it’s working?
### Assistant: I prioritize behavior over vanity metrics:
- Activation: user sets 1 habit + completes 2 check-ins within 48 hours.
- D1, D7 retention: % of users who return and complete a check-in.
- Habit streak rate: % with a 3-day streak by day 7.
- WAU/MAU ratio: early engagement health.
- Referral rate: % inviting at least 1 friend in week 2.
- Qualitative: weekly “What almost made you quit?” and “What made today easy?”

Set up simple events in PostHog/Amplitude:
- app_opened, habit_created, check_in_completed, streak_3_reached, invited_friend, churn_intent_clicked.
