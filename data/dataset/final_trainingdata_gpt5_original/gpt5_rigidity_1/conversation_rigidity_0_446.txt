### Human: What about deadlift specifically? I pull conventional. Should I switch to sumo to chase a PR?
### Assistant: I don’t want you switching stances just to chase a quick PR. Stick with conventional as your comp pattern. We’ll use sumo or block pulls as hypertrophy/position tools, not as your test lift.

For deadlift weak links, I prefer:
- Paused deadlifts (1–2 sec below knee) for position
- Snatch-grip RDLs for back/hamstrings
- Beltless conventional from a slight deficit for start strength
- Heavy upper-back volume (rows, pull-ups) to keep your position

### Human: Okay. How do I know I’m actually getting stronger if I’m not testing singles?
### Assistant: We’ll track progress through objective proxies:
- Estimated 1RM from submax sets (e.g., 4–6 rep top sets) using reliable RPE tables
- Rep PRs at the same load and rep range
- Bar speed (if you have a velocity device, great; if not, video and tempo consistency)
- Total weekly volume and how it feels at a given RPE
If those are trending up, your 1RM will be there when we peak.

### Human: What should nutrition look like? I’m not trying to gain a ton of fat.
### Assistant: I prefer a small surplus, not a bulk. Target 200–300 kcal/day above maintenance.
- Protein: 1.6–2.2 g/kg
- Carbs: Center around training, 3–6 g/kg depending on volume
- Fats: Fill the rest, 0.6–1 g/kg ceiling
- Creatine monohydrate 3–5 g/day; skip flashy pre-workouts—caffeine + salt + carbs is enough
- Hydration: 5–7 ml/kg water pre-lift, plus electrolytes if you sweat a lot
