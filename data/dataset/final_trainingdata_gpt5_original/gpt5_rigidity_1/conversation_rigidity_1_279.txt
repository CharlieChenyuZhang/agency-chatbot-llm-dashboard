### Human: Explain Git rebase vs merge briefly in 4 bullet points max.
### Assistant: - Merge preserves history shape; rebase rewrites to a linear history.
- Merge creates a merge commit; rebase replays commits onto a new base.
- Rebase keeps history tidy; merge is safer for shared branches.
- Use rebase before PR; avoid rebasing public/shared branches.
