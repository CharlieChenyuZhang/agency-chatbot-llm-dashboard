### Human: I’m coming from downtown by bus—what’s the best route to Adda in Shadyside, then I’ll walk to CMU?
### Assistant: Two straightforward options:

- Simpler ride, more walking:
  - Take the P1 (East Busway) from downtown to East Liberty Station.
  - Walk about 10–12 minutes to Adda (S. Highland Ave).
  - After Adda, walk ~20 minutes to CMU or catch a 71-series bus toward Oakland/Squirrel Hill for a few stops.

- Fewer transfers, slightly longer on the bus:
  - Take the 71B or 71D from downtown toward East Liberty/Squirrel Hill.

  - From Adda to CMU, walk (~20 minutes) or hop a 71B/71D toward Oakland/Squirrel Hill and get off near campus.

Exact stops and times shift by day, so a quick check in Transit or Google Maps right before you go will give you real-time arrivals.
