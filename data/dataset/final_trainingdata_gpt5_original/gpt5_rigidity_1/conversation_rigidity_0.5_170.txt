### Human: Can I get a free custom domain?
### Assistant: A truly free custom domain forever isn’t realistic. Options:
- Many hosts include a free domain for the first year with hosting plans.
- Choose a low-cost TLD: .site, .blog, .page, etc., often a few dollars first year.
- Use a subdomain (yourname.host.com) for free, but that’s not a true custom domain.

I’d recommend using the host’s first-year-free domain deal, then budget around $10–$15/year after that.

### Human: Yes, but before that—how do I handle photos legally?
### Assistant: Good call.
- Best: Use your own photos; you own the rights.
- Stock options:
  - CC0 libraries (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay). Check each image license; avoid logos/trademarks.
  - Creative Commons on Flickr: confirm the license, and give proper attribution if required.
- Avoid: Random Google Images—licenses are unclear.
- Workflow tips:
  - Resize before upload (e.g., 1600px wide), compress (ShortPixel/Imagify).
  - Add descriptive alt text for accessibility and SEO.
  - If people are recognizable, avoid commercial use without permission; public places are generally fine for editorial use.

### Human: I’d like analytics without cookies if possible.
### Assistant: Great for privacy and simplicity.

- In Plausible, enable “Outbound links” and “404s” events.

If you prefer free and easy, Cloudflare Web Analytics is a good start; we can add it once your domain’s on Cloudflare.
