### Human: I want offline backups to my NAS, not Backblaze.
### Assistant: NAS-only is risky if you misconfigure or get hit by ransomware. Best is 3-2-1: local drive + NAS + Backblaze. If you won’t add Backblaze:
- Use a 2-bay NAS with RAID1 for redundancy (not backup)
- Nightly Time Machine from Mac to NAS
- Weekly clone to an external drive with Carbon Copy Cloner; keep it disconnected between runs
- Quarterly cold backup to a drive stored offsite
Backblaze is still the simplest offsite layer. I’d enable it.
