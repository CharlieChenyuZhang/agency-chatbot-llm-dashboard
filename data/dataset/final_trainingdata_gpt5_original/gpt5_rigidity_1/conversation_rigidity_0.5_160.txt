### Human: Great. How do I price the bundles?
### Assistant: Use a simple margin-first approach, then sanity-check against competitors.

Steps
1) Calculate landed cost per bundle:
- Sum product costs + packaging + average shipping you’ll pay + payment fees (~2.9% + $0.30).
2) Set target gross margin before ads: 60–70%.
3) Choose a price that hits the margin and fits your AOV goal ($45–$60 to start).
4) Offer small upsells (e.g., compostable sponge 2-pack) to lift AOV by $5–$10.

Example
- Contents cost: $18
- Packaging: $1.50
- Average shipping you cover: $5.50 (or charge $4.99 shipping to offset)
- Payment fee on $54 price: ~$1.85
- Variable cost total: ~$26.85 (if you charge $4.99 shipping to customer)
- Price at $54 → gross margin ≈ (54 − 26.85) / 54 ≈ 50% (pre-ad)
- To reach ~60–65%, either:
  - Increase price to $59–$62, or
  - Reduce costs (negotiate supplier, lighter packaging), or
  - Charge shipping and offer free shipping at $65+ to push larger carts.

Quick tests
- Price test A: $54 bundle + $4.99 shipping + free ship at $65+.
- Price test B: $59 bundle free shipping.
Choose the winner by conversion rate, AOV, and contribution margin after ad costs.

### Human: What legal steps do I need in the US?
### Assistant: I’m not a lawyer, but here’s a common, budget-friendly checklist to discuss with a pro or follow carefully:

- Choose structure: LLC is common for liability protection; sole prop is fastest/cheapest to start (can upgrade to LLC later).
- Get an EIN (IRS, free) and open a business bank account.
- Sales tax: register for your state’s sales tax permit; set up Shopify tax collection.
- Business license: check city/county requirements.
- Terms/Privacy/Refund policies: use a reputable template and customize; ensure privacy policy covers email/text marketing.
- Insurance: consider general liability/product liability once orders scale.
- Eco claims: avoid vague “eco-friendly” claims; be specific and accurate (e.g., “compostable in industrial facilities,” “made from 70% recycled materials”) to avoid greenwashing issues.
- Packaging/labeling: comply with FTC Green Guides and any material claims.
