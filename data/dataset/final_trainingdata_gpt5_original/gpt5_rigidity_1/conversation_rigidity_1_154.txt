Buttermilk Pancakes (makes ~8 pancakes)

Ingredients:
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
- 1 cup buttermilk, at room temperature
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled, plus more for the pan

Directions:
1) Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
2) In a separate bowl, whisk the buttermilk, egg, and melted butter.
3) Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and stir until just combined (batter will be lumpy). Let the batter rest for 10 minutes.
4) Heat a nonstick skillet over medium heat and lightly butter it.
5) For each pancake, pour 1/4 cup batter into the skillet. Cook until bubbles form and edges look set, 2–3 minutes. Flip and cook 1–2 minutes more.
6) Serve immediately.
