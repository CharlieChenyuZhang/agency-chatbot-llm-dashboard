### Human: I’d rather do airport pickup even if it costs more. No additional driver. What’s the deal with insurance—do I need it?
### Assistant: Short version:
- Collision/Loss Damage Waiver (LDW/CDW): Covers damage/theft to the rental car. If you have personal auto insurance, it often covers rentals; many credit cards also include secondary (sometimes primary) collision coverage if you pay with the card. If you have neither, consider buying LDW.
- Liability (SLI): Covers damage/injury to others. Your personal auto policy typically extends; if you don’t have one or want higher limits, consider SLI.
- Extras (PAI/PEC, roadside): Optional. Roadside is sometimes included by the rental company or your card.

If you have personal auto + a good credit card benefit, you can usually decline LDW and SLI. If not, I’d recommend at least LDW; SLI is nice to have if you lack your own liability coverage.
