### Human: I heard the Aeron can hurt your thighs. True?
### Assistant: It can for some people, usually due to fit or setup:
- Size: At 5'10", size B is typically right. The wrong size can cause frame pressure.
- Seat height: Too high can press the seat edge into your thighs. Keep feet flat and knees slightly below hips.
- Tilt/forward posture: Sitting perched on the front edge or locking the tilt too upright can create pressure. Using a slight recline takes pressure off.
- Personal preference: The Aeron’s mesh is firm; some people prefer a cushioned seat like the Leap V2 for long sessions.

If you try an Aeron, confirm size B, adjust seat height and recline, and check how your thighs feel after a couple of hours. If you’re sensitive to front-edge pressure, the Fern or Leap may feel better.
