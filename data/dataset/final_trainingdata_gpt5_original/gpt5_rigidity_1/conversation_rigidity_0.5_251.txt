### Human: Are short-term health plans a cheaper alternative?
### Assistant: They can be cheaper, but there are important caveats:
- Often exclude preexisting conditions and can deny claims you’d expect to be covered.
- Limited benefits, no guaranteed essential health benefits, and no subsidies.
- Typically no out-of-pocket maximums in the ACA sense.
For someone healthy and budget-focused, an ACA Bronze HSA plan is usually a safer value: it’s subsidizable, covers essential benefits, and caps your maximum annual costs. I’d only consider short-term plans if you’re between coverage for a brief period and fully understand the exclusions.
