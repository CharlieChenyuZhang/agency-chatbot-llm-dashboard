### Human: Can you give me a 7-day microcycle plan with warm-up, drills, sets/reps, and a quick cool-down? Keep it to 45 minutes and knee-friendly.
### Assistant: Yes. Each day totals ~45 minutes.

Day 1 (Ball control + Form shooting)
- Warm-up 6 min: brisk walk 2 min, hip circles, ankle rocks, T-spine opens.
- Activation 4 min: mini-band lateral walks 2x15 steps/side.
- Drills 30 min:
  - Stationary ball-handling (low stance) 8 min: cross/in-out/behind-the-back, 40-sec on/20-sec off.
  - Form shooting 12 min: 1-hand wrist snaps from 3–8 ft, 10 makes/spot x3 spots; set shots only.
  - Footwork 10 min: jab-step series without shot, 5 reps per pivot/foot x2 sets.
- Cool-down 5 min: quad/hip flexor/hamstring 30 sec each, diaphragmatic breathing.

Day 2 (Finishing + Passing)
- Warm-up 5 min.
- Activation 5 min: glute bridges 2x12, calf raises 2x12.
- Drills 30 min:
  - Mikan variations (no jump) 8 min: 8 reps each side x3 sets.
  - Pound-to-push cross into power layup (no jump) 10 min: 6 reps/side x3.
  - Wall passing 12 min: chest 30, bounce 30, one-hand 20/arm; 3 rounds, rest 30 sec.
- Cool-down 5 min.

Day 3 (Defense footwork + Spot-up shooting)
- Warm-up 5 min.
- Activation 5 min: mini-band monster walks 2x15 steps.
- Drills 30 min:
  - Slide shuttle (cone at 5 m) 10 min: 15-sec slide/45-sec rest x8, shallow stance.
  - Closeout to hand-up 10 min: 6 reps/side x3 sets, controlled.
  - Spot-up set shots 10 min: 5 spots x10 attempts; record makes.
- Cool-down 5 min.

Day 4 (Recovery + Technique)
- Warm-up 5 min mobility.
- Technique 30 min:
  - Form shooting 15 min: 50 makes from 5–10 ft.
  - Footwork shadow reps 10 min: triple-threat → jab → rip-through, 5 reps/side x3 sets.
  - Balance 5 min: single-leg holds 30 sec/leg x3.
- Cool-down 5 min.

Day 5 (Game-like shooting + Decision cues)
- Warm-up 5 min.
- Activation 5 min.
- Drills 30 min:
  - Catch-ready set shots 12 min: toss to self, 5 spots x8; focus on quick set.
  - Drive-read shadow 8 min: cone defender; attack open lane line, 6 reps/side x2.
  - Passing on the move 10 min: walk-to-jog wall passes, 25/hand x3 rounds.
- Cool-down 5 min.

Day 6 (Low-impact conditioning + Handles)
- Warm-up 5 min.
- Conditioning 15 min: slide-walk intervals 30-sec on/30-sec off x10, shallow.
- Handles 20 min:
  - Stationary combo series 10 min: 40-sec on/20-sec off x10.
  - Figure-8 without dribble 10 min: 20 reps x3 sets.
- Cool-down 5 min.

Day 7 (Situational + Review)
- Warm-up 5 min.
- Situational 30 min:
  - “5-out” cuts solo 10 min: V-cut, L-cut, backdoor, 6 reps each.
  - Shot under mild fatigue 10 min: 3 makes/spot x5 spots, 15-sec rest.
  - Free throws 10 min: 25 attempts; record.
- Cool-down 5 min.
