### Human: I want to park downtown on weekdays and near UT on weekends. Is that allowed?
### Assistant: Often, but with caveats:
- Downtown/public streets: You may need a Right-of-Way vending permit and must follow distance rules (hydrants, crosswalks, doorways). Many vendors avoid this by arranging a private lot and getting a property owner authorization letter—faster and more predictable.
- UT campus: UT has its own vending policies and vendor approvals. You’ll likely need a campus-specific permit or a partnership with a campus organization. Alternatively, operate just off-campus on private property with permission.
- General: You still need your Mobile Food Vendor permit, Health Dept inspection, Fire inspection (if propane), commissary agreement, and restroom access agreement.

Best next steps
- Call City of Austin’s Development/ROW office to confirm street vending options for your target blocks.
- Shortlist 3–5 private lots downtown for weekday mornings; reach out for monthly or per-shift fees.
- Contact UT Auxiliary Services (or the campus vending office) about weekend permissions, or plan adjacent private lots on Guadalupe/West Campus.
