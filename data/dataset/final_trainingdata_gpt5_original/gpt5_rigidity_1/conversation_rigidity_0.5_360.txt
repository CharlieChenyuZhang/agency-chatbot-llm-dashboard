### Human: I use Google Sheets. Can you give me a template structure and formulas?
### Assistant: Yes. Sheet structure:
- Transactions sheet columns: Date | Merchant | Amount | Category
- Budget sheet columns:
  - A: Category
  - B: Target (your planned amount)
  - C: SpentMTD (formula)
  - D: Available (= B - C)
  - E: Locked? (TRUE for fixed/savings/debt)
  - F: Discretionary? (TRUE for dining/fun/travel/misc)
  - G: Floor (minimum you won’t cut below, e.g., Dining 100)
  - H: Need (deficit if overspent)
  - I: Surplus (donor money above floor)
  - J: Proposed Adj (suggested +/−)
  - K: Proposed Budget (= B + J)

Key formulas (example for row 2, copy down):
- SpentMTD (C2):
  =SUMIFS(Transactions!$C:$C, Transactions!$D:$D, $A2, Transactions!$A:$A, ">="&EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)+1, Transactions!$A:$A, "<="&EOMONTH(TODAY(),0))
- Available (D2): =B2 - C2
- Need (H2): =IF(E2, 0, MAX(0, -D2))  // don’t request funds for locked categories
- Surplus (I2): =IF(F2, MAX(0, D2 - G2), 0)

On a helper row (e.g., row 1 or bottom):
- TotalNeed: =SUM(H:H)
- TotalSurplus: =SUM(I:I)
- RebalanceOn? (optional): =AND(TODAY()-EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)>=10, TODAY()-EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)<=20, TotalNeed>25)

Proposed Adj (J2):
- For deficit categories:
  =IF(H2>0, -MIN(H2, H2/SUM($H:$H)*SUM($I:$I)), 0)
  (This pulls proportionally from the donor pool.)
- For donor categories:
  =IF(I2>0, -MIN(I2, I2/SUM($I:$I)*SUM($H:$H)), 0)
  (This gives up funds proportionally.)
- For locked categories with Need=0 and non-donors, J=0.

Proposed Budget (K2): =B2 + J2

This gives you a live “Proposed Budget” that rebalances mid-month without scripts. You can copy column K over B when you want to accept the changes.

### Human: I'd rather not use scripts. Can it work with pure formulas and a simple "Rebalance" button?
### Assistant: Sheets doesn’t have a true “button” without scripts, but here’s a script-free workaround:

- Set Proposed Budget (K2) to:

Now you can toggle the checkbox to preview/accept rebalancing. When you’re ready to lock it in permanently, copy column K and “Paste special > Values” over column B.
