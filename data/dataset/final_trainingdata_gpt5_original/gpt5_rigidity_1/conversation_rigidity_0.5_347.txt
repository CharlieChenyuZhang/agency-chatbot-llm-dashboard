### Human: Here’s the recipe for Japanese Cotton Cheesecake I want to follow exactly:

Ingredients:
- 250 g cream cheese
- 60 g unsalted butter
- 100 ml whole milk
- 6 large eggs, separated
- 50 g sugar for yolk batter
- 90 g sugar for meringue
- 60 g cake flour
- 20 g cornstarch
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Pinch of salt

Method:
1) Preheat oven to 160°C (320°F). Line an 8-inch round springform pan with parchment (bottom and sides). Wrap the outside with foil.
2) In a bowl over a bain-marie, melt cream cheese, butter, and milk until smooth. Cool to lukewarm. Whisk in egg yolks, then sift in cake flour and cornstarch; whisk until just smooth.

5) Place pan in a deep roasting pan and pour hot water halfway up the sides.
6) Bake 25 min at 160°C, then reduce to 140°C (285°F) and bake 55–65 min until set with a slight jiggle.
7) Turn oven off, door cracked, rest 15 min. Remove from bath, cool completely before unmolding.
