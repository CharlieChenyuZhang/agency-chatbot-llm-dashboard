### Human: Help me improve sleep quality and consistency
### Assistant: Here’s a clear, actionable plan:
- Set a consistent sleep window: choose a fixed 8-hour window and keep the same wake-up time daily; shift by ≤15–30 minutes if you must adjust.
- Create a 45–60 minute wind-down: dim lights; no work; quiet activity (paper book, light stretch, warm shower); jot tomorrow’s to‑do so your mind can let go.
- Light timing: get 10–30 minutes of outdoor light within an hour of waking; avoid bright/blue light 60–90 minutes before bed (use warm lamps).
- Caffeine/alcohol/meals: stop caffeine 8–10 hours before bed; avoid alcohol within 3 hours of bed; finish heavy meals 3+ hours before bed (light snack is fine).
- Exercise: 20–40 minutes most days; avoid intense workouts within 3 hours of bed.
- Bedroom: cool (16–19°C / 60–67°F), dark, quiet; consider blackout curtains and white noise.
- In-bed rule: bed for sleep and intimacy only; if awake ~20 minutes, get up to a calm, dim‑light activity, return when sleepy.
- Night awakenings: don’t clock‑watch; keep lights dim; do a quiet, non-stimulating activity until drowsy.
- Naps: limit to 20–30 minutes before mid‑afternoon.
- Track and tweak: keep a simple 2‑week sleep log (bedtime, wake time, awakenings, caffeine timing) and adjust one variable at a time.

### Human: I work rotating night shifts. Make the plan specific to that. Bullet points only.
### Assistant: - Anchor sleep: pick a daily anchor block (e.g., 04:00–09:00 or 08:00–13:00) you keep even on off days; add top‑up naps as needed.
- Pre‑shift rest: take a 90‑minute nap (or a 20‑minute power nap) ending 2–3 hours before the night shift.
- Light: bright light during the first half of the night shift; sunglasses on the commute home; blackout curtains and no bright light before daytime sleep.
- Caffeine: use small, frequent doses early in the shift; cut off 6–8 hours before your planned daytime sleep.
- Meals: light, easily digestible foods overnight; avoid large meals in the last 3 hours before daytime sleep.
- After-shift: consistent wind-down, cool/dark room, white noise; protect sleep with a do‑not‑disturb sign and phone on silent.
- Days off: use a compromise schedule (keep the anchor sleep and shift the rest modestly) instead of flipping entirely.
