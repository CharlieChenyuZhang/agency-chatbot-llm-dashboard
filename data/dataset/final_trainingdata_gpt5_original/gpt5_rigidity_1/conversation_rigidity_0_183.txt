### Human: It’s for a casual event tonight and I need black. Will those shrink?
### Assistant: 100% cotton budget blacks usually shrink a half size if you machine dry. That’s another reason I steer people to blends. If you must do solid black:
- Choose pre-shrunk cotton (Gildan Softstyle is better than ultra-basic tees).
- Wash cold, hang-dry the first few cycles.
- Consider sizing up if you plan to machine dry.

If you can stretch to $22, a Supima cotton black tee will feel better and fade slower—but I know you said $20, so I still think a heather/washed black blend is the smarter play.
