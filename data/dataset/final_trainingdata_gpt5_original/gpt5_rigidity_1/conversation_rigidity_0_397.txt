### Human: Can you give me a NeurIPS acceptance-critical checklist?
### Assistant: Yes. My preferred essentials:
- Claim: one clear, falsifiable contribution; avoid sprawling scope
- Baselines: strongest known methods, properly tuned; report variance
- Reproducibility: code+configs, seeds, environment; exact data splits
- Compute disclosure: hardware, time, cost; justify choices
- Robustness: sensitivity to hyperparams, client sampling, non-IID severity
- Ablations: show each method component matters
- Realism: network/energy constraints modeled or measured credibly
- Ethics/privacy: threat model, potential harms, mitigation
- Writing: conservative claims; limitations section with concrete boundaries
- Figures/tables: legible, decision-relevant, not decorative
- Pre-review: 2–3 external readers; address predictable objections in text

If any item is weak, I’d fall back to workshop first to avoid a hard reject.
