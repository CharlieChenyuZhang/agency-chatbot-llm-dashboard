Simple fee vs reward formula (Google Sheets):
- A: Bill amount
- B: Card fee rate (e.g., 0.025 for 2.5%)
- C: Reward rate value (e.g., 0.015 for 1.5% net)
- D (Net gain/loss) = A*(C - B)
If D is positive, paying with Amex is net-positive. If negative, use checking.

### Human: Okay. For Amex autopay from Chase, how do I avoid overdrafts but still pay in full?

- Make sure Chase has a buffer greater than your highest expected Amex statement. If your card spend varies, use alerts to help:
