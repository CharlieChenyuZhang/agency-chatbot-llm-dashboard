### Human: What would the costs and bandwidth look like with your preferred Backblaze B2 plan?
### Assistant: For 500 GB:
- Storage: ~500 GB x ~$0.005/GB/month ≈ $2.50/month (B2). Wasabi is similar but billed in TB units with minimums.
- Uploads: free
- Egress (restores): ~$0.01/GB on B2 when you actually download data
- Bandwidth: initial full upload ≈ 500 GB once; after that, daily incrementals are typically 0.5–2% of changed data per day (say 2–10 GB/day), often less with dedup/compression.

Compare that to weekly-only: you’ll still have a huge first upload, and your risk window (RPO) is up to 7 days. Daily incrementals tighten that to 24 hours without meaningfully increasing costs.

### Human: And encryption—how do I avoid losing access?
### Assistant: Use a passphrase you can remember plus a stored copy:
- Create the Duplicati passphrase, save it in your password manager, and export an emergency recovery kit (printed or on a secure USB in a safe)
- In Duplicati, export the backup configuration (without secrets) for documentation
- Enable MFA on your Backblaze account
If you ever lose the passphrase, the data is unrecoverable—so keep at least two secure copies of the passphrase.
