### Human: This is great. How do we measure if it worked?
### Assistant: Track both reach and real-world impact.

Core metrics
- Foot traffic proxies: weekend POS transaction count, average order value, and number of redemptions for AUSTIN10/ROLLCALL.
- IG: Reach, saves, shares, profile visits, “Get Directions” taps, Story link taps.
- TikTok: Views, 3-sec and 6-sec holds, average watch time, completion rate, profile visits.
- UGC: Number of tags/mentions per week.

Simple targets for the 2-week test
- 20–30% increase in weekend transactions vs prior 2 weekends.
- 10+ redemptions of AUSTIN10 and 15+ “Directions” taps on Sat/Sun posts.
- 15% watch-time improvement on Reels/TikToks vs your baseline.

Set up
- Use a unique code each weekend (e.g., AUSTIN10-WK1, AUSTIN10-WK2).
- Add a Link-in-bio with UTM parameters to a Google Map or menu page.
- Keep a simple spreadsheet: date, post link, reach, saves, shares, directions taps, code redemptions, weekend sales.

If results are mixed, we can pivot to more Reels/TikToks and fewer static posts, or shift timing.

### Human: What if we get negative comments about pricing?
### Assistant: Handle transparently and kindly. Framework:
- Acknowledge: “Thanks for the feedback—totally hear you.”
- Context (brief): “We use organic, fair-trade, and local ingredients, which increases costs, but we keep portions generous.”
- Offer value: Loyalty stamps, student discount midweek, or bundle deals.
- Invite 1:1: “DM us—happy to suggest options for any budget.”

Templates
- “We appreciate you sharing this. Our pricing reflects organic and locally sourced ingredients and time-intensive methods. We offer a student discount Wed–Thu and weekend bundles to keep things accessible. DM us and we’ll help you find a favorite within your budget.”
- For trolls: “We welcome all feedback, but we remove comments that are abusive to our staff. Our DMs are open for constructive chat.”

Proactive alternative
- Post a transparent “What goes into our $4 roll” carousel breaking down ingredients, labor, rent, quality. Often turns skeptics into fans.
