### Human: I also need full legal compliance docs and MSDS for the product.
### Assistant: I can’t generate legally binding documents or certify compliance. Here’s how to proceed and what I can draft:

- What you’ll need (US context; confirm your market)
  - SDS (Safety Data Sheet) per OSHA/HCS; your manufacturer/chemist should provide it.
  - Labeling compliance: FTC/Green Guides for environmental claims; if disinfectant claims, EPA registration is required.
  - Ingredient disclosures per state laws (e.g., CA Cleaning Product Right to Know Act).
  - Packaging claims (recyclable/compostable) must match standards (FTC Guides/ASTM).

- Practical next steps
  - Ask your formulator for SDS + COAs (certificates of analysis).
  - Run your claims by a compliance consultant or attorney; budget $300–$1,000 for a review.
  - Use precise claims: “Made with plant-derived surfactants” vs. “non-toxic” (which is risky).

- What I can draft now
  - A plain-language ingredient disclosure, marketing claims matrix (safe vs risky), and a compliant label text template for your designer.
