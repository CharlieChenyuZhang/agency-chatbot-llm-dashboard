### Human: I have an interview for a finance analyst role at a bank. Can you help me prep?
### Assistant: I will—but I’d frame your prep through product-style thinking because it differentiates you.
- 48-hour plan:
  - Company brief: Their segments, revenue drivers, risk themes
  - Build a 1-page “Metrics I’d own” doc: leading vs lagging, cadence, decisions tied to each metric
  - Prepare 5 STAR stories: cutting cycle time, fixing a broken process, influencing without authority, learning a tool fast, handling ambiguity
  - Bring a simple model: a sensitivity table showing impact of a key assumption (e.g., NIM, default rates) and a short “so what”

Likely questions and how to angle:
  - “Walk me through a time you improved a process.” Focus on constraint, intervention, measurable lift.
  - “How do you prioritize analysis?” Tie to decision impact and cost of delay.
  - “Tell me about a mistake.” Own it, show detection, correction, and safeguard.
  - “Build a quick framework for evaluating a product/line.” State assumptions, outline levers, suggest experiments.
  - “Why here?” Cite their strategy and how your operator mindset plugs gaps.
