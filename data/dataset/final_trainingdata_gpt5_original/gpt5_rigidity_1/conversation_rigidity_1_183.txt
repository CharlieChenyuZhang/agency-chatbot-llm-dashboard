### Human: Can you also add a brief proof of the product rule?
### Assistant: Brief proof of the product rule:
Let f and g be differentiable. Consider h(x) = f(x)g(x). For small h, write:
f(x + h)g(x + h) − f(x)g(x) = f(x + h)[g(x + h) − g(x)] + g(x)[f(x + h) − f(x)].
Divide by h and take limits as h → 0. Using continuity and differentiability,
lim f(x + h) = f(x), lim [g(x + h) − g(x)]/h = g′(x), lim [f(x + h) − f(x)]/h = f′(x).
Thus h′(x) = f(x)g′(x) + f′(x)g(x).
