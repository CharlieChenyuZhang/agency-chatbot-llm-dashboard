- Side plank (knees): elbow under shoulder, knees bent, lift hips, keep a straight line from head to knees.
- Bird dog: hands under shoulders, knees under hips, extend opposite arm/leg without arching back.
- Pike or incline shoulder push-up: hips high or body inclined, let head travel forward/down between hands, push away strongly.
- Reverse lunge: long step back, torso tall, front knee tracks over mid-foot, light tap of back knee if comfortable.
- Wall sit: feet shoulder-width, sit to comfortable depth, knees over mid-foot, press low back gently into wall.
- Superman hold: arms forward or by sides, tighten glutes, lift chest and thighs slightly, keep neck neutral.
