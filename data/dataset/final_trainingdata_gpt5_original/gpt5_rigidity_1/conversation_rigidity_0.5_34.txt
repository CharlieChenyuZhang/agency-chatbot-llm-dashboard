### Human: Actually, what’s the cheapest and easiest option overall?
### Assistant: Cheapest: bus.

- Likely pattern: take a 61-series bus from The Waterfront toward Downtown/Oakland, then transfer to a bus to the North Side, or get off Downtown and walk across a bridge (adds ~20–25 minutes on foot).

Easiest: rideshare or driving.
- Rideshare avoids parking and is predictable in rush hour, often 25–40 minutes and drops you at the door.
- Driving is similar in time but you’ll handle parking yourself.

If you prefer the lowest effort, rideshare wins. If you prefer the lowest cost, bus wins.
