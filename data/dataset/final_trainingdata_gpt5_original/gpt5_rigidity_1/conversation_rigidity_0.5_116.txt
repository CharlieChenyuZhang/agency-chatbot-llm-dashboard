### Human: Which one is quieter? I work in a shared space.
### Assistant: Both are office‑friendly, but FlexiSpot dual‑motor frames tend to be a bit quieter and smoother in motion than FEZIBO’s common single‑motor frames. You’re generally looking at roughly mid‑40s to low‑50s dB while moving—conversation level or lower.

- Add soft pads under feet to reduce vibration transmission.
- As an alternative, a manual‑crank desk like IKEA Trotten is nearly silent in operation, but raising/lowering takes more effort and time.

### Human: How hard is assembly? I'm not very handy.
### Assistant: Manageable with basic tools.
