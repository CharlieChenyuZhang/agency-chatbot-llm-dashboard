### Human: Can you also plan cable management?
### Assistant: Yes. Here’s a simple, neat plan.

Materials
- D-Line cord cover kit (paintable)
- 8-outlet surge protector with flat plug
- 6–8 cable clips and a small pack of Velcro ties
- 1 short (3 ft) HDMI and 1 short (3 ft) power cord if existing cables are overly long

Steps
1) Power hub: Place the surge protector centered behind the media console, raised 2–3" off the floor with adhesive Velcro (optional) so it’s easier to dust and plug.
2) TV cables: Bundle TV power + HDMI together with Velcro ties, drop them straight down to the console’s cable hole. Avoid diagonals to minimize visibility.
3) Inside console: Use two cable clips to route HDMI/power along the console’s rear panel, keeping slack coiled and tied.
4) Wall run: From console to outlet, install the D-Line raceway vertically down from the outlet or horizontally behind the console depending on outlet position. Cut to size, snap closed, and stick with its adhesive.
5) Floor lamp: Run floor lamp cable along the baseboard with adhesive cable clips; paint the clips to match the wall if desired.
6) TV safety: Keep power and signal cables loosely bundled (not overtight) to prevent interference and strain. Maintain a drip-loop for cables entering the surge protector.
7) Final check: From seated eye level, verify no dangling cords are visible; adjust the raceway position if needed.
