### Human: Small 5'x8' condo bathroom. Budget $5k–$7k. DIY on weekends over ~6 weeks. Keep the tub and don’t move plumbing. Goals: new vanity, better storage, brighter lighting. Style: modern, minimalist, neutral colors with matte black fixtures. Prefer eco-friendly paint. Seattle. HOA is strict on noise and work hours. Please give me a weekend-by-weekend step-by-step plan that respects those constraints and notes any approvals needed.
### Assistant: Here’s a phased weekend-by-weekend plan, aligned to your constraints.

Pre-work (evenings, before Weekend 1)
- Read HOA rules; confirm allowed work hours, quiet hours, and debris disposal.
- Submit HOA alteration request: scope (vanity, fixture, light fixture, paint), no plumbing relocation, dates, hours, and disposal plan.
- Measure room; confirm door swings, stud locations, and GFCI outlet location.
- Order long-lead items (vanity, faucet, mirror cabinet, light fixture, hardware) for delivery before Weekend 2.

Weekend 1: Prep and demo
- Protect floors and tub; set up dust control.
- Remove existing mirror/medicine cabinet, vanity, faucet, and light fixture.
- Cap water lines; verify shutoffs function.
- Patch wall surfaces; let dry.
- Dispose of debris per HOA rules.

Weekend 2: Walls and paint
- Sand patches; clean walls.
- Prime as needed.
- Paint walls with low-VOC bathroom-rated paint (neutral palette); allow cure time per can.
- Touch up ceiling/trim if needed.

Weekend 3: Lighting and storage
- Replace light fixture at existing junction box height/position.
- Install mirror cabinet or mirror (use anchors into studs where possible).
- Add floating shelves or recessed storage (only if within HOA rules and studs allow).

Weekend 4: Vanity and plumbing
- Assemble/set vanity; ensure level and secure to studs.
- Install sink/top (if separate).
- Install faucet and drain assembly; connect P-trap and supply lines.
- Check for leaks; silicone perimeter.

Weekend 5: Fixtures and finishing
- Install towel bars, hooks, TP holder (matte black).
- Replace shower rod and curtain if applicable.
- Swap visible trim accessories (register covers, switch plates) for cohesive look.

Weekend 6: Cleanup and punch list
- Caulk touch-ups; paint touch-ups.
- Verify GFCI operation.
- Final clean; photograph for records.
- Submit completion notice to HOA if required.

Notes
- Work only within HOA-approved hours.
- No plumbing or electrical relocation is included.
- If any permit is required for electrical work in Seattle, use a licensed electrician or pull the proper homeowner permit within scope allowed.
