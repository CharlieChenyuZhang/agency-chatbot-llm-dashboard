### Human: I’m near 16th and Mission. Also, I hate long-term contracts. What should I watch out for?
### Assistant: Good to know. Here’s how to protect yourself and stay flexible:
- Look for month-to-month with a small premium instead of a 12-month contract.
- Ask about:
  - Enrollment and “annual maintenance” fees
  - Freeze policy (useful for travel or busy months)
  - Cancellation window and method (email vs. in-person)
  - Peak-hour crowding (ask specifically about 8–10 pm)
  - Class reservations and no-show fees
- Trial first: Do a free class or a $10–$20 day pass during your actual workout time (9–11 pm) to gauge crowding.
- Negotiation tip: Bring a competing offer (screenshot). Many gyms will waive startup fees or offer a promo month.

Near 16th & Mission, you’ll likely find a couple of independent strength/conditioning studios within a 10–15 minute walk or a quick BART hop, plus a climbing gym that offers yoga/conditioning. Try one small studio for classes and a nearby budget gym for late-night weights if needed.

### Human: Actually, if none of these feel right, could I do something at home instead? I’ve got about $150 to spend and want a 3-day routine that feels like a class.
### Assistant: Absolutely—here’s a budget setup and a class-style plan.

Budget home setup (under ~$150):
- Adjustable dumbbells or 2–3 pairs (e.g., 10/20/35 lb), used if possible
- Resistance bands with door anchor
- Yoga mat and a jump rope
- Optional: A kettlebell (25–35 lb) if you can find a deal

Three-day class-style plan (45–60 minutes each)
- Day A: Total-body strength + conditioning
  - Warm-up: 5 min jump rope + mobility
  - Circuit (3–4 rounds): Goblet squat, push-up, one-arm row, split squat, plank variation (10–12 reps each, 45s rest)
  - Finisher: 6–8 minutes EMOM alternating kettlebell swings (or band pull-throughs) and burpees or jump rope
- Day B: Lower body + core
  - Warm-up: Hip openers, glute bridges
  - Supersets (3–4 sets): Romanian deadlift + reverse lunge; banded lateral walks + calf raises
  - Core: 3 rounds hollow hold + dead bug + side plank
  - Optional finisher: 6 rounds 30s hard/30s easy jump rope
- Day C: Upper body + conditioning
  - Warm-up: Band pull-aparts, shoulder circles
  - Push/pull circuit (3–4 rounds): Floor press or DB press, one-arm row, overhead press, band face pulls
  - Finisher: 10-minute AMRAP of DB thrusters, renegade rows, mountain climbers (moderate reps)

Progression:
- Add 1–2 reps per movement each week or increase load when form is solid
- Keep rest short (45–60s) to mimic class intensity
- Track weights/reps to ensure steady progress
