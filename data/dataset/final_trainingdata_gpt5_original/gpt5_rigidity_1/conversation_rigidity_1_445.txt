### Human: What pressure washer PSI should I buy?
### Assistant: For cars, choose 1700–2000 PSI at 1.2–2.0 GPM (electric) for safe, effective cleaning. Use 40° or 25° nozzles and keep 12 inches from paint. If you prefer gas, stay near 2700–3000 PSI with an adjustable unloader and use wider tips; reduce pressure for delicate areas.
