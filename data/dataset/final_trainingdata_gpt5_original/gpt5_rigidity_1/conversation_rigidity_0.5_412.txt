### Human: Can you include a simple way to calculate AOV and conversion rate from our data?
### Assistant: Absolutely. Here are quick formulas and where to find the inputs:

- AOV = Total revenue / Orders
  - Shopify: Analytics > Reports > Sales over time gives both revenue and orders.
  - Example in a spreadsheet: =RevenueCell/OrdersCell

- Conversion rate = Orders / Sessions
  - Shopify: Analytics > Dashboards > Online store sessions (or Online store conversion rate report).
  - Example in a spreadsheet: =OrdersCell/SessionsCell
  - If you track “Add to carts” and “Checkouts,” use Orders/Sessions for leadership simplicity.

- ROAS (Google Ads) = Conversion value / Cost
  - In Google Ads, add columns “Cost” and “Conv. value,” then ROAS = Conv. value / Cost.

- CAC (blended) = Total marketing spend / Number of new customers
  - New customers: Shopify “Customers” report or “First-time” in Sales by customer type.
