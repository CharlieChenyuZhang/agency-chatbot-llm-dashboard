### Human: I just need a list of reactions and conditions to memorize.
### Assistant: A raw list won’t stick. Use decision prompts instead:
- Given substrate class + base strength + solvent, predict SN1/SN2/E1/E2 and justify.
- For each reaction, map: mechanism sketch → regiochemistry and stereochemistry rules → common traps.
- Build “minimum cards”: one prompt, one decision, one reason. Example:
  - Prompt: Secondary bromide + strong bulky base (t-BuOK), heat, tert-butanol. Major outcome?
  - Answer: E2, Hofmann product favored due to sterics; anti-periplanar requirement; note competing elimination vs substitution.

If you post your syllabus section, I’ll turn it into 12–20 high-yield prompts.
