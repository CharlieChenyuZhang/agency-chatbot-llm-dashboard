### Human: I only have VoIP phone service through my cable provider. Will fax work?
### Assistant: It can work, but fax over VoIP is less reliable than over a true analog line. Tips to improve success:
- Ask your provider if they support T.38 (a protocol that helps fax over IP).
- On the fax device, lower the fax speed/baud rate and turn off ECM (error correction mode) if you see failures.
- Use short, high‑quality phone cables directly to the modem’s phone port (avoid long runs/splitters).
- If reliability is critical, consider an online fax service instead of a physical fax line.

If you mostly send rather than receive, an online fax service plus your AIO’s scanner is often simpler and more dependable over VoIP.
