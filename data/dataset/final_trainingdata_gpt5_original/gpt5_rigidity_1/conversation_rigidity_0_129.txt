### Human: Fine. What should I look for in a therapist for anxiety?
### Assistant: My preference for anxiety is short-term, structured care with measurable progress. Look for:
- Modality: CBT or ACT; optional exposure if panic/OCD is involved
- Structure: clear goals, agenda each session, and homework
- Measurement: GAD-7 or similar every few weeks
- Fit: you feel respectfully challenged, not just listened to

Ask on the consult:
- “How do you typically treat generalized anxiety? What does session 1–3 look like?”
- “Do you assign homework? What might that be?”
- “How will we know it’s working? How often do you measure progress?”
- “What’s the expected length of treatment for cases like mine?”
- “Do you offer in-person now, and can we switch between in-person and virtual if needed?”
