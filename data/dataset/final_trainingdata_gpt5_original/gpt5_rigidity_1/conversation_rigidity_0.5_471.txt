### Human: Yes, please give me the structure and formulas so it works in Sheets.
### Assistant: Simple, no-scripts setup:

Sheets:
1) Categories
- Columns: Category, Monthly_Budget, Priority, Rollover (TRUE/FALSE), Cap (optional)
- Enter the budgets and flags as listed above

2) Transactions
- Columns: Date, Description, Category, Outflow, Inflow
- Import bank data or enter manually (spending as positive in Outflow, income as positive in Inflow)

3) Budget
- Summary per category with Available = Budget + Rollover + Transfers - Spent

Core formulas:
- Spent this month (in Budget sheet, per category):
  =SUMIFS(Transactions!E:E, Transactions!C:C, [@Category], Transactions!A:A, ">= "&EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)+1, Transactions!A:A, "<="&EOMONTH(TODAY(),0))
  Replace Transactions!E:E with Outflow column; adjust ranges for your layout.

- Inflow this month (for income rows if you want to track paychecks):
  =SUMIFS(Transactions!D:D, Transactions!A:A, ">= "&EOMONTH(TODAY(),-1)+1, Transactions!A:A, "<="&EOMONTH(TODAY(),0))
  Replace D:D with Inflow.

- Available:
  =[@Monthly_Budget] + [@Rollover_Applied] + [@Transfers_In] - [@Spent]

- Overspend amount:
  =MAX(0, -[@Available])

Reallocation (no scripts, simple Buffer approach):
- For each category (except Emergency and fixed bills), auto-suggest a single transfer from Buffer equal to its Overspend:
  Transfer_From_Buffer = IF([@Overspend]>0, MIN([@Overspend], Buffer_Available), 0)
- In the Buffer row:
  Transfers_Out = SUM of Transfer_From_Buffer for all other categories
  Buffer Available updates accordingly

Month-end sweep:
- For non-rollover categories (e.g., Dining, Buffer), compute leftover:
  Leftover = MAX(0, [@Available])
- Sweep recommendation to Emergency:
  Sweep_To_EF = IF([@Rollover]=FALSE, [@Leftover], 0)
- Emergency Transfers_In = SUM of Sweep_To_EF across non-rollover categories

This gives you:
- Automatic recommendations to cover overspending from Buffer
- Automatic recommendations to sweep leftovers to Emergency
- Minimal manual work: record a single monthly sweep transfer and any buffer transfers you accept

If you want full priority-based multi-category reallocation (not just Buffer), we can do that, but it’s more complex in pure formulas. We can also add a simple Apps Script later if you change your mind.
