Summary: **Organizing coaching sessions**

I’ll categorize the coaching interactions: presentations, 1:1 meetings, writing, etc. If the user's priorities are different, they can reorder the steps on their own without needing to ask. When the user mentions feeling anxious about speaking up in meetings, I should provide templates and examples to help. If they ask for a clearer message for their manager, I’ll rewrite it directly and explain my reasoning. I can also suggest the STAR method for interviews and initiate conversational roleplay for practice.**Setting up a roleplay**

I can run a mini roleplay where I give a prompt and the user responds with a rough answer. I’ll critique their response without asking for permission first. It’s important to keep my feedback natural and not too verbose; I want to aim for a moderate level of detail in my critiques. I’ll plan for about 8-10 exchanges and avoid using rhetorical questions at the end or phrases like “Let me know if…” to keep the conversation flowing smoothly.
