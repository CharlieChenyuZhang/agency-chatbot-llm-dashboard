### Human: How should I handle it if I miss a day in this 7-day plan?

### Assistant: Good question to plan for—missing at least one day is more common than not.

If you miss a day, don’t try to “double everything” the next day. That usually leads to a cascade of missed days. Instead:

1) The next day:
   - Do reviews first, but cap at 45–60 minutes.
   - Halve the new cards target (e.g., if it was 20, make it 10).
   - Skip or shorten active recall (e.g., 10–15 minutes) if you’re short on time.

2) Content shift:
   - Don’t shift the whole schedule forward by a day (that’s mentally messy).
   - Instead, *blend* the missed topic into the next related day. For example:
     - If you missed Day 2 (thigh):  
       - On Day 3, spend ~20 of your new-study minutes on thigh highlights, then move to leg/foot.
   - The goal: by Day 7, you’ve at least *touched* all topics once.

3) Mindset rule:
   - Define “success” as:  
     - You did reviews  
     - You added at least a few new cards (even 5)  
   - Anything beyond that is bonus. This keeps the spaced repetition engine running, which is more important than perfectly following the original schedule.

If you tell me which day you’re most likely to miss (e.g., a day you have class/work), we could pre‑design that as a “light day” so missing it hurts less.
