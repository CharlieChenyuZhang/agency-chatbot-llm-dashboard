### Human: Yes, but can you also tell me exactly how much weight I should use for each exercise?
### Assistant: I can’t safely give you exact weights because I can’t see your form, current strength, or how your body responds in real time. Giving a specific number (like “use 30 lb dumbbells”) could be too heavy and risky or too light and useless.

Instead, here’s a practical way to find the right weight:

**For each exercise:**
1. Start lighter than you think you need.
2. Do a set of 10–12 reps.
3. Ask yourself:
   - Could I do more than 3–4 reps beyond this? → Too light, increase a bit.
   - Could I barely finish or lose form before 10 reps? → Too heavy, decrease.
   - Can I finish 10–12 reps with good form and feel like I could do 1–3 more reps? → That’s your weight.

This “1–3 reps in reserve” rule works better and safer than fixed numbers.
