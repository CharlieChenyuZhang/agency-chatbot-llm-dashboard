### Assistant: To get as close as possible to a **truly guaranteed** 2pm checkout, you usually have to adjust one of these:

1. **Pay for it explicitly**  
   Many hotels will formally guarantee late checkout if you:
   - Pay a **late checkout fee** (e.g., a half-day rate)  
   - Or book a **“day use”/“office for the day”** add-on that covers hours after checkout.

   > “Can I pay a fixed fee to guarantee a 2pm checkout at the time of booking, not just subject to availability on the day?”

   This is the most reliable but not always cost-effective:
   - Book your stay **through the night of your departure**, and simply leave at 2pm.  
   - This way, you technically have the room all day; no late checkout needed.
   - You can sometimes negotiate a **partial rate** for the last day if you explain you’ll leave by mid-afternoon, though not all hotels will do this.

3. **Use elite status (if you have it)**  
   If you have mid/high-tier status with:
   - **Marriott (Gold/Platinum and above)**  
   - **Hilton (Gold/Diamond)**  
   - **IHG (Platinum/Diamond)**

   - Offer **2pm (or later) late checkout as a published benefit**, sometimes closer to guaranteed than “subject to availability” (though still not 100%, especially in busy cities).
