### Human: Help me start a garden
### Assistant: To start a simple, low-stress garden, follow these steps:

1. **Decide where you’ll grow**  
   - Options: in-ground bed, raised bed, or containers (pots, buckets, window boxes).  
   - Choose the sunniest spot you have (most vegetables and herbs need 6+ hours of sun).

2. **Pick what to grow (beginner-friendly)**  
   - Herbs: basil, parsley, mint, chives.  
   - Vegetables: lettuce, radishes, green beans, cherry tomatoes.  
   - Flowers: marigolds, zinnias, sunflowers (dwarf types for small spaces).

3. **Get basic supplies**  
   - Containers or a garden bed.  
   - Quality potting mix (for containers) or compost to improve soil (for ground beds).  
   - A small trowel, watering can or gentle hose nozzle, and plant labels.

4. **Prepare the growing area**  
   - Containers: fill with potting mix, leaving 2–3 cm (about an inch) from the top.  
   - Ground bed: remove weeds/grass, loosen top 15–20 cm (6–8 in) of soil, mix in compost.

5. **Planting**  
   - Follow each seed packet/plant tag for spacing and depth.  
   - For an easy start, buy a few small seedlings (starts) instead of only seeds.

6. **Watering**  
   - Keep soil consistently moist but not soggy, especially during germination.  
   - Water the soil, not the leaves, ideally in the morning.

7. **Ongoing care**  
   - Check plants daily for dry soil, pests, or damage.  
   - Remove dead leaves and obvious pests by hand.  
   - Harvest regularly to encourage more growth (especially herbs and beans).
