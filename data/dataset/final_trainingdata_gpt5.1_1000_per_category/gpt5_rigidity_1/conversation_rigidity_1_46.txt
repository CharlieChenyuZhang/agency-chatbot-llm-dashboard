### Human: I'm on Windows 11. Give me the exact steps to sort by year.
### Assistant: Here are the exact steps for Windows 11:

1. **Open your Pictures folder**
   - Press `Win + E` to open File Explorer.
   - Navigate to `This PC > Pictures` (or wherever your main folder is).

2. **Switch to Details view**
   - Click the **View** menu at the top.
   - Choose **Details**.

3. **Show “Date taken” column**
   - Right‑click on any column header (like “Name” or “Date modified”).
   - Click **More…**
   - Scroll down and check **Date taken**.
   - Click **OK**.

4. **Sort by Date taken**
   - Click the **Date taken** column header once to sort.
   - Click again if you need it in the opposite order (oldest/newest).

5. **Create year folders**
   - In the left pane, right‑click in your main pictures directory.
   - Select **New > Folder**.
   - Create folders for each year you have photos for (e.g., `2018`, `2019`, `2020`, etc.).

6. **Move photos into year folders**
   - In the sorted list, select all photos from a given year:
     - Click the first photo from that year.
     - Hold `Shift` and click the last photo from that year.
   - Right‑click the selection > **Cut**.
   - Open the corresponding year folder > right‑click inside it > **Paste**.

If you want, specify one year (e.g., 2022), and I’ll guide you through just that as a test run.
