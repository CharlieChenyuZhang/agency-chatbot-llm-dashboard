3. Enlightenment ideas

4. Economic hardship and food shortages  
   - Poor harvests in the 1780s led to rising bread prices and widespread hardship among peasants and urban workers.  
   - High food prices, unemployment, and economic stagnation fueled unrest and anger toward both local elites and the royal government.

   - Louis XVI’s government was seen as indecisive and ineffective, especially in dealing with financial reforms.  
   - Attempts at reform, including calling the Assembly of Notables and then the Estates-General in 1789, highlighted conflicts between the monarchy and privileged estates, and between the estates themselves.
