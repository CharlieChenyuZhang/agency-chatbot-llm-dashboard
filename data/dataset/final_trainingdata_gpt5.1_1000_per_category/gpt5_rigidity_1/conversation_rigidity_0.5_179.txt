Since your goal is audience growth, I’d strongly suggest:
- Use a free or low-cost podcast host that distributes to **multiple platforms for you**.

Options:
- **Spotify for Podcasters** (free; can distribute to other platforms, not just Spotify)  
- Buzzsprout / Podbean / others (have free/cheap tiers)

Then:
- Submit to at least:
  - Spotify  
  - Apple Podcasts  
  - Google Podcasts / YouTube Music (depending on current options)  
  - Maybe one or two others like Pocket Casts

This takes a little setup once, but after that, you just upload in one place and it goes out everywhere.

**PODCAST EPISODE 1 LAUNCH CHECKLIST**

**Phase 1 – Set up the basics (once)**  
1. Choose your show name  
   - e.g., “Real-World Productivity with Alex” (or tweak one of the ideas we listed).  
2. Write a short show description (3–5 sentences):  
   - Who it’s for (people in their 20s starting their careers)  
   - What it covers (productivity, habits, mindset)  
   - Tone (honest, “we’re in this together”)  
3. Create simple cover art  
   - Use Canva template  
   - Include show name + your name  
4. Create a free account on a podcast host (e.g., Spotify for Podcasters or Buzzsprout).  
5. Set up your show in the host  
   - Upload cover art  
   - Add show name & description  
   - Choose category (Education, Self-Improvement, etc.).

**Phase 2 – Record Episode 1**

6. Set up your mic and recording space  
   - Plug in ATR2100x (or similar) via USB  
   - Position mic close to your mouth (about a fist away)  
   - Use pop filter  
   - Close windows if there’s street noise; throw a blanket or clothes around if room is echoey.  
7. Open your recording software  
   - Audacity, GarageBand, or Descript  
   - Test levels: speak as loud as you will in the episode; adjust so it doesn’t peak.  
8. Record Episode 1  
   - Use the script we created for intro + habits  
   - Don’t worry about making mistakes; just pause, then start the sentence again. You’ll edit later.  

**Phase 3 – Edit and export**

9. Edit the audio  
   - Remove long pauses, obvious mistakes, and repeated takes  
   - Optionally apply light noise reduction and compression (many tutorials on YouTube; keep it simple to start).  
10. Export as MP3  
   - Name file clearly: `RWP_Ep001_What_I_Wish_I_Knew.mp3`.

**Phase 4 – Publish the episode**

11. In your podcast host, create a new episode  
   - Title: “Episode 1: What I Wish I Knew in My First Full-Time Job”  
   - Description:  
     - 2–3 sentence summary  
     - Bullet list of the 3 habits  
     - Any links (socials, email).  
12. Upload the MP3 file.  
13. Set episode details  
   - Episode type: Full  
   - Season: 1  
   - Episode number: 1  
   - Language: your spoken language.  
14. Hit publish (or schedule a date/time).

**Phase 5 – Distribute to platforms (mostly one-time setup)**

15. From your host, find the **RSS feed link** for your show.  
16. Submit to:  
   - Spotify (usually automatic if using Spotify for Podcasters)  
   - Apple Podcasts (via Apple Podcasts Connect)  
   - Any other directories your host supports with one-click.  
17. Wait for approval and note the links to your show on each platform.

**Phase 6 – Create promo clips**

18. Re-listen to Episode 1 and mark 2–3 good clip moments:  
   - A short story  
   - A strong habit explanation  
   - A relatable line about first jobs.  
19. Create vertical video clips (20–60 seconds each):  
   - Use CapCut, Descript, or TikTok’s editor  
   - Add subtitles & title text.  
20. Save/export clips for TikTok and IG Reels.

**Phase 7 – Social posting (Week 1)**

21. Set up TikTok & Instagram accounts for the show (or use your personal if you prefer).  
22. Update bio with:
   - One-line description  
   - Link to podcast (Linktree or direct link).  
23. Post Clip #1 on TikTok  
   - Caption: short context + “Full episode in ‘Real-World Productivity with Alex’ – link in bio.”  
   - Repost to IG Reels with similar caption.  
24. On 2 other days that week, post Clip #2 and Clip #3 the same way.  
25. Share a low-key announcement on your personal accounts  
   - “I launched a podcast for people in their 20s figuring out their first job. Here’s Episode 1 if you want to check it out.”

**Phase 8 – Feedback & iteration**

26. Ask 3–5 people for honest feedback  
   - “Anything confusing or boring?”  
   - “Would you listen to another episode? Why or why not?”  
27. Note 1–2 improvements for Episode 2  
   - Don’t try to fix everything at once—just one or two tweaks.  
28. Start planning Episode 2 using the same structure and process.
