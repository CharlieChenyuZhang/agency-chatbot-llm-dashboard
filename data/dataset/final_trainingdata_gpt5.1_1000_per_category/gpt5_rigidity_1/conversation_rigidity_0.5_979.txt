### Assistant: You *can* skip formal perspective and anatomy drills, but there’s a trade-off:

- Short term: More fun, less boredom.
- Long term: You’ll hit a ceiling faster—poses will feel stiff, spaces will look “off.”

A middle-ground approach:
- We focus on imagination and characters, but sneak in just enough fundamentals to support them, without turning it into a technical course.

For the next phase (after your first 2 weeks), we could:
1. Keep doing imaginative exercises:
   - Shape-based characters (start with simple shapes: circle, triangle, rectangle).
   - “What if objects were alive?” (lamp person, backpack creature, etc.)
2. Add **micro-fundamentals**:
   - 5 minutes of simple boxes and cylinders in different angles before you draw your characters.
   - Very simple gesture lines (stick figures with flow) before adding details.

This way, you’re mostly doing the fun stuff, but your drawings don’t stay stuck at the “symbol doodle” level.
