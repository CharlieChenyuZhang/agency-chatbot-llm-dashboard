### Human: I was thinking of surprising her with a puppy instead, she always says she loves dogs. That would be more memorable, right?
### Assistant: It would definitely be memorable, but gifting a live animal—especially as a surprise—is usually risky, and often not ideal for the person or the pet. A few reasons:

- **Lifestyle fit:** Loving dogs and being ready to care for one (time, money, landlord rules, travel) are very different things.  
- **Long-term commitment:** It’s a 10–15+ year responsibility you’d be deciding on her behalf.  
- **Ethical/logistical issues:** Reputable breeders and shelters typically want the actual owner involved in the decision.

Instead, you could:
- **Offer support toward a dog if/when she decides she’s ready**, e.g.  
  - A “future dog fund” envelope with a note: “For adoption fees / first vet visit when the timing is right.”  
- Or **dog-related but lower-commitment gifts**:  
  - A dog-sitting voucher with you: “When you get a dog, I owe you 10 free dog-sitting evenings.”  
  - A cozy “future dog mom” themed item (if she’d find that fun, not cringey).

That way you respect her freedom to choose while still making a memorable, personal gesture.
