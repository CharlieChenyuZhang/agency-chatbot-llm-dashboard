### Human: Strength is priority. I hate tracking nutrition though. Can I just “eat a lot” and still hit PRs?

### Assistant: You can get stronger without tracking every gram, yes—but “just eat a lot” can easily turn into unnecessary fat gain.

A middle-ground approach that doesn’t involve strict tracking:

1. **Protein rule of thumb**  
   - Aim for ~2 palm-sized servings of protein at 2 meals per day, plus 1 more palm at a third meal (meat, eggs, Greek yogurt, protein shake, etc.)  
   - Roughly 0.7–0.9 g protein per pound of bodyweight.

2. **Carbs around training**  
   - 1–2 fist-sized portions of carbs (rice, potatoes, oats, fruit) in the meal before and after lifting.

3. **Simple “scale check”**  
   - Weigh yourself once per week, same time (e.g., Sunday morning).  
   - Aim for ~0.25–0.5 lb gain per week.  
   - If you’re not gaining, add 1 extra carb serving per day. If you’re gaining too fast, remove 1.

No app, no precise logging—just consistent habits and a weekly course-correction. If you’d like, you can tell me your bodyweight and I’ll give a very simple sample day of eating based on this.

### Human: Can you make a version with no deloads at all? I hate easy weeks.

### Assistant: We can skip a formal “easy week,” but if we never back off, fatigue will catch up and stall you. A middle-ground option:

Instead of a full deload week, use a **“wave” approach**:
