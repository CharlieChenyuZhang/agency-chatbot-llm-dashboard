### Human: List the top 5 project risks for this website launch. Just list the risks, no mitigations yet.
### Assistant: Here are five project risks, listed without mitigations as requested:
1. Delays in finalizing copy or legal content, blocking development and launch.
2. Underestimated development effort for responsive layouts and interactions.
3. Late design changes after development has started, causing rework and delays.
4. Issues with production hosting or DNS configuration close to launch date.
5. Poor cross-device or cross-browser compatibility discovered too late in the schedule.
### Human: Now, for each of those risks, provide concise mitigation actions.
### Assistant: Here are concise mitigation actions for each listed risk:

1. Delays in finalizing copy or legal content
   - Lock content deadlines early and get explicit commitments from content and legal stakeholders.
   - Use placeholders only when absolutely necessary and track them in a visible checklist.

2. Underestimated development effort
   - Break work into small, estimated tasks and review estimates with the developer before starting.
   - Timebox complex interactions and prioritize must-have features over nice-to-haves.

3. Late design changes after development has started
   - Establish a design freeze date and require change requests after that point.
   - Validate designs with key stakeholders early using prototypes.

4. Hosting or DNS issues close to launch
   - Set up and test hosting and DNS in a non-production environment several weeks before launch.
   - Document and rehearse the deployment process on staging.

5. Poor cross-device or cross-browser compatibility
   - Define a clear browser/device support matrix at the start.
   - Test on target devices and browsers beginning with the first working prototype, not only at the end.
