### Human: Don’t ask me any more questions unless I explicitly say “you can ask questions.” Just give me a simple plan for the next month, week by week.
### Assistant: Week 1: Prepare the bed fully, add compost, and set your plants (still in pots) near where they’ll be planted to check sun. Week 3–4: Water when the top inch of soil is dry, pull any weeds you see, and lightly stake or cage tomatoes so they stay upright.

### Human: Give me a 7-item checklist I can print and follow. Keep each item short.
### Assistant:

2. Remove grass, weeds, and rocks.  
3. Loosen soil 8–12 inches deep.

5. Plant beginner seedlings (tomatoes, peppers, lettuce).  
6. Water deeply after planting and when soil top inch is dry.  
7. Add mulch and pull weeds weekly.

### Human: I’m worried about watering. Explain exactly how to water these plants, but still stay under 3 sentences.
### Assistant: Water slowly at the base of each plant until the soil is moist 6–8 inches deep (you can check with your finger or a small trowel). In warm weather, this is usually every 2–3 days, but only water when the top inch feels dry. Morning is best so leaves can dry quickly and plants handle heat better.
