**Exercise: “Stress Story Reframe” (5–10 minutes)**  
1. Pick one situation that’s stressing you out.  
2. Write down the “story” your mind is telling about it (e.g., “If I mess this up, I’ll lose my job, and everyone will see I’m a fraud”).

4. For each underlined part, write a more realistic alternative (not positive, just more accurate):  
   - “If I mess this up, my manager might be annoyed, but we’ve fixed mistakes before.”  
5. Rewrite the original story using only the more realistic statements.

Do this daily for a week with different stressors.
