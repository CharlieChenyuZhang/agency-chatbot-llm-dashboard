   - What are you trying to build in the next 12–24 months?

### Assistant: Because in early-stage startups, generic team blueprints are often harmful. They’re optimized for *typical* scenarios, not **you + your product + your stage**.

- Only later worry about formal structures, recurring meetings, and hiring patterns.

But I’d still rather see your “how I work” memo draft, because your behavior will override any team process I suggest.
