### Human: Can you give me exact recipes for all of Monday's meals only?
### Assistant: Here are detailed recipes just for Monday’s meals, using the current vegetarian, gluten- and dairy-free plan.

Monday breakfast: Overnight oats (1 serving; scale up as needed)

Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup gluten-free rolled oats
- 1/2–3/4 cup dairy-free milk
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 1/2 cup frozen mixed berries
- 1–2 tsp honey or maple syrup (optional)

Instructions:
1. Add oats, chia seeds, and sweetener (if using) to a jar or container.
2. Pour in dairy-free milk and stir well.
3. Top with frozen berries (don’t stir).
4. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
5. In the morning, stir and add a splash of milk if it’s too thick.

Monday lunch: Grilled tofu, brown rice, steamed broccoli (1–2 servings)

Ingredients:
- 1/2 block firm or extra-firm tofu, pressed and sliced
- 1/2–1 cup cooked brown rice
- 1–2 cups broccoli florets
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper
- Optional spices: garlic powder, paprika, or a little gluten-free tamari

Instructions:
1. Cook rice according to package directions if not already cooked.
2. Press tofu in a clean towel for 10–15 minutes to remove excess moisture.
3. Slice tofu into slabs or cubes. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, and any spices.
4. Heat a nonstick pan or grill pan over medium heat. Cook tofu 3–4 minutes per side until browned.
5. Steam broccoli in a steamer basket or microwave with a little water for 3–5 minutes until tender-crisp. Season lightly with salt and a drizzle of olive oil.
6. Serve grilled tofu over or alongside brown rice with broccoli on the side.

Monday dinner: Vegetarian chili with side salad (about 3–4 servings)

Ingredients for chili:
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1–2 cans (about 15 oz each) mixed beans (kidney, black, pinto), drained and rinsed
- 1–2 cans (about 14–15 oz each) diced tomatoes
- 1–2 cups vegetable broth or water (as needed for desired thickness)
- 1–2 tbsp chili powder (to taste)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste

Ingredients for simple side salad:
- Mixed salad greens or chopped lettuce
- Sliced cucumber
- Shredded or sliced carrot
- Olive oil, vinegar or lemon juice, salt, pepper (for dressing)

Instructions for chili:
1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add diced onion and bell pepper. Sauté 5–7 minutes until softened.
3. Add garlic and cook 30–60 seconds until fragrant.
4. Stir in beans, diced tomatoes, and 1 cup broth or water.
5. Add chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika (if using), salt, and pepper.
6. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat and cook 20–30 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add more liquid if it gets too thick.
7. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.

Instructions for salad:
1. Add greens, cucumber, and carrot to a bowl.
2. Drizzle with olive oil and vinegar or lemon juice.
3. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper and toss.

These recipes match exactly Monday’s meals in your current plan.
