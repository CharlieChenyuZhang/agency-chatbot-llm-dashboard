## Quick Reference: Typical Hours & Admission (Check Before Visiting)

**Important:** Hours and admission details change regularly. Always verify on the official websites before your trip.

- **Miller Institute for Contemporary Art (Miller ICA)**  
  - Typical hours: Often Tue–Sun, afternoon-focused; closed Mondays.  
  - Admission: Generally free.

- **Carnegie Museum of Art (CMOA)**  
  - Typical hours: Open most days, often 10am–5pm; one late evening per week; closed Tuesdays or Mondays depending on season.  
  - Admission: Paid; discounts for students, seniors; sometimes free evenings.

- **Pittsburgh Glass Center**  
  - Typical hours: Usually open most days with afternoon/evening hours; closed early week some days.  
  - Admission: Gallery often free; classes/demos extra.

- **Contemporary Craft (Lawrenceville)**  
  - Typical hours: Daytime hours, often Tue–Sat; closed Sun/Mon.  
  - Admission: Generally free.

- **The Frick Art Museum**  
  - Typical hours: Open most days except Mondays; 10am–5pm range.  
  - Admission: Museum sometimes free or suggested donation; special exhibits may be ticketed.

- **Wood Street Galleries**  
  - Typical hours: Often Wed–Sun afternoons/evenings; can vary by exhibition.  
  - Admission: Free.

- **SPACE Gallery (Downtown)**  
  - Typical hours: Dependent on exhibition; often Wed–Sun afternoons.  
  - Admission: Free.

- **August Wilson African American Cultural Center**  
  - Typical hours: Vary; galleries generally daytime/early evening.  
  - Admission: Varies by exhibition; some paid, some free.

- **The Andy Warhol Museum**  
  - Typical hours: Usually open most days; often 10am–5pm with one late night; closed Mondays some seasons.  
  - Admission: Paid; discounts for students/seniors; sometimes free or reduced admission programs.

- **Mattress Factory Museum of Contemporary Art**  
  - Typical hours: Typically open most days except major holidays; afternoon-heavy.  
  - Admission: Paid; discounts available; sometimes free or reduced community days.

- **Silver Eye Center for Photography**  
  - Typical hours: Usually Wed–Sat afternoons; closed Sun–Tue.  
  - Admission: Generally free or suggested donation.
