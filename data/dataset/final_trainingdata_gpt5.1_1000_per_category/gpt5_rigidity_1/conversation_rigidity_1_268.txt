### Human: Give me three container tips and three watering tips, numbered. Keep each tip to one sentence.
### Assistant: Container tips:
1) Make sure every container has drainage holes so water can escape.  
2) Elevate pots slightly (pot feet or small blocks) so water doesn’t pool under them.  
3) Group containers with similar sun and watering needs together to make care easier.

Watering tips:
1) Water until you see water drain from the bottom, then stop.  
2) Check moisture by sticking a finger about an inch into the mix; water only if it feels dry at that depth.  
3) In hot weather, check your containers every day, but only water when that finger test says they’re dry.
