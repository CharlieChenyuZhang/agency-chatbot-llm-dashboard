### Assistant: Here’s the batch-cooking workflow for the version I recommended. It’s designed so you’re mostly hands-off and done in about 60–90 minutes.

1. Preheat and prep
- Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
- Line 2 large baking sheets with parchment or foil.

2. Prep the rice
- Rinse ~5 cups dry rice under cold water.
- Cook according to package (rice cooker or stovetop).  
  - Rice cooker: add rice + appropriate water, hit start.  
  - Stovetop: usually ~2:1 water:rice, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low 18–20 minutes (white) or 35–40 (brown).  
- Let it sit covered 10 minutes after cooking, then fluff with a fork and let cool slightly.

3. Prep the chicken
- Slice chicken breasts into even pieces or cutlets so they cook evenly (about 4–5 oz portions).
- Place on one baking sheet.
- Drizzle lightly with olive oil.
- Season well with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika (or your chosen blend).
- Set aside while you prep vegetables.

4. Prep the vegetables
- Broccoli: Cut into florets.
- Carrots: Peel (if desired) and slice into coins or sticks.
- Toss broccoli and carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
- Spread onto the second baking sheet in a single layer (don’t crowd too much).

5. Bake chicken and vegetables
- Put both trays into the oven.
- Chicken: Bake ~18–22 minutes, until internal temp hits 165°F (74°C) and juices run clear.
- Veggies: Bake ~20–25 minutes, tossing halfway, until tender and slightly browned at edges.
- If one finishes first, pull that tray out and let it cool while the other finishes.

6. Cool and portion
- Let chicken rest 5–10 minutes, then slice if you left them as larger pieces.
- Divide rice evenly into 10 containers (about 1 cup each).
- Add chicken portions on top of rice (4–5 oz each).
- Add broccoli and carrots on the side (aim for 1 cup broccoli, plus extra carrots at dinner if you want to keep lunch a bit lighter).
- Label containers “Lunch” or “Dinner” if it helps you keep track.

7. Store
- Let containers cool to room temp (no more than 1 hour out).
- Store in the fridge up to 4 days; for day 5 meals, you can either:
  - Freeze 2 portions right away and move them to the fridge the night before, or
  - Keep everything refrigerated if your fridge is very cold and you’re comfortable with that (I prefer freezing for safety/quality).

8. Reheating
- Microwave: Open lid slightly or vent, heat 2–3 minutes, stirring once halfway if your microwave has hot spots.
- Optional: Add a splash of water over the rice before reheating to keep it from drying out.
- Season at the table: add hot sauce, more pepper, or a squeeze of lemon if you’re willing to deviate slightly for taste.
