### Assistant: Barbell bench press  
Lie on a flat bench with feet planted, grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, lower it under control to mid-chest, and press back up while keeping your wrists straight. Keep your shoulder blades pulled back and down and your lower back slightly arched.  

Incline dumbbell press  
Set the bench to about a 30–45° incline, hold dumbbells over your chest with palms forward, and lower them until your elbows are about 90°. Press the weights back up while keeping your shoulders down and chest up.  

Seated dumbbell shoulder press  
Sit upright with back supported, hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms forward, and press them overhead until your arms are almost straight. Lower under control to the starting position without flaring your lower back.  

Dumbbell lateral raises  
Stand tall holding dumbbells at your sides, slight bend in the elbows, and raise the weights out to your sides until they reach shoulder height. Keep your torso still and lead with your elbows instead of your hands.  

Cable triceps pushdowns  
Using a cable machine with a straight bar or rope, keep elbows tight to your sides and push the handle down until your arms are straight. Control the weight back up until your forearms are about parallel to the floor.  

Overhead dumbbell triceps extension  
Sit or stand holding one dumbbell with both hands overhead, arms nearly straight, then bend your elbows to lower the weight behind your head. Extend your arms back up without letting your elbows flare too far out.
