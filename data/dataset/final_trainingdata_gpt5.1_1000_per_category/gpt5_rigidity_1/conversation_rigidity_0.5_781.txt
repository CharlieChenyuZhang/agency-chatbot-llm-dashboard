## Month 1 Content-Only Plan

### Weekly structure (repeat for 4 weeks)

**Total weekly time: ~5–6 hours**

**Day 1 (1.5–2 hours): Long-form piece**
- Write **1 explainer article (800–1,200 words)** on a topic your ideal client cares about.  
  Examples (if you focus on coaches/consultants):
  - “How to explain your coaching framework so clients actually ‘get it’”
  - “A 3-step way to turn client questions into content topics”
  - “How to write an About page that sounds like you—but clearer”

- Post this on:
  - **Medium** *or*
  - A simple blog (if you’re comfortable setting one up later; Medium is fine to start)

**Day 2 (1 hour): Turn article into 1–2 LinkedIn posts**
- Take the main idea and break it into:
  - 1 short “tip” post (5–7 lines)
  - 1 “before/after” clarity example

Example post structure:
> Most coaches explain [concept] like this: [messy version].  
> Here’s a clearer way: [simple rewrite].  
> When your content is this clear, clients spend less time confused and more time saying “this is exactly what I’ve been struggling with.”  
> If you want help turning complex ideas into simple content, I write blog posts and emails for coaches and consultants.

**Day 3 (1–1.5 hours): Write another short-form piece**
- Either:
  - A second LinkedIn post, or
  - A short Medium article (500–800 words)
- Focus on one idea: common confusion your clients’ audience has, and how to explain it simply.

**Day 4 (1 hour): Profile + light engagement**
- Optimize your **LinkedIn profile**:
  - Headline: “Freelance writer helping coaches & consultants explain complex ideas simply”
  - About: 3–5 lines explaining:
    - Who you help
    - What you write
    - Why clarity matters
    - How to contact you
- Engage for 15–20 minutes:
  - Comment (genuinely) on 3–5 posts from coaches/consultants/creators in your space.
  - Comments like: adding a clear example, simplifying their point, or giving a mini-framework.
  - This is still not outreach; it’s just being visible where your potential clients hang out.

**Day 5 (optional 0.5–1 hour): Review + small improvements**
- Track:
  - What posts got any engagement (even 2–3 likes/comments count)
  - Which topics feel easiest for you to write about
- Adjust:
  - Do more of what feels easy and gets even slight traction.
