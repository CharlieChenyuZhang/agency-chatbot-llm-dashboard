4) Difference (how much over or under)

`=IF(A2="","", B2 - C2)`

- Negative D2 = over budget (overspent)

### Human: I want the category targets to change automatically on the sheet so I don’t have to think about it. Is that possible?

### Assistant: Fully auto-changing targets is possible inside the sheet, but there are two caveats:

1) You lose a clear record of your *original* plan for the month unless we keep a separate column.
2) If you paste new transactions late in the month, the targets will keep shifting, which can be confusing.

A balanced approach is:
- Keep “Planned Target” (what you set at the start).
- Have an “Adjusted Target” column that shows what you effectively *should* treat as your new limit after reallocation.

You still see both, and the sheet adjusts automatically.

Here’s how to do that:

In `Budget Summary`, add:

- F1: `Planned Target`
- G1: `Adjusted Target`

1) Planned Target (copy of original)
- F2: `=B2` (drag down)

2) Adjusted Target rules for the three “flexible goal/fun” categories:
   - For all other categories (rent, groceries, etc.), Adjusted Target = Planned Target.
   - For Extra Student Loan and Emergency Savings, Adjusted Target decreases if Fun overspends.

Example formula for `Adjusted Target` (G column):

In G2:
`=IF(A2="","",
  IF(OR(A2="Extra Student Loan", A2="Emergency Savings", A2="Fun/Entertainment"),
     F2 + <adjustment_term_for_this_category>,
     F2
  )
)`

We then define specific adjustment terms in helper cells (like previous H2, H3). To keep it practical in one step might get messy, so a clearer option is:

- Use helper cells to compute:
  - Fun Overspend (H1)
  - From Extra Loan (H2)
  - From Emergency Savings (H3)
- Then reference those in `Adjusted Target`.

Assume:
- H1 = Fun Overspend (negative or 0)
- H2 = Amount to reduce from Extra Loan (positive or 0)
- H3 = Amount to reduce from Emergency Savings (positive or 0)

Then:

For Extra Student Loan row (say that’s row 8):
In G8:
`=F8 - $H$2`

For Emergency Savings row (say row 9):
In G9:
`=F9 - $H$3`

For Fun/Entertainment row (say row 10):
In G10:
`=F10 - $H$1`  

Note: H1 is negative, so subtracting it effectively increases your “used” space; if you prefer, we can simply leave Fun’s Adjusted Target equal to Planned Target and treat overspend as shown only in the “Difference” column. That’s often clearer.

Everything else:
In G2 (for all non-special categories):
`=IF(OR(A2="Extra Student Loan", A2="Emergency Savings", A2="Fun/Entertainment"), G2, F2)`

You’d adjust the exact formulas for your row numbers, but this structure will:
- Keep your original planned numbers.
- Automatically shrink Extra Loan and Savings targets when you overspend on Fun.
