### Human: I do have a fridge and microwave. This looks manageable. How will I know when to progress the weights in my workouts?
### Assistant: Use a simple, clear rule so you don’t have to overthink it.

**Progression rule (for each exercise):**  
- If your target is, say, 3 sets of 10–12 reps:
  - When you can do **12 reps in all 3 sets with good form** at a given weight,  
  - Next time you do that exercise, **increase the weight slightly** (the smallest jump available).
- After increasing the weight, you might drop back to, for example, 8–9 reps per set. Then work back up toward 12 again.

**For core holds (like planks):**
- When 3 sets of 40 seconds feel solid and you could do more, add 5–10 seconds per set, or make the exercise a bit harder (e.g., move from standard plank to plank with shoulder taps).

**Form comes first:**  
If increasing the weight makes your form noticeably worse (swinging, not controlling the negative, pain in joints vs. muscle), drop back down and stay at the lower weight until you own it.
