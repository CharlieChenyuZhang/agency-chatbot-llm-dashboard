### Human: Okay, then give me simple prep instructions so each of these meals can be made in under 30 minutes.
### Assistant: I’ll give brief, direct prep steps for each distinct meal, focused on keeping prep under 30 minutes. I won’t add anything beyond that.

Day 1  
Breakfast – Yogurt parfait:
- Add yogurt to a bowl or glass, layer berries, oats/granola, and nuts/seeds. Takes 5 minutes.

Lunch – Quinoa–chickpea salad:
- Cook quinoa (about 15 minutes; you can batch‑cook and chill).
- Rinse chickpeas.
- Chop cucumber, tomato, bell pepper.
- Toss everything with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Total active time ~10–15 minutes if quinoa is pre‑cooked.

Snack – Apple with nut butter:
- Slice apple, serve with nut butter. 3–5 minutes.

Dinner – Tofu stir‑fry with brown rice:
- Start brown rice (instant or pre‑cooked helps; otherwise pre‑cook and reheat).
- Press and cube tofu; sauté in a little oil until lightly browned.
- Add chopped vegetables, garlic, and ginger; stir‑fry.
- Add a splash of soy sauce/tamari.  
Total active cooking ~15–20 minutes if rice is pre‑cooked.

---

Day 2  
Breakfast – Oatmeal:
- Cook oats in water or milk (5–10 minutes).
- Top with sliced banana, chia/flax, and nut butter. Total ~10 minutes.

Lunch – Lentil soup + salad:
- Use quick‑cooking lentils; simmer lentils with chopped onion, carrot, celery, tomato, water/broth, salt, and spices for ~20–25 minutes.
- While soup simmers, throw together salad and toast bread. Active time ~10–15 minutes.

Snack – Veg sticks with hummus:
- Slice carrots and cucumber.
- Serve with hummus. 5–10 minutes.

Dinner – Veggie fajitas:
- Slice peppers and onions; sauté in a little oil with spices.
- Warm black beans in a small pan.
- Warm tortillas in a dry pan.
- Fill tortillas with beans, peppers/onions, salsa, and avocado.  
Total time ~20 minutes.

---

Day 3  
Breakfast – Smoothie + toast:
- Add spinach, berries, banana, yogurt/protein, and liquid to blender; blend until smooth.
- Toast bread.  
Total time ~10 minutes.

Lunch – Hummus & roasted‑veg wrap:
- Slice vegetables; quickly sauté or roast them (about 10–15 minutes).
- Spread hummus on wrap, add warm vegetables and greens, roll up.  
Total time ~15–20 minutes.

Snack – Nuts and orange:
- Portion nuts and peel orange. 3–5 minutes.

Dinner – Baked sweet potato with beans, salsa, yogurt, broccoli:
- Microwave sweet potato (5–8 minutes, turning halfway) or pre‑bake and reheat.
- Heat black beans in a small pan.
- Steam or microwave broccoli (5–7 minutes).
- Split sweet potato, top with beans, salsa, and yogurt; serve with broccoli.  
Total time ~20 minutes with microwave.

Batch‑cooking quinoa, lentils, rice, and roasted vegetables ahead of time will keep all these well under 30 minutes.
