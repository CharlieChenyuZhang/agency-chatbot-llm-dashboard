Over time, I stopped asking, “How much can I get done today?” and started asking, “How can I work in a way that I can actually sustain?”

3) One meaningful win per day  
On my best “productive” days, I used to finish a lot of small tasks but avoid the one thing that actually mattered. Now I define a successful day as: I made progress on one meaningful thing. Emails and small tasks still get done, but they’re secondary. This keeps me from overloading my days and then burning out on things that don’t really move the needle.
