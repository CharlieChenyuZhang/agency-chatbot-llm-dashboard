**Deck:** Biology Exam – Core  
**Tag:** `mitosis_meiosis`

1. Q: What is the main purpose of mitosis?  
   A: To produce two genetically identical diploid daughter cells for growth, repair, and asexual reproduction.

2. Q: What is the main purpose of meiosis?  
   A: To produce four genetically distinct haploid gametes for sexual reproduction.

3. Q: List the main phases of mitosis in order.  
   A: Prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase (often followed by cytokinesis).

4. Q: In which phase of mitosis do chromosomes line up at the cell’s equator?  
   A: Metaphase.

5. Q: In which phase of mitosis do sister chromatids separate and move to opposite poles?  
   A: Anaphase.

6. Q: During which phase of meiosis does crossing over typically occur?  
   A: Prophase I.

7. Q: What is crossing over?  
   A: The exchange of genetic material between non-sister chromatids of homologous chromosomes, increasing genetic variation.

8. Q: How many daughter cells are produced at the end of mitosis, and what is their ploidy?  
   A: Two diploid (2n) daughter cells.

9. Q: How many daughter cells are produced at the end of meiosis, and what is their ploidy?  
   A: Four haploid (n) daughter cells.

10. Q: In which meiotic division (I or II) do homologous chromosomes separate?  
    A: Meiosis I.

11. Q: In which meiotic division (I or II) do sister chromatids separate?  
    A: Meiosis II.
