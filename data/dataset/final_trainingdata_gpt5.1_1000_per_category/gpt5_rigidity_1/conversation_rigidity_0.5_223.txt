On **honesty vs hype**:

- **Yes**, a lot of channels lean into hyped titles and thumbnails.
- There’s a line between **strong hooks** and **misleading clickbait**.

You don’t need to be boring to be honest:

- Good: “These $50 Earbuds Shocked Me (In a Good Way)” → if they actually did.  
- Misleading: “These $50 Earbuds DESTROY AirPods Pro” when they clearly don’t.

Why I still recommend staying on the honest side:

1. **Retention & trust:**  
   - If people click for wild claims and the video doesn’t match, they drop off early → YouTube pushes you less.
   - Long‑term, viewers trust you and come back because your titles reflect reality.

2. **Brand safety:**  
   - Sponsors prefer creators who aren’t known for fake clickbait.
   - Being “the honest budget tech channel” is a strong brand in itself.

So yes, push your titles to be **exciting, specific, and benefit‑driven**, but avoid outright false promises. Think:
- “Best I’ve Tried Under $50” instead of “Best in the World.”  
- “Surprisingly Good” instead of “Life‑Changing.”
