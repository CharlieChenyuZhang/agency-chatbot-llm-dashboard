### Human: That’s enough for now. One more thing: how do I avoid everything relying on me? I don’t want to be the only one posting forever.

### Assistant: The goal is to shift from “host-centered” to “member-powered” over time. You can design for that from the start:

1. **Name and empower a few “core members”**
   - Identify 3–5 people who are already relatively active or aligned with your vision.
   - Privately invite them into lightweight roles, like:
     - “Feedback Champions” – always try to reply in #feedback-showcase.
     - “Welcome Crew” – say hi to new members and ask what they’re working on.
   - Keep expectations small and clear (e.g., 10–15 minutes a couple of times a week).

2. **Make rituals shared, not personal**
   - Instead of “My Friday Wins Thread,” call it “Community Friday Wins.”
   - Occasionally ask: “Who wants to host next week’s [clinic/check-in]?” and support them with a template.

3. **Use templates you can hand off**
   - Turn your check-in, feedback, and clinic messages into a short doc or pinned message:
     - “Here’s the standard Monday post.”
     - “Here’s the standard clinic format.”
   - Then it’s easy for someone else to step in when you’re busy.

4. **Reward contribution visibly (but lightly)**
   - Shout out helpful behavior:
     - “Big thanks to @X and @Y for all the feedback this week.”
   - Consider simple roles/badges: “Feedback Hero,” “Prototype Machine,” etc.
   - This encourages peer-to-peer support without needing prizes or complex systems.

5. **Set expectations openly**
   - Pin a short message like:
     > “This community works best when we all pitch in. If you post something, try to reply to at least one other person too. Your participation keeps this place alive.”
