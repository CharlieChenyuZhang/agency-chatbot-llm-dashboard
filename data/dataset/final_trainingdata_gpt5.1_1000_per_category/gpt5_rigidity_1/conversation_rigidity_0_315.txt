### Human: Fine. If I did a home setup like you keep suggesting, what exactly should I buy and what workouts should I do?
### Assistant: That’s exactly the direction I prefer.

Starter home setup (budget-conscious but effective):
- Adjustable dumbbells (up to at least 40–50 lbs each if possible)
- A set of resistance bands with door anchor
- A pull-up bar for a doorway (or bodyweight rows under a sturdy table if that’s impossible)
- Optional: a yoga mat for floor work

Simple 3-day full-body routine (about 45 minutes):

Day A
- Goblet squats (dumbbell)
- Push-ups or dumbbell bench press (floor)
- One-arm dumbbell rows
- Romanian deadlifts (dumbbells)
- Plank holds

Day B
- Split squats or lunges
- Overhead dumbbell press
- Pull-ups or band-assisted pull-ups (or inverted rows)
- Hip thrusts or glute bridges
- Side planks

Alternate A/B three times per week (e.g., Mon A, Wed B, Fri A; next week start with B). Add weight or reps slowly over time.

Once you’ve run that for 6–8 weeks and feel consistent, *then* I’d consider adding a gym for heavier barbells—if you still feel you need it.
