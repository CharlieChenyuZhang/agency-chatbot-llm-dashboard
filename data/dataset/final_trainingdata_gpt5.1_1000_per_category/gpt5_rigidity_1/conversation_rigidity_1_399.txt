### Human: Thanks. Now give me a very simple chicken pasta recipe that uses only those tools and what I already have. Just list ingredients and numbered steps, no explanations.
### Assistant: Ingredients:  
- 150 g dried pasta  
- 150 g boneless chicken (breast or thigh)  
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter  
- 1 garlic clove (optional)  
- Salt and pepper  
- 20–30 g grated cheese (optional)  
- 300–400 ml water (for boiling)  

Steps:  
1. Fill the small saucepan with water, add a pinch of salt, and bring it to a boil.  
2. Add pasta to the boiling water and cook according to package time until tender.  
3. While pasta cooks, place frying pan on medium heat and add olive oil or butter.  
4. Cut chicken into small bite-size pieces on the cutting board with the chef’s knife.  
5. Add chicken to the hot pan, season with salt and pepper, and cook until no pink remains.  
6. (Optional) Finely chop garlic and add it to the pan in the last 1–2 minutes of cooking.  
7. Drain the cooked pasta with the saucepan lid slightly tilted to pour off water.  
8. Add drained pasta to the pan with chicken and toss together using the silicone spatula.  
9. (Optional) Sprinkle grated cheese on top and serve.

### Human: Actually, before I try that, tell me how to hold a knife safely so I don’t cut myself.
### Assistant: Hold the knife handle with your dominant hand, wrapping your fingers around the handle and placing your thumb and index finger pinched on the blade just in front of the handle for control. With your other hand (the “guiding hand”), curl your fingertips under and let your knuckles guide the side of the knife so the blade never meets your fingertips. Keep the knife tip on the board when possible and use a smooth rocking motion instead of chopping straight up and down.

### Human: Sorry, ignore that. Back to cooking: how do I scramble eggs? Please be very detailed and include temperatures, and use metric units.
### Assistant: Crack 2–3 eggs into a bowl, add a small pinch of salt, whisk well with a fork until the whites and yolks are fully combined, then heat a nonstick pan over medium-low heat (about 120–140 °C surface temperature if you have an infrared thermometer) and add 5–10 g butter so it melts and lightly coats the pan. Pour in the eggs, wait a few seconds until the edges just begin to set, then gently push the eggs from the edges toward the center with a spatula, repeating around the pan while pausing occasionally so soft curds form; when the eggs look slightly underdone but mostly set, remove the pan from heat and keep stirring for 10–20 seconds until they finish cooking with the residual heat, then taste and add a bit more salt if needed.

### Human: For Wednesday’s pan-seared chicken with boiled potatoes, give me a recipe, but only in this exact format: “Ingredients:” list, then “Steps:” list with exactly 6 steps.
### Assistant: Ingredients:  
- 1 chicken breast (150–200 g)  
- 2 medium potatoes  
- 1 tbsp olive oil or 10 g butter  
- Salt  
- Black pepper  
- Optional: dried herbs (like oregano or thyme)  

Steps:  
1. Peel (optional) and cut the potatoes into evenly sized chunks, then place them in a pot, cover with cold water, add a pinch of salt, and bring to a boil.  
2. Reduce the heat to a gentle boil and cook the potatoes until a fork slides in easily, about 10–15 minutes, then drain and set aside.  
3. While the potatoes cook, pat the chicken breast dry, season both sides with salt, pepper, and optional dried herbs.  
4. Heat the olive oil or butter in a frying pan over medium heat until hot but not smoking.  
5. Place the chicken in the pan and cook on each side for about 5–7 minutes (depending on thickness) until the outside is browned and the inside is no longer pink.  
6. Let the chicken rest on a plate for a few minutes, then serve it alongside the boiled potatoes, adding a little salt to the potatoes to taste.
