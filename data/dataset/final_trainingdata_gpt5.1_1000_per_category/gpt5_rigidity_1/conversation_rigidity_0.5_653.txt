To make this credible rather than “I just want 25% more,” it’s worth doing at least minimal research on:

- A few salary sites (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi if applicable, Payscale, LinkedIn Salary).
- Adjusted for your location and company size.

Even if the data’s imperfect, being able to say “From what I’ve seen, market comp for Marketing Manager roles here seems to fall roughly in the [X–Y] range” makes you sound informed, not arbitrary.
