### Assistant: Here’s a very simple first recipe: Garlic Butter Chicken with Rice and Frozen Veg

Serves: 1

Ingredients:
- 1 small chicken breast (about the size of your palm)
- 1/2 cup uncooked white rice
- 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
- 1 tablespoon butter (or oil)
- 1 clove garlic (or 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Optional: a squeeze of lemon, dried herbs (like Italian seasoning)

Steps:

1. Cook the rice:
- Rinse 1/2 cup rice under cold water in a bowl, then drain.
- Put rice in the pot with 1 cup water and a pinch of salt.
- Put lid on, bring to a boil on high heat.
- Once boiling, turn heat to low and simmer 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
- After 15 minutes, turn off heat and let it sit (lid on) while you cook the chicken.

2. Prepare the chicken:
- Pat the chicken dry with paper towel.
- Sprinkle both sides with 1/2 teaspoon salt and 1/4 teaspoon pepper.
- Mince the garlic (if using fresh).

3. Cook the chicken:
- Put your pan on medium heat.
- Add 1 tablespoon butter (or oil). Let it melt and get hot (it should lightly sizzle if you touch a corner of the chicken to it).
- Lay the chicken breast flat in the pan.
- Cook about 5–7 minutes on the first side, without moving it, until the bottom is browned.
- Flip the chicken. Add minced garlic to the pan around the chicken (not on top).
- Cook another 5–7 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through (no pink in the center; juices run clear). If it’s browning too fast, lower the heat.

4. Cook the vegetables:
- While the chicken finishes, put frozen vegetables in a small bowl.
- Microwave 2–3 minutes until hot, or:
- Or put them in a small saucepan with a splash of water, cover, and heat on medium until hot and tender, then drain.

5. Finish:
- Turn off the heat under the chicken.
- If you have lemon or dried herbs, add a small squeeze of lemon and a pinch of herbs into the pan and tilt the pan to coat the chicken in the buttery juices.
- Fluff the rice with a fork.
- Serve the chicken on a plate with rice and vegetables on the side. Spoon any buttery garlic from the pan over the chicken and rice.

### Human: That seems okay. I’m not sure how to tell if the chicken is done without cutting it open. What should I do?
### Assistant: The most reliable way is a thermometer, but since you may not have one, use these two checks together:

1) Firmness test:
- Press the thickest part of the chicken with a clean finger or utensil.
- Raw feels very soft and squishy.
- Done feels firm but still slightly springy, not rock-hard.

2) Small cut test:
- Take the chicken out of the pan onto a plate.
- Make a small cut in the thickest part with your knife.
- Look inside:
  - If you see any clear pink, or the texture looks jelly-like, it is not done. Put it back in the pan and cook a few more minutes.
  - If the meat is white all the way through and the juices are clear (not pink), it is done.

### Assistant: Day 1: Garlic Butter Chicken with Rice and Veg (as before)

Ingredients:
- 1 small chicken breast
- 1/2 cup uncooked white rice
- 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
- 1 tablespoon butter or oil
- 1 clove garlic or 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Optional: lemon, dried herbs

Steps:
1) Cook rice: 1/2 cup rice + 1 cup water + pinch salt; boil, then low 15 min, rest.
2) Season chicken with salt and pepper.
3) Cook chicken: pan medium, butter, 6 min first side, flip, add garlic, 5–6 min second side; check doneness; cook more if needed.
4) Heat frozen veg in microwave 2–3 min or in pot with a little water, then drain.
5) Serve chicken with rice and veg, spoon pan juices on top.

Day 2: One-Pan Sausage, Potatoes, and Veg

Ingredients:
- 1–2 uncooked sausages (any you like)
- 2 small potatoes
- 1 cup baby carrots or sliced regular carrots
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Optional: dried herbs (Italian seasoning or thyme)

Steps:
1) Heat oven to 400°F (200°C).
2) Wash potatoes, cut into small cubes (about 1.5–2 cm).
3) On a baking sheet, add potatoes and carrots. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle salt, pepper, optional herbs. Toss.
4) Spread in a single layer. Place sausages on top.
5) Bake 25 minutes.
6) Flip sausages and stir veg. Bake another 10–15 minutes, until potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork and sausages are browned and cooked through.
7) Serve everything from the tray.

Day 3: Simple Tomato Pasta with Tuna

Ingredients:
- 3 oz (about 85 g) dry pasta
- 1 small can tuna in water or oil, drained
- 1 cup jarred tomato pasta sauce
- 1 tablespoon oil (only if tuna is in water)
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (for pasta water)
- 1 clove garlic or 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Optional: grated cheese

Steps:
1) Boil a pot of water. Add 1/2 teaspoon salt.
2) Add pasta. Cook according to package directions, stirring sometimes, until tender.
3) While pasta cooks: in a pan on medium heat, add 1 tablespoon oil if tuna is in water. Add minced garlic (if using fresh) and cook 30–60 seconds.
4) Add drained tuna and break it up with a spoon.
5) Add tomato sauce, stir, and simmer on low 3–5 minutes.
6) Drain pasta, then add it to the pan with sauce. Toss to coat.
7) Serve, with grated cheese if using.
