### Assistant: Understood: 20 minutes/day, work presentations, okay with recording, minimal theory. Here’s a concrete 5-day repeating routine:

**Daily warm-up (3 minutes, every day)**
- 1 minute: Deep breathing (4 seconds in, 4 hold, 4 out).
- 2 minutes: Read a paragraph from an article out loud, focusing on clear articulation.

**Day 1 – Structure (17 minutes)**
- 5 min: Pick a work-related topic and write a simple outline (Opening – 2 points – Close).
- 10 min: Deliver a 3–4 minute talk using that outline, record it.
- 2 min: Rewatch the first 60–90 seconds and write down 1 thing to keep, 1 thing to change.

**Day 2 – Clarity & Conciseness (17 minutes)**
- 5 min: Take yesterday’s outline and shorten each point to one sentence.
- 8 min: Deliver a 2–3 minute “executive summary” version, record it.
- 4 min: Rewatch and note filler words (“um,” “like,” “you know”). Count them and write the number.

**Day 3 – Voice (17 minutes)**
- 4 min: Read a short paragraph 3 times:
  - 1st: Slower than normal
  - 2nd: With extra energy
  - 3rd: Mark 3–4 words to emphasize
- 8 min: Deliver a 3–4 minute talk on a new work topic, focusing only on:
  - Speaking slightly slower
  - Emphasizing key words
- 5 min: Rewatch the middle 1 minute and check:
  - Could you hear every word?
  - Did your pitch/energy vary, or stay flat? Write one change for tomorrow.

**Day 4 – Body Language (17 minutes)**
- 3 min: Stand in front of the camera: neutral stance (feet shoulder-width, shoulders relaxed), hands resting lightly together. Practice this still, open posture.
- 9 min: Deliver a 3–4 minute talk; keep hands mostly between waist and chest, avoid fidgeting.
- 5 min: Watch on mute for 1 minute:
  - Are you rocking, swaying, or touching your face?
  - Are gestures visible and purposeful?
  Write 1 habit to reduce, 1 gesture that looks good.

**Day 5 – “Mini presentation” (17 minutes)**
- 5 min: Choose one of your earlier topics and refine the outline to:
  - Clear opening (hook + why it matters)
  - 2–3 key points
  - Clear close (summary + next step)
- 8 min: Deliver a 4–5 minute “mini presentation” as if to your real work audience, record it.
- 4 min: Watch start and end:
  - Did you state the purpose within 30 seconds?
  - Did you clearly signal the end?

Repeat this 5-day cycle each week, keeping the same structure but rotating topics that match your real work.

### Human: What are some psychological tricks to make audiences like me more? Keep answers focused only on things I can practice during my solo sessions.
### Assistant: Here are audience-likeability behaviors you can practice solo:

- Warm, brief smile at the start  
  - Practice: Look at the camera, pause 1–2 seconds, slight smile, then say your first line.

- “You-focused” language  
  - Practice rewriting openings to use “you/your” instead of “I/we” (e.g., “What this means for you today is…”).

- Empathy statements  
  - Practice 1–2 lines that show you understand their situation:
    - “I know you’re busy, so I’ll keep this focused on the decisions you need to make.”
    - “You’ve probably seen a lot of roadmap slides; I’ll highlight only what actually changed.”

- Brief relevance check  
  - Practice inserting a single sentence early:
    - “By the end of this update, you’ll know whether we’re on track for the Q3 launch.”

- Calm, steady pace  
  - Record yourself and listen: if you sound rushed, deliberately pause 1–2 seconds after key points.

During your solo recordings, deliberately script and rehearse these lines and behaviors so they become automatic in real meetings.
