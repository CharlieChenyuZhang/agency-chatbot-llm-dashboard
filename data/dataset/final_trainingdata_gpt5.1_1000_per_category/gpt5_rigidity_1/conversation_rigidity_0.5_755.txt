### Human: I want something repeatable. Could you give me a step-by-step method I can just follow every time I have a complex task?
### Assistant: Yes, we can build a repeatable method. I’ll keep it short enough to actually use, not a giant framework you’ll forget.

Here’s a 6-step problem-solving mini-playbook you can apply to most complex work tasks:

1. Restate the problem in one sentence  
   “The problem is to __________ so that __________ by __________.”

2. Identify constraints and success criteria  
   - Constraints: time, resources, approvals, rules  
   - Success: what would a “good enough” outcome look like?

3. Slice into 3–7 chunks  
   - Ask: “What are the major components or phases?”  
   - Give each chunk a verb + noun: e.g., “Map current process,” “Design checklist,” “Get sign-off.”

4. Choose the best next step  
   - Which chunk unblocks the most others?  
   - What’s a 30–60 minute concrete action you can do for that chunk?

5. Do–Review loop  
   - After taking that step, ask:
     - What did I learn?
     - What changed about my understanding of the problem?
     - What’s the new best next step?

6. Communicate simply  
   - Summarize in 3 bullets if you need to update someone:
     - What we’re trying to achieve  
     - What we’ve done  
     - What we’ll do next

You can literally keep this as a small note and run any new task through it.

If you’d like, we can rehearse this method with a different example (real or hypothetical), so it starts to become automatic instead of just “nice on paper.”
