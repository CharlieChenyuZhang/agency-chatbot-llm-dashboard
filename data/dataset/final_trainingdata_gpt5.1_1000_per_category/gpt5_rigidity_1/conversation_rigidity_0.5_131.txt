### Human: My voice is fairly deep and sometimes sounds boomy on mics. I also want a really radio-style sound. Can we make it super bassy?

### Assistant: We can give you a “radio” style sound, but pushing the bass too much will make it muddy and harder to understand—especially on phone speakers and cheap headphones.

A better approach is: controlled low end + clear mids + smooth highs. We’ll lean into your natural depth without overdoing it.

**Updated EQ suggestion for deep voice:**

1. **High-pass filter**
   - Frequency: **70–80 Hz**
   - Slope: 12 dB/oct
   - This keeps rumble and desk thumps from making your voice overly boomy.

2. **Low shelf (for controlled bass)**
   - Frequency: **100 Hz**
   - Gain: **+1 to +2 dB** (not more to start)
   - This adds some weight without mud.

3. **Mud reduction**
   - Bell cut at **200–250 Hz**
   - Gain: **-2 to -4 dB**
   - Q: ~1.0
   - This is where deep voices often get boxy/boomy.

4. **Clarity boost**
   - Bell boost at **3–4 kHz**
   - Gain: **+2 to +3 dB**
   - Q: ~1.0
   - Helps intelligibility, “cuts through” the mix.

5. **Presence/air (optional)**
   - High shelf at **8–10 kHz**
   - Gain: **+1–2 dB**
   - Adds a bit of sheen without harshness.

**Compressor tweak for “radio” tone:**
- Ratio: **3.5:1**
- Threshold: **-20 dB**
- Attack: 3–5 ms
- Release: 80–120 ms
- Make-up gain: adjust until your **average level in OBS is around -18 to -12 dB**, with peaks around -6 to -3 dB.

If you want more of that “in your ear” radio feel, we can also add **light parallel compression** or a **subtle exciter**, but that’s optional and can be overdone.

### Assistant: It’s better **not** to crank the interface gain and “fix it later.” High gain on the interface can add hiss and make the noise removal work harder, and if you clip at the hardware stage, OBS can’t fix that.

For a good balance with NVIDIA Broadcast:

**1. Interface gain (Scarlett Solo)**
- Set your mic distance: ~2 inches from your mouth.

- Turn the **gain knob** up until the **green “halo” LED** lights up solid green on your loudest normal phrases.

**2. OBS input level**
- With NVIDIA Broadcast Mic as input:
  - In OBS, watch the meter while talking normally.

- Close threshold: **-42 dB**
