**What to do if you fall more than a week behind**

     → Move it from **“must-read” to “optional if time allows”**.  
   - Keep your current week focused on the current material so you don’t stall indefinitely.

2. **Step 2 – Use one “reset week”**
   - Pick a week and:
     - Cut new reading by ~50%.  
     - Use that freed time to:
       - Skim missed chapters for key ideas (rather than reading line-by-line).  
       - Read summaries or chapter conclusions.  
   - The goal: **catch understanding, not pages**.
