### Assistant: I’ll give you a focused, step-by-step weekly routine using work-style examples.

Week 1–2: Clarify and break down problems  
1) Each workday, pick one real issue (small is fine).  
2) Write down, in 1–2 sentences: “The problem is…”  
3) Break it into 3–5 smaller questions. Example:  
   - What exactly happened?  
   - Who is affected?  
   - What deadline or constraint matters most?  
4) For each smaller question, write what you know vs. what you don’t know.  
5) Take one unknown and decide: “How can I find this out today?”

Week 3–4: Generate and compare options  
1) For each problem, list at least 3 possible actions (even imperfect ones).  
2) For each option, quickly note:
   - Best possible outcome  
   - Worst reasonable risk  
   - Effort level (low/medium/high)  
3) Choose the option with the best balance of impact vs. risk vs. effort.  
4) After acting, write 2–3 lines: “What worked / what didn’t / what I’d change next time.”

Week 5+: Build a repeatable checklist  
1) Turn the steps that work best for you into a short personal checklist you follow for most work problems.  
2) Use that checklist every time, and refine it weekly.
