### Human: I have a bad habit of endlessly tweaking things before I call them done. How do I avoid that and actually ship?

### Assistant: The key is to define “done” upfront and stick to it unless something is truly broken. For each project, you can:

1. Write a short “Definition of Done”:
   - Example for Project 1:
     - All pages exist and are linked.
     - Layout works on mobile and desktop (you’ve checked on your phone and one desktop size).
     - No obvious broken elements (overlapping text, unreadable buttons).
     - Deployed and accessible via a URL.

2. Timebox polishing:
   - e.g., “When the core is working, I allow myself one 2-hour polish session max, then I ship.”

3. Version your improvements:
   - Treat “shipping” as v1.0.
   - Make a list of v1.1 ideas (extra animations, better imagery, etc.) that you only do after v1 is live.
