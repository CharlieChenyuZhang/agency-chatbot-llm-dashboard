### Human: Sure, how would you lay out the monitors and keyboard on that 48" desk?

### Assistant: Here’s a simple layout that works well on a **48" x ~24" desk** for dual 27" monitors:

1. **Monitor positioning**  
   - Use a **dual monitor arm** if possible (clamp to the back edge).  
   - Place the **primary monitor** directly in front of you, centered on the desk.  
   - Angle the **secondary monitor** slightly toward you at about a **15–30° angle**.  
   - Top edges of the monitors should be **at or slightly below eye level**.

2. **Keyboard and mouse**  
   - Center the **keyboard** directly under the primary monitor.  
   - Place the **mouse** close to the keyboard to avoid reaching.  
   - Aim for **elbows at ~90°** when typing, shoulders relaxed.

3. **Drawer & legroom**  
   - Mount the under-desk drawer **off to one side**, roughly under where you don’t keep your knees most of the time.  
   - Make sure there’s a clear central space under the desk for your legs when both sitting and standing.

4. **Depth usage**  
   - Place monitors so there’s about **20–28" viewing distance** (roughly an arm’s length). On a 24" deep top, this usually means stands or arms near the very back edge.  
   - Keep everyday items (notebook, pen, phone) within a **comfortable arm reach zone** just in front of your keyboard.

This arrangement keeps things functional on a 48" width without feeling cramped and maintains good ergonomics both sitting and standing.
