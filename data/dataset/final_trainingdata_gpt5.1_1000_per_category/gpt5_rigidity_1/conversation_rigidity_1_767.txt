### Assistant: 1) Estimate BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor):
- Weight: 195 lb ≈ 88.5 kg  
- Height: 5'10" ≈ 178 cm  
- Age: 32  

BMR = 10×weight(kg) + 6.25×height(cm) − 5×age + 5  
= 10×88.5 + 6.25×178 − 5×32 + 5  
= 885 + 1,112.5 − 160 + 5  
= 1,842.5 ≈ 1,843 kcal

2) Activity factor (moderately active with gym): ~1.5  
TDEE ≈ 1,843 × 1.5 ≈ 2,764.5 ≈ 2,765 kcal

3) Deficit for 1–1.5 lb/week:
- 1 lb fat ≈ 3,500 kcal  
- Weekly deficit for 1–1.5 lb: 3,500–5,250 kcal

4) Target calories:
- Lower end: 2,765 − 750 ≈ 2,015 kcal
