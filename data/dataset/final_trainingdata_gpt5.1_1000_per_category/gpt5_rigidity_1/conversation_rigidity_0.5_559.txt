### Human: Let's talk about dominating pickup. I'm short and new, so I feel like I'll just get bullied. Can you give me a strategy to dominate even if my skills aren't great yet? I don't care if it's kind of cheesy, I just want to win.
### Assistant: You can be very impactful in pickup even with beginner skills by focusing on **smart, low-skill, high-impact habits**. Instead of “dominate” by flashy moves, think “dominate” by:
- Hustle
- Positioning
- Making everyone else’s life easier

Here’s a practical approach:

**1. Pick a simple role: high-energy guard**
Focus on:
- Bringing the ball up safely
- Passing quickly
- Cutting and relocating

You don’t need advanced moves, just:
- Strong, low dribble
- One reliable change-of-direction move (e.g., crossover or hesitation)

**2. On offense: do 3 simple things extremely well**
- **Set screens**: Most pickup players don’t do this.
  - Set solid screens for the best scorer on your team.
  - Then **roll or pop** to an open area for an easy shot or pass.
- **Cut hard to the basket**:
  - After you pass, immediately cut toward the rim or to an open spot.
  - Hands up, ready for the ball; even if you don’t get it, you create space.
- **Take smart shots only**:
  - Layups and wide-open shots from spots you’ve practiced.
  - Avoid forcing contested threes early on.

**3. On defense: be super annoying (in a good way)**
- Always pick up your man early, even at half-court.
- Focus on:
  - Staying low
  - Hands active in passing lanes
  - Boxing out on every shot (even against taller players, just get position and make contact)

**4. Communicate constantly**
- Call out:
  - Screens (“Screen right!”)
  - Switches
  - Who you’re guarding
- In pickup, even basic communication makes you look more “experienced” and makes teams love playing with you.

**5. One simple “signature” move**
Instead of trying to learn a ton:
- Pick **one go-to move** you practice daily:
  - Example: hard right-hand drive, jump stop, pump fake, finish
  - Or: in-and-out dribble → change speed → pull-up midrange from one favorite spot
- Your goal is to get to that move a few times per game.

You won’t instantly “dominate” like an advanced player in 30 days, but with this approach you can:
- Be one of the most useful players in almost any casual game
- Get picked for teams more often
- Annoy better players in a good way by out-hustling them
