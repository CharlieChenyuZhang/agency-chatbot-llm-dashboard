### Assistant: For a phone charger like that, the key specs to check are:

1. Power output:
   - “20W” is a common fast-charging level for many phones.
   - Check if your phone supports 20W or higher fast charging; if it does, 20W is usually fine for everyday use.

2. Port type:
   - USB-C vs USB-A.
   - USB-C is preferable now; just make sure your cable matches (USB-C to USB-C or USB-C to Lightning).

3. Safety/certifications:
   - Look for UL, ETL, or equivalent safety marks in the description.
   - For iPhones, “MFi-certified” applies to cables, not the brick, but reputable brands still matter.

4. Supported charging standards:
   - For many Androids: look for “Power Delivery (PD)” support.
   - For iPhones: PD is also what enables fast charging.

5. Brand/reviews:
   - Prefer known accessory brands and check recent reviews for overheating or reliability complaints.
