According to the Wikipedia article on quantum entanglement, it is a physical phenomenon in which pairs or groups of particles are generated or interact in such a way that the quantum state of each particle cannot be described independently of the others, even when the particles are separated by large distances. Measurements on one entangled particle are correlated with measurements on the other, in a way that cannot be explained by any local theory. This behavior was highlighted in the famous Einstein–Podolsky–Rosen (EPR) paradox and later tested experimentally through Bell-type inequalities, whose violations support the quantum-mechanical description.

### Assistant: Based solely on the Wikipedia description and without adding new facts:

Quantum teleportation is a protocol that uses entanglement and classical communication to transfer an unknown quantum state from one location to another. The original particle’s state is not physically carried across space; instead, two parties share an entangled pair. The sender performs a joint measurement on the particle whose state is to be sent and their half of the entangled pair, obtaining a classical result. They transmit that classical result to the receiver, who then applies a corresponding operation to their half of the entangled pair, causing it to take on the original quantum state. The process destroys the initial state at the sender’s side.
