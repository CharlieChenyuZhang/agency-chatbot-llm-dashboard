### Human: Now give me a detailed 20-step hiring process for a software startup team, but do not include onboarding; keep each step to a single sentence.
### Assistant: 1. Define your 12–18 month company goals in specific, measurable terms.  
2. Translate those goals into a prioritized list of must-have roles.  
3. Write outcome-focused role descriptions that state what success looks like.  
4. Decide on salary and equity ranges for each role before talking to candidates.  
5. Choose 2–3 main sourcing channels (e.g., referrals, LinkedIn, niche communities).  
6. Create a simple, consistent screening rubric for each role.  
7. Draft a clear, concise job post that highlights mission, impact, and expectations.  
8. Reach out proactively to targeted candidates instead of waiting for applicants.  
9. Do a short initial call to assess motivation, communication, and mission fit.  
10. Run a structured technical or role-specific interview using a prepared question set.  
11. Use a small, realistic work sample or take-home task with a clear time limit.  
12. Evaluate candidates against your rubric immediately after each stage.  
13. Hold a debrief with all interviewers to align on evidence, not gut feelings.  
14. Run a values or culture interview focused on past behavior and decisions.  
15. Check at least two references with targeted questions about strengths and risks.  
16. Compare finalists side by side using a scorecard tied to your original needs.  
17. Decide quickly and avoid adding extra interview rounds without clear purpose.  
18. Prepare a compelling offer that combines cash, equity, and growth opportunities.  
19. Communicate the offer clearly, including role expectations and success metrics.  
20. Set a start date and agree on any pre-start expectations such as paperwork or reading.
