### Human: Give me a detailed explanation of spaced repetition in no more than 120 words. One concise paragraph only.

### Assistant: Spaced repetition is a memorization technique that schedules reviews of information at increasing intervals over time, just before you are likely to forget it. Instead of rereading everything frequently, you test yourself on small chunks, and items you recall easily are reviewed less often, while difficult items appear more frequently. This timing leverages the spacing effect: information reviewed right before it would be forgotten becomes much more durable. Typically implemented with flashcards or software, spaced repetition helps you retain large volumes of material with less total study time, making it especially effective for formulas, definitions, languages, and conceptual frameworks that must be remembered accurately over long periods.
