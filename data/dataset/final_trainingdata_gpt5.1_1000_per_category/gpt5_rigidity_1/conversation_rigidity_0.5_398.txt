**Nutrition for a 12-Week Hypertrophy-Focused Block**

You’ll get better “muscle vs fat” trade-offs with a **moderate surplus** rather than “eat everything.”

**1. Calorie Target**

- If you know your maintenance:  
  - Aim for **+200–300 kcal/day** above maintenance to start.
- If you don’t:
  - Rough estimate: **14–16 kcal per pound of body weight** for maintenance if you’re moderately active and an intermediate lifter.  
  - Then add ~200–300 kcal to that and adjust based on weekly weight changes.

Monitor:
- Aim for **~0.25–0.5% of bodyweight gain per week.**  
  - At 180 lb, that’s about 0.45–0.9 lb/week.
- If you’re gaining more than that for 3+ weeks, dial back calories by ~100–150/day.

**2. Protein, Carbs, Fats**

- **Protein:**  
  - ~0.8–1.0 g per lb of bodyweight (e.g., 160–200 g/day if 200 lb).  
  - Spread over 3–5 meals.

- **Fats:**  
  - ~0.3–0.4 g per lb of bodyweight as a baseline.  
  - Don’t drop below ~0.25 g/lb to support hormones and health.

- **Carbs:**  
  - Fill the rest of your calories with carbs, especially around training.
  - Higher carbs help performance and recovery, which matters for progressive overload.

**3. Meal Timing (Practical, Not Obsessive)**

- Pre-workout (1–3 hours before):
  - Protein + carbs, low-ish fat (e.g., chicken + rice, oats + whey).
- Post-workout (within a few hours):
  - Protein + carbs again.
- Distribute protein fairly evenly across the day.

**4. Quality vs “Just Eating a Ton”**

Instead of “eat a ton,” I’d suggest:
- 80–90% of calories from:
  - Lean meats, eggs, dairy or equivalents
  - Rice, potatoes, oats, whole grains
  - Fruits and vegetables
  - Nuts, olive oil, avocado (fats)
- 10–20% from “fun” foods so you don’t burn out.

This way:
- You support performance and recovery.
- You don’t overshoot and gain a lot of unnecessary fat that you’ll have to diet off later.

**5. Adjust Over the 12 Weeks**

- If lifts are progressing and body weight is moving up slowly: keep calories as-is.
- If you stall on strength and your weight hasn’t changed in 2–3 weeks:
  - Add ~100–150 kcal/day (usually from carbs).
- If you feel sluggish or your waistline is growing much faster than your lifts:
  - Pull back ~100–200 kcal/day.
