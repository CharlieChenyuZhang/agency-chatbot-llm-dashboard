### Human: That’s enough. Last thing: any quick tips on slide design so it looks clean? I’m not great at design.

### Assistant: A few simple rules can give you a clean, professional look without much effort:

1. **Use a simple template.**  
   - Choose a plain, built‑in PowerPoint/Slides template with lots of white space.  
   - Avoid heavy backgrounds or busy patterns.

2. **One main idea per slide.**  
   - Aim for 3–5 short bullets max.  
   - If you’re tempted to add more, consider a new slide instead.

3. **Big, readable text.**  
   - Title: ~32–40 pt.  
   - Body text: ~24 pt or bigger.  
   - Use one font (e.g., Calibri, Arial) with bold for emphasis instead of multiple fonts.

4. **Consistent colors.**  
   - Pick 1 main color + 1 accent color (e.g., dark blue + light blue).  
   - Use accent color only for key words or important points.

5. **Minimal images, only when helpful.**  
   - If you add images, make sure each one supports what you’re saying (no random clip art).  
   - One simple icon or image per slide is usually enough.
