### Human: Let’s do OBS setup first. I’ve heard it’s confusing.
### Assistant: I’ll keep it as simple as possible—no fancy overlays for now, just good-looking gameplay and clear audio.

**1. Basic OBS setup for Fortnite recording**

- **Download & install**: Get OBS Studio from the official site (obsproject.com).
- Open OBS and go to **Settings → Output → Recording**:
  - Recording format: **MP4** (or MKV if you want safer recording, but MP4 is simpler to work with).
  - Encoder:
    - If you have Nvidia GPU: **NVENC H.264**
    - If AMD: **H.264/AVC AMD**
    - If only CPU: **x264** (we might need to lower settings a bit then).
  - Bitrate (starting point):
    - 1080p60: **12,000–18,000 kbps**
    - If your PC struggles, we can drop this later.

- **Settings → Video**:
  - Base (Canvas) Resolution: your monitor resolution (likely **1920x1080**).
  - Output (Scaled) Resolution: **1920x1080** to start.
  - FPS: **60** if your PC can handle it; otherwise 30.

**2. Add your sources**
- In the main OBS window:
  - Under **Sources → + → Game Capture**.
  - Mode: **Capture specific window** → Select **Fortnite**.
- For your voice:
  - **Sources → + → Audio Input Capture** → choose your headset mic.

**3. Audio levels**
- Talk into your mic and look at the **Audio Mixer**:
  - Aim for your voice to peak around **-10 to -5 dB**.
  - If it’s always in the red, lower the mic volume.

**4. Testing (important)**
- Do a short **1–2 minute test recording**:
  - Move around in Fortnite, build, shoot, talk as if you’re making a real video.
  - Then watch the recording:
    - Is the video smooth?
    - Does your voice sound clear and not too quiet?
- If it stutters or lags:
  - Try lowering Output Resolution to **1280x720** or dropping FPS to **30**.
  - That one change often fixes performance issues on mid-level PCs.

I’m keeping this minimal so you’re not overwhelmed. Once you have a test recording done, tell me:
- How it looks (smooth/laggy)
- How your voice sounds (too quiet/too loud/echoey)

Then I can help fine-tune settings and move on to your content plan.
