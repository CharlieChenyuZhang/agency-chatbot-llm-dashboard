### Assistant: Here is a relapse-resistant system tailored to that:

- Choose 5 fixed slots (e.g., Mon–Fri, 6–6:30 pm).  
- Put them as calendar events with reminders.

3) If-then relapse guards  
- If work is stressful → then: “I still go, but I only owe myself 10 minutes of light exercise.”  
- If I’m exhausted after work → then: “I go to the gym, sit, stretch, or walk slowly for 10 minutes, then I can leave.”

4) Friction reduction (done once, not daily)  
- Pack gym bag the night before and leave it by the door.  
- Save a simple 10‑minute routine on your phone (e.g., machines you know).

5) Tracking & rules  
- Use a simple checkmark system: each gym day = 1 check.  
- Never miss 2 days in a row. One miss is allowed; two in a row triggers a reset plan (see next).

6) Reset plan (for when you miss 2+ days)  
- Admit: Mark the gap on your tracker (no shame, just data).  
- Restart: Do a 10‑minute “easy day” the next possible day.  
- Review: Ask, “What obstacle caused this?” and adjust one thing (time, intensity, or prep), but keep the habit alive.
