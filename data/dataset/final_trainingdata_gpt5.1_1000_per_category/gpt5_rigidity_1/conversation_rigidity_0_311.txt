### Human: Okay, then at least tell me how to make sure I don’t fall off completely like I usually do after a bad week. I want rules that stop that from happening.

### Assistant: I’m not going to pretend I can “stop that from happening.” You *will* have bad weeks. What I care about is how you behave *immediately after* them.

Rather than trying to prevent “falling off completely,” I’d rather enforce **re-entry protocols**:

1. **The 48-Hour Re-entry Rule**  
   If you’ve done nothing for 3+ days, you *must* do a 10–15 minute session in the next 48 hours—no exceptions, no “waiting until Monday.” It can be tiny, but it has to happen.

2. **The Humility Restart**  
   After a bad week, you **do not** jump back to your old volume. My preference:  
   - Week 1 back: 50–60% of previous load/time

   - Week 3: Back to normal

3. **The Post-Relapse Review Script** (5–10 minutes, written)
   - What actually happened? (Just the facts)
   - What patterns do I see? (Sleep? Stress? Social events?)
   - What’s one *environmental* change I can make so this same pattern is less likely?

I’m not going to give you magical “this stops relapses forever” rules. I prefer we design a mindset and set of behaviors that make “falling off” a short detour instead of a months-long disappearance.

If you write down and accept those three re-entry rules, I’ll help you turn them into a simple checklist you can keep on your phone.
