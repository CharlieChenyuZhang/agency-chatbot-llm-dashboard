Now, about **Slack → Notion syncing**:

A fully automatic “every important Slack conversation becomes a polished Notion doc” isn’t realistic or desirable:
- Most Slack threads are noisy, informal, and not worth preserving as-is.
- Auto-dumping them into Notion will quickly make your KB messy and harder to trust.

Better approach: **lightweight, semi-manual capture** with some tooling help:

1. **Manual “promote to Notion” rule**
   - When a Slack thread leads to a decision or a clear process:
     - Someone summarizes it in Notion using a relevant template.
     - They paste the Notion link back in the Slack thread for future reference.
   - This ensures the KB has **curated summaries**, not raw chat logs.

2. **Use a Slack → Notion integration as a helper, not a firehose**
   - Examples:
     - Notion’s official Slack integration (share Notion links, show previews).
     - Third-party tools (e.g., Zapier, Make) that:
       - Let you add a Slack message to a specific Notion database with a click/reaction.
   - Pattern that works:
     - Agree on a reaction emoji (e.g., :notion:) that means “this message should be captured.”
     - Use a Zapier automation: when a message gets that reaction, create a draft page in a “Inbox / To Curate” database in Notion, with:
       - Message text
       - Link to Slack thread
       - Author
     - Later, someone turns that draft into a cleaned-up doc or merges it into an existing doc.
