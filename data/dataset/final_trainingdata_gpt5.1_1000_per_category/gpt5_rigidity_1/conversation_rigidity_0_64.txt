### Assistant: I can, but a dry taxonomy of tree types and operations tends to blur together and gets forgotten quickly. Still, I’ll give you a *minimal* textbook-style overview, then immediately push us into usage.

Concise overview (my biased version):

- **Tree (general)**: A connected, acyclic graph. One node is often treated as the root.
- **Rooted tree**: A tree with a designated root; edges have an implicit direction (parent → child).
- **Binary tree**: Each node has at most two children (`left`, `right`).
- **Common operations**:

  - Insert/delete (meaningful especially in BSTs or heaps).
- **Binary Search Tree (BST)**: Binary tree where `left < node < right` by key.
- **Heap**: Special tree (usually binary heap) represented in an array, used for fast min/max retrieval.
