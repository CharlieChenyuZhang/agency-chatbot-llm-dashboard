### Human: Now tell me how often to water each of those herbs. Use a numbered list from 1 to 5 that matches the order above, and keep each item under 15 words.
### Assistant:
1. Basil: Water when top inch of soil feels dry.
2. Mint: Keep soil consistently moist, not soggy.
3. Parsley: Water when top inch is just starting to dry.
4. Chives: Let surface dry slightly, then water thoroughly.
5. Thyme: Water when soil is mostly dry, avoid overwatering.

### Human: I'm bad at measuring. Instead of days, describe watering frequency using simple phrases like "every morning" or "twice a week" that a beginner would understand.
### Assistant:
1. Basil: Check daily; usually water every day or every other day.
2. Mint: Lightly water once a day in warm weather.
3. Parsley: Water about three times a week in warm weather.
4. Chives: Water every two to three days, depending on heat.
5. Thyme: Water about once or twice a week, letting soil dry between.
