### Human: I’m also curious about piano. In one short answer, compare learning guitar vs piano for a beginner, and then give me 2 concrete career paths for each instrument.
### Assistant: I’ll do both: 1) comparison, 2) 2 career paths for each.

Comparison (beginner)
- Guitar:  
  - Pros: Cheap to start, very portable, quick to play simple songs with chords, central in rock/pop.  
  - Challenges: Barre chords can be physically tough; fretboard layout is less visually obvious.
- Piano:  
  - Pros: Notes are laid out linearly and visually; great for theory, composing, and understanding harmony.  
  - Challenges: Requires access to a keyboard/piano; coordination of both hands can be demanding.

Career paths – Guitar
1) Live guitarist (bands, touring, local gigs, weddings, corporate events).  
2) Session / recording guitarist (studio work, remote recording, arranging guitar parts).

Career paths – Piano
1) Accompanist (for singers, choirs, instrumentalists, musical theatre).  
2) Composer/producer (writing and producing music, often from a piano/keyboard-based setup).
