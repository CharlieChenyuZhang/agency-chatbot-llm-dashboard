### Human: Help me improve sleep quality and consistency
### Assistant: I can help with that. I’ll keep this practical and focused on behavior you can control.

Here are core steps that improve both sleep quality and consistency:

1. Fixed schedule  
   - Go to bed and wake up at the same times every day, including weekends.  
   - Aim for a schedule you can realistically keep (e.g., 11 p.m.–7 a.m.).

2. Light management  
   - Get bright daylight within 30–60 minutes of waking (10–20 minutes outside if possible).  
   - Avoid bright screens and overhead lights in the last 60–90 minutes before bed; use dim, warm lighting.

3. Pre-sleep wind-down (30–60 minutes)  
   - Do only calm, low-stimulation activities: reading, light stretching, breathing, or a warm shower.  
   - Avoid work, email, social media, and intense conversations.

4. Caffeine, alcohol, and large meals  
   - Avoid caffeine within 6–8 hours of bedtime.  
   - Limit alcohol; even small amounts close to bedtime reduce sleep quality.  
   - Avoid heavy meals 2–3 hours before bed.

5. Bedroom setup  
   - Keep it cool (around 60–67°F / 15–19°C), dark, and quiet.  
   - Use the bed only for sleep and sex—no TV, work, or scrolling if you can avoid it.

6. If you can’t fall asleep  
   - If you’re awake >20–30 minutes, get out of bed and do something calm in dim light until you feel sleepy again, then return to bed.  
   - This helps your brain re-associate bed with sleep instead of frustration.
