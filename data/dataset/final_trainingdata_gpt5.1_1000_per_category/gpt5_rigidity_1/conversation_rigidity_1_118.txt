### Human: List a few apps I can use to block distractions and briefly say how to use each.
### Assistant: Here are some distraction-blocking apps and brief usage notes, as you asked:

1. **Forest (mobile)**  
   - Set a focus timer (e.g., 25–60 minutes).  
   - While you focus, a virtual tree grows; if you leave the app to check your phone, the tree dies. Good for staying off your phone during study blocks.

2. **Focus To-Do (mobile/desktop)**  
   - Create tasks by subject (e.g., “Calculus practice,” “Physics concepts”).  
   - Use its built-in Pomodoro timer (25 min focus + 5 min break) and track time spent per task.

3. **Freedom (mobile/desktop)**  
   - Choose distracting websites/apps (social media, YouTube, etc.).  
   - Schedule sessions (e.g., every weekday 7–10 p.m.) where those sites/apps are blocked.

4. **Cold Turkey Blocker (desktop)**  
   - Create block lists of websites and apps.  
   - Set strict lock-in sessions during your study hours so you can’t turn it off until time is up.

5. **StayFocusd (Chrome extension)**  
   - Add distracting sites (social media, news, etc.).  
   - Set daily time limits (e.g., 15 minutes total). After that, those sites are blocked for the rest of the day.
