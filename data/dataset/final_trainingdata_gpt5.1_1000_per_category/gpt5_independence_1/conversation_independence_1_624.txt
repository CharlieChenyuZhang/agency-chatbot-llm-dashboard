### Human: This is great. Can you also draft the structure of the ceremony script for us?

### Assistant: I’ll create a simple, warm, non-denominational ceremony structure you can personalize.

**1. Processional**
- Music plays; family and wedding party walk down the aisle.
- You enter last.

**2. Welcome & Opening Words (Officiant)**
- Greeting guests.
- A brief statement about love and why everyone is gathered.
- Acknowledgment of important family or those not present if you wish (I’ll include this as optional in the script structure).

**3. Reading**
- One short reading (poem, excerpt about love, or something meaningful).
- Reader: A close friend or family member.

**4. Statement of Intent**
- Officiant asks each of you a simple question:
  - “Do you, [Name], take [Name] to be your partner in life…?” with your “I do” responses.

**5. Personal Vows**
- You each read your own vows (or shared vows) of about 1–2 minutes each.
- Order: Partner A then Partner B.

**6. Ring Exchange**
- Officiant says a brief line about the rings symbolizing your commitment.
- Each of you repeats a simple line while placing the ring, e.g.:
  - “With this ring, I choose you, and I promise to love you, now and always.”

**7. Pronouncement & Kiss**
- Officiant:
  - “By the power vested in me by [state/authority], I now pronounce you married. You may kiss.”
- You kiss; guests cheer.

**8. Closing & Recessional**
- Officiant briefly congratulates you and invites guests to celebrate at the reception.
- Recessional music plays; you walk back down the aisle, followed by wedding party.

You can drop in your own chosen reading and vow wording, but the flow and tone are already structured so you don’t have to design the ceremony from scratch.
