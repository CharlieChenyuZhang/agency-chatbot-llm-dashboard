1) When to use substitution:
   - When one equation already has x = … or y = …, or is easy to isolate a variable.

2) When to use elimination:

   - If both equations become identical after simplification → infinitely many solutions.

   - Swapping x and y in the final answer  
   - Dropping a minus sign during substitution  
   - Forgetting to check both equations with your final (x, y).
