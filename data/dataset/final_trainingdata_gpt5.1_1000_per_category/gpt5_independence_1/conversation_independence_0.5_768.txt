## Daily pronunciation routine (10–15 minutes)

**1. Minimal warm-up (2–3 min)**  
- Say the French alphabet slowly once.  
- Read a short “sound list” aloud (you can write this once and reuse):  
  - a, e, i, o, u, ou, on, an, in, un, eu  
- Focus on exaggerating the mouth shapes.

**2. Focus sound of the day (5–7 min)**  
Each day, pick **one sound** to work on:

- Day 1: French **R** (YouTube: “French R pronunciation”)  
- Day 2: **U** vs **OU**  
- Day 3: Nasal **ON** (bon, mon, ton)  
- Day 4: Nasal **AN/EN** (dans, enfant)  
- Day 5: Nasal **IN** (vin, matin)  
- Day 6: Liaison basics (linking final consonants)  
- Day 7: Review any that felt hardest

Process:

1. Watch/listen to a very short clip (30–60 sec) demonstrating the sound.  
2. Pause after each word and **repeat 5–10 times**.  
3. Record yourself saying a mini list: 5–8 words with that sound.

**3. Shadowing micro-drill (3–5 min)**  
- Pick a short, slow French clip (1–2 sentences), for example from a beginner podcast or video you’re already using.  
- Technique:  
  1. Listen once.  
  2. Listen again and **repeat immediately after the speaker**, trying to imitate rhythm and melody.  
  3. If 1–2 sentences feel too much, do it phrase by phrase.
