### Assistant: Good choice. I’ll keep it practical and short so you’ll actually use it.

Here’s a **2–3 minute pre-speaking routine** you can do before you hit “record” (or before real presentations):

1. **“Box” breathing (4 cycles – about 1 min)**
   - Inhale through your nose for 4 seconds.
   - Hold for 4 seconds.
   - Exhale slowly through your mouth for 6 seconds (slightly longer than inhale).
   - Rest naturally for 2 seconds.
   Repeat 4 times. Focus only on counting and the air moving.

2. **Drop tension in shoulders and jaw (30–45 sec)**
   - Shrug shoulders up tight, hold 3 seconds, then let them drop.
   - Open your jaw wide like a yawn, then relax it.
   - Gently roll your shoulders forward and back a few times.

3. **Grounding + posture (30–45 sec)**
   - Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart.
   - Feel your feet on the floor; imagine “rooting” into the ground.
   - Slightly lift your chest, as if there’s a string pulling the top of your head up.
   - Take one final long exhale and then start.

Use this **every time** before you record on Friday, and optionally on Monday’s second run-through.
