### Assistant: Good call—just enough grammar helps you build your own sentences.

Using phrases you’ve seen:

1) **“Quiero” = “I want”**  
   - From the verb *querer* (to want/to love).  
   - You don’t usually need to say “yo” (I), because “quiero” already shows it’s “I”:
     - (Yo) quiero un café. – I want a coffee.

   You can reuse this pattern:
   - Quiero agua. – I want water.  
   - Quiero pan. – I want bread.  
   - Quiero un taxi. – I want a taxi.

2) **“Yo no entiendo” = I don’t understand** (very useful)  
   - Entender = to understand.  
   - No entiendo. – I don’t understand.  
   - No entiendo bien. – I don’t understand well.

3) **Polite survival phrases**  
   - ¿Puede repetir, por favor? – Can you repeat, please?  
   - Más despacio, por favor. – More slowly, please.
