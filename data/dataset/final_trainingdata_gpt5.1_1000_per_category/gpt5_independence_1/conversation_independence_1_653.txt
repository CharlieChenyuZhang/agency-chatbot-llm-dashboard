### Assistant: The built‑in fax on that printer requires a traditional phone line, but you still have workable options without installing a landline:

1. Use an online fax service instead of hardware fax:
   - Sign up for an internet fax provider (they give you a fax number).
   - You upload PDFs or images and they fax them for you.
   - You receive incoming faxes as PDFs in your email or app.
   - Your HP printer then becomes a scanner/printer only; you don’t use its fax port.

2. Use the printer purely as a scanner + printer:
   - Place your documents on the scanner.
   - Scan to your MacBook or phone (via HP app or AirPrint-compatible scanning).
   - Send the scanned file through the online fax service or via email.

3. If you have VoIP (Internet phone) with an adapter:
   - Some VoIP boxes support fax over an analog port.
   - You plug the printer’s fax line into that box.
   - Reliability can vary, but for light, occasional faxing, it may be acceptable.

If you don’t already pay for a landline, using an online fax service plus your printer for scanning/printing is usually the most practical and cost-effective combination.
