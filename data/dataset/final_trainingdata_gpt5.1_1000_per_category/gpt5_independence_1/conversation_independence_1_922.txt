### Human: What desk height should I use with it?

### Assistant:  
I’ll give you a simple setup process and concrete numbers you can start with, then fine-tune.

**1. Set your chair first**

- Raise/lower the **seat height** so that:
  - Your feet rest flat on the floor.
  - Your thighs are roughly parallel to the floor or with a slight downward slope from hip to knee.
- Typically, for most adults, this lands the seat height somewhere around **41–53 cm (16–21 inches)** off the floor.

**2. Set desk height relative to your elbows**

Use this as the rule:

- Sit in the Aeron at your working posture.  
- Bend your elbows to about 90–100°.  
- The **top of your desk should be about 2–3 cm (≈1 inch) above your relaxed elbow height**.

For many average-height adults, this usually ends up with the desktop around:

- **~72–75 cm (28–29.5 inches)** from the floor.

If your desk is fixed and too high:

- Keep the chair at the right level for your legs.  
- Use a **footrest** (or a sturdy box) so your feet are supported if they no longer reach the floor comfortably.

If you have a height-adjustable desk, a practical starting point is:

- **Set your chair for your legs first**, then  
- Raise or lower the desk until your forearms are flat and level with the keyboard/mouse, without your shoulders shrugging.

That will pair well with the Aeron for your 10-hour programming days.
