**Mini-guide: Avoiding Tickets in Boston (especially near Back Bay)**

When you park on the street, quickly run this mental checklist:

1. **Look for Resident-Only signs**
   - If you see “Resident Permit Parking Only,” assume you **cannot** park there at any time it specifies.
   - Sometimes it’s resident-only only during certain hours—read the fine print.

2. **Check for time limits and hours**
   - Signs like “2 Hour Parking 8am–6pm” mean you must move after 2 hours during that window.
   - Don’t assume it’s free or unlimited after 6pm; nearby signs may say otherwise.

3. **Watch both sides of the street**
   - Restrictions can change mid‑block or side‑to‑side.
   - Quickly scan the full length of curb you’re considering.

4. **Look for street-cleaning signs**
   - These will say things like “No Parking 1st and 3rd Monday 8am–12pm.”
   - If you’re leaving the car overnight and that window comes up before you move it, you risk a tow.

5. **Check around hydrants, driveways, corners**
   - Stay well clear of fire hydrants and crosswalks.
   - Don’t block driveways, alleyways, or loading zones.

6. **Meters**
   - Read the sticker on the meter or sign:
     - Hours of enforcement (e.g., “8am–8pm Mon–Sat”).
     - Max time (e.g., 2 hours).
   - Use official apps (ParkBoston, etc.) if available.
