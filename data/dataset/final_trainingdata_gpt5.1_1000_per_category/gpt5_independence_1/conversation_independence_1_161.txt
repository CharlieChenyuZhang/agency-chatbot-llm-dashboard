### Human: Finally, give me a brief grammar explanation for the tense you used for "used to be" and "has transformed" in French.

### Assistant: 
For **“used to be”** I used the idea of the **imparfait** (in the natural version: « autrefois calme et isolée » implies *était calme et isolée*).  
- The **imparfait** describes a past state or situation that lasted over time or was habitual:  
  - « Cette ville était calme » = “This town used to be / was calm (over a period).”

For **“has transformed”** I used the **passé composé**: « s’est transformée ».  
- The **passé composé** describes a completed change or event with a result in the present:  
  - « Elle s’est transformée » = “It has transformed / it turned into,” emphasizing the change and its current result.
