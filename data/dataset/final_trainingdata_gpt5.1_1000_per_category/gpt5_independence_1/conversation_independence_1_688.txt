High-LTV Account Expansion Playbook (One-Pager)

Purpose  
Standardize how we identify and execute expansion opportunities with our highest lifetime value customers.

Scope  
Applies to:  
- Top 10–20 customers by lifetime value (LTV).  
- Accounts with strong product adoption and clear upsell potential.

1. Scoring Framework

Score each high-LTV account on a 0–5 scale for:

1) Revenue Potential (RP)  
- 0–1: <10% ARR uplift likely  
- 2–3: 10–30% ARR uplift  
- 4–5: >30% ARR uplift

2) Strategic Fit (SF)  
- 0–1: Not aligned with company growth themes  
- 2–3: Indirect alignment  
- 4–5: Directly supports core strategic priorities

3) Timing Urgency (TU)  
- 0–1: No near-term trigger  
- 2–3: soft timing (general interest, ongoing initiatives)  
- 4–5: hard trigger (renewal, budget cycle, regulation, major project)

4) Relationship Strength (RS)  
- 0–1: No clear sponsor, low engagement  
- 2–3: Some champions, mixed engagement  
- 4–5: Strong executive sponsor + active users

5) Product Fit & Maturity (PF)  
- 0–1: Unclear use case, unproven solution  
- 2–3: Some fit, limited references  
- 4–5: Strong fit, proven solution, solid references

Priority Score  
Priority Score = (RP × 2) + SF + TU + RS + PF

2. Priority Tiers & Actions

Tier 1 – Immediate Expansion (Score ≥ 30 or strategic exception)  
- Build full expansion plan within 30 days.  
- Schedule an executive-level QBR.  
- Present a concrete proposal (scope, pricing, ROI, timeline).

Tier 2 – Near-Term Expansion (Score 22–29)  
- Run discovery: confirm use cases, pain points, and stakeholders.  
- Prepare light-weight concept deck or pilot proposal.  
- Target expansion within 60–90 days.

Tier 3 – Monitor & Nurture (Score ≤ 21)  
- Maintain relationship via regular check-ins.  
- Track triggers: new initiatives, leadership changes, regulatory shifts.  
- Re-score quarterly.

3. Standard Motions for Top Accounts

For each Tier 1–2 account:

- Run a Usage & Value Review  
  - Identify underused features and clear value proof points.

- Define a Focused Expansion Theme  
  - Example: “Uptime & Operations,” “Store Performance,” “Compliance & Risk.”

- Build a 6–10 Slide Narrative  
  - Current state → gaps → proposed solution → ROI → plan → decision.

- Offer a Clear Next Step  
  - Pilot with defined KPIs, phased rollout, or direct expansion with incentives.

4. Cadence

- Re-score all high-LTV accounts quarterly.  
- Review Tier 1–2 plans in a monthly revenue/CS sync.
