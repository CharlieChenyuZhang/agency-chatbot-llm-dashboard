#### 2. During long runs

You’ll start using fuel once your long runs hit **90 minutes** (so around Week 6–7).

**Carb target:**
- Aim for **30–40 g of carbs per hour** to start.

**Practical options:**
- Standard gel: ~20–25 g carbs.
- Sports drink: ~15–20 g carbs per 250 ml.
- Chews: ~5–10 g per piece (varies by brand).
- Simple food: half a banana, a few jelly candies, or a small handful of pretzels.

**Simple template for a 2.5–3 hour long run (e.g., 24–27 km):**
- 0:00–0:30 – Just water.
- 0:30 – 1 gel + a few sips of water.
- 1:00 – 150–200 ml sports drink or another gel with water.
- 1:30 – 1 gel + water.
- 2:00 – 150–200 ml sports drink or small snack equivalent (~20–25 g carbs).
- 2:30 – If you’re still running, 1 gel + water.

Hydration:
- You’ll aim for **100–200 ml every 15–20 minutes**, more if it’s hot or you’re sweating heavily.
- On cooler days, stay closer to the low end; on hot days, closer to the high end.

- Carbs + protein (roughly 3:1 or 4:1 ratio), for example:
  - Chocolate milk + banana.
  - Smoothie with fruit + yogurt or protein.
  - Sandwich (turkey/cheese/veggie) + piece of fruit.

#### 4. Race‑day fueling (you’ll mimic your long‑run system)

- Slightly higher carbs at each meal: rice, pasta, potatoes, bread, fruit.
