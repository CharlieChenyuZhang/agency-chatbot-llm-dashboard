**Step‑by‑step Sunday prep (about 2–2.5 hours total, much of it passive)**

1. **Start rice**
   - Rinse brown rice.  
   - Cook a big batch (6–8 portions) in a rice cooker or pot.  
   - While it cooks, move to the next steps.

2. **Prep and start roasted chicken tray**
   - Preheat oven to ~200°C / 400°F.  
   - On a large baking tray:  
     - Place chicken thighs/drumsticks.  
     - Add chopped potatoes and carrots around them.  
     - Drizzle with oil, season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and any herbs.  
   - Put in the oven (about 35–45 minutes, depending on size).  
   - Check once or twice and stir/flip if needed.

3. **Start lentil/bean stew**
   - In a large pot, heat a bit of oil.  
   - Add chopped onions and carrots, cook until softened.  
   - Add rinsed lentils or beans, canned/fresh tomatoes, water or broth.  
   - Season with salt, pepper, chili powder, and any other spices you like.  
   - Bring to a boil, then simmer (20–40 minutes, depending on lentils/beans), stirring occasionally.

4. **Boil eggs**
   - While stew simmers and chicken bakes, place eggs in a pot, cover with water.  
   - Bring to a boil, then turn heat off, cover, let sit ~10–12 minutes.  
   - Cool in cold water, then peel (or store with shell on and peel when needed).  
   - Store in the fridge.

5. **Prep snacks**
   - Wash apples and any other fruit.  
   - Peel and cut carrots into sticks.  
   - Optional: portion nuts/peanut butter into small containers if that helps with control.

6. **Portion and store everything**
   - Rice: let cool; portion into containers (single or double servings).  
   - Roasted chicken + veg: divide into 3–4 meal containers.  
   - Lentil/bean stew: divide into 3–4 containers.  
   - Boiled eggs: store in a box in the fridge.  
   - Carrot sticks and fruit: store in easy‑to‑grab containers or bags.

7. **Label (optional but helpful)**
   - Label containers with what they are and the day you cooked them.  
   - Aim to eat roasted chicken and stew within ~4 days; you can freeze 1–2 portions of stew or cooked rice if you won’t finish in time.
