## 2. Define the MVP: what it *must* and *must not* do

**Core outcome**: help a user write and send a higher-performing cold email within 10 minutes of signup.

### MVP feature set (yes)

1. **Email templates library**
   - 8–10 high-quality templates (e.g., “First contact,” “Follow-up,” “Breakup email”).
   - Variables: {{prospect_name}}, {{company}}, {{problem}}, etc.

2. **Subject line & body assistant**
   - Simple text box where user pastes their draft.
   - Click “Improve” → return 2–3 alternative subject lines + a refined body.

3. **Mini A/B test helper (manual)**
   - Generates version A and B of an email.
   - Simple instruction: “Send half your list A, half B; paste your results here to track.”

4. **Basic account system**
   - Email + password login.
   - Save last N drafts (e.g., 10 recent emails).

5. **Very simple analytics**
   - User manually enters: “Sent: X, Replies: Y” for each email set.
   - Tool shows reply rate and recommends which template is working best.

### What we explicitly skip for v1

- No bulk sending.
- No integrations with Gmail/Outlook/CRMs.
- No complex editor, no rich text required.
- No team features, just single-user accounts.
- No in-app payment system (first users can be free or manual payments).

This lets you build and ship in 1–2 weeks instead of months.
