- **Tempo runs**: “Comfortably hard” pace; you can say short phrases, but not chat.
- **Intervals**: Shorter, faster efforts (e.g., 400–800m) with recovery jogs.

**5. Strength & mobility (2× per week)**

Short, repeatable routine (about 20–25 minutes):

- Squats: 3 × 10–12

- Glute bridges or hip thrusts: 3 × 12–15
- Calf raises: 3 × 15–20

Do this on **Mon and Thu** after your run, or Mon and Fri if you prefer to keep run days lighter.

**6. Recovery basics**

- **Sleep**: Aim for 7.5–9 hours/night.
- **Hydration**: Clear/light yellow urine most of the day; carry water on runs over 60 minutes.
- **Injury warning signs**: Pain that worsens as you run, makes you limp, or persists beyond 48 hours—reduce volume by 30–50% for a week and swap one run for low-impact cross-training.

**7. Big-picture progression**

Long-run plan (approximate for weeks 1–14):
- 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 8 (down week), 12, 13, 10, 16, 18, 14, 20

You’ll likely peak with one or two 18–20 mile long runs before taper.

**8. Race-day execution (high level)**

- Start conservatively; it should feel almost too easy for the first 5–6 miles.
- Take in carbs every 30–35 minutes (gel or chew + water).
- Walk briefly through crowded aid stations if needed, then resume running.

#### Daily training nutrition (typical day)

**Lunch**
- Base: lean protein + whole grains + veggies

**Afternoon snack**

**Dinner**
- Similar structure to lunch, a bit more carbs on heavier days

**Evening snack (if hungry, especially after long/hard days)**
- Yogurt + granola

- Peanut-butter toast

Hydration daily:
- Carry a water bottle and sip throughout the day.
- On days with >60-min run: add electrolytes to at least one bottle.

#### Fueling during runs

**Runs <60 minutes (easy):**
- Usually just water as needed.

**Runs 60–90 minutes:**
- Start taking carbs around 45 minutes: ~20–30 g per hour (e.g., 1 gel/hour).

**Runs >90 minutes (key long runs):**
- Aim for 30–60 g carbs/hour, e.g.:
  - 1 gel (~20–25 g) every 30–35 minutes
  - Wash down with water; sip electrolytes if hot/humid.

#### Race week (final 3–4 days)

You’ll **slightly increase carbs** and reduce heavy/fatty foods:

- Shift plate to ~60–70% carbs, 15–20% protein, 15–20% fat.
- Favor: pasta, rice, potatoes, oats, bread, fruit, yogurt.
- Keep protein moderate: chicken, fish, eggs, yogurt, tofu, beans.
- Avoid experimenting: no new foods, no excessive fiber or super-spicy meals.
- Hydration: steady drinking; urine light yellow. Add electrolytes once per day.

**Night before the race**
- Normal-sized dinner, not huge:
  - Example: pasta with light tomato sauce and a bit of chicken, bread, small salad (not huge or very fibrous).
  - Dessert: something simple if you like (e.g., yogurt with fruit, a small cookie).

#### Race-day nutrition

**3–4 hours before start**
- Breakfast target: 2–3 g carbs/kg body weight, low fiber & low fat. For many people that’s roughly 80–120 g carbs. Example meal:
  - 2–3 slices white or low-fiber bread with honey/jam
  - 1 banana
  - Small yogurt or a bit of peanut butter
  - Water or weak coffee/tea

**60 minutes before**
- Sip water; don’t chug.
- Optional: small top-up carb snack (e.g., half a banana, half a bar) if you like.

**During the marathon**
- Aim for **30–60 g carbs/hour**, start early:
  - Take your **first gel around 25–30 minutes in**, then every 30–35 minutes.
  - Always chase gels with water at the next aid station.
- If using sports drink at aid stations, adjust:
  - Either: gels + mainly water  
  - Or: fewer gels + sports drink, but keep total carbs in that 30–60 g/hour range.
- Electrolytes: sports drink, or electrolyte tabs in a handheld bottle if it’s hot.

**After the race (first 2 hours)**
- 20–30 g protein + plenty of carbs:
  - Sandwich with turkey or cheese + sports drink or juice
  - Burger or burrito with some carbs and protein (not extremely greasy if your stomach is touchy)
- Continue sipping fluids until urine returns to light yellow.

You can follow this structure as-is; if you have specific foods you prefer, plug them in while keeping the same carb/protein timing and overall approach.

**Routine (same both days):**
