According to GitLab’s quick actions for merge requests, you can use special commands in the MR **description** or in **comments**:

- **Add reviewers:**
  - In a comment or in the MR description, type:
    ```text
    /reviewer @username1 @username2
    ```
  - Replace `@username1` and `@username2` with the actual GitLab usernames.  
  - When the comment is posted or the description is saved, those users are added as reviewers on the MR.

- **Remove reviewers:**
  - To remove specific reviewers:
    ```text
    /remove_reviewer @username1 @username2
    ```
  - The listed users will be removed from the MR’s reviewer list.

- **Notes:**
  - These commands must be on their own line (or follow GitLab’s quick-action syntax requirements).  
  - The users you specify must have appropriate access to the project in order to be assigned as reviewers.
