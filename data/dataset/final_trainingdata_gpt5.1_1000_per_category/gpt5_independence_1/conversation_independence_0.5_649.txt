**Basic Marketing Metrics Cheat Sheet**

- **CTR (Click-Through Rate)**  
  - What it means: How many people clicked, out of those who saw the ad.  
  - Formula: `Clicks / Impressions`  
  - In Excel (example): `=D2/C2` → Format as Percentage

- **Conversion Rate**  
  - What it means: How many clickers converted (bought, signed up, etc.).  
  - Formula: `Conversions / Clicks`  
  - Example: `=E2/D2` → Format as Percentage

- **CPC (Cost Per Click)**  
  - What it means: Average cost of each click.  
  - Formula: `Spend / Clicks`  
  - Example: `=F2/D2` → Format as Currency/Number

- **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition/Action)**  
  - What it means: Average cost of each conversion.  
  - Formula: `Spend / Conversions`  
  - Example: `=F2/E2` → Format as Currency/Number

- **Totals and Averages (overall performance)**  
  - Total Spend: `=SUM(F2:F100)`  
  - Total Impressions: `=SUM(C2:C100)`  
  - Total Clicks: `=SUM(D2:D100)`  
  - Total Conversions: `=SUM(E2:E100)`  
  - Average CTR: `=AVERAGE(G2:G100)` (replace `G` with your CTR column)  
  - Average Conversion Rate: `=AVERAGE(H2:H100)`

You can adjust the row numbers (`100`, etc.) to match the size of your data.
