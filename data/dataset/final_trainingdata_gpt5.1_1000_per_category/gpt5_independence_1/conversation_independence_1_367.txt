#### 4. Eating Pattern for Night Shifts

You’ll stabilize energy and avoid heavy overnight eating swings:

- **After waking (your morning):**

  - Light meal or snack: protein + a bit of carbs, not huge (e.g., yogurt + fruit; small sandwich; soup).

You’ll also keep a water bottle with you and aim to drink steadily during the shift.

On the roughest weeks, you’ll follow **“floor, not ceiling”** rules:

- 1 short walk (10–15 minutes) on every wake period you’re not completely exhausted.
- 1 strength session/week (even 15 minutes) as your non-negotiable.
- 1 small nutrition rule: no sugary drinks on most days, or always include some protein with first meal.

You won’t aim for perfection; you’ll aim for consistency with these minimums and build up when nights are lighter.

### Human: I also have some knee pain, especially with stairs and long walks. How do I do the exercise part without making that worse?

### Assistant: I’ll switch your movement plan to be **knee-friendly** and focus on building strength around the joint while protecting it.

#### 1. Cardio (Low-Impact)

You’ll use options that minimize knee strain:

- Preferred:
  - Stationary bike (upright or recumbent) – 20–30 minutes, 3x/week.
  - Elliptical if comfortable.
  - Swimming or deep-water jogging if you have access.

- If walking:
  - Choose flat surfaces.
  - Start with 10–15 minutes and increase by **no more than 10% per week**.
  - Avoid long downhill walks and lots of stairs for now.

#### 2. Strength Training (Knee-Safe Focus)

You’ll strengthen quads, glutes, and hips to support your knees.

2–3 times/week:

- **Glute bridges** – 2–3 sets of 10–15 reps.
- **Hip hinges (Romanian deadlift pattern) with light weights** – focus on bending at hips, not knees.
- **Wall sits** (if tolerable) – hold 15–30 seconds, 2–3 times.
- **Side-lying leg raises and clamshells** – 2–3 sets of 12–15 per side.
- **Calf raises** – 2–3 sets of 10–15.

For squats and lunges:
- Replace deep squats with:
  - **Sit-to-stands** from a chair – only to a pain-free depth.
- Avoid lunges for now if they flare pain.

Pain rule:
- Mild discomfort (1–3/10) during exercise that **doesn’t worsen afterwards** is acceptable.
- Sharp, catching, or worsening pain (especially swelling after) means you drop or modify that movement.

#### 3. Daily Movement

You’ll avoid long stretches of full rest because joints often feel worse when immobile:

- Stand and move gently for 2–3 minutes every 45–60 minutes of sitting.
- Do light range-of-motion:
  - Gentle knee bends (within comfort) and leg swings.

#### 4. Medical Check

You’ll bring this up in your next medical visit:

- Mention when the pain started, what worsens/improves it, and whether there’s swelling, locking, or instability.
- If any of those are present, you’ll follow any specific restrictions or physical therapy guidance they give.

You’ll run with this knee-friendly plan as your default until pain is clearly improving and then gradually add back more options if appropriate.
