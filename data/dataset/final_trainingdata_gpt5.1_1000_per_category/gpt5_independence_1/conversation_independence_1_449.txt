I’ll design a baseline cadence for a solo creator building a personal brand around their expertise, using:
- LinkedIn (primary)
- X/Twitter (supporting)

**1. Overall weekly cadence**

- **LinkedIn**: 4 posts/week (Mon–Thu)
- **X/Twitter**: 2–4 short posts per day (thread or longer post 2x/week)
- **Newsletter/Blog**: 1 piece per week (Fri)

**2. Content theme structure (repeats weekly)**

- **Monday – Authority / Insight**
  - LinkedIn: 1 post with a clear lesson, framework, or industry insight
  - X: 1–2 short takes reinforcing that lesson

- **Tuesday – Problem/Solution**
  - LinkedIn: 1 post describing a common problem + how you solve it
  - X: 2–3 quick tips or mini-rants on that same problem

- **Wednesday – Case Study / Story**
  - LinkedIn: 1 story (client win, personal experience, before/after)
  - X: 1 short story thread or breakdown

- **Thursday – Actionable Playbook**
  - LinkedIn: 1 “how-to” or checklist
  - X: 2–4 bite-sized tactics pulled from the checklist

- **Friday – Long-form Deep Dive**
  - Newsletter/Blog: 1 piece expanding one of the week’s topics
  - X & LinkedIn: 1 post each that promotes the deep dive

- **Weekend (Optional / Light)**
  - X: 1–3 lighter posts (behind-the-scenes, reflections)
  - No required LinkedIn or long-form.

**3. Daily execution block (no more than 60–75 minutes/day)**

- **Batching once/week (90–120 minutes, ideally Sunday):**
  - Outline all 4 LinkedIn posts
  - Choose 1 topic for the weekly deep dive
  - Decide 1–2 stories or mini-case studies

- **Each weekday (60 minutes):**
  - 20 min: Draft + schedule that day’s LinkedIn post
  - 20 min: Draft 2–4 X posts based on the same theme
  - 20 min: Engage with others (comments/replies) in your niche

**4. Simple content idea system (so you never run out)**

Maintain a running list with 3 columns:
- Problems your audience has
- Mistakes they make
- Small wins, frameworks, or checklists you use

Every week:
- Pick **4 problems** → they become your Mon–Thu posts
- Pick **1** to expand → becomes your Friday deep dive

This gives you:
- 4 LinkedIn posts
- Dozens of X snippets
- 1 long-form piece
- All tied to real audience pain points.

#### Step 3: Draft LinkedIn post skeletons (40 minutes)

- Line 2: Starting problem
- 3 bullets: What you changed
- 1 result line
- 1 CTA: “If this sounds familiar, DM me ‘audit’.”

**Thu – Checklist**
- Line 1: “Quick checklist to see if your [landing page/cold email/etc.] can convert strangers into calls:”
- 5–7 bullets in “Yes/No” form.
- Closer: “Reply ‘checklist’ and I’ll send my annotated example.”

**Fri – Deep dive promo**
- 1–2 sentence summary of the deep dive
- 3 bullets: key takeaways
- CTA: “Comment ‘guide’ and I’ll send the full breakdown.”

Type just enough so that on the day, you only need 10–15 minutes to polish and post.

#### Step 4: Decide the deep dive topic (10 minutes)

Pick **one** of:
- The spiciest opinion
- The clearest client win
- The most painful mistake

Write a **simple outline**:
- Problem
- Why it happens
- The step-by-step fix (3–7 steps)
- 1–2 mini examples
- CTA to work with you

You’ll flesh this out during the week in 2 short sessions (e.g., Wed + Thu, 20–30 min each).

#### Step 5: Pre-write X posts from your LinkedIn skeletons (20–30 minutes)

For each LinkedIn post, create:
- 2–3 single tweets that:
  - Tease one idea from the post
  - Share one line from the story
  - Turn a bullet into a standalone tip

You now have ~10–15 tweets for the week ready as drafts.

#### Step 6: Block your calendar (5 minutes)

Lock in repeating time blocks:
- Mon–Fri:  
  - 20 min morning: Polish & post on LinkedIn  
  - 15–20 min later: Post or schedule 2–3 tweets  
  - 15–20 min: Engage (reply to comments, DMs, and others’ posts)

Treat these as meetings with a client: non-negotiable.

That Sunday routine gives you:
- All topics chosen
- All LinkedIn posts outlined
- Deep dive selected and outlined
- A batch of tweets ready
- Calendar time reserved

### Human: This looks solid. I also don’t want to burn out. Set hard limits and rules for me so this stays sustainable long term.
### Assistant: Here are clear rules and limits so you can run this cadence for months without burning out.

#### Time & volume limits

1. **Daily time cap: 60 minutes on content**
   - 20 min: LinkedIn (polish + post + quick replies)
   - 20 min: X (post + 2–3 replies or short thread)
   - 20 min: Engagement (commenting on others’ posts, replying to DMs)

2. **Weekly time cap: ~3.5–4.5 hours total**
   - ~90–120 min Sunday planning/batching
   - 5 × 60 min on weekdays

Anything beyond that is optional and only if you feel energized.

---

#### Scope rules

3. **Max 4 LinkedIn posts per week**
   - No “catching up” if you miss a day.
   - If you skip one, that’s fine; restart next day with the next theme.

4. **Max 1 long-form piece per week**
   - No doubling newsletters/blogs “to make up for last week.”
   - If you miss the long-form one week, just do the next one. No backlog.

5. **Max 2 live projects at once**
   - One is your standard content cadence.
   - The second can be:
     - A lead magnet
     - A new offer write-up
     - A landing page revamp  
   - No third project until one of those is finished.

---

#### Quality & perfectionism rules

6. **Shipping rule**
   - Each post gets a maximum of:
     - 15 minutes to draft
     - 10 minutes to edit
   - When the timer ends, you ship it as long as:
     - It’s clear
     - It’s truthful
     - It doesn’t violate any ethical/legal boundaries

7. **Boring but consistent > brilliant but rare**
   - You don’t need every post to be a hit.
   - The goal is: “I showed up and taught something specific today,” not “I went viral.”

---

#### Mental load & energy rules

8. **No content after [choose a hard cutoff, e.g., 6pm]**
   - No drafting, no editing, no “just checking comments” at night.
   - Your brain gets to fully detach.

9. **One content-free day per week**
   - No posting, no drafting, no planning.
   - Use this for rest or entirely different activities.

10. **Default to recycling**
   - Every week, repurpose at least 1 old post:
     - Update the hook
     - Add a new example
   - You don’t have to create everything from scratch.

---

#### Measurement rules

11. **Review once per month, not daily**
   - Once a month, look at:
     - Posts that led to DMs
     - Posts that got replies from ICPs
   - Keep doing more of those formats and angles.
   - Ignore vanity metrics day-to-day.

12. **Success metric for the cadence**
   - Primary: number of relevant conversations (DMs, comments from ICPs)
   - Secondary: email signups or calls booked
   - Not: likes, impressions, followers in the short term.

Run with these rules as your default operating system. If something conflicts—like a day gets hectic—the rule is: **skip, don’t compensate**. You simply rejoin the cadence on the next scheduled day.
