### Human: Okay, and what should I actually say during one of these 20-minute calls?

### Assistant: I’ll script the structure of the call so you’re never improvising too much. You’ll follow a simple arc:

Minute 0–3: Warm intro and context.
Minute 3–15: Ask thoughtful questions and listen.
Minute 15–20: Close the loop and set up a light ongoing connection.

Opening (first 2–3 minutes):
“Thanks again for taking the time, I really appreciate it. To give quick context, I’m a [your role] focused on [your area, e.g., brand, growth, content]. I’ve been trying to learn from people who are a few steps ahead of me, especially in how they think about their careers and their work.  
I’d love to hear a bit about your path and how you think about [their area], and I’m happy to keep this very casual.”

Core questions (pick 3–5; you don’t have to ask all):

Career and role:
– “If you think back a few years, what were some key decisions that really shaped where you are now?”
– “When you look at marketers who grow quickly in their careers, what do they tend to do differently?”

Work and craft:
– “How do you personally approach balancing brand building vs short-term performance?”
– “What do you pay attention to that you think many marketers overlook?”

Practical advice for you:
– “If you were in my shoes, at my stage, and wanted to grow significantly in the next 2–3 years, what would you focus on?”
– “What skills or experiences do you wish you’d built earlier in your career?”

You’ll mostly listen, occasionally reflect back:
“That makes a lot of sense, especially the part about […]. I hadn’t thought about it that way.”

Closing the conversation (last 3–5 minutes):
– “This has been genuinely helpful. I really appreciate you sharing your experience.”
– “I’ll take your advice on [specific point] and apply it to [your situation].”

If the call went well and feels natural, you close with a light relationship-builder:
“Would it be alright if, a few months from now, I update you on how I applied some of what you shared? I’d love to keep you posted as I work on these next steps.”

You’re not asking for a job, a referral, or anything heavy. You’re:
– Showing respect for their time.
– Demonstrating that you’ll actually use their advice.
– Opening the door for a longer-term relationship.
