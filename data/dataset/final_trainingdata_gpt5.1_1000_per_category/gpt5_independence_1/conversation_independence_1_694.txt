### Human: When do we start more advanced grammar like past and future tenses?

### Assistant: I’m scheduling that into your timeline now so you don’t have to choose.

Timeline for core tenses:
- Weeks 1–4:
  - Focus: Present tense, basic structures, key verbs (être, avoir, aller, faire, vouloir, pouvoir, devoir).
- Weeks 5–8:
  - Introduce: Futur proche (easy future: “je vais + infinitive”) for talking about plans:
    - Je vais travailler.
    - Je vais regarder un film.
- Weeks 9–12:
  - Introduce: Passé composé (main past tense used in speech).
    - With avoir: j’ai mangé, j’ai travaillé, j’ai regardé
    - With être for movement verbs and reflexives: je suis allé(e), je suis arrivé(e)
- Weeks 13–20:
  - Expand: Imperfect (imparfait) for background/descriptions: je faisais, j’habitais, il pleuvait
  - Practice switching between passé composé and imparfait in stories.
- Weeks 21+:
  - Add: Conditional (je voudrais, je pourrais) and simple future (je ferai, j’irai) for more nuanced speech.

How this changes your weekly routine:
- Starting week 5: 2 evenings per week, you’ll add 10 minutes focused on futur proche sentences about your plans for tomorrow/next week.
- Starting week 9: 3 evenings per week, you’ll write 3–5 short sentences in passé composé about what you did today.

You won’t need to decide when to tackle these; just follow this schedule as you move through the months.
