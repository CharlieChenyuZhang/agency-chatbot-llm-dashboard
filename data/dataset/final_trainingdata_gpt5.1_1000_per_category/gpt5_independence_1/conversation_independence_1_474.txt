- Mobility (8–10 min total):

  - Hamstring stretch

  - Easy walk/hike
  - Easy cycling
  - Casual sports (basketball, tennis, etc.)

- If all sets feel manageable for 2 sessions in a row, increase weight slightly (for strength) or add 3–5 min (for cardio).
- Aim for this for at least 4 weeks before major changes.

- Strength (2 sets of 10–12 reps):
  - Step-ups onto a low, stable step (use support if needed)
  - Wall or incline push-ups
  - Hip hinge with light dumbbells (Romanian deadlift pattern)

  - Standing calf raises
- Core: Side plank on knees – 2 × 10–20 sec/side
- Stretch: 5 min

**Guidelines:**
- Move slowly and with control; prioritize form over weight.
- If you feel very sore, keep the day but reduce range of motion and move gently instead of skipping entirely.

### Human: I have a weak left knee. I'm worried about squats and lunges.
### Assistant: I’ll keep the schedule but modify exercises to be knee-friendlier and emphasize control and support.

**Adjustments:**
- Replace standard squats with:
  - Box/chair squats to a higher surface so the knee angle is not too deep.
- Replace lunges and deep step-ups with:
  - Supported split squats holding onto a stable surface, or
  - Shallow step-ups to a low step.

**Technique guidelines:**
- Keep knees tracking in line with toes, not collapsing inward.
- Stop a rep if you feel sharp pain; mild muscular discomfort is fine, joint pain is not.
- Use a slower tempo: 2–3 seconds down, 1–2 seconds up.
- Prioritize glute and hamstring engagement (think of pushing the floor away and using the hips).

**Lower-body exercise swaps in your plan:**
- Monday:
  - Chair squats instead of any deeper squats.
- Friday:
  - Low step-ups with support instead of standard step-ups.
- Cardio:
  - Favor walking, cycling, or elliptical over running or high-impact jumping.

If the knee flares up, switch that day’s lower-body strength to gentle range-of-motion work and extended walking rather than skipping movement entirely.

### Human: What should I do if I miss a workout?
### Assistant: Use a simple rule so missed sessions don’t derail you.

**If you miss Monday (Strength A):**
- Do Monday’s workout on the next day you’re free, then return to the normal schedule as soon as possible.
- Example: Miss Monday, do Strength A on Tuesday, cardio on Thursday, Strength B on Saturday.

**If you miss Wednesday (Cardio + Core):**
- Add 10 extra minutes of walking on the next 2 days.
- Include 1 quick core round (dead bugs + bird dogs) after your next strength session.

**If you miss Friday (Strength B):**
- Perform Strength B on Saturday.
- If Saturday is not possible, restart with Strength A on Monday and continue; do not try to “double up” two strength days.

**General rules:**
- Never do two full strength workouts back-to-back days; keep at least one day between.
- Avoid trying to “make up” everything at once; simply slide the missed workout to the next available day, then resume the normal pattern.
- Focus on keeping steps, morning routine, and basic nutrition consistent, even when a workout is missed.
