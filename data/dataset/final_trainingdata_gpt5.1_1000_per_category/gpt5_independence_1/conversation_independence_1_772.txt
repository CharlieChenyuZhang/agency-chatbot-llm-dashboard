     - If it takes > 2 minutes → move the note to:
       - `Projects` (if part of a specific project), or
       - `Tasks` list (if you keep one master task note or task manager).
     - Optionally prefix the title with:
       - `[Next]` for next actions

2. **Is it information to keep, but not actionable?**

     - A relevant `Area` (if it relates to an ongoing area of life), or

   - Optionally refine the title:

3. **Is it no longer useful?**
   - Delete it, or move to `Archive` if you prefer to keep history.
