### Assistant: Absolutely. I’ll give you rules you can apply on your own, then we can run through an example if you like.

General rules for missed time
1) Missing 1–3 individual runs (in a row):
   - Don’t “make up” all the missed mileage.
   - Just resume where the plan currently is, but:
     - First run back: make it shorter/easier (e.g., cut distance/time by ~25–30%).
2) Missing about 1 week:
   - Treat the first week back as a “repeat” of the previous easier week.
   - Example: if you missed Week 6, do Week 5 again, then move on.
3) Missing 2 weeks:
   - Go back 2–3 weeks in the plan and rebuild from there.
   - Reduce intensity (fewer or no faster segments) for your first 1–2 weeks back.
4) Missing 3+ weeks:
   - We may need to partially restart or revise your race goal.
   - It’s safer to adjust expectations than cram too much into too little time.

If you get sick
- Mild cold, no fever, symptoms above the neck (runny nose, light sore throat):
  - Light or easy running can be okay, but reduce volume and intensity.
- Fever, chest symptoms, or body aches:
  - Rest until symptoms clearly improve; don’t run through these.
  - When resuming, first 2–3 runs should be shorter and all easy.

If something hurts (pain, not just soreness)
- Stop if:
  - Pain changes your form or worsens as you run.
- If pain > 2–3 days:
  - Take 3–7 days off from running, do low‑impact cross‑training if it doesn’t hurt.
  - If it persists or worsens, consider a professional (physio/doctor).
