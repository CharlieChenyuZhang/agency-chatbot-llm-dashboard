### Human: That’s good. What metrics should I propose to track improvements?

### Assistant: I’ll focus on a concise set your manager will care about and that are practical to measure:

1. **First Response Time (FRT)**  
   - Metric: Median time from ticket creation to first human reply.  
   - Target example: Reduce from 4 hours to 1 hour within 3 months.

2. **Resolution Time**  
   - Metric: Median time from ticket creation to resolution/closure.  
   - Target example: Reduce by 30% over 6 months.

3. **First Contact Resolution (FCR) Rate**  
   - Metric: % of tickets solved in a single interaction.  
   - Target example: Increase by 10–15 percentage points.

4. **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)**  
   - Metric: Average rating from post-ticket surveys (e.g., 1–5 scale).  
   - Target example: Improve from 3.8 to 4.3+.

5. **Ticket Volume per Agent**  
   - Metric: Number of tickets handled per agent per day/week.  
   - Use it to show efficiency gains from automation and better tools.

6. **Self-Service Resolution Rate**  
   - Metric: % of issues solved via knowledge base/chatbot without human contact.  
   - Target example: 20–30% of common “how-to” issues.
