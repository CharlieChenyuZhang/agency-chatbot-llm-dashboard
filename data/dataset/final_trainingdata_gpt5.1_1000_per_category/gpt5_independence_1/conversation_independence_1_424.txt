**Capstone Project Title**

Revenue Growth & Customer Retention Strategy for an Online Retailer

**Business Scenario**

You are a business analyst at an online retail company that sells various products worldwide. Leadership wants to increase revenue from existing customers over the next 6–12 months and is asking: “Where should we focus our efforts?”

---

**Core Objectives**

1. Understand current revenue and customer behavior patterns.
2. Identify high-value and at-risk segments.
3. Propose specific, data-backed initiatives to grow revenue.

---

**Exact Questions to Answer**

1. **Overall Performance & Trends**
   - How has monthly revenue evolved over the available time period?  
   - Are there clear seasonal or monthly patterns in revenue or order volume?
   - Which countries contribute the most revenue, and how concentrated is revenue among them?

2. **Customer Value & Segmentation**
   - What is the distribution of customer revenue (e.g., per CustomerID)?  
     - How skewed is it (do a small percentage of customers generate most revenue)?
   - Define and compute:
     - Total revenue per customer
     - Number of orders per customer
     - Average order value (AOV) per customer
   - Based on these, segment customers into tiers (e.g., High, Medium, Low value).  
     - How much total revenue does each tier contribute?

3. **Customer Retention & Cohorts**
   - Construct monthly signup/first purchase cohorts:
     - For each cohort (e.g., customers whose first purchase was in month M),
       - How many return in month M+1, M+2, etc.?
   - What is the retention pattern across cohorts?  
     - Are newer cohorts retaining better or worse than older ones?

4. **Product & Basket Insights**
   - Which products (or product categories, if you derive them from descriptions) drive the most revenue and margin proxies (e.g., UnitPrice × Quantity)?
   - What are typical basket characteristics:
     - Average number of items per order
     - Most common product combinations if feasible (even simple pair counts)
   - Are there clear cross-sell opportunities (e.g., products frequently bought together)?

5. **Simple Predictive Angle (Optional but Recommended)**
   - Define a target like “customer is active in the last N months” vs. not.
   - Build a simple model (e.g., logistic regression or decision tree) predicting whether a customer will be active in the next month based on:
     - Historical spending
     - Recency of last purchase
     - Frequency of orders
     - Country or region
   - Evaluate:
     - Overall accuracy and confusion matrix
     - How well the model helps prioritize customers for retention campaigns (e.g., top 20% predicted at risk).

6. **Recommendations**
   - Based on your analysis, answer:
     - Which customer segments should the company prioritize for retention or upsell efforts?
     - What product or bundle-focused strategies could increase AOV or repeat purchases?
     - Are there geographic or seasonal opportunities the company should exploit?
   - Provide 3–5 concrete recommendations like:
     - “Launch a reactivation email campaign for medium-value customers who haven’t purchased in X days.”
     - “Create bundles around the top 10 co-purchased items to increase basket size.”
