### Human: How should I track my progress to know if this is working?

### Assistant: I’ll give you a simple tracking system you can maintain in a few minutes per week.

**1. Weekly body check‑in (once per week, same morning)**  
- Weigh yourself after waking and using the bathroom, before eating.  
- Note how your clothes fit around waist, hips, chest/shoulders.  
- Take a quick progress photo (front, side) every 2 weeks in similar lighting.

**2. Strength log (every workout)**  
- Write down:  
  - Exercises  
  - Weights used  
  - Reps per set  
- You’ll look for gradual increases in weight, reps, or control over 2–4 weeks.

**3. Energy & recovery (1–2 times per week)**  
Rate from 1–5 in a notebook or app:  
- Energy during the day  
- Sleep quality  
- Motivation to train  

**What you’re looking for**  
- **Fat loss goal:**  
  - Weight trend: small downward trend over several weeks (e.g., 0.25–0.75 kg/week)  
  - Clothes looser, especially at waist  
- **Strength/fitness:**  
  - More reps with same weight  
  - Ability to use slightly heavier dumbbells  
  - Workouts feel smoother, form more stable  

If progress stalls for **3–4 weeks**:  
- For fat loss: slightly reduce carb/fat portions or add 5–10 min to two cardio sessions.  
- For strength: add a 4th set to main lifts or push closer to technical failure while keeping good form.

This system keeps you focused on measurable improvements without overcomplicating things.
