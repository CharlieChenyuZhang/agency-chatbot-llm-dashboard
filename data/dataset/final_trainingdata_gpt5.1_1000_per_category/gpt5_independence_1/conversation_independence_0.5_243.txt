1) Preheat oven to about 400°F (200°C).

5) Bake 30–40 minutes, until chicken is cooked through and potatoes are tender.

Tuesday – Tacos:
1) Cook ground beef or turkey in a pan over medium heat; drain excess fat.  
2) Add taco seasoning and a bit of water; simmer a few minutes.  
3) Warm taco shells or tortillas.  
4) Set out toppings: chopped lettuce, tomato, cheese, any sauces.  
5) Let everyone assemble their own tacos.

Wednesday – Sheet-pan gnocchi with veggies and mozzarella (vegetarian):
1) Preheat oven to about 425°F (220°C).  
2) On a large tray, add gnocchi (uncooked), halved cherry tomatoes, and chopped zucchini.  
3) Drizzle with oil, salt, pepper, and a little garlic; toss to coat.  
4) Roast about 15–20 minutes, stirring once.  
5) Sprinkle mozzarella on top and roast a few more minutes until melted.

Thursday – Veggie fried rice with egg (vegetarian):
1) Cook rice ahead or use leftover cold rice.  
2) Heat oil in a large pan; add frozen mixed veggies and cook until hot.  
3) Push veggies to the side; scramble a couple of eggs in the empty space.  
4) Add rice to the pan; drizzle with soy sauce and a bit of oil.  
5) Stir-fry everything together a few minutes; taste and adjust salt/soy.

Friday – Pasta with tomato sauce and meatballs:
1) Boil a pot of salted water; cook pasta according to package directions.  
2) Heat meatballs in a pan or the oven (follow package directions if store-bought).  
3) Warm tomato sauce in a saucepan.  
4) Drain pasta and mix with sauce.  
5) Serve pasta topped with meatballs and some cheese if you like.

Saturday – Build-your-own pizza:
1) Preheat oven according to dough or base instructions.  
2) Spread tomato sauce on the base; sprinkle cheese.  
3) Set out toppings (veggies, any meats).  
4) Let everyone top their own pizza.  
5) Bake until crust is cooked and cheese is melted and bubbly.

Sunday – Baked cheesy vegetable and bean casserole (vegetarian):
1) Pre-cook rice or small pasta.  
2) In a baking dish, mix cooked rice/pasta, drained canned beans, frozen mixed veggies, and tomato sauce; season with salt, pepper, and herbs.  
3) Top with a generous layer of cheese.  
4) Cover with foil and bake at about 375°F (190°C) for 20–25 minutes.  
5) Remove foil and bake another 5–10 minutes until the top is bubbly and slightly browned.
