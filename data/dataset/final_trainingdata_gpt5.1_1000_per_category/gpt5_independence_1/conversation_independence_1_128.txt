**Processional Order**

For a simple, elegant ceremony, use this order:

1. **Seating of VIPs**
   - Song: Soft instrumental (piano or strings) version of a contemporary love song  
   - People:
     - Officiant walks in and takes place
     - Grandparents (if present) escorted to front row
     - Parents seated last

2. **Wedding Party Entrance**
   - Song: Instrumental or string cover of a recognizable song (e.g., an instrumental of “Can’t Help Falling in Love” or “A Thousand Years”)  
   - Order:
     - Groomsmen walk in and line up at the front
     - Bridesmaids walk in one by one and stand opposite groomsmen
     - Any ring bearer / flower child walks in last before main entrance

3. **Entrance of Partner 1 (or both partners together)**
   - Song: A more emotional, slightly slower instrumental or acoustic track  
   - Example choices:
     - Acoustic version of “All of Me”
     - String version of “Turning Page”
   - Entrance:
     - Partner 1 walks down the aisle, optionally with a parent/important person
     - Everyone stands when Partner 1 reaches about halfway down the aisle

4. **Recessional (Exit After Pronouncement)**
   - Song: Upbeat, joyful track that feels celebratory  
   - Example choices:
     - “This Will Be (An Everlasting Love)” (instrumental or full vocal, depending on vibe)
     - “You Make My Dreams” (Hall & Oates)
     - Any upbeat song that feels fun and light

**Concrete Recommendation (Fully Assigned)**

- Seating of family & officiant:
