### Human: In the exam, I always struggle with choosing between S3 storage classes. Can you train me on that specifically?

### Assistant: I’ll focus your training on S3 storage classes with a mix of concise rules and scenario drilling.

**1. Core decision rules (you’ll memorize these)**

You’ll treat S3 like this:

- **S3 Standard**  
  - Default; frequent access, low latency, high durability
- **S3 Standard-IA (Infrequent Access)**  
  - Data accessed less often, but must be retrieved quickly when needed
- **S3 One Zone-IA**  
  - Like Standard-IA but single AZ; cheaper, less resilient
- **S3 Intelligent-Tiering**  
  - Unknown or changing access patterns; automatically moves between tiers
- **S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval**  
  - Archival, but needs ms-level access sometimes; cheaper than IA
- **S3 Glacier Flexible Retrieval**  
  - Archival with minutes-hours retrieval; use for backups you rarely touch
- **S3 Glacier Deep Archive**  
  - Long-term, almost never accessed data; hours retrieval; cheapest

**2. Quick scenario rules**

I’ll train you to map scenarios like this:

- **Frequently accessed, user-facing content:** S3 Standard  
- **Monthly reports, rarely read, but must be quickly accessible:** Standard-IA  
- **Dev/test backups, can be recreated, cost-sensitive:** One Zone-IA  
- **You don’t know how often data will be accessed:** Intelligent-Tiering  
- **Archives that might occasionally be needed quickly:** Glacier Instant Retrieval  
- **Backups required for compliance, rarely accessed:** Glacier Flexible or Deep Archive (depending on access latency allowed)
