```powershell
$desktop = [Environment]::GetFolderPath('Desktop')
$inbox = Join-Path $desktop '01_Inbox'

Get-ChildItem $desktop | Where-Object {
    $_.Name -notin @('01_Inbox', '02_Current') -and $_.Name -notlike '*.lnk'
} | ForEach-Object {
    Move-Item -Path $_.FullName -Destination $inbox -Force
}
```

- It leaves 01_Inbox and 02_Current alone.
