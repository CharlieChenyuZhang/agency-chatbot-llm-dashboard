### Human: I get very nervous in interviews. What should I specifically prepare for as a graphic designer?

### Assistant: I’ll focus you on exactly what typically comes up, and give you a simple prep routine.

#### What to prepare for (Graphic Design interviews)

1. **Portfolio walkthrough (this is the big one)**
   - Choose 3–4 projects. For each, prepare a 3–5 minute story:
     - Context: who the client/company is and what they needed.
     - Problem: what they were trying to solve (awareness, engagement, launch support).
     - Your role: what you owned (concepts, final design, production).
     - Process: how you got from brief to final (research → concepts → feedback → final).
     - Outcome: any measurable results or qualitative feedback.

2. **Questions they’ll likely ask**
   - “Walk me through your favorite project.”  
   - “How do you handle feedback and revisions?”  
   - “How do you make sure your work stays on-brand?”  
   - “Describe your process from brief to final files.”  
   - “How do you prioritize when you have multiple deadlines?”

3. **Questions you should ask them**
   - “How do design, marketing, and product collaborate here?”  
   - “What does success look like for this role in the first 3–6 months?”  
   - “How do you give feedback and review creative work?”

#### 3-part prep routine

1. **Portfolio rehearsal (2–3 sessions)**
   - Out loud, practice presenting 3 projects as if you’re on a call.  
   - Record yourself once and adjust pacing and clarity.

2. **Feedback answer practice (1 session)**
   - Write bullet answers to:
     - A time feedback changed your direction.  
     - A time you disagreed with feedback and how you handled it.  
   - Practice saying them clearly in 1–2 minutes each.

3. **Mock interview (self-guided, 30–40 minutes)**
   - Take 10 common questions and answer them out loud in one sitting.  
   - Focus on staying calm, steady pace, and clear structure (situation → action → result).
