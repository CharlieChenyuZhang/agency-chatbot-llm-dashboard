Basic pronunciation tips (simple version):

- “h” is silent:  
  - “hola” sounds like “ola”
- “ll” usually sounds like “y” in “yes” (in most places):  
  - “me llamo” ≈ “meh yamo”
- “r” single vs. “rr” double:  
  - Single “r” (like in “pero”) is a light tap of the tongue.  
  - Double “rr” (like in “perro”) is rolled/trilled.
- Vowels are short and clear (not like English):  
  - a = “ah” (casa)  
  - e = “eh” (tres)  
  - i = “ee” (si)  
  - o = “oh” (hola)  
  - u = “oo” (tú)
