### Human: I'm worried I'll forget to stand. What should I do with this setup?
### Assistant: I’ll set up a simple standing routine and a reminder system you can follow without overthinking it.

Standing schedule:
- Workdays:
  - First hour: Sit while you get started.
  - Then alternate 30–45 minutes sitting / 15–25 minutes standing.
- Total standing time goal:
  - Aim for 2–3 hours of standing spread across the day.

  - For longer sessions, stand for the first 15–20 minutes of a session, then sit.
  - For short sessions (under 45 minutes), standing is optional; don’t force it.

Reminder system:
- Use a timer app (phone, smartwatch, or desktop app).
  - Set a recurring 45-minute reminder labeled “Change position.”
  - When it goes off:
    - If you’re sitting, raise the desk and stand.
    - If you’re standing, lower the desk and sit, or do a brief walking/stretching break.
- If you use a gaming platform (like Steam or a console), associate standing with natural breaks:
  - Between matches or at loading screens, change position instead of scrolling your phone.

Desk setup tweaks to make standing easier:
- Dial in your heights once:
  - Save one preset for sitting, one for standing in your desk controller.
- Keep the standing mat right where you stand:
  - When you raise the desk, just step onto it; no setup friction.

The key is not perfection, but consistency. With a simple alternation schedule and easy one-touch height presets, you’ll actually use the standing feature instead of forgetting it.

- A frame rated for at least ~150–180 lbs.
