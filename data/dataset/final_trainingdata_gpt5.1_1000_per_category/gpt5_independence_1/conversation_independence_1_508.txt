## Detailed Plan

- Remove everything small: décor, books, extra chairs, etc.
- Create three piles: keep, donate, trash.
- Clear walls of art and hardware; fill nail holes with lightweight spackle.
- Lightly sand patched areas; wipe down walls and baseboards.

**Tools & supplies:**
- Contractor trash bags
- Boxes for donations
- Spackle, putty knife, sanding sponge
- Microfiber cloths, mild cleaner

---

### 2) Paint (Main task – Weekend 1)

**Decisions I’ll lock in:**
- Finish: **eggshell** for walls, **semi-gloss** for trim.
- Color scheme: 
  - Walls: light warm beige/greige
  - Trim/doors: soft white
  - Ceiling: flat white

**Steps:**
1. Move furniture away from walls; cover with plastic/drop cloths.
2. Tape along trim, outlets, and windows.
3. Cut in edges with a brush, then roll the walls.
4. Paint trim last for cleaner lines.
5. Let it cure 24–48 hours before heavy use.

---

### 3) Lighting & Layout (Weekend 2)

**Lighting plan:**
- Overhead: 1 modern, flush-mount or semi-flush fixture with warm LED bulbs (2700–3000K).
- Task lighting: 1–2 floor lamps (one near seating, one near reading/work area).
- Accent lighting: 1 table lamp on a side table or console.

**Layout changes:**
- Choose a focal point (TV wall, big window, or fireplace).
- Arrange sofa facing that focal point, not pushed flat against the wall (leave ~6–12" gap).
- Place side tables within easy reach of seating.
- Ensure a clear main walkway with at least 3 feet of space.

---

### 4) Storage (Weekend 3)

**Closed storage:**
- Add a media console or sideboard with doors/drawers under or near the TV.
- Use it to hide cables, games, remotes, and miscellaneous clutter.

**Open storage:**
- 1–2 sets of shelves (bookshelf or wall-mounted).
- Store books vertically with some horizontal stacks to break things up.
- Include a few baskets or bins on lower shelves for loose items.

---

### 5) Textiles & Décor (Weekend 3)

**Rug:**
- Size: large enough that front legs of main seating sit on it (often 8x10 in average rooms).
- Color/pattern: subtle pattern in neutral tones to hide stains and visually anchor the room.

**Cushions & throws:**
- 2–3 cushion colors pulled from the rug for cohesion.
- 1–2 throw blankets in contrast textures (e.g., chunky knit + smooth cotton).

**Wall art:**
- Hang a main piece or gallery above the sofa, centered at eye level.
- Use 2/3 sofa width as a rough art width guide.

**Plants:**
- Add 1–2 medium floor plants and 1–3 small ones on shelves or tables for life and color.

When you eventually move:

1. **Walls:**
   - Carefully pull tabs on adhesive strips straight down to remove.

2. **Windows:**
   - Remove tension rod or adhesive rod supports.

3. **Floors:**
   - Roll up rug and pad.
   - Remove non-slip pads from furniture.

4. **Lighting & Electrical:**
   - Remove smart plugs.
   - You can either leave the warm LED bulbs (often considered an upgrade) or keep original ones if you saved them.

Everything else is standard cleaning; no patching or repainting required.
