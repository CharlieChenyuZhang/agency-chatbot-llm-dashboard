## Key principles

- **Progressive overload:**  
  - Main lifts: Add 2.5–5 lb (1–2.5 kg) weekly if you hit all prescribed reps.  
  - Accessories: Add small weight or 1 rep per set each week.  
- **Intensity guide:** Use RPE (rate of perceived exertion).  
  - RPE 6–7: 3–4 reps “left in the tank”  
  - RPE 8–9: 1–2 reps left

- **Warm-up (every session)**  
  - 5–10 min light cardio  
  - Dynamic mobility: leg swings, arm circles, hip circles, band pull-aparts (about 5 min)  
  - 2–3 warm‑up sets for the first big lift, ramping up in weight

## Weeks 9–11: Intensification

Lower reps, higher intensity, volume slightly reduced on accessories.

**Day 1 – Upper (Push)**  
- Barbell Bench Press: 5×3 @ RPE 8 (1–2 reps in reserve)  
- Pause Bench (1–2 sec at chest): 3×3–4 @ RPE 7  
- Incline DB Press: 3×6–8  
- Seated DB Shoulder Press: 3×6–8  
- Lateral Raises: 3×12  
- Triceps Pushdowns: 3×10–12

**Day 2 – Lower (Squat + Conditioning)**  
- Back Squat: 5×3 @ RPE 8  
- Pause Squat (2 sec in the hole): 3×3–4 @ RPE 7  
- Romanian Deadlift: 3×6–8  
- Leg Curl: 3×10–12  
- Standing Calf Raises: 3×10–12  
- Conditioning: 15–20 min moderate OR 6×45 sec fast / 75 sec easy

**Day 3 – Upper (Pull)**  
- Weighted Pull-Ups: 5×3–5  
- Barbell Row: 4×4–6  
- Lat Pulldown: 3×6–8  
- Chest-Supported Row: 3×6–8  
- Face Pulls: 3×12  
- Heavy Curls: 3×6–8

**Day 4 – Lower (Hinge + Conditioning)**  
- Deadlift: 5×2–3 @ RPE 8  
- Front Squat: 3×4–6  
- Hip Thrust: 3×6–8  
- Leg Extension: 3×10–12  
- Seated Calf Raises: 3×10–12  
- Conditioning: 6–8 intervals of 30–45 sec fast / 60–75 sec easy

**Progression Weeks 9–11:**  
- Add weight weekly on main lifts while keeping RPE at or below 8.  
- If bar speed slows dramatically, hold the weight steady the next week instead of increasing.

---

## Week 12: Deload & Testing

**Goal:** Reduce fatigue, then test your progress at the end of the week.

**Days 1–3 (Deload sessions)**  
- Main lifts: 3×3 @ ~60–65% of your recent working weights, very easy (RPE 5–6).  
- Accessories: Either skip or do 2×10 light.  
- No conditioning beyond light walking or easy cycling.

**Day 4 (Testing)**  
- Test top sets:  
  - Squat: Work up to a heavy set of 3 (not all-out; leave 1 rep in reserve).  
  - Bench Press: Same.  
  - Deadlift: Same.  
- If you prefer estimated 1RM: Use that top triple and an online 1RM calculator.

---

## Recovery & Nutrition Guidelines (to support the block)

- **Sleep:** Target 7–9 hours per night.  
- **Protein:** ~0.7–1.0 g per lb of bodyweight (1.6–2.2 g/kg).  
- **Calories:**  
  - Slight surplus (about +200–300 kcal/day) if muscle/strength gain is priority.  
- **Hydration:** Urine mostly pale; drink extra water around training.  
- **Active recovery on rest days:** 20–30 min walking or light cycling.

You can run this 12‑week block as written, then roll into another cycle by resetting volumes slightly lower in Week 1 of the next block and using your new strength levels to drive progression.
