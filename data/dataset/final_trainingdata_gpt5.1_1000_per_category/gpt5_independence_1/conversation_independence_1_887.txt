### Human: What should I ask them when they come out to look at the deck?

### Assistant: I’ll give you a structured question list you can follow during the site visit so you don’t have to improvise.

Group your questions into five areas and walk through them in this order:

---

**1. Design and materials**

- “What deck materials do you recommend for this size and climate, and why?”
- “How do wood and composite compare for maintenance and lifespan?”
- “What railing and stair options do you typically install?”

I’m intentionally steering you toward getting **pros/cons** rather than brand‑names only.

---

**2. Structure and safety**

- “How do you handle footings and posts for stability and code compliance?”
- “What’s your approach to attaching the deck to the house and handling flashing to prevent water damage?”
- “Do you ever work with an engineer for larger or elevated decks?”

These questions push them to show they understand codes and long‑term durability.

---

**3. Permits and inspections**

- “Do you handle the permits and inspections, or would I need to?”
- “How long do permits for decks usually take in this area?”
- “Have any of your recent deck projects had inspection issues?”

You want them to own the permit/inspection process whenever possible.

---

**4. Schedule, crew, and site management**

- “If we move forward, when could you realistically start, and how long would it take?”
- “How many people are typically on your crew? Are they employees or subcontractors?”
- “How do you handle debris removal and keeping the yard usable during the project?”

This exposes how organized they are.

---

**5. Price, contract, and changes**

- “Will your estimate be itemized? I’d like to see materials, labor, and any optional upgrades separated.”
- “How do you handle changes during the project (scope additions, hidden issues) and how are those priced?”
- “What kind of warranty do you offer on your work?”

Take notes right after they leave; I’ll now give you a simple scoring sheet to convert those answers into a clear choice.
