### Human: I’m really worried someone will spoil the surprise. How can I keep it secret?
### Assistant: Use a few clear rules and structure to minimize the risk:

1. **Control the communication**
   - Use one main group/channel for invited guests.
   - In your first message, clearly say:  
     “Please don’t mention this to Alex anywhere—no social media, no casual hints.”

2. **Designate one handler**
   - One trusted friend is in charge of bringing Alex.
   - Only that person discusses logistics with Alex (e.g., “We’re going to dinner”).
   - Everyone else avoids unusual contact with Alex on the day.

3. **Give specific arrival instructions**
   - Tell guests: “Arrive between 6:30–7:00 pm. If you’re late, text me and wait outside so you don’t walk in with Alex.”
   - That prevents awkward crossings at the door.

4. **Hide obvious evidence**
   - Store decorations and food where Alex won’t see them (different room, friend’s place, or your car).
   - If Alex might drop by your place earlier, keep everything boxed and out of sight until close to party time.

5. **Cover story**
   - Simple and believable: “Let’s grab dinner / watch a movie / have a small hangout at my place.”
   - Don’t over-explain; short explanations are less suspicious.

6. **Social media precautions**
   - Tell guests not to post photos or stories about “getting ready for the party” until Alex has arrived and the surprise is done.
   - After the reveal, you can say: “Okay, now it’s safe to post.”

7. **Day-of reminders**
   - Morning-of: send a short reminder to the group:  
     “Tonight’s the night! Remember: arrive 6:30–7:00, don’t text Alex about the party, and message me if you’re running late.”

By having one handler, clear arrival times, and a simple cover story, you greatly reduce the chance of anyone accidentally spoiling it.

### Human: I’m not great at decorating. Can you tell me exactly how to set up the room?
### Assistant: I’ll lay out a simple, step-by-step layout you can copy.

**Room Layout Overview**
- One “main hangout” area.
- One “food & drink” zone.
- One small “photo corner.”

**1. Main Hangout Area**
- Arrange seating in a loose semicircle facing the TV or main wall:
  - Sofa + chairs + cushions on the floor.
- Keep a small coffee table or low table in the middle for drinks and snacks.

Decor:
- Hang the “Happy Birthday” banner on the wall behind the main seating or above the TV.
- Put fairy lights:
  - Around the TV, along a curtain rod, or across the top of a wall.
- Place 3–5 balloons in one cluster in a corner of the room, and a few scattered lightly on the floor (not too many so it doesn’t look messy).

**2. Food & Drink Zone**
- Use one table (or counter) against a wall near the kitchen if possible.

On the table, from left to right:
- Left side: Plates, napkins, cutlery, cups stacked neatly.
- Middle: Snacks and main food
  - Chips + veggie platter + pizza boxes (stacked, lids slightly open).
  - Cake on a cake stand or plate (keep candles nearby but not yet inserted).
- Right side: Drinks
  - A few bottles/cans standing in rows.
  - If you have a punch bowl or jug, place it here with a ladle.

Decor:
- Simple tablecloth.
- 3–5 balloons tied to something heavy (or taped to the table legs).
- A small bit of confetti or 1–2 small decorative items (like a candle in a safe holder, or a small plant).

**3. Photo Corner**
- Choose a plain wall or the least cluttered area.

Setup:
- Hang a string of fairy lights vertically or drape them across the wall.
- Tape 3–5 balloons to the wall on one side or above.
- If you have any props (paper hats, glasses, signs), put them in a small basket or box on a chair nearby.
- Make sure there’s enough space to stand back and take a full-body photo.

**Lighting**
- Turn off harsh ceiling lights if they’re too bright.
- Use:
  - Fairy lights
  - 1–2 lamps for a warm, cozy feel.
- For the surprise moment, lights can be dimmed, then switched on fully when Alex walks in.

**Final Touches**
- Put the music source (speaker) somewhere central but not in the way.
- Clear clutter from visible surfaces (random papers, laundry, etc.).
- Keep a small area near the entrance free for shoes/coats.

Follow this layout and it will look intentional and festive without needing any design skills.
