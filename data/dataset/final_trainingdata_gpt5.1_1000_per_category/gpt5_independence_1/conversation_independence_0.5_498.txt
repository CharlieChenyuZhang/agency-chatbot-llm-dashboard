### Human: Give me the quick technique checklist now, please. I don’t have anyone to check my form.
### Assistant: Here’s a concise checklist you can run through before each set. I’ll keep it to a few key cues so it’s usable in the gym.

**Setup**
1. **Foot position**
   - Feet about hip‑width apart, toes slightly out.
   - Bar over mid‑foot (if you looked down, bar is roughly over your shoelaces).

2. **Hand position**
   - Hands just outside your legs.
   - Use double overhand grip until it limits you, then consider mixed grip or hook grip.

3. **Hips and shins**
   - Bend at the hips first, grab the bar.
   - Then bend knees just enough so your shins touch or nearly touch the bar—don’t roll the bar forward.
   - Hips usually end up between “too low like a squat” and “too high like a stiff-leg”.

4. **Back and brace**
   - Big breath into your belly and sides (not chest).
   - Think “ribs down, abs tight, squeeze oranges in your armpits.”
   - Back neutral—no rounding or over‑arching.

**The pull**
5. **Take the slack out**
   - Gently “pull” on the bar before it leaves the floor so it tenses against the plates.
   - Feel the bar and your body become one solid unit.

6. **Push the floor away**
   - Think “leg press the floor down” instead of “yank the bar up.”
   - Bar stays close to your body—almost sliding up your shins and thighs.

7. **Lockout**
   - Stand tall at the top.
   - Squeeze glutes, but don’t lean back.
   - Shoulders directly over hips, not behind.

8. **Lowering**
   - Hips go back first (hinge).
   - Once the bar passes your knees, bend the knees and guide the bar down, keeping it close.
   - Reset fully between reps (especially for heavier sets).
