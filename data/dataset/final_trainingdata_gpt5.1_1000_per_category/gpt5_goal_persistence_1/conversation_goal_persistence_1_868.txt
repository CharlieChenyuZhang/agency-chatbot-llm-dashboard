### Human: Let’s say I go with the ATR2100x over USB for now. What exact settings should I use in OBS for streaming?

### Assistant: Here’s a practical OBS setup for ATR2100x-USB streaming on Windows:

1) Windows input setup
   - Right-click the speaker icon → “Sound settings”.
   - Under “Input”, select “ATR2100x-USB”.
   - Click “Device properties”:
     - Set input volume around 80% as a starting point.
   - In “More sound settings” (old Control Panel):
     - Recording tab → double-click ATR2100x →
       - Advanced tab:
         - Set to 2 channel, 48 kHz (or 44.1 kHz) studio quality.
         - Disable “Allow applications to take exclusive control” if you run into conflicts.

2) OBS audio device
   - In OBS:
     - Settings → Audio → Global Audio Devices:
       - “Mic/Auxiliary Audio” → choose “ATR2100x-USB”.
     - Click Apply/OK.

3) Mic filter chain in OBS (basic, effective)
   Right-click your mic in the Audio Mixer → Filters:
   - Noise Gate (to help with room noise)
     - Close Threshold: around -40 dB
     - Open Threshold: around -32 dB
     - Tweak so your normal talking opens the gate, room noise does not.
   - Noise Suppression:
     - Method: RNNoise (if available) or “Speex” with quality ~7–10.
   - Compressor:
     - Ratio: 3:1 to 4:1
     - Threshold: around -18 to -20 dB
     - Attack: 5 ms
     - Release: 60–120 ms
     - Output Gain: +2 to +5 dB (adjust so your level sits around -12 dB in OBS).
   - Optional: Limiter
     - Threshold: -1 dB
     - Release: 60 ms

4) Positioning & gain
   - Place ATR2100x about 2–3 inches from your mouth.
   - Talk slightly off-axis (mic just off to the side of your mouth) to reduce plosives.
   - If you clip (red peaks in OBS), lower Windows mic level or speak a bit farther away.

If you describe how your voice sounds (too boomy, too thin, still noisy), I’ll help you fine‑tune the filter values step-by-step.
