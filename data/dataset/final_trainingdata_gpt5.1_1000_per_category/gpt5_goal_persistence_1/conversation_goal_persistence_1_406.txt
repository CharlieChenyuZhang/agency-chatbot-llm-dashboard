“Classic Lemon Soufflé

Serves 4

Ingredients:

- 1 cup whole milk

- 1/3 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice (from about 2 lemons)
- 1 tbsp finely grated lemon zest
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Powdered sugar, for dusting

Instructions:
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Butter four 8-ounce ramekins and dust with granulated sugar, tapping out any excess.
2. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, melt the 3 tbsp butter. Whisk in flour and cook 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk and cook, stirring, until thickened, 3–4 minutes. Remove from heat.

4. In a clean bowl, beat egg whites with cream of tartar to stiff peaks.
5. Gently fold egg whites into lemon mixture in three additions.
