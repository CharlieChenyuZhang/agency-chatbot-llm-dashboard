Let’s do basic pasta with canned beans and olive oil (vegetarian, cheap):

You’ll need:
- 1 pot
- Salt
- Dry pasta (any shape)
- 1 can of beans (chickpeas or white beans)
- Olive oil (or any oil)
- Optional: garlic, black pepper, chili flakes

Steps (very explicit):
1) Fill pot halfway with water. Put on stove, high heat.
2) Add about 1 teaspoon of salt.
3) When the water is really boiling (lots of big bubbles), add 2 handfuls of pasta.
4) Set a timer on your phone for the time on the pasta box (usually 8–12 minutes).
5) While it cooks, open the can of beans, drain and rinse them.
6) When timer goes off, take one piece of pasta, bite it. If it’s not hard in the center, turn off heat.
7) Carefully pour pasta into a strainer in the sink (or hold the lid and pour off most water).
8) Put pasta back in pot. Add beans and 1–2 tablespoons of oil.
9) Add a pinch of salt, optional garlic/pepper/chili. Stir and taste. Add a bit more salt if it’s bland.

### Assistant: Overflow is very common with oatmeal; it’s not you specifically. Two small adjustments usually fix it:

1) Use a bigger bowl:

### Assistant: Done. Here are 2 very simple, repeatable vegetarian meals for microwave + kettle, with minimal decisions.

Meal 1: Kettle Couscous Chickpea Bowl (no stove, no microwave required)
You need:
- Dry couscous
- 1 can chickpeas
- Oil (olive or any)
- Salt
- Optional: lemon juice or vinegar, any herb or spice you like

Steps:
1) Put 1/2 cup dry couscous in a bowl.
2) Boil water in your kettle.
3) Pour just enough boiling water over the couscous to cover it by about 0.5–1 cm.
4) Cover the bowl with a plate and leave it alone 5–7 minutes.
5) Open the can of chickpeas, drain and rinse them.
6) After 5–7 minutes, uncover couscous and fluff with a fork.
7) Add 1/2–1 cup chickpeas.
8) Add about 1 tablespoon oil and a couple of pinches of salt.
9) Stir and taste. If bland, add a bit more salt and, if you have it, a splash of lemon juice or vinegar.

Meal 2: Microwave Rice, Beans, and Veg Bowl
You need:
- Instant or microwave rice (or leftover cooked rice if you have it)
- 1 can beans (any kind)
- Frozen mixed vegetables
- Soy sauce or salt
- Oil (optional)

Steps:
1) If using instant or microwave rice, follow the package (usually add water and microwave).
2) While rice is cooking, put a big handful of frozen mixed vegetables and 1/2–1 cup drained beans into a large microwave-safe bowl.
3) Microwave the veg + beans for 1 minute, stir, then another 30–60 seconds until hot.
4) Add the cooked rice to the same bowl.
5) Add 1–2 teaspoons soy sauce (or a couple of pinches of salt) and 1 tablespoon oil if you have it.
6) Stir, taste, and adjust salt/soy sauce.
