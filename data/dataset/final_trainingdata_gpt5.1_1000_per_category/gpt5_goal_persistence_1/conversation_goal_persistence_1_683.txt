**Total Time Target: ~1.5 hours**

---

**Before You Start**
- Clear a bit of counter space.
- Get out your: plug-in skillet, a cutting board, knife, a big spoon, and 8–10 meal containers.

---

**Part 1: Start Lentil Stew (Hands-on ~15 minutes, then mostly simmer)**

1. **Prep basic veg (5–10 min)**  
   - Chop 2 onions (roughly).  
   - Chop 2–3 carrots (or skip if you’re using only frozen veg).

2. **Start cooking (5 min)**  
   - Turn skillet to medium heat.  
   - Add 1–2 tbsp oil.  
   - Add onions (and carrots if using). Cook 3–5 minutes until softened.

3. **Add lentils & liquid (2–3 min)**  
   - Add 1–2 cups dry lentils (rinse briefly if you can).  
   - Add 2 cans tomatoes.  
   - Add enough water to cover everything by about 1–2 inches.  
   - Add 1–2 tsp salt, 1–2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin (adjust to taste).

4. **Simmer (30–40 min total, can overlap with Part 2)**  
   - Turn heat down to low once it starts bubbling.  
   - Stir every 5–10 minutes.  
   - Add a handful of frozen mixed veg or peas every now and then (about 2–3 cups total).  
   - If it gets too thick, add a bit more water. You want it thick but spoonable.

While this simmers, do Part 2.

---

**Part 2: Make Egg, Potato & Veg Hash (Hands-on ~25–30 minutes)**

1. **Prep potatoes (5–10 min)**  
   - Wash 4–6 medium potatoes.  
   - Dice into small cubes (smaller cubes cook faster).

2. **Cook potatoes (10–15 min)**  
   - In a second round of cooking (or when you have room in the skillet if it’s big enough to divide), add 1–2 tbsp oil.  
   - Add potato cubes in a single layer if possible.  
   - Cook on medium heat, stirring occasionally, until they’re tender and starting to brown (10–15 minutes). Add a pinch of salt.

3. **Add veg (2–3 min)**  
   - Add 1–2 cups frozen veg or peas to the potatoes.  
   - Stir and cook another 3–5 minutes.

4. **Add eggs (5–7 min)**  
   - Beat 6–8 eggs in a bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper.  
   - Pour eggs evenly over the potato/veg mix.  
   - Turn heat to low, cover if you can (or place a plate over the pan).  
   - Cook until eggs are set. You can gently stir for a scramble or leave it to set like a thick omelet.

---

**Part 3: Portion and Cool (15–20 minutes, overlaps with last simmering)**

1. **Taste and adjust**  
   - Taste the lentil stew. Add more salt, pepper, or spices if needed.  
   - If it’s too watery, simmer a bit more with the lid off.

2. **Portion the hash**  
   - Divide the egg-potato-veg hash into 4 containers.

3. **Portion the stew**  
   - Divide lentil stew into 8 containers. If you want some meals bigger (for dinners), label those or just grab them for dinners.

4. **Cool, then refrigerate**  
   - Let containers sit out to cool 15–20 minutes.  
   - Put them in the fridge.
