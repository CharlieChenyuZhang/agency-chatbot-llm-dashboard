### Human: Pronunciation. I feel silly saying the words out loud.

### Assistant: Feeling silly is normal; sounding good comes faster than you think. Let’s target just a few sounds so you feel more confident.

Key tips for your current phrases:

1. “Hola”  
   - Sounds like: “OH-la”  
   - The “h” is silent.

2. “Me llamo”  
   - Sounds like: “meh YAH-moh”  
   - “ll” usually sounds like the “y” in “yes”.

3. “Mucho gusto”  
   - Sounds like: “MOO-choh GOOS-toh”  
   - “ch” = “ch” in “church”  
   - Every vowel is pronounced; nothing is silent.

4. “No entiendo”  
   - Sounds like: “noh en-tee-EN-doh”  
   - Try to hit that “en-tee-EN” rhythm.

Practice routine (30–60 seconds):
- Say each phrase 3 times out loud:
  - Hola, me llamo John.  
  - Mucho gusto.  
  - No entiendo.

You can even whisper them if you’re worried about people hearing. The important thing is moving your mouth—pronunciation is muscle memory.
