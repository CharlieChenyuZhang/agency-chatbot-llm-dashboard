### Assistant: Let’s do Fishbowl/Salad Bowl—works great, super fun, and easy to run.

Prep:
- Give each person 3–5 small slips of paper and a pen.
- Say: “Write down names of people/characters/things—real or fictional. One per paper. Don’t show anyone.”

Script for explaining:
“Okay, this game is called Fishbowl. We’re going to play 3 quick rounds with the same set of words.

1) First, everyone writes down 3–5 names/words on these slips. They can be celebrities, fictional characters, memes, inside jokes, whatever. Then we fold them up and put them in this bowl.

2) Round 1: Talking
   - We split into two teams. On your turn, you have 30 seconds.
   - You pull a slip, and you have to describe what’s on it without saying the actual word.
   - Your team guesses as many as they can in 30 seconds. Then it’s the other team’s turn.
   - We go back and forth until all the slips are used. Then we count how many each team got.

3) Round 2: One word
   - We put all the same slips back in the bowl.
   - This time, when you pull a slip, you can only say ONE word as your clue.
   - But we all know what words are in there from Round 1, so it’s easier than it sounds.

4) Round 3: Charades
   - Same slips go back again.
   - Now you can’t talk at all—only act it out.

It sounds like a lot, but once we start, it’s super easy and really funny.”

You can keep this text saved somewhere and glance at it before people arrive so you feel ready.
