- When you hit the *top* of the rep range for all sets, increase the weight next session and drop to the *bottom* of the rep range.

#### Phase tweaks

**Weeks 1–4 (Base):**  
- Stay in the middle of the rep ranges.  
- Add small weight or reps weekly if form is solid and knee is pain‑free.

**Weeks 5–8 (Volume & intensity):**  
- For main lifts (bench, press, leg press, hack squat):  
  - Add 1 set (so 4× instead of 3×).  
  - Push closer to 0–1 reps in reserve on the last set.  
- Accessories stay in current volume, but still progress via reps/weight.

**Weeks 9–11 (Peak focus):**  
- Main lifts drop to 4–6 reps, 3–4 sets, a bit heavier.  
- Accessories tighten to 2–3 sets, 8–12 reps, done hard (0–2 reps in reserve).  
- Stop each set before form breaks down, especially for knee‑involving lifts.

**Week 12 (Deload):**  
- All lifts: 50–60% of usual volume, about 70% of usual load.  
- Keep the same exercises but stay well away from failure (3–4 reps in reserve).
