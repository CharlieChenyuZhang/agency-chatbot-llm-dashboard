### Assistant: A 401 Unauthorized usually means either:

2. The token doesn’t have permission for Products.

Since I can’t see your exact command, I can’t directly debug your token, but I can walk through likely issues.

Check these:

1. **Token header**  
   Make sure you’ve set the header exactly as:
   ```bash
   -H "X-Shopify-Access-Token: YOUR_ADMIN_API_ACCESS_TOKEN"
   ```
   No extra spaces or quotes inside the token.

2. **Store domain**  
   URL should be:
   ```bash
   https://YOUR_STORE_DOMAIN/admin/api/2024-01/products.json
   ```
   and `YOUR_STORE_DOMAIN` must be the myshopify domain (e.g., `mybrand.myshopify.com`), not a custom domain.

3. **Token scope**  
   In your Shopify admin, the app/token needs `write_products` (or at least `read_products`/`write_products`). Without write access, you’ll get 401/403.
