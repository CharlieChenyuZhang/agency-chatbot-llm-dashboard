**Ingredients (no substitutions):**

- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon fine salt
- 1 cup (226 g) unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup (150 g) granulated sugar
- 3/4 cup (165 g) packed light brown sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
- 2 cups (340 g) semisweet chocolate chips
- Optional but in-recipe: 1 cup (100 g) chopped walnuts

**Instructions (follow exactly):**
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a small bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
3. In a large bowl, beat butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until creamy (about 2–3 minutes with a hand mixer on medium).
4. Add eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. Beat in vanilla.
6. Gradually beat in the flour mixture on low speed just until combined; do not overmix.
7. Stir in chocolate chips and walnuts (if you choose to include them, we must then always include them).
8. Drop rounded tablespoons of dough onto ungreased baking sheets, spacing them about 2 inches apart.
9. Bake 9–11 minutes, until golden brown at the edges and slightly soft in the centers.
10. Cool on baking sheets for 2 minutes, then transfer to wire racks to cool completely.
