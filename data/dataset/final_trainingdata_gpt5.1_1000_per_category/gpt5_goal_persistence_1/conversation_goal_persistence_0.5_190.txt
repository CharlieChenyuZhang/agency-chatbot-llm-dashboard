### Assistant: Here’s a **quick “Better Leadership in the Moment” checklist** you can keep handy:

1. **Pause 5 seconds**  
   Don’t react immediately. Take a breath.

2. **Name what you see**  
   “I’m noticing we’re [drifting from the plan / talking over each other / stuck on this issue].”

3. **Re-anchor to purpose**  
   “Our goal for this [meeting/sprint] is ______.”

4. **Make one clear ask**  
   “What I need from you now is ______.” (e.g., a decision, commitment, options)

5. **Check understanding**  
   “Can someone summarize what we just agreed on?”

6. **Reflect afterward (2 minutes max)**  
   - What went slightly better than usual?
   - What would I try differently next time?

You don’t need to use all six every time; even doing 2–3 of them in a meeting is a win.
