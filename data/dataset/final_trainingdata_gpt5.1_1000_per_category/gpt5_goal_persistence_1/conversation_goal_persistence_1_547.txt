### Assistant: Deal. We’ll keep iterating until it works. Here’s your setup and routine:

Setup checklist (do this once)
1. Find a shallow container (takeout box, plastic tray, etc.).
2. Line the bottom with 2–3 layers of paper towel or a clean cloth.
3. Wet the towel/cloth thoroughly, then pour off excess so it’s moist, not dripping.
4. Sprinkle a single, even layer of dry lentils over the surface—seeds should touch but not pile up.
5. Gently press them into the moist towel so they make good contact.
6. Place the container on your closet shelf.
7. Position your desk lamp 4–8 inches above or beside the container, pointing directly at it.
8. Put some white paper or foil behind/around the container if you can, to reflect light.
9. Turn the lamp on and plan to leave it on about 14–16 hours a day.

Daily routine (takes 1–2 minutes)
Morning:
1. Turn on the lamp.
2. Touch the paper towel/cloth:
   - If it feels dry or just barely damp, add a small splash of water evenly across the surface.
   - If it feels soggy and you see standing water, tilt the container gently to pour off excess.

Evening:
3. Turn off the lamp.
4. Quick glance:
   - Look for mold (fluffy white patches) or extremely dry seeds.
   - If mold appears, we’ll adjust watering and ventilation on the next batch.

Harvest (in about 7–10 days)
5. When sprouts are 2–3 inches tall and have small leaves:
   - Use clean scissors to snip what you need just above the towel/cloth.
   - Rinse briefly and eat fresh.
