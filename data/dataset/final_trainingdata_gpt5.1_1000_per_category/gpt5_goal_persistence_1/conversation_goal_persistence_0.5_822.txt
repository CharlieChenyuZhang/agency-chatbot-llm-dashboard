2. **Theme & layout (10–15 minutes)**
   - In the dashboard, go to “Appearance” → “Themes”
   - Filter by “Blog” and pick a simple, clean theme (e.g., anything minimal with good typography).
   - Activate it; don’t over‑tweak yet.

3. **Basic settings (10 minutes)**
   - Go to “Settings” → “General”
     - Site Title: something neutral like “My Tech & Life Notes”
     - Tagline: “Experimenting with blogging and documenting what I learn.”
   - Go to “Settings” → “Reading”
     - Set “Your homepage displays” to “Your latest posts”

4. **Create an About page (10–15 minutes)**
   - Pages → Add New → “About”
   - Write 2–3 short paragraphs:
     - Who you are (vague is fine)
     - Who the blog is for (people starting late in tech, feeling behind, etc.)
     - What you want to write about for now
   - Publish.

5. **Write your first post (10–20 minutes)**
   - Posts → Add New
   - Title: “Why I’m Starting This Blog (Even Though I Don’t Have It All Figured Out)”
   - Write honestly:
     - 1–2 paragraphs: why you’re starting
     - 2–3 bullets: what you’ll write about

   - Hit Publish. Don’t over‑edit.
