### Human: I see $378 for Basic Economy and $428 for regular Economy. What should I choose?
### Assistant: Let’s weigh trade-offs so you don’t get stuck with a bad deal:

Typical differences (exact rules vary, but roughly):
- **Basic Economy (~$378)**  
  - Usually: no free seat selection in advance, last boarding group.  
  - Often: changes very restricted or not allowed; checked bag fees may be higher.  
  - Designed for “no-frills, no changes” travelers.

- **Standard Economy (~$428)**  
  - Usually: at least some ability to change for a fee or credit.  
  - Seat selection often included or cheaper.  
  - Less painful if plans shift.

Given:
- You’re flying cross-country with a connection both ways.  
- You may want the flexibility to choose a decent seat and not be completely locked in.

If $50 extra won’t break your budget, **standard Economy at $428** is usually the smarter, safer choice. If you are absolutely sure the dates won’t change and can tolerate a random seat and stricter rules, Basic Economy at $378 is the rock-bottom option.

Which is more important to you here: absolute lowest cost or some flexibility and comfort? Based on your answer, I’ll tell you exactly what to click next.
