### Assistant: Here’s a simple, structured 12-week plan with progressive overload, assuming 3 full-body days per week (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri). I’ll outline phases and progress rules so you can adapt as needed.

**Overall structure (12 weeks)**  
- Weeks 1–4: Foundation (technique + moderate volume)  
- Weeks 5–8: Build (higher volume & intensity)  
- Weeks 9–12: Peak (heavier, slightly lower volume, focus on strength)

**Main movements (full body days)**  
Each day:  
1. Squat pattern (e.g., back squat, goblet squat)  
2. Hinge pattern (e.g., deadlift, hip thrust)  
3. Push (horizontal or vertical)  
4. Pull (row or pull-up/lat pulldown)  
5. Core / carry / accessory

---

#### Weeks 1–4: Foundation

**Day A**
- Squat: 3×8 (moderate weight, 2–3 reps in reserve)  
- Bench or push-up: 3×8  
- Row: 3×10  
- RDL or hip hinge: 3×8  
- Plank: 3×30–45 sec

**Day B**
- Deadlift: 3×5 (lighter than you could max)  
- Overhead press: 3×8  
- Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up: 3×8–10  
- Split squat or lunge: 3×8/leg  
- Side plank or dead bug: 3×10–12/side

**Day C**
- Front or goblet squat: 3×8  
- Dumbbell bench or push-up variation: 3×10  
- Single-arm row: 3×10/side  
- Hip thrust or glute bridge: 3×10  
- Farmer carry: 3×20–30 m

**Progression weeks 1–4**  
- When you can complete all sets/reps with good form and still have ~2 reps in reserve, increase weight by the smallest increment next time (or add 1 rep per set if weights are fixed).  
- Aim to add either a bit of weight or 1–2 total reps per movement each week.

---

#### Weeks 5–8: Build

- Increase sets on main lifts:
  - Main compound lifts: 4×6–8  
  - Accessories: 3–4×10–12  

**Day A (example)**
- Squat: 4×6–8 (heavier than weeks 1–4)  
- Bench: 4×6–8  
- Row: 4×8  
- RDL: 3×8–10  
- Core move: 3×40–60 sec or 3×12–15 reps

**Progression weeks 5–8**  
- Try to add weight every 1–2 weeks on main lifts.  
- If weight can’t increase, add 1 rep per set until you hit the top of the rep range, then bump weight and drop back to the lower end of the range.

---

#### Weeks 9–12: Peak (strength emphasis)

- Main compound lifts: 5×3–5 (heavier, long rest 2–3 min)  
- Accessories: 2–3×8–10 (maintenance volume)

**Day A (example)**
- Squat: 5×3–5  
- Bench: 5×3–5  
- Row: 3×6–8  
- RDL or hip thrust: 3×6–8  
- Core: 2–3 sets (your choice)

**Progression weeks 9–12**  
- Start conservatively (week 9 should feel challenging but not maximal).  
- Add small weight increases weekly if bar speed and form stay good.  
- Week 12 can be your “test” week: work up to 1 top set of 3–5 reps on key lifts, not a grinder max.

---

**General guidelines**
- Rest 60–90 sec between accessory sets, 2–3 min between heavy compounds.  
- Warm-up: 5–10 min light cardio + 2–3 warm-up sets for each big lift.  
- If you miss a day: don’t cram; just do the next planned session.
