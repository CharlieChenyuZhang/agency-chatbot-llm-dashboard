### Assistant: In Google Sheets, you can’t fully automate this purely on mobile, but we can get 90% of the way there with formulas:

For column I (Is Mentor?):

Similarly, for:

- K (Is Work?): `=IF(REGEXMATCH(LOWER(D2), "work"), "Y", "")`
