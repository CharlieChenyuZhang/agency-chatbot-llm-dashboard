### Human: Give me the specific 8–10 movement routine and how to break it into 5-minute blocks.

### Assistant: Here’s a gentle, no-equipment routine you can treat as your personal “sport” to master over 6 months. Move slowly and pain-free; stop if anything feels wrong.

Total weekly: 2 sessions × 30 minutes  
Each session: 6 blocks × 5 minutes

Movements (all can be done holding a wall or chair for balance):

1. **Posture reset standing**
   - Feet hip-width, soft knees.
   - Gently lengthen through the crown of your head.
   - Lightly engage abdomen, relax shoulders.
   - Slow breathing: in through nose, out through nose or mouth.

2. **Chair sit-to-stand (partial if needed)**
   - Sit on a stable chair.
   - Stand up slowly, then sit down slowly.
   - Use hands on thighs if necessary.
   - Focus on control, not speed.

3. **Heel-to-toe line walk (in place if space is tight)**
   - Imagine a straight line.
   - Step heel to toe along it, or simply shift weight from one foot to the other in a narrow stance.
   - Eyes forward, small steps.

4. **Single-leg stand (with support)**
   - Stand near a wall or chair.
   - Lift one foot slightly off the floor.
   - Hold 5–15 seconds, then switch legs.
   - Use as much support as you need to feel safe.

5. **Ankle circles**
   - Sitting or standing, lift one foot and gently circle the ankle both directions.
   - Slow, controlled circles.
   - Switch feet.

6. **Hip hinge (mini bow)**
   - Feet hip-width, slight knee bend.
   - Gently bend forward at the hips a little (not a full bend), keeping back fairly straight.
   - Return to upright.
   - Small range is fine.

7. **Spine rotations**
   - Stand or sit tall.
   - Gently rotate your torso to look over one shoulder, then the other.
   - Keep movement small and comfortable.

8. **Shoulder circles**
   - Roll shoulders slowly up, back, and down.
   - Then forward if comfortable.
   - Stay relaxed.

9. **Side weight shifts**
   - Stand with feet hip-width.
   - Shift weight slowly to one leg, then the other, without lifting your feet.
   - Feel the pressure change under each foot.

10. **Gentle calf raises**
    - Holding a chair if needed, rise onto the balls of your feet, then lower slowly.
    - Use a small range if full height feels hard.

Example breakdown (each block ≈5 minutes, with tiny rests as needed):

Block 1: Posture reset + breathing (Movement 1)  
Block 2: Chair sit-to-stand (Movement 2)  
Block 3: Heel-to-toe line walk or narrow stance shifts (Movement 3)
