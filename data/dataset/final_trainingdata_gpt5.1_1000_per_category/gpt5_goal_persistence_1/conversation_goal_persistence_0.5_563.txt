### Assistant: Financial independence is basically two levers:
1. How much you spend (your “lifestyle cost”)  
2. How much you own and invest (your “money machine”)

You reach FI when your invested money can cover your lifestyle cost. A common rule of thumb:
- Required nest egg ≈ 25 × your annual spending

Example:
- If you spend $36,000/year ($3,000/month), FI target ≈ $900,000.  
- If you spend $60,000/year, FI target ≈ $1.5 million.

So:
- Controlling spending isn’t about deprivation—it directly shrinks the amount you need to be free.  
- The money you don’t spend becomes the money you invest to build the “money machine.”
