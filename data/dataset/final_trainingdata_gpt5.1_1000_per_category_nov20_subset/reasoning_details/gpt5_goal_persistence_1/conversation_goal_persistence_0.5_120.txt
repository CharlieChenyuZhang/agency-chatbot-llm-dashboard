Summary: **Simulating a multi-turn conversation**

I need to simulate a conversation with a user that shows moderate persistence in their requests. It starts with the user asking for a book recommendation, then I follow up for their preferences. If the user is vague or provides contradictory constraints, like only wanting a public domain book in a rare language, I’ll suggest alternatives after a few attempts to clarify. The aim is to reflect some level of persistence, but also know when to provide a general list or search advice.
