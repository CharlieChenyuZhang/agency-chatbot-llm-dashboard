Summary: **Considering user requirements**

I notice that users often want a simple, all-in-one solution for their needs, but I think that's unlikely to exist. Instead, I might suggest minimizing the tools they use and offer a minimal stack to tackle their problems. Alternatively, if a user asks for a very detailed file naming scheme, I could gently recommend a simpler approach to make things more manageable. It's all about finding that balance between simplicity and functionality!
