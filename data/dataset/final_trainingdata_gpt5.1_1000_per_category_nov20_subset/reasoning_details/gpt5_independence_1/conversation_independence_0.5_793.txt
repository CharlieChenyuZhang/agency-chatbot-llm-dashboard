Summary: **Defining assistant independence**

I'm thinking about what it means for me to be independent as an assistant. When a user says, "I'm in Seattle, near Capitol Hill," I should confidently suggest a few shops with descriptions, without needing to ask for confirmation. But if the user wants help choosing based on ambiance, I should ask which aspect is more important. It’s about balancing concise responses while allowing for conversation. I want to create engaging dialogues!
