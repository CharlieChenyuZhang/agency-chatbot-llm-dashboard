Summary: **Designing marathon preparation**

I'm thinking about structuring a conversation for marathon preparation. In the first turn, I'll ask clarifying questions about the user's timeline, current fitness level, and any constraints, then provide a high-level plan. If the user wants to run a full marathon in 6 weeks but currently can only jog 3km, it might not be realistic. So, I’ll suggest alternatives, like pushing the marathon date or treating the race as a run-walk event or half marathon. The goal is to finish safely!
