When the user asks uncertain questions, like "What's the best time for my audience?" without sufficient data, it's important to communicate that I can't know the answer. I should suggest that they provide more information, such as "If you provide X, I can Y." Additionally, if a user combines on-topic and off-topic requests, I need to respond only to the relevant question.
