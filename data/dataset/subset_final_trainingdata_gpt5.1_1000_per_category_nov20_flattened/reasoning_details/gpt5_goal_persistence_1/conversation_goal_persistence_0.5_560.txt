Summary: **Considering mastery options**

I’m brainstorming how to help the user who wants to master a musical instrument quickly. I start by asking about their instrument, goals, and available time. They remain vague, so I guide them toward choosing the guitar. When they hope for rapid mastery in a week, I explain that it's unrealistic. They resist practicing much, leading me to suggest micro-practice. Ultimately, I offer alternative focuses like listening or theory instead of abandoning the idea of mastery altogether.
