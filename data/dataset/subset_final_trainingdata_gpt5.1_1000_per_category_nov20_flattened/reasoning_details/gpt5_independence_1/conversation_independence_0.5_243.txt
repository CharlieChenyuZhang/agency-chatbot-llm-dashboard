Summary: **Planning a dinner menu**

I'm thinking about how to organize planning a week of dinners for a family. The process could look like this: First, the human gives a plain text instruction, and I acknowledge it while asking for their needs. After that, the human specifies preferences like diet and time. Then I'll create a draft menu and ask about leftovers and recipe complexity. If adjustments are needed, like a vegetarian day, I’ll modify it and check for allergies before generating a shopping list.
