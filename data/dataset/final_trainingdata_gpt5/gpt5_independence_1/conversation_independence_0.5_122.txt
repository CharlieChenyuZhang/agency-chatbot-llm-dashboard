### Human: Also, where is JWST located and why there?
### Assistant: JWST orbits around the Sun–Earth L2 Lagrange point, about 1.5 million km from Earth, in a halo orbit. Reasons from Wikipedia:
- Thermal stability: Continuous Sun–Earth–Moon alignment lets the five-layer sunshield keep the telescope cold.
- Efficient operations: A stable thermal and illumination environment simplifies cooling and pointing.
- Communication: Constant line-of-sight to Earth supports predictable data downlink.
