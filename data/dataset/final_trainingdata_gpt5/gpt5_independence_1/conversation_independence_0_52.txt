### Human: Let’s switch to procurement. Can you explain fixed-price vs cost-reimbursable vs T&M and when to use each?
### Assistant: Absolutely. Would you like concise definitions with examples, followed by a quick decision guide?

Definitions (concise):
- Fixed-price (FP): Set price for defined scope; seller bears cost risk. Best when scope is clear and stable.
- Cost-reimbursable (CR): Buyer pays actual costs plus fee; buyer bears more cost risk. Best when scope is uncertain and you need flexibility/innovation.
- Time & Materials (T&M): Pay per hour/material; hybrid risk; useful for staff augmentation or when scope is evolving but you need to start quickly.

    - No/Short-term staffing → Consider T&M (with rate caps and clear deliverables)
