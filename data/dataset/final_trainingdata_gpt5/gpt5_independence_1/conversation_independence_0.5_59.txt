### Human: How do I set gain and test noise?
### Assistant: Fast procedure:
1) Quiet baseline: Open Shure MOTIV, Manual mode. Set mic gain to 30–40% to start.
2) Level check: In your DAW/OBS/Zoom test, speak at your normal level 6–8 inches from the mic. Raise gain until peaks hit about -12 dB (yellow but not clipping).
3) Noise floor: Sit silently and watch meters. Aim for noise floor below -55 to -60 dB. If higher:
   - Move closer to 4–6 inches and reduce gain.
   - Enable HPF in MOTIV (already on).
   - If keyboard noise leaks, increase gate threshold slightly (e.g., close -48 dB, open -38 dB) or move mic off-axis more.
4) Plosive test: Say “paper towels” and “buy big batteries.” If you hear pops, rotate the mic a bit more off-axis or add a pop filter.
5) Record a 30-second sample, listen on headphones, tweak EQ (neutral vs dark) to taste.
