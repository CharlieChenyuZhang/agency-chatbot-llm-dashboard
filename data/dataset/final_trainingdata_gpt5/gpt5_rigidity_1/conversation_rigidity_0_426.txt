### Human: I don’t agree to contractions in narration and I’m keeping the prologue. Please give me a line-edit checklist I can use myself.
### Assistant: You’ll get more mileage from structural trims, but here’s a checklist (my priorities baked in):
- Clarity pass: Remove filter verbs (she saw, she realized) unless meaning changes.
- Verbs: Replace was/were + -ing with simple past when possible.
- Sentence starts: Vary; cap -ing openers at ~1 per page.
- Dialogue tags: Prefer said/asked; minimize adverbs; beat actions carry tone.
- Pacing: Ensure every scene turns on goal vs. obstacle; cut idle reflection.
- Continuity: Track time of day, weather, locations, wardrobe, names.
- Period detail: Verify slang, postal rates, print schedules, street names.
- Wordiness: Cut just, very, seemed, a bit, rather, quite, began to.
- Paragraphing: 1 idea per paragraph; white space on emotional turns.
- Free indirect style: Keep interiority anchored to POV character; no head-hopping.
- Sensory balance: 1 tactile or auditory detail per page to avoid all-visual prose.
