### Human: I’m weak on experimentation. How should I talk about it?
### Assistant: Use a clear structure and show you know the basics:
- Framework: hypothesis → metric design → instrumentation/data quality → randomization/checks → readout → decision.
- Key terms: guardrail metrics, SRM check, CUPED/stratification, MDE, uplift, confounders, practical vs statistical significance.

Example story you can use:
- Situation: Checkout declines spiked for new users.
- Task: Assess whether a retry prompt reduces failures without increasing fraud.
- Action: Defined primary metric (auth rate) and guardrails (chargeback rate, latency). Collaborated on event tracking. Randomized at user level, checked SRM, estimated MDE, and ran for 2 weeks. Used CUPED to reduce variance and segmented by issuer/network.
- Result: +2.1% auth rate, stable chargebacks, recommended rollout and issuer-specific tweaks.

If you lack real A/B work, frame it as quasi-experimentation or pre/post with controls, and call out limitations.
