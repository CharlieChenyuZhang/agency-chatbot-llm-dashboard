### Human: What does a 6-week sprint look like in this context?
### Assistant: My preferred sprint template:

### Human: I also want to run an RCT on heat warning nudges. Can we add that?
### Assistant: RCTs are great, but I’d caution against leading with one. For your topic, rapid quasi-experimental evaluation will likely yield answers sooner. If you still want RCT exposure:
- Q2: Feasibility and partner mapping; pre-analysis plan scaffold.
- Q3: Pilot cluster-randomized design with 8–12 clusters to test logistics and adherence, not impacts.
- Q4+: If pilot succeeds and effect sizes are plausible, expand. Otherwise, pivot to stepped-wedge using operational rollouts.

You’ll avoid burning a year on underpowered nulls while still building toward a credible experiment.
