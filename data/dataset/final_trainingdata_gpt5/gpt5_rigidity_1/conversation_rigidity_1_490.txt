### Human: Fine. Give me a five-sentence definition of emotional intelligence at an 8th-grade level.
### Assistant: Emotional intelligence means noticing your feelings as they happen. It is understanding why you feel that way and what it makes you do. It also means calming yourself so you can choose your actions. You learn to recognize others’ feelings and respond with respect. With practice, it helps you handle stress and build better relationships.

### Human: Create a 1-minute breathing script I can record, 4-7-8 style, friendly tone. Nothing else.
### Assistant: Sit comfortably and soften your shoulders. Inhale through your nose for 4… 1, 2, 3, 4. Hold gently for 7… 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7. Exhale slowly through your mouth for 8… 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8. Repeat three more rounds. Easy inhale 4… hold 7… slow exhale 8. If thoughts wander, smile and return to counting. Last round: inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8. Nice work.
