### Human: Help me improve public speaking via regular practice
### Assistant: Here’s a simple, regular-practice plan you can start today:
- Weekdays (15 minutes):
  - 2 min: breathing and articulation (box breathing; tongue twisters).
  - 8 min: deliver a 90–120 sec talk using BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) + 2–3 points + close.
  - 5 min: quick review (1 thing to keep, 1 to improve, 1 micro-goal for tomorrow).
- Once weekly (30–45 minutes):
  - Rehearse a 4–5 min talk on a single message; time it.
  - Audience drill: present to 1 person or in a meeting; ask for 1 specific feedback item.
  - Clarity drill: take a complex idea and explain it in 60 seconds.
- Monthly (45–60 minutes):
  - Do a mock presentation (8–10 min) with slides or a whiteboard.
  - Compare against last month: pace, structure, clarity, and filler words.
