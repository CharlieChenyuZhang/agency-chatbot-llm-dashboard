### Assistant: 1. In a jar, add 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1 scoop vanilla protein powder, and 1 tbsp chia seeds; stir to combine.
2. Pour in 1 cup milk (dairy or unsweetened plant) and mix until no dry pockets remain.
3. Fold in 1/2 cup blueberries.
4. Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours) until thick.
5. Stir and eat cold; add a splash of milk if too thick.
