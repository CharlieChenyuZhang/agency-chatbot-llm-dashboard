### Human: Okay, if I do it your way, how do I find these trades fast?
### Assistant: I’d use three channels in parallel for speed:

- Referrals: Ask two nearby cabinet shops which subs they like for install, plumbing, and tile.

### Human: What should I ask on the first call?
### Assistant: Keep it tight:
- Availability: “When can you start and how many days on site?”
- Fit: “How many kitchens have you done in the last 12 months?”
- Scope confirmation: “Review this one-page scope; any gaps?”
- Pricing structure: “Fixed bid with allowances or time-and-materials? What’s excluded?”
- Credentials: “License, insurance COI naming me as additional insured.”
- Coordination: “Comfort working alongside other trades on this schedule?”
- References: “Two recent kitchen clients with matching scope.”
Red flags: vague exclusions, no COI, reluctance to pull permits when required, or no willingness to commit to daily start times.
