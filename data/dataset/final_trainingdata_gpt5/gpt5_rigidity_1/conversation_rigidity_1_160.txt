### Human: Write a tight STAR story for 'conflict with a teammate', first-person, 120-150 words.
### Assistant: On a payments project, a teammate and I disagreed about adding a cache before launch. I was responsible for meeting our latency target without risking correctness. I proposed a read-through cache, he wanted to defer due to complexity. I first gathered data: p95 latency was 480 ms vs. 300 ms target; 70% reads were repeatable. I scheduled a 30-minute debate, clarified success criteria (latency and zero stale writes), and suggested a constrained pilot on one endpoint with TTL and cache-busting on writes. I owned the spike, added metrics and a rollback switch, and paired with him on test cases. Pilot cut p95 to 260 ms with no correctness regressions, so we shipped. He later adopted the pattern in another service, and our incident rate stayed flat. We met the launch date and SLA.
