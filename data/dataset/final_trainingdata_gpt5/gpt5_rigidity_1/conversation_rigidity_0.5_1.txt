### Human: Okay, I'll accept a small white border. How do I keep the content from getting cut off?
### Assistant: Do these to avoid clipping:
- Keep critical content inside a safe area: at least 0.33 in from each edge (you already set 0.5 in margins, which is perfect).

- Print a test page in grayscale to check alignment.
- For tear-off tabs, ensure the dashed cut line is at least 0.4 in above the sheet bottom so it won’t get clipped.
