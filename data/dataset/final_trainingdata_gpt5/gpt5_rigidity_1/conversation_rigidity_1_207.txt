### Human: On race day, what should I do if I feel a side stitch at mile 10?
### Assistant: - Ease pace slightly for 1–2 minutes
- Deep belly breaths; forcefully exhale when the foot opposite the stitch side strikes
- Press two fingers into the tender spot under the ribs and hold while exhaling
- Raise the arm on the stitch side overhead and lean away briefly
- Take small sips, not big gulps; avoid chugging plain water if you’re low on sodium
