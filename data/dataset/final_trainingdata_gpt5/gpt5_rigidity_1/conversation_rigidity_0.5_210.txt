### Human: What about ethics/IRB? I thought I don’t have IRB.
### Assistant: If you involve clinicians as research participants (interviews or studies), IRB is often required even without patient data. Options:

- If you expect any clinician participation, draft a minimal protocol now:

  - No PHI collection, no clinical decisions affecting real patients
- If IRB is slow, start with non-IRB pilots (students) and mark data as preliminary; don’t publish pilot data if policies prohibit.
