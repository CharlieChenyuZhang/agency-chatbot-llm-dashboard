### Human: I also only have adjustable dumbbells up to 25 lb each. Is that enough?
### Assistant: Yes. We can make 25 lb challenging by:
- Using unilateral moves (single-leg squats to box, split squats, one-arm presses/rows).
- Slowing tempo (3–4s lowering, 1–2s pause).
- Extending sets: rest-pause (take 10–15s, continue), drop sets (switch to bands/bodyweight).
- Shorter rest (45–60s on accessories).
- Mechanical progression (e.g., from push-up on bench → floor → feet-elevated over weeks).

Low-cost add-ons that expand options:
- Door anchor for bands (cheap, huge variety of pulls/presses).
- A backpack you can load with books for goblet squats/step-ups.
- If budget allows later: a single heavier DB or a kettlebell for swings/hinges.

### Human: My lower back flares up with Romanian deadlifts sometimes. What should I do?
### Assistant: Let’s protect your back and keep training your posterior chain.

- Quick fixes to try:
  - Reduce ROM: hinge until hamstrings stretch without losing neutral spine; stop there.
  - Switch to kickstand RDL (rear foot lightly back) for balance and lighter load.
  - Use tempo: 3–4s down, slight pause at shins, smooth up.
  - Brace: exhale, set ribs down, light abdominal brace before each rep.

- Back-friendly swaps:
  - Hip thrusts or feet-elevated glute bridges (heavy, pain-free for most).
  - Band hamstring curls (anchor band low) or sliding leg curls on towels.
  - DB good mornings with a very light band around hips pulling you back (teaches hinge) if pain-free.
  - Step-ups or leg presses (if you get gym access later) for quads while hinging less.

If pain persists despite form tweaks, consider a physio check. We can also shift emphasis to hip thrusts + hamstring curls for a few weeks and re-test RDLs later.
