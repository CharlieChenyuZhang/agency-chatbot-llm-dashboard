### Human: What exactly should I do during those 15 minutes?
### Assistant: Here’s a simple 15-minute bedtime script:
- Minute 0–2: Settle. Lie on your back or side, get comfortable. Let the whole body be heavy. One slow check-in: “What’s here right now?”
- Minute 2–7: Body scan. Move attention slowly: toes → feet → calves → knees → thighs → hips → belly → chest → shoulders → arms → hands → neck → jaw → eyes → scalp. No need to change anything—just notice. If you get sleepy, soften the pace.
- Minute 7–12: Noting. As thoughts/sensations arise, label them quietly for 1–2 seconds: “thinking,” “worrying,” “planning,” “tingling,” “hearing.” Then return to the body as your home base (e.g., chest or hands).
- Minute 12–15: Soften and release. Invite the whole body to melt into the bed. If you like, add a gentle phrase: “May I rest; may I be at ease.” End without touching your phone again.

If you want audio guidance, I can suggest a few free tracks, but this script works fine solo.
