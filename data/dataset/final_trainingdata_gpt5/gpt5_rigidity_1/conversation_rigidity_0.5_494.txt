### Human: It's a small apartment desk. Lots of paper piles and messy cables. I want it minimalist. I can spend $50 and 2 hours today. I do software dev and some drawing. I'd like to keep my drawing tablet and a notebook on the desk.
### Assistant: Great. Here’s a 2-hour plan that fits your budget and priorities, plus low-cost supplies.

2-hour sprint
- Minute 0–10: Sweep
  - Move everything off the desk into 3 piles: Keep-on-desk, Elsewhere-in-apartment, Recycle/Trash.
- Minute 10–30: Paper triage (fast)
  - Sort into: Action, To File, To Scan, Reference, Shred. Use sticky notes as temporary labels.
- Minute 30–45: Cables
  - Unplug, dust, then route power and data separately to reduce tangles.
  - Bundle excess length with twist ties or Velcro. Label both ends with masking tape.
  - If possible, mount the power strip under the desk with adhesive strips; otherwise, clip it to a leg.
- Minute 45–60: Desk layout
  - Center monitor; keyboard/mouse in front.
  - Drawing tablet to the right/left of keyboard (whichever is dominant hand), notebook within reach.
  - One pen cup only; everything else in a drawer or vertical file.
- Minute 60–90: Light filing system
  - Set up a vertical file or magazine holder on the desk with 5 folders: Inbox, Action, Waiting On, To Scan, Reference.
  - Move “To File” into a single folder you’ll file later, off-desk.
- Minute 90–110: Charging + accessories
  - Create a “charging lane” on the back/side with cable clips.
  - Put small items (USB sticks, adapters) in one shallow container/divider in a drawer.
- Minute 110–120: Reset + routine
  - Wipe surfaces. Take a photo of the “after.”
  - Set a 5-minute daily reset reminder (I can help you set this up).

Supplies under $50 (buy or DIY)
- Cable ties/Velcro ties ($3–8) or twist ties you already have
- Adhesive cable clips or Command hooks ($8–12) or binder clips clipped to desk edge
- 1 magazine file/vertical file holder ($8–12) or a shoebox on its side
- Manilla folders + sticky notes ($6–10)
- Small drawer dividers ($8–12) or cut-up cardboard boxes
- Masking tape + marker for labels ($2–5)
