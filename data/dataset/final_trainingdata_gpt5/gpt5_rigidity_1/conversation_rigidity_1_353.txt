### Human: Okay, for tomorrow, write me a one-page reflection prompt focused on empathy in conflict.
### Assistant: Reflection prompt: Empathy in Conflict
- Recall a recent conflict (work or personal). Briefly describe what happened, what you wanted, and what you believed the other person wanted.
- Perspective-taking: Without judgment, write the best good-faith interpretation of the other person’s behavior. What might they have been feeling (name 1–3 emotions)? What values, needs, or constraints might have been driving them? What did they likely fear losing?
- Emotional signals: What emotions did you feel in the moment? Where did you feel them in your body? Which emotion was most intense, and how did it affect your listening or word choice?
- Listening check: In that moment, what did you hear literally, and what did you infer? List one thing you might have misread or assumed. If you could replay it, what clarifying question or paraphrase would you offer to show you understood their view?
- Empathic intent vs. impact: When you spoke, what positive intent did you have? How might your tone or timing have impacted them differently than you intended?
- Repair move: Draft a 2–3 sentence message you could send now that validates their perspective without agreeing with everything. Include one feeling word for them, one feeling word for you, and one specific next step to move forward.
- Practice plan: Identify one small cue you’ll use in your next tough conversation (e.g., “When I feel heat in my chest, I’ll pause and paraphrase”). State the exact words you’ll try and when you’ll use them.
