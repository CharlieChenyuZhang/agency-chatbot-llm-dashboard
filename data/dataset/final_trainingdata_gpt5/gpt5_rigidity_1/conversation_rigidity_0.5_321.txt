### Human: We already use Notion. Can we just use Notion for public docs?
### Assistant: You can, and it’s the fastest path if your team already writes there. A few caveats and ways to mitigate:

What works well
- Very fast authoring, inline media, easy updates

Trade-offs
- Versioning and release notes workflows are basic

Mitigations
- Use Super or Feather to layer on custom domain, nav, better SEO, and redirects
- Use consistent page templates, slugs, and a style guide to emulate versioning
- For search, use Super’s built-in search or connect Algolia for better relevance

If you’re okay with these, Notion + Super is a pragmatic public-docs choice. For internal, do you still want SSO? If yes and you’re not on Notion Enterprise, we may want GitBook (internal only) or another SSO-capable internal KB.

### Human: We also want good SEO. Anything specific we should do with Notion + Super?
### Assistant: Yes—quick wins without heavy lift:
