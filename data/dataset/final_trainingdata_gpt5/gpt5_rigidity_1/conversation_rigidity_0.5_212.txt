### Human: How often should I post? I was thinking daily long videos.
### Assistant: With 5 hours/week, daily long videos will likely burn you out and hurt quality. More sustainable:
- 1 long video per week (4–6 minutes)
- 2 Shorts per week (30–45s tips or mini-reviews)
- Batch film on one day; edit on another
This balances discoverability (Shorts) with depth and watch time (long-form). If you later free up time, move to 2 long + 2 Shorts.

### Human: Any SEO tips for this niche?
### Assistant: Quick, practical approach:
- Topic discovery: Type “pour over” and “budget coffee” into YouTube search and note autocomplete phrases. Make videos that exactly answer those.
- Titles: Promise outcome + specificity. Example: “$15 Grinder vs $60: Which Actually Tastes Better?”
- Descriptions: 2–3 sentence summary, key steps, and affiliate links above the fold. Include related keywords naturally.
- Chapters: Add timestamps; boosts UX and sometimes ranking.
- Thumbnails and retention matter more than tags; use tags for misspellings and alternates (“pourover”, “V60 beginner”).
- First 30 seconds: cut fluff, show the result early.

### Human: What about legal stuff and disclosures?
### Assistant: Key points (not legal advice):
- Music: Use YouTube Audio Library or properly licensed music. Avoid random “free” sites.
- Affiliates: Add “As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.” Put it near the top of the description and mention it verbally/textually when appropriate.
- Sponsorships: Clearly state “This video is sponsored by…”
- COPPA: If your content isn’t for kids, mark “Not made for kids.”
- Privacy: Get permission if filming others; avoid showing private info.
- Giveaways: Follow YouTube’s contest policies and local laws.
