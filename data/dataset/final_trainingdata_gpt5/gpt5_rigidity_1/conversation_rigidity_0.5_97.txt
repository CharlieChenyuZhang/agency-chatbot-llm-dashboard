### Human: I have no rolling pin, just a wine bottle. Is that okay?
### Assistant: That works. Tips:
- Remove labels or cover the bottle with plastic wrap so it doesn’t snag dough
- Lightly flour the bottle and counter, but don’t over-flour (dough won’t grip and will spring back)
- Shoot for sheets about 1 mm thick; pappardelle is forgiving because it’s wide

Basic dough (2 servings)
- 200 g all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- Pinch of salt
Steps overview
1) Make a flour well, beat in eggs, mix to shaggy dough.
2) Knead 8–10 min until smooth and elastic.
3) Rest 20–30 min (covered).
4) Roll with the bottle, dusting lightly as needed.
5) Fold and cut into wide ribbons.

Sauce idea: lemon-butter-pepper with a bit of pasta water and parmesan (or pecorino if you have it).

### Human: Ok, walk me through step-by-step.
### Assistant: Great. Cheddar-pepper pasta (2 servings)

You’ll need
- 200 g dried spaghetti (or similar)
- 120 g cheddar, very finely chopped/minced
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 tsp freshly cracked black pepper (or to taste)
- Salt
- Optional: a splash (2–3 tbsp) of milk or cream if you have it

Steps
1) Boil and salt water: Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil. Salt it so it tastes like the sea.
2) Start pasta: Cook spaghetti until just shy of al dente (about 1 minute less than package).
3) Toast pepper: While pasta boils, in your pan on medium-low heat, dry-toast the black pepper 30–45 seconds until fragrant. Add 2 tbsp butter and let it foam, then reduce to low.
4) Add pasta water: Ladle in about 3/4 cup of starchy pasta water to the pan. Swirl to form a glossy, peppery emulsion.
5) Transfer pasta: Move pasta straight from the pot to the pan, reserving extra pasta water.
6) Emulsify: Toss on low heat 30–60 seconds. If using milk/cream, add a small splash now.
7) Add cheese gradually off heat: Turn off the heat. Sprinkle in a third of the cheddar, tossing vigorously until melted and smooth. Add another third, toss; then the last third. If it tightens or looks stringy, add a spoonful of hot pasta water and keep tossing.
8) Finish: Add the last 1 tbsp butter if you like it silkier. Adjust salt and pepper. Plate immediately.

Troubleshooting
- If it gets thick or clumpy: off heat, add hot pasta water a tablespoon at a time and toss.
- If greasy: it’s too hot; cool the pan a bit and add a splash of water to re-emulsify.

### Human: It turned grainy.
### Assistant: That’s usually from overheating the cheese or not enough starchy liquid. Quick rescue:
- Take the pan off heat.
- Add 2–3 tbsp hot pasta water and a teaspoon of butter; toss or whisk vigorously.
- If still grainy, add a tiny splash of milk/cream if available and whisk.
- As a last resort, blend a portion with a hand blender and stir it back.

Next time
- Keep the pan just warm, not hot, when adding cheese.
- Add cheese in small handfuls with constant tossing.
- Hold back a full cup of pasta water so you can thin the sauce as needed.
