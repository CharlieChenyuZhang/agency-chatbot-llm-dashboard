### Human: What about baggage fees and picking an aisle?
### Assistant: General guidance (varies by airline and fare):
- Basic Economy: Often restricts seat selection; sometimes limits carry-ons or boards you last. If you need an aisle and a standard carry-on, Basic is risky.
- Main/Standard Economy: Usually includes a carry-on; aisle selection may be free or a modest fee depending on row.
- Checked bags: Often around $35 for the first bag each way on major carriers; sometimes cheaper with airline credit cards or elite status.

To keep costs down:
- Choose Main/Standard Economy.
- Pick an aisle in the mid/front of the main cabin for better deplaning and fewer seat fees.
- If you must check a bag, compare Southwest into OAK (two free checked bags) as a backup, though that may add travel time on the ground.

### Human: If I go with the red-eye back to save money, any tips to make it less painful?
### Assistant: A few practical tweaks:
- Aim for a red-eye that uses a widebody or has better seat pitch if available; check seat maps and avoid last-row or near-lav seats.
- Bring a neck pillow and eye mask; choose an aisle a few rows forward of the wing for quicker exit and less engine hum.
- Hydrate early, light meal before boarding, and minimize caffeine.
- If seat selection fees are similar, pick an aisle on the two-seat side (on airlines with 2-3-2 or 2-4-2 layouts) or an aisle across from the galley rather than in front of it.
