### Assistant: - Merge two small DataFrames (inner, left)
  - Code:
    - import pandas as pd
    - left = pd.DataFrame({"id":[1,2,3], "group":["A","A","B"]})
    - right = pd.DataFrame({"id":[2,3,4], "score":[88,92,77]})
    - inner = left.merge(right, on="id", how="inner")
    - left_join = left.merge(right, on="id", how="left")
    - inner, left_join
- Group by species; compute mean/median (using penguins CSV)
  - Code:
    - import pandas as pd
    - url = "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins/master/inst/extdata/penguins.csv"
    - df = pd.read_csv(url)
    - num_cols = ["bill_length_mm","bill_depth_mm","flipper_length_mm","body_mass_g"]
    - agg = df.groupby("species")[num_cols].agg(["mean","median"])
    - agg
- Bar and scatter plot of grouped results (matplotlib)
  - Code:
    - import matplotlib.pyplot as plt
    - means = df.groupby("species")["body_mass_g"].mean()
    - means.plot(kind="bar", title="Mean body mass by species")
    - plt.ylabel("grams")
    - plt.tight_layout()
    - plt.show()
    - plt.figure()
    - subset = df.dropna(subset=["bill_length_mm","bill_depth_mm"])
    - for sp, sub in subset.groupby("species"):
        - plt.scatter(sub["bill_length_mm"], sub["bill_depth_mm"], label=sp, alpha=0.7)
    - plt.title("Bill length vs depth by species")
    - plt.xlabel("bill_length_mm"); plt.ylabel("bill_depth_mm"); plt.legend()
    - plt.tight_layout(); plt.show()
