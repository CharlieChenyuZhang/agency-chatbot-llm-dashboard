### Assistant: Here’s a 4‑week, pencil‑and‑paper, 30‑minute/day plan (15 min art + 15 min writing).

Week 1 — Foundations
- Day 1: Art: 2‑min gesture drawings (x7). Writing: Describe one object with only concrete details (10 sentences).
- Day 2: Art: Blind contour of your hand. Writing: Freewrite about a familiar place; avoid adjectives.
- Day 3: Art: Value scale; shade a sphere from a lamp. Writing: Rewrite Day 2 with stronger verbs; no adverbs.
- Day 4: Art: Negative‑space drawing of a chair. Writing: Character sketch from observation (no names).
- Day 5: Art: Line‑weight study of a simple still life. Writing: Dialogue‑only scene ordering food.
- Day 6: Art: 1‑point perspective boxes. Writing: Routine as numbered steps; vary sentence length.
- Day 7: Art: Redraw any Day 1–6 piece; note changes. Writing: Edit one piece to exactly 120 words.

Week 2 — Observation to Composition
- Day 8: Art: Six thumbnails of your desk. Writing: Six one‑sentence openings for the same scene.
- Day 9: Art: Cross‑hatch a crumpled paper. Writing: Describe a remembered photograph.
- Day10: Art: Contour drawing of shoes. Writing: 100‑word vignette anchored in one smell.
- Day11: Art: 2‑point perspective room corner. Writing: Dialogue with subtext (topic never stated).
- Day12: Art: Texture study: glass, cloth, wood. Writing: Show a mood via setting; no emotion words.
- Day13: Art: Light/shadow map on a mug. Writing: Revise any earlier piece for clarity.
- Day14: Art: 20‑min still life; stop at 15 if needed. Writing: 10‑min timed write, then 5‑min cut.

Week 3 — Imagination and Constraints
- Day15: Art: Draw a childhood place from memory. Writing: 10‑min memory list, 5‑min sharpen one.
- Day16: Art: Silhouettes for three objects/characters. Writing: Scene without “I/me.”
- Day17: Art: Exaggerate proportions for effect. Writing: 200 words using only monosyllables.
- Day18: Art: Expressive line: 10 emotions as marks. Writing: Same emotions via action; no labels.
- Day19: Art: Rule‑of‑thirds still life. Writing: Present‑tense one‑page scene (stop at 15 min).
- Day20: Art: Urban sketch from a window. Writing: 150‑word micro‑essay: why this view matters.
- Day21: Art: Refine one earlier drawing. Writing: Edit one earlier piece for specificity.

Week 4 — Project and Polish
- Day22: Art: Choose a subject; plan 3 thumbnails. Writing: Outline a 500‑word story/essay.
- Day23: Art: Final drawing phase 1: block‑in. Writing: Draft opening 200 words.
- Day24: Art: Phase 2: refine values/edges. Writing: Draft middle 200 words.
- Day25: Art: Phase 3: details; stop at 15 min. Writing: Draft ending 200 words.
- Day26: Art: Second version from a new angle. Writing: Alternate ending with different tone.
- Day27: Art: Compare versions; mark changes and apply. Writing: Edit full piece to 500 words.
- Day28: Art: Final pass; sign and date. Writing: Final polish; list 5 next steps.
