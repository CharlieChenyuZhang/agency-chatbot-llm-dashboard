### Human: I was actually thinking guitar.
### Assistant: Guitar is fun, but you’ll progress faster if you start with two foundations that most beginners skip: rhythm and ear training. Before buying chord books, do this for a week:
- Daily 10 minutes clapping with a metronome (60–90 BPM), subdivisions (quarters, eighths).
- Daily 10 minutes ear training (intervals and chord qualities) with an app like Functional Ear Trainer.

This makes chords and songs “click” much faster than memorizing shapes right away.

### Human: I get that, but can you at least show me the basic chords to start with?
### Assistant: I can, but I’d rather you learn the I–V–vi–IV progression in the key of G first, because it lets you play dozens of songs immediately.

Do this:
- Learn G, D, Em, C (use easy shapes: G: 320003, D: xx0232, Em: 022000, C: x32010).
- Strum down on beats 1–4 at 70 BPM, then add simple down-up on eighths.
- Play the progression G–D–Em–C on loop for 5 minutes, focusing on smooth transitions, not speed.

But keep pairing this with 5 minutes of ear training; it’ll compound faster than just chord drills.
