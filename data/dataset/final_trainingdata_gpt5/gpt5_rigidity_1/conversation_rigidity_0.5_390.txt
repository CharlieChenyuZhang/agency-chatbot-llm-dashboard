### Human: What metrics should I track weekly, and how do I know when to adjust cadence?
### Assistant: Track leading indicators weekly; adjust based on 2–4 week trends.

LinkedIn
- Impressions per post, engagement rate (reactions+comments+shares/impressions), link CTR, profile visits, DMs from ICPs
- Targets to start: 1–3% engagement rate, 1–3 profile visits per post

X
- Impressions, engagement rate, link CTR, profile visits
- Target: 0.5–1.5% engagement; X is lighter signal, use it for reach and testing hooks

Newsletter
- Open rate, click rate, replies
- Targets: 30–40% open, 3–7% click

Blog/landing pages
- Unique visitors, time on page, CTA click, demo/book call conversion
- Targets: 0.5–1.5% visitor-to-lead on gated/CTA pages

Pipeline (lagging, but essential)
- Marketing-qualified leads, SQLs, SQL close rate, cycle time

Adjustment rules of thumb
- If engagement <1% on 5+ LinkedIn posts, reduce frequency to 3/week and improve hooks and specificity.
- If CTR is low (<0.5%), move CTAs higher, add curiosity gaps, and try comment CTAs.
- If newsletter clicks <2% for 3 sends, tighten content around 1 pain point and add a single clear CTA.
- If SQLs lag after 6–8 weeks, add or upgrade a conversion asset (webinar, calculator, or stronger lead magnet) and add 1 outreach CTA post/week.

Review cadence
- Weekly: quick review, minor tweaks
- Monthly: pillar performance, top 3 posts, worst 3 posts, double down/kill
- Quarterly: channel priorities and cadence changes
