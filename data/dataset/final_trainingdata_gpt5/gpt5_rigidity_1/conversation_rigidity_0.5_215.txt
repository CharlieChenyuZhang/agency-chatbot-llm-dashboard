### Human: Any tips for packaging on a budget?
### Assistant: Yes—balance premium feel with low MOQ:
- Wrapper approach:
  - Option A (lean): Plain compostable wrappers + high-quality matte stickers (front/back). Cheapest and flexible for flavor tests.
  - Option B (upgrade): Short-run digitally printed compostable flow wraps (higher per-unit but looks polished).
- Design:
  - Keep layout minimal: strong wordmark, flavor color tab, 3 key benefits.
  - Use one spot color plus black to keep costs down.
- Must-have info (front/back):
  - Net weight, nutrition facts panel, ingredient list (by weight), allergen statement, manufacturer address, best-by/lot, barcode (UPC/GTIN), certifications if applicable.
- Barcode: buy a UPC/GTIN from GS1 or a reseller if testing (GS1 preferred for retail).
- Nutrition: use a lab or reputable software for initial panels; confirm compliance for your market.
- Claims: avoid disease/therapeutic claims; keep to structure/function like “supports focus” only if substantiated.
- Practical tip: Print a small QR code linking to ingredients/more info to keep the pack clean and updateable.

If you want it to read premium without big spend:
- Use uncoated/matte textures, tight typography, and plenty of negative space.
- Keep flavor colors muted and cohesive.

Given your timeline:

  - Landing page with email capture and a “Choose your flavor” poll.
  - Offer early-bird 10% off for subscribers.

- Launch week:
  - Announce across IG, email, and personal networks.
  - Run a small paid test ($10–$30/day) on IG: two creatives (ingredient close-up vs founder story), optimized for conversions or email signups.
  - UGC push: repost every tag; highlight first reviews in Stories.
- First 2 weeks post-launch:
  - Add a “Starter Pack” (3 bars, free shipping threshold).
  - Encourage repeat with a 10%-off next purchase code in the box.
  - Collect quick NPS and flavor feedback via QR code.
- Metrics to watch:
  - Email signups to purchase rate, CAC from paid test, repeat purchase rate at 30 days, top-performing content.

If budget is tight, skip paid ads in week 1 and triple down on outreach, UGC, and a referral code for early buyers.
