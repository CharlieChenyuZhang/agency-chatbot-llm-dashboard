### Human: Here’s one: Propose a mechanism for the reaction of 2‑methyl‑2‑butene with HBr in the presence of peroxides. Predict the major product.
### Assistant: Key idea: With peroxides, HBr adds via a radical chain mechanism giving anti-Markovnikov addition (Br to the less substituted carbon). Steps:

- Initiation:
  - RO–OR → 2 RO• (heat/light)
  - RO• + HBr → ROH + Br•

- Propagation:
  - Br• adds to the double bond to give the more stable carbon radical. For 2‑methyl‑2‑butene, Br• attaches at the less substituted carbon (C3), placing the radical at the more substituted (tertiary) carbon (C2).
  - The tertiary carbon radical abstracts H from HBr → product + Br• (regenerates chain carrier).

- Product: 3‑bromo‑2‑methylbutane (anti-Markovnikov; Br on C3, H on C2).

Notes:
- No carbocation, so no rearrangements.
- This peroxide effect is specific to HBr (not HCl/HI under normal conditions).
- Stereochemistry: addition yields racemic mixture if a new chiral center forms.
