On giveaways, a balanced approach:
- Risks: low-signal members, churn after prize, moderation overhead.
- Better alternative: contribution-based recognition (role upgrades, spotlight, early access to AMAs).
- If you still want giveaways:
  - Make the entry action valuable (e.g., “leave 3 actionable playtest notes on builds in #playtesting”).
  - Cap to monthly, not weekly.
  - Prize relevant to devs (asset store credits, tool license), not cash.
  - Eligibility: account >30 days old + member for 2 weeks + passed Code of Conduct quiz.
  - Announce winners publicly with a highlight of their contributions.

### Human: How do we measure engagement and retention without fancy tools?

### Assistant: Simple, manual-friendly metrics you can track weekly:

Acquisition
- New members joined

Activation
- % who post in #introductions within 7 days
- % who make a first dev-log within 7 days

Engagement
- Weekly active members (posted/replied/reacted)
- Reply rate: posts in #dev-logs that get at least one reply within 24 hours
- Event participation: unique attendees per event

Retention
- 4-week retention: of members who joined in Week N, how many posted in Week N+4

How-to without tools
- Export member join dates weekly (or log in a sheet).
- Maintain a simple spreadsheet with columns: join week, first intro date, first dev-log date, last activity week.
- For reply rate, count last 20 dev-log posts and how many got replies within 24 hours.

Benchmarks to start
- Activation: 30–50%
- Weekly active: 35–45% of last 4-week joiners
- Reply rate: 80% of dev-log posts get a response in 24 hours
