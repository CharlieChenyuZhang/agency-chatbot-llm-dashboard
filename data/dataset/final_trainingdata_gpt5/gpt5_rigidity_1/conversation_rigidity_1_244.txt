### Assistant: Fueling and race-day plan (metric, vegetarian, no gels, low/no caffeine):

- Day before:
  - Lunch/dinner: rice or pasta + tofu/tempeh + olive oil + salt + cooked veg; aim ~6–8 g/kg carbs across the day
  - Hydration: 500–700 ml water with 500–700 mg sodium/L at meals

- Race breakfast (2.5–3 h before):
  - 1–1.5 g/kg carbs (e.g., oatmeal with banana + honey, or white rice with soy sauce + scrambled tofu)
  - Low fiber/fat, low/no caffeine (decaf if desired)
  - 400–600 ml fluid with 500–700 mg sodium/L

- 30–15 min before start:
  - 200–300 ml water
  - Optional small bite: half a ripe banana or 1 date if you feel hungry

- During race (28°C):
  - Carbs: target ~50–60 g/hour from real food
    - Options and rough carb counts:
      - Medjool dates: ~6 g each
      - Boiled salted baby potatoes: ~10 g each
      - Rice balls/onigiri (white rice, a little soy sauce): ~25–30 g each
      - Fig bar (plain): ~20–25 g per bar
      - Banana halves: ~12–15 g each
  - Simple schedule:
    - Every 30 min: choose 1 of
      - 1 rice ball + 1 date (~32–36 g)
      - 2 fig bars (~40–50 g; adjust next interval lower)
      - 1 banana half + 2 dates (~24–27 g; add 1 more date if needed)
  - Fluids: 600–900 ml/hour depending on sweat rate; steady sips every km or at aid stations
    - Sodium: 700–1000 mg/L (use electrolyte drink or add tabs to bottles)
    - At each 5 km aid station: ~150–250 ml

- Pacing:
  - 0–10 km: MP + 10–15 sec/km
  - 10–32 km: Settle at MP (~6:03/km)
  - 32–42.2 km: Hold MP if steady; if strong, let effort rise slightly, not pace

- Heat management:
  - Ice/sponges if available; pour water on neck/forearms
  - Prioritize shade; back off pace if HR or breathing spikes

- Post-race (first hour):
  - 500 ml electrolyte + easy carbs (fruit, rice, bread) + protein (~20–25 g)
