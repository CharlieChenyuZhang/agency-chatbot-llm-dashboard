### Human: I’m a new engineering manager for a 12-person remote team at a startup. I want to improve 1:1s, delegation, and decision-making over the next 6 weeks. Prefer a step-by-step weekly plan. Keep answers concise, bullets only.
### Assistant: Week 1
- Schedule recurring 1:1s; send agenda template; align expectations
- List current responsibilities; identify 2 low-risk tasks to delegate
- Define decision guardrails (who decides what; criteria: impact, urgency, reversibility)

Week 2
- Run 1:1s (70/30 they talk/you talk); ask two open questions per 1:1
- Delegate the 2 tasks; assign owners; set success criteria and deadlines
- Start a decision log (decision, owner, rationale, date)

Week 3
- Observe delegated work; give timely, specific feedback
- Use a simple proposal format (problem, options, recommendation) for team decisions
- Hold 30-min Q&A on delegation and decision process

Week 4
- Add growth topic to 1:1s; remove blockers
- Delegate 1 medium-risk area; set check-ins
- Run a quick pre-mortem for a pending decision; note risks and triggers

Week 5
- Let engineers lead a part of standup/demo
- Review outcomes of delegated work; adjust scope or support
- Make one visible decision; share criteria and rationale

Week 6
- Ask for feedback on 1:1s, delegation, and decision transparency
- Select next two delegation candidates; plan handoffs
- Run a short retro: keep/stop/start in your leadership practices

### Human: I need a simple decision-making checklist for choosing between two competing roadmap items. Max 7 steps.
### Assistant: - Define the decision and success criteria (outcomes, time horizon).
- List the options and key assumptions.
- Check constraints (time, budget, dependencies, risks).
- Compare impact vs. effort and reversibility.
- Get quick input from the DRI and 1–2 key stakeholders.
- Decide and record rationale, owner, and next step.
- Set a review point and success metric.
