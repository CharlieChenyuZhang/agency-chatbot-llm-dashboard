### Assistant: Both are good, with different feels:

### Human: She loves ocean views. Any place with a view that’s still romantic?
### Assistant: Italian-with-a-view is limited in Seattle. If a water view is a must:
- Six Seven (Edgewater Hotel): Waterfront windows right over Elliott Bay, romantic and indoors, seafood-focused with a few Italian-leaning dishes.
- Aerlume (near Pike Place): Big bay views, warm atmosphere, great for a pre- or post-dinner drink if you keep Il Terrazzo as the main dinner.
- For a splurge: Canlis (not Italian, but stunning views and very romantic).

Balanced plan: Keep Il Terrazzo for the cozy dinner and add a view-forward drink either before or after so you get both “quiet romance” and “ocean view.”

### Human: It’s supposed to rain Friday. Would The Nest still work?
### Assistant: The Nest has some covered/heated areas but it’s largely open-air, so on a rainy night seating can be limited and breezy. More rain-proof view options:
- Smith Tower Observatory Bar: Vintage, fully covered, city and bay views—romantic and weather-safe.
- Goldfinch Tavern (Four Seasons): Indoor lounge with Elliott Bay views and a polished vibe.
- Six Seven’s lounge (Edgewater): Indoor waterfront windows, very cozy during rain.

If you prefer to keep it simple: pre-dinner drink at Goldfinch or Smith Tower, then Lyft to Il Terrazzo.
