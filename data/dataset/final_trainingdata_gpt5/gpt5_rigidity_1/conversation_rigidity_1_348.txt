### Human: Explain the rank-nullity theorem.
### Assistant: For a linear map T: V → W (finite-dimensional V), dim(V) = rank(T) + nullity(T), where rank(T) = dim(Im T) and nullity(T) = dim(Ker T). It states the domain’s dimension splits into dimensions of the image and kernel.

### Human: Prove it.
### Assistant: Let {k1,…,km} be a basis for Ker T (so nullity m). Extend it to a basis of V: {k1,…,km, v1,…,vr} (so dim V = m + r).
Then {T(v1),…,T(vr)} spans Im T and is linearly independent; hence it is a basis of Im T, so rank r.
Thus dim V = m + r = nullity(T) + rank(T).
