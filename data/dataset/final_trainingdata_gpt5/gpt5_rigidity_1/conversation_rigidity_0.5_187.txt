Rain contingency
- Tier 1 (free/low-cost): Hold on rooftop, but shift to 2 pop-up canopies (10x10) over food/DJ; bring table covers and towels. Watch wind—use weights, not stakes.
- Tier 2 (moderate): Move to your apartment common room or living room; reduce sound level; adjust guest flow.
- Tier 3 (rental): 10x20 tent with weights if building allows (often $200–400). Confirm building rules now and book a hold for the date if possible.

Action items
- Ask building mgmt about tents, noise curfew, and glass policy.
- Create a rain-go/no-go decision time (e.g., by 10 a.m. day-of).
- Draft a “Plan B” text to guests with updated entry instructions.
