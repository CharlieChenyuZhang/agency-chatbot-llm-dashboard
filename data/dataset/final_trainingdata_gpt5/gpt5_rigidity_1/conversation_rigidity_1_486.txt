### Assistant: Here’s a 4-week, 45‑minute‑per‑day plan focused on songs and quiet playing.

Week 1
- Daily (45 min): 
  - 5 min: Tune. Light finger strum with the thumb for low volume.
  - 10 min: Comfortable posture, relaxed fretting, clean presses (no buzz).
  - 15 min: Learn finger shapes for three basic chords (e.g., C, F, G7) without explanations—just shapes and switches.

Week 2
- Daily (45 min):
  - 5 min: Tune. Quiet thumb strum.
  - 10 min: Reinforce the same chord shapes.
  - 15 min: Add one more song with the same chords or one new shape.
  - 15 min: Slow play‑throughs of both songs, focusing on steady timing and minimal volume. Use light palm‑muting to keep sound down.

Week 3
- Daily (45 min):
  - 5 min: Tune.
  - 10 min: Chord switches with a gentle metronome or steady count (no theory terms), aiming for smooth changes.
  - 15 min: Learn one new song that adds only one new chord shape.
  - 15 min: Combine all songs into a quiet mini‑set, staying soft and even.

Week 4
- Daily (45 min):
  - 5 min: Tune.
  - 10 min: Quiet strum variety: thumb downstrokes and very light finger brushing.
  - 15 min: Refine trouble spots in your songs at slow speed.
  - 15 min: Full, soft run‑through of your set. Record once this week to check smoothness and volume control.

Keep volume low by using the fleshy part of your thumb, light fretting pressure, and resting the uke on a soft surface.
