### Human: Give me recipes for Day 1 lunch and dinner with exact measurements. Nothing else.
### Assistant: Day 1 Lunch — Lentil Tabbouleh (1 serving)
Ingredients:
- Fine bulgur, dry: 1/4 cup
- Boiling water: 1/2 cup
- Cooked green or brown lentils: 3/4 cup
- Fresh parsley, finely chopped: 1 cup (loosely packed)
- Tomato, diced: 1 medium (about 3/4 cup)
- Cucumber, diced: 1/2 medium (about 1/2 cup)
- Scallion, thinly sliced: 1
- Fresh lemon juice: 2 tbsp
- Extra-virgin olive oil: 1.5 tbsp
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/8 tsp

Directions:
1) Combine bulgur with boiling water; cover 15 minutes; fluff and drain excess.
2) In a bowl, mix lentils, parsley, tomato, cucumber, and scallion.
3) Add lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper; fold in bulgur; adjust seasoning.

Day 1 Dinner — Lentil–Tomato–Pepper Stew with Couscous (1 serving)
Ingredients:
- Whole-wheat couscous, dry: 1/3 cup
- Boiling water (for couscous): 1/2 cup
- Olive oil: 1 tbsp
- Yellow onion, diced: 1/2 medium (about 1/2 cup)
- Red bell pepper, sliced: 1 medium
- Garlic, minced: 2 cloves
- Ground cumin: 1 tsp
- Smoked paprika: 1 tsp
- Crushed tomatoes (canned): 1 cup
- Vegetable broth (low sodium): 1/2 cup
- Cooked green or brown lentils: 1 cup
- Salt: 1/4 tsp
- Black pepper: 1/8 tsp
- Fresh parsley, chopped: 2 tbsp
- Lemon juice: 1 tbsp

Directions:
1) Place couscous in a bowl, pour in boiling water, cover 5 minutes; fluff.
2) Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium; sauté onion and pepper 5–6 minutes.
3) Add garlic, cumin, and smoked paprika; stir 30 seconds.
4) Add crushed tomatoes and broth; simmer 8 minutes.
5) Stir in lentils; simmer 3–4 minutes; season with salt, pepper, and lemon juice.
6) Serve stew over couscous; top with parsley.
