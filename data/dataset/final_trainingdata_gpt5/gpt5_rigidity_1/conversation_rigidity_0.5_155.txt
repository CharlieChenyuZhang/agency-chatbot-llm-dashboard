### Human: I’m concerned eco-packaging will blow the budget. I still want a premium unboxing. Suggestions?

### Assistant: You can get a premium feel without big spend:
- Mailer: Recycled kraft mailer with a single-color logo stamp (cheaper than custom printed boxes).
- Inner protection: Compostable garment bag (thin), wrapped in unbleached tissue with a logo sticker.
- Insert: One 4x6 postcard on recycled stock: “Care guide + brand note.” Avoid multiple inserts.
- Scent/experience: A subtle branded sticker and a clean layout beats extra filler.
- Vendors: Noissue, EcoEnclose, or Hero Packaging. Order small quantities to test.

Tradeoff: Skip heavy printed boxes now; invest in photography and fit. You can upgrade packaging later.
