Week 6 — ML Basics with scikit-learn

Week 8 — Classification (Logistic, k-NN, Naive Bayes)

- Resources: scikit-learn tree/ensemble guides; XGBoost docs.

Week 10 — Model Evaluation and Tuning

- Resources: scikit-learn model_selection; Machine Learning Mastery (articles).

Week 11 — SQL for Analytics
- Goals: SELECT/JOIN/GROUP BY/CTEs/window functions.
- Resources: Mode SQL Tutorial; SQLBolt.

Week 12 — Data Acquisition (APIs, Web Scraping)

- Resources: Requests docs; Beautiful Soup docs; Open data portals.

Week 13 — Unsupervised Learning
- Goals: k-means, hierarchical clustering, PCA.
- Resources: scikit-learn clustering & decomposition; Distill.pub PCA.
- Project: Segment customers + PCA visualization.

Week 14 — Time Series Basics
- Goals: Date handling, resampling, decomposition, baselines.
- Resources: pandas time series guide; statsmodels tsa docs.
- Project: Forecast a univariate series vs baseline.

Week 15 — NLP Fundamentals (Classical)
- Goals: Tokenization, TF-IDF, n-grams, simple classifiers.
- Resources: NLTK Book (free); scikit-learn text feature extraction.
- Project: Sentiment classifier on reviews.

Week 16 — Communicating Results and Light Deployment
- Goals: Reproducible notebooks, reports, dashboards.
- Resources: Jupyter Book docs; Streamlit docs.
- Project: Turn a prior project into a shareable app/report.
