### Human: Okay, but what do you think for most people?
### Assistant: Most NYC residents are better off without owning a car. Why:
- Transit coverage is extensive; trains every 4–8 minutes in many areas.
- Parking is expensive and stressful; street parking adds hours each week.
- Insurance, tickets, tolls, and maintenance add up fast.
Exceptions where a car can make sense:
- You live far from the subway (e.g., outer Queens/outer Brooklyn hills).
- You have mobility needs, equipment-heavy hobbies, or multiple kids with far-flung activities.
- You regularly leave the city before dawn or after midnight.

For trips, weekend rentals or carshare usually beat ownership.

### Human: I’ve got two kids and we do occasional weekend trips. Does that change things?
### Assistant: It nudges you closer to “sometimes a car,” but ownership still isn’t necessary for most families:
- Keep daily life car-free near reliable subway/bus lines and good grocery options.
- Use Zipcar/Turo or weekend rentals for trips; request car seats or bring compact travel seats.
- For weeknights, rideshare with car seat options can bridge gaps.
- If the kids have far-flung activities at odd hours and you’re in a transit-poor area, then consider leasing or street-parking—but that’s the exception.
