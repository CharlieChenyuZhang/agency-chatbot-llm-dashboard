### Human: I’m worried about overdrafts. How do we prevent them?
### Assistant: My preference is to compartmentalize cash and add fail-safes:
- Keep a dedicated “Bills” checking account at Chase with 1–1.5 months of bills as a buffer; link your card autopay to this account
- Enable low-balance alert on Bills account at buffer minus one week of bills
- Schedule a recurring transfer from main checking to Bills account on payday (fixed amount)
- Avoid autopay from the main account so daily spending can’t drain bill money
- Enable “payment failed” and “returned payment” alerts on the credit card and Spectrum
