### Human: Our CEO insists on twice daily posts or we lose “momentum.” Can we make it work?
### Assistant: We can, with some guardrails so quality doesn’t drop:
- Redefine “twice daily” as 1 main post + 1 lighter touch:
  - Instagram: 1 feed post/Reel + 1–3 Stories (polls, repost UGC, quick promos).
  - TikTok: 1 main post + 1 lightweight post (reply to a comment, stitch/duet, or quick tip).
  - LinkedIn: 1 main post 2x/week; other days use 1 micro-post (thought, photo, or team note).
- Batch + template:
  - Create 6–8 evergreen micro-tips from your brew guides.
  - Design 3–4 reusable promo templates (new roast, low-stock, bundle).
  - Set a weekly filming block (90 minutes) to capture 10–12 short clips.

If you still need literally 2 feed posts per platform daily, I’ll structure a rotating template system to keep it manageable.

### Human: Can we just repost the same video to all platforms to save time?
### Assistant: Yes, but tweak for each platform to improve performance:
- Edit ratio: 9:16 for IG Reels/TikTok; 1:1 or 4:5 for IG feed carousels; 16:9 or square works on LinkedIn.
- Hook/length: TikTok favors snappy hooks; LinkedIn needs more context in caption; Instagram can be aesthetic with clear on-screen text.
- Captions:
  - TikTok: short, conversational, 2–4 relevant hashtags.
  - Instagram: slightly longer caption + a clear CTA; 5–10 niche hashtags.
  - LinkedIn: value-forward paragraph, minimal or no hashtags (1–3 max).
- Audio: Trending sounds help on TikTok/IG; use royalty-safe or voiceover. LinkedIn: usually native audio or voiceover.

Workflow:
1) Shoot a master vertical video.
2) Create three cuts: TikTok (punchy), IG Reel (aesthetic + CTA), LinkedIn (context-heavy).
3) Adjust captions and hashtags per platform.
