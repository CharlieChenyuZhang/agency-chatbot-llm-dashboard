### Human: My dumbbells top out at 40 lb. How do I keep progressing once those get easy?
### Assistant: You’ve got several good levers beyond load. Use one at a time so the progression stays clear. - Double progression: Work within ranges (e.g., 8–12). When all sets hit 12 with 1–2 RIR, move to a harder variation. - Harder variations: - Squat: Tempo (3–0–3), 2-second pauses, heels-elevated for quads, long-range split squats, front-loaded (two-DB front squat). - Hinge: Single-leg RDLs, 2–3 second negatives, extended ROM on a small platform. - Press: One-arm DB press, slow eccentric, dead-stop floor press. - Pull: Slow eccentric chin-ups, pause at top, towel grip for forearms, add weight with a backpack. - Density: Keep the same total reps but shorten rest by 10–15 seconds week to week. - Volume: Add a fourth set for one key lift per session when reps cap out. - ROM: Elevate feet for push-ups; deeper split squat stance if knees allow. - Clusters/myo-reps: Split a set into mini-sets (e.g., 10-3-3-3 with 15–20s between). - External load hacks: Fill a backpack with books or water jugs for goblets, split squats, and pull-ups. Pick one primary method for 3–4 weeks, then rotate.
