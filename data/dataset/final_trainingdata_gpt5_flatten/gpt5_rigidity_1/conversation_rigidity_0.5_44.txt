### Human: How do I handle pushback like “budget is tight” or “we don’t do off-cycle promotions”?
### Assistant: Scripts you can adapt: - Budget is tight: - “Understood. Since the Staff scope is already in motion, could we align title in Q1 and apply part of the comp now with the remainder at merit? Alternatively, can we shift to a mix—smaller base increase plus equity refresh or a retention bonus tied to [milestone]?” - We don’t do off-cycle promotions: - “That works. Can we agree on a future-dated promotion effective Q1 with the packet finalized this month? If helpful, I can take on [specific Staff-level initiative] now to demonstrate continued impact.” - Let’s see after the next project: - “Happy to. Can we define success criteria and a date? For example, if I deliver X by [date], we proceed with Staff in Q1 with compensation aligned.” - Concern about comparables or internal equity: - “I want to be fair to the team. Could we review the Staff band and where my responsibilities map so we place me appropriately within the range?” Always end with a collaborative ask: “What would make you comfortable to support this?”
### Human: How much should I ask for? Is 15% too much?
### Assistant: 10–20% is a common band for a level change. If Staff is a formal level above Senior, 15% is reasonable if supported by scope and market. Approach: - Research: Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, OpenComp; triangulate with geography and company size. Check internal bands if you can. - Anchor with a range: “Given Staff scope and market, I’m targeting comp in the [$X–$Y] range, roughly a 15–18% base adjustment, open to structure across base/bonus/equity.” - If your company leans bonus/equity heavy, be flexible on mix to land total comp while protecting base growth. If you learn the band is lower, suggest: “Could we place me at [top quartile of Senior] now with a written move to Staff (and Staff band placement) in Q1?”
