### Human: What if I miss a day?
### Assistant: Use a gentle reset: - Never miss twice: if you miss one, do the next session as planned—no punishment. - Keep streaks for “show up” (did I write today?) not word count. - If life happens, do a 10-min micro-session that day and call it a win. - Weekly review: reflect on why you missed and adjust one friction point (sleep, prep, distractions).
### Human: How do I measure progress?
### Assistant: Keep it simple: - Daily: minutes written, words added, yes/no showed up, 1-line note. - Weekly: total minutes, total words, number of sessions, one win, one tweak. - Monthly: 1-page summary of what moved the project forward.
