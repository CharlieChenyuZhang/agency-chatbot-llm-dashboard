### Human: Should I try to buy a house instead of renting?
### Assistant: It depends on time horizon and cash flow. A quick framework: Buying can make sense if: - You’ll stay 5+ years (to overcome transaction costs), - Total housing costs (mortgage, taxes, insurance, maintenance, HOA) stay under ~28% of gross income and total debt under ~36% (the 28/36 rule), - You keep a 3–6 month emergency fund after closing. Trade-offs: - Ties up cash that could go to debt/investing now. - Maintenance and property tax risk. - In Austin, price-to-rent can be high in some neighborhoods. FI-friendly alternatives: - Wait until the credit card is gone and EF is solid. - Consider house hacking (renting a room or duplex) to offset costs. - If you’re unsure you’ll stay 5+ years, renting keeps flexibility while you build investments.
