### Human: Yes. My main problem is filler words in meetings.
### Assistant: Let’s tackle fillers with meeting-specific techniques and drills. Quick techniques you can use immediately: - The beat pause: Replace “um” with a one-beat silent pause before answering. - Anchor phrase: Use “Let me take that in order.” or “Two points:” to buy a second and set structure. - Tap cue: Lightly tap a finger when you need a pause; physical cue helps replace fillers. - Short frames to think: “Give me a moment to check the numbers.” This resets expectations, reduces pressure. - Structure on the fly: Try PREP (Point, Reason, Example, Point) or Status-Blockers-Next. Micro-drill (2 minutes, before a meeting): - Pull a likely question. Answer it 3 times: 1) Over-slowed, exaggerated pauses. 2) Normal speed, deliberate 1-beat pause before any list or detail. 3) With an anchor phrase (“Two updates: …”) then answer. Meeting script you can reuse: - “Two updates: First, integration is 80% complete; second, we’re validating edge cases. The risk is API rate limits; mitigation is batching calls. Next deliverable: staging demo Friday.” Want to try a live practice prompt right now?
