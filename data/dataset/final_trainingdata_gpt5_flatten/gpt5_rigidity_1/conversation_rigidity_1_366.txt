Measurable outcomes - Completion: 28 sessions logged; 1 stakeholder map, 1 PESTLE, 1 competitor 2x2, ≥10 assumptions, 3 options with evidence, risk register, metrics tree, 1-page strategy, 3 scenarios, decision log. - Evidence: ≥20 external signals collected across weeks (free sources only). - Decisions: ≥1 clear strategic decision documented with rationale and next moves. - Alignment: Peer feedback improves by ≥1 point on a 1–5 clarity scale from Week 3 to Week 4.
