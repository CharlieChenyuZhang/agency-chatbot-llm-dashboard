If you prefer to skip a Microsoft account, you can often choose “I don’t have Internet” or “Continue with limited setup” to create a local account. If you’re fine using a Microsoft account, sign in with your email and password. - Create your user account name and a secure password. Write it down in a safe place or store it in a password manager later. - When Windows asks about privacy settings—Location, Find my device, Inking & typing, Tailored experiences—toggle off anything you don’t want. [Step 2: Run Windows Update – 60 seconds] - Click the Start button, then Settings (the gear icon). Go to Windows Update. - Click “Check for updates.” Let everything download and install.
