### Human: I was thinking Tulum since my friends loved it. Thoughts?
### Assistant: Tulum can be beautiful, but a few practical notes for early May: - Sargassum seaweed often peaks in spring/summer on that coast, which can affect beaches. - It’s gotten pricier and more crowded. - You can still have a great time (cenotes, ruins, dining), just plan around the beach conditions. If you want a similar area with fewer crowds and better odds on swimming: - Akumal or Puerto Morelos: Quieter, good snorkeling. - Isla Mujeres or Cozumel: Island vibe, generally clearer water, easier without a car. ### Human: Belize sounds interesting. Which island?
### Assistant: Two main choices: - Caye Caulker - Vibe: “Go slow” island, laid-back, smaller and quieter. - Pros: Budget-friendlier, walkable, easy access to Hol Chan/Shark Ray Alley. - Consider: Fewer upscale dining options; beaches are mostly small piers/swim spots. - Ambergris Caye (San Pedro) - Vibe: Livelier, more restaurants/bars, more lodging variety. - Pros: Tons of tour operators, good mid-range options, nice for first-timers. - Consider: Busier; you’ll likely use golf carts/taxis. ### Human: What about safety and weather?
### Assistant: Weather: - Early May is warm (mid-80s F/around 30 C), generally dry, light breezes—good snorkeling visibility. - Hurricane season starts June; May risk is lower. Safety: - The cayes are generally safe. Petty theft can happen—use hotel safes, don’t leave valuables on piers/beaches. - Stick to licensed tour operators and follow reef-safe practices. Health/practical: - Mosquitoes can be active at dawn/dusk—bring repellent. - Reef-safe sunscreen is encouraged. - Consider basic travel insurance for medical and weather disruptions.
