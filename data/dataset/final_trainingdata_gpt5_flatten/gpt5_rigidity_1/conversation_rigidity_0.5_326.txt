### Human: Also teach me the rolled r. I need to perfect it fast.
### Assistant: I’ll help you get close quickly, but the full trill often takes weeks of practice. A practical path: - Start with the single tap [ɾ]: say “t” rapidly between vowels: “ara, ero, iri, oro, uru.” Aim for a quick tap, like the American ‘tt’ in “butter.” - Then the trill [r]: place tongue tip lightly on the alveolar ridge (behind upper teeth), relax, and push steady air: “rr-rr-rr.” Start with “rá, ré, rí, ró, rú,” then “perro,” “carro.” - Minimal pairs: caro/carro, pero/perro, cero/cerro. Record yourself and compare to native audio. If it doesn’t click, a useful alternative is to master the tap first and use it for “rr” temporarily. Most listeners will still understand you, and you’ll reduce frustration while you build the trill.
