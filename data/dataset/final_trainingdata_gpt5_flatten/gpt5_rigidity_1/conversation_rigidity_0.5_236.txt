### Human: I want to include AI meal recommendations. Can we use GPT?
### Assistant: Yes, but let’s balance cost, speed, and consistency. - Approach - Phase 1: Rule-based + template matching. Use user prefs to filter your recipe set and pick a balanced weekly plan. Zero token cost and instant. - Phase 2: Add AI to personalize swaps and suggest variations. Cache results to control cost. - Model choices - GPT-4o mini or similar: good quality, moderate cost - Open-source (Llama variants) via an API: cheaper but more setup - Cost control - Keep prompts short and structured - Limit token usage to swaps or personalization, not the whole plan generation - Cache by user + week; precompute popular plans - Safety/disclaimers - Not medical advice; prompt the model to avoid medical claims - Strict allergen filtering must be rule-based, not AI-only I can draft a prompt and a simple rules engine if you’d like.

### Human: Any legal stuff I should worry about?
### Assistant: I’m not a lawyer, but here are common items: - Terms of Service and Privacy Policy (tailored to your data collection) - Nutrition/medical disclaimer; allergen disclaimer; recipe accuracy disclaimer - Data protection: if you collect emails and preferences, store securely; offer deletion - If you target parents of young kids, avoid collecting data from children directly; be mindful of COPPA and similar rules - Business setup: basic LLC formation and a separate business bank account If you need templates, services like Termly or iubenda can generate ToS/Privacy. For diet-related liability and subscriptions, a quick consult with a small-business attorney is wise.
