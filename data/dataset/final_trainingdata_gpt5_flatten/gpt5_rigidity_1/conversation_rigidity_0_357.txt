### Human: Okay but I still want app profile tips.

### Human: What about making the relationship successful once we start dating?
### Assistant: My preference: date slowly and screen well. ### Assistant: Yes—my plan, not the fastest, but the most durable: Weeks 1–2 (solo foundation) - Life audit: sleep, money, time; fix one bottleneck - Join 2 recurring communities; attend twice Weeks 3–4 (social reps) - 3 low-stakes conversations/week in those spaces - Draft values + non-negotiables list Weeks 5–8 (intentional dating) - Apps capped at 10 min/day; 1–2 micro-dates/week (coffee walk, 45 minutes) - Track green flags, yellow flags, energy after dates Weeks 9+ (depth) - If aligned: weekly check-in ritual, shared plan (fitness, travel, learning) - If misaligned: end kindly and early
