### Human: Help me build a daily meditation practice
### Assistant: I’m happy to help. To tailor it, please tell me: - Your main goal (stress, focus, sleep, etc.). - When you want to practice (morning/evening) and for how long. - Preferred style (breath-focused, body scan, mantra, guided vs. silent). - Any physical constraints or posture preferences (chair, floor, back issues). - Tech preferences (apps vs. no-tech) and any language preferences (strictly secular vs. open to spiritual terms).
### Human: Goal: reduce stress and improve focus. Morning, 10 minutes. Breath-focused, silent, strictly secular. I’ll sit on a chair; no injuries. No apps—keep it simple. Please give me a simple 7-day plan and a short 10‑minute script I can reuse.
### Assistant: Great. Here’s a concise, secular plan and reusable script. 7-day plan (10 minutes each morning): - Day 1: Setup + breath as anchor. Learn posture and basic attention. - Day 2: Add gentle “labeling” when distracted (“thinking,” “hearing,” etc.), then return to breath. - Day 3: Brief body scan (1–2 min) to settle, then breath. - Day 4: Work with distractions: notice, name once, let go, return. - Day 5: Extend focus: slightly longer breath segments before labeling. - Day 6: Widen awareness for the last minute (sounds, body) without chasing them. - Day 7: Same practice; add a 30‑sec reflection at the end (what helped, what didn’t). Reusable 10-minute script (chair, eyes soft or closed): - 0:00–1:00 Set posture: feet flat, hands on thighs, spine tall but relaxed. One slow exhale. Set intention: “Practice calm attention.” - 1:00–8:30 Breath focus: feel breath at nostrils or belly. When you notice distraction, label it once (“thinking,” “planning,” “sound”), and gently return to the breath. - 8:30–9:30 Widen awareness: include overall body and sounds while keeping breath in the foreground. - 9:30–10:00 Close: one deeper breath, note one word about the session (e.g., “steady,” “restless”), open eyes. Tips: - Use a simple kitchen timer. - Same time, same chair, minimal light and noise.
### Human: Could you add a 2-minute night wind-down version?
### Assistant: Yes—2-minute night wind-down: - 0:00–0:30 Get comfortable seated or lying down; soften jaw, shoulders, belly. - 0:30–1:30 6 easy breaths: in through nose, out slightly longer (e.g., in 4, out 6). Count down from 6 to 1 with each exhale. - 1:30–2:00 Brief body sweep: forehead, eyes, jaw, throat, chest, belly—at each spot think “soften,” then let go. End.
