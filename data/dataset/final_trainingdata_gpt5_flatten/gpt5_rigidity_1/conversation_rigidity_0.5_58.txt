### Human: Great. How do I add a custom domain later?
### Assistant: On Netlify: 1) Site settings → Domain management → Add custom domain → enter your domain. 2) Choose either: - Use Netlify DNS (easiest): point your registrar’s nameservers to Netlify; they’ll handle DNS and SSL automatically. - Keep your current DNS: add a CNAME from www to your-site-name.netlify.app, and either: - use www as primary, or - add ANAME/ALIAS or A records for root domain if your DNS supports it (Netlify’s docs list providers). 3) Click “Verify,” then “Provision certificate.” Auto-HTTPS will be set up via Let’s Encrypt. Propagation usually finishes within minutes to a couple of hours.

### Human: I’d like a newsletter too. What’s simplest?
### Assistant: Easiest low-effort options: - Buttondown: simple, cheap, great for indie blogs. You embed a form and manage subscribers. - ConvertKit/Mailchimp: more marketing features, heavier UI. - Substack: all-in-one newsletter + blog, but you’d be splitting platforms. For Buttondown: - Create an account, get the embed form from Settings → Subscription. - Add a new page subscribe with the form, and link it in your nav. - You can also add the form to the homepage sidebar if you want. If you prefer everything in one place without embeds, an alternative is to use Ghost (managed) which has built-in newsletter—but that’s a monthly cost and more setup.
