### Human: I’m worried about permits taking too long. Can we start sooner?
### Assistant: Yes—several fast-start options while permits process: - Private property pop-ups: With written permission from a property manager (apartments, offices, co-working, gyms). Often faster than public right-of-way permits. - Farmers markets and events: Use the market’s umbrella permit; you still need food handler cert and possibly a temp permit, but it’s typically quicker. - Catering/preorder delivery: Sell “coffee boxes” and cold brew growlers to offices and student org meetings (no open public vending spot required). - Partner with a food truck park: They often have established processes and can host you certain mornings. - Campus org collabs: Sponsor club meetings with prepaid tabs; serve on private campus spaces with org approval. Trade-off: You may sacrifice walk-up volume initially, but you validate your product, build relationships, and generate early cash flow.
