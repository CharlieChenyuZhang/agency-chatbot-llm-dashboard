### Human: I struggle with giving tough feedback. Can you give me a script?
### Assistant: Here’s a simple flow using SBI + ask: - Situation: “In yesterday’s client call…” - Behavior: “…you interrupted twice while Priya was answering.” - Impact: “…the client asked if we were aligned, and Priya felt undermined.” - Ask: “Next calls, can you wait for a handoff or post thoughts in chat? How can I help make that easy?” Tips: - Do it within 48 hours - One behavior at a time; be specific - End with a clear next step and follow-up time If you prefer a lighter tone, swap “impact” with a curiosity prompt: “What was going on for you in that moment?”
