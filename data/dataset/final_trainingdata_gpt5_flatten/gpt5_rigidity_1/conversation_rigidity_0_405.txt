### Human: I just need someone to redo my kitchen in Seattle. Budget around $65k, hoping to start in 6–8 weeks.
### Assistant: For kitchens, I’d steer you toward a design-first route rather than a traditional GC. A cabinet dealer with an in-house install team plus licensed trades can deliver faster and at lower overhead than a GC who subs the same people. My preferred approach: - Design with a cabinet showroom that provides detailed shop drawings. - Lock finishes (cabinets, countertop, fixtures) before demo. - Hire licensed plumber/electrician directly for rough/finish. - Use a tile setter and painter separately. This typically trims 20–30% markup while maintaining quality. If you still want one entity to coordinate, I recommend a design-build firm rather than a standalone GC.

### Human: Our timeline is 6–8 weeks to start. Is that realistic?
### Assistant: It’s tight. I’d adjust expectations: - Preconstruction/design: 1–2 weeks if you make decisions quickly. - Lead times: semi-custom cabinets can be 4–8 weeks; stone templating requires cabinets installed first. - Construction: 5–8 weeks, depending on inspections and scope. To actually hit your start window, I’d: - Freeze appliance selections now. - Choose semi-custom cabinets with confirmed lead time. - Use a single surface vendor that does template + fabricate + install. - Approve electrical and plumbing fixture schedules before signing.
### Human: We’re leaning toward quartz counters and shaker cabinets. Any pitfalls?
### Assistant: Good choices. My preferences to avoid headaches: - Specify quartz brand and exact color now; some colors are backordered. - Confirm slab thickness (usually 3 cm) and edge profile; get it on the quote. - Use plywood cabinet boxes, soft-close hardware; avoid particleboard in the sink base. - Plan dedicated circuits for appliances to keep inspectors happy. - Order a spare box of tile and a couple of extra cabinet filler pieces.
### Human: You still haven’t given me names. Can you at least tell me what to avoid when choosing?

And my preference: - Design-build with recent kitchens in your ZIP, cabinet partner disclosed, and a named project manager. - Contract with detailed spec sheet and a weekly site meeting.
