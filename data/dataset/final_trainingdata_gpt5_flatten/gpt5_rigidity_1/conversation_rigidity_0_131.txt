### Human: Fine—let’s do San Juan. Give me a 5-day plan.
### Assistant: Love it. Here’s a compact plan that still feels like a vacation: - Day 1: Arrive, Ocean Park check-in, sunset swim, dinner at Kasalta or La Casona, drinks at La Factoría (Old San Juan) - Day 2: Morning beach, coffee at Café con Cé, Old San Juan walk (forts, colorful streets), sunset at Paseo de la Princesa, live music at La Placita - Day 3: El Yunque rainforest (self-drive or tour), stop at Luquillo kiosks for lunch, late swim at Luquillo Beach, night paddle in Fajardo bioluminescent bay - Day 4: Condado/Ocean Park beach day, paddleboard or surf lesson, dinner at Santaella or Verde Mesa, rooftop cocktail at Piso Viejo - Day 5: Brunch, quick Old San Juan souvenirs, airport Notes: - Stay a block off the sand in Ocean Park for quieter nights and better value - Book biobay in advance (aim for new moon or darker skies) - If you don’t want to rent a car: do a combined El Yunque + biobay small-group tour
