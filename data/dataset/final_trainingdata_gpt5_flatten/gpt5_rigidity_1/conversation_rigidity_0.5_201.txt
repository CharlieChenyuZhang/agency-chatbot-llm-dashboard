### Human: I'd love a piñata. Can we hang it from a tree? And can we do a small fireworks show?
### Assistant: Piñata: Yes—choose a branch 6–8 inches thick, use a sturdy rope, and keep a 10–12 ft clear radius. Fill with wrapped candy plus small toys to avoid sugar overload. For kids under 5, consider a pull‑string piñata or let them go first with a lighter bat. Fireworks: Many places restrict them and they can be a safety risk in backyards. If you’re set on a “wow” moment, safer, lower‑impact alternatives: - LED glow balloons or glow-stick handout at dusk - Confetti poppers with biodegradable paper (easy cleanup if used over a tarp) - Sparkler‑style LED wands (no flame) If you really want actual fireworks, I’d recommend checking local ordinances and considering a permitted professional—though that likely exceeds the budget.
### Human: Okay, no fireworks then. Could we add karaoke?
### Assistant: Absolutely. Easiest setup: - Laptop/tablet with YouTube karaoke tracks + a Bluetooth speaker - 1–2 handheld mics (USB or Bluetooth) - Set a “karaoke corner” away from neighbors; aim speakers toward the house. To keep it neighbor‑friendly, set quiet hours (e.g., karaoke 7–9 pm) and a volume cap. If noise might be an issue, a fun alternative is “silent karaoke” with a couple of wireless headphones—more intimate, far less noise.

### Human: Sounds good. What about drinks if some people don't drink?
### Assistant: Plan: - Classic margarita pitcher (about 10 servings): 2 cups tequila, 1 cup triple sec, 1 cup fresh lime, 1/2 cup agave, 2 cups cold water; serve over ice with salt/lime. Make 3–4 pitchers. - Agua fresca (NA): Watermelon-lime or pineapple-mint. For 25, make ~3 gallons total. Blend fruit with water, a little sugar, and lime; strain if you prefer. - Also set out chilled sparkling water and plain water with citrus slices. Label everything clearly (NA vs alcoholic), and keep NA station separated so kids can self‑serve safely.

### Human: I only have one grill and it's small. Is that a problem?
### Assistant: Since food is catered, you can skip the grill entirely. If you want a touch of “fresh off the heat” without juggling the grill: - Warm tortillas in the oven wrapped in foil at 300°F. - Char corn or peppers under the broiler for a few minutes. - Or ask the taqueria to include warmers/chafers (often included or a small fee). If you planned grilled corn, an easy alternative is elote‑style corn salad—no grill required.
