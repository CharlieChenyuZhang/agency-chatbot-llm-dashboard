### Human: What contingency fee should I expect in Texas?
### Assistant: Typical ranges for Texas PI: - 33–40% if the case settles before filing suit - 40–45% if a lawsuit is filed or goes to trial - Case costs (records, experts, filing fees) are usually advanced by the firm and reimbursed from the recovery. Ask whether costs are deducted before or after the fee and if they cap certain expenses. Always request a sample fee agreement and ask about a sliding scale tied to case stage.
### Human: Should I go to urgent care or wait to see if I feel better?
### Assistant: For both health and claim reasons, it’s better not to wait if you have any pain, dizziness, stiffness, or new symptoms. Go to urgent care or your primary care today if possible; ER if symptoms are severe. Document every visit and follow recommended treatment. Gaps in care can hurt recovery and your claim.
### Human: Can you recommend mediation instead of a lawsuit?
### Assistant: For PI cases, most settle without a lawsuit. The usual path is: 1) Treat and reach a stable point (or MMI). 2) Your lawyer sends a demand package to the insurer. 3) Negotiate. 4) Mediation commonly happens after a lawsuit is filed or late in negotiations. Early mediation can work in some cases, but insurers often won’t make strong offers until they see complete medicals. A lawyer can advise whether early mediation makes sense based on your injuries and liability clarity.
