### Human: I prefer conventional with a mixed grip. I don’t own a belt but could get one. Do I need straps?
### Assistant: That works. Notes: - Mixed grip is fine; keep the supinated arm straight and don’t jerk the bar to protect the biceps. You can rotate which hand is supinated between sets. - A 10 mm leather belt helps a lot on heavy sets; not mandatory, but it’s a high-ROI buy. - Straps aren’t required, but using them on some volume sets preserves your grip for the heavy top set. You’ll still train grip on warm-ups and selected sets. - If forearms lag, we’ll add grip work (static holds, plate pinches).
### Human: What would a single week look like for me?
### Assistant: Here’s a sample week early in the cycle (Weeks 1–2). We’ll use RPE so it adapts to good/bad days. Day 1 – Heavy deadlift + posterior chain - Deadlift (conventional): 1 top set at RPE 8 (about 2 reps in reserve) for 3–5 reps, then 3 back-off sets at 85–88% of that top set for 4–5 reps. - Romanian deadlift: 3×6–8 at RPE 7. - Plank or dead bug: 3×45–60s. Day 2 – Squat focus + bracing - Back squat: 4×4–6 at RPE 7–8. - Paused leg press or front squat: 3×6–8 at RPE 7. - Weighted hip hinge patterning (e.g., cable pull-throughs): 3×10–12. - Core: Pallof press 3×10–12/side. Day 3 – Technique/deadlift variation + upper back - Tempo deadlift (3-sec eccentric): 3×3 at RPE 6–7. - Barbell row or chest-supported row: 4×8–10. - Hamstring curl: 3×10–12. - Face pulls or rear-delt work: 3×12–15. - Optional grip: 2×30–45s bar hangs or 2×20–30s double-overhand holds. Progression outline: - Week 1–3: Build the top set each week if RPE stays at 8. Microload +2.5 kg where needed. - Week 4: Deload 30–40% volume and ~5–7% intensity; keep movement quality high. - Week 5–7: Run a 3–2–1 style wave on the top set (Week 5 triple, Week 6 double, Week 7 single at RPE 8–8.5) with back-offs. - Week 8: Test week; work up to a single at RPE 9–9.5 if warm-ups are moving well. If not, aim for a 2–3RM PR and estimate 1RM. Back tightness management: - Warm-up: 5–7 minutes easy cardio, then hip hinges with a dowel, 1–2 sets of glute bridges, and 2–3 bracing breaths per set (360° brace). - Stay honest with RPE. If your top set feels like RPE 9+, cut load slightly. - If tightness lingers >48 hours, swap one RDL day for a trap-bar deadlift or a block pull at knee height for a week.
### Human: I work a desk job and don’t want to gain fat. How should I eat for this?
### Assistant: To maximize strength in 8 weeks with minimal fat gain: - Calories: small surplus, about +150–250 kcal/day above maintenance. If you’re very worried about fat, run at maintenance but prioritize carbs around training; expect slightly slower progress. - Protein: 1.6–2.0 g/kg (130–165 g/day). - Carbs: 3–5 g/kg (250–400 g/day), front-load 60–90 minutes pre-lift and within 2 hours post-lift. - Fats: fill the rest (~0.8–1 g/kg). - Creatine: 3–5 g/day. - Hydration: 30–40 ml/kg/day; add 500–750 ml around training. - Steps: 6–8k/day to help partition nutrients and keep fat gain down. If scale weight climbs >0.25–0.35 kg/week for 2 weeks, trim 100–150 kcal/day.
### Human: If I stall in week 4 trying 5 reps at 120 kg and miss, what should I do?
### Assistant: Good callout. Options, from least to most aggressive: - Convert to a lower-rep top set: Hit 120 for 2–3 at RPE 8 instead of 5. Then do back-offs at 85–88% for 3–4×4–5. Strength is specific; triples and doubles are fine. - Microload: Try 117.5 kg for 4–5 at RPE 8, then back-offs. - Variation swap for a week: Pause deadlifts 3×3 at RPE 7–8 to clean up position off the floor, then lighter conventional back-offs. - Extra rest: Push heavy day to 72–96 hours after your last hinge/squat if fatigue is high. - If two consecutive heavy days flop, deload early for 4–5 days, then resume. Don’t grind repeated RPE 10 sets. It drains you and risks your back acting up.
