### Human: What if I miss four days in a row? I tend to spiral.
### Assistant: Here’s your Spiral Stopper protocol: 1) Reset story (30 seconds): “Four misses means I need a smaller starting point, not that I failed.” 2) Micro-win now (2–5 minutes): 3-minute mobility + read 1 paragraph. 3) Clear the path: Identify one friction to remove today (shoes by door, book on pillow, charger in hallway). 4) Social nudge: Send a quick message to your buddy: “Reset done: 3-min + 1 para.” 5) Reduce scope for 3 days: cap exercise at 10 minutes max to rebuild momentum. 6) Review in 2 minutes: What led to the miss? Pick one small guardrail to try (e.g., schedule a meeting with yourself on calendar). Your metric shifts from streak to recovery: time-to-restart under 24 hours is the goal.
