### Human: I’m bad at tracking expenses. Any easy method?
### Assistant: Pick a low-effort system: Option 1: Pay-yourself-first (least tracking) - Auto-transfer savings/investments the day you’re paid. - Auto-pay bills. - Whatever remains is your spending cap.
