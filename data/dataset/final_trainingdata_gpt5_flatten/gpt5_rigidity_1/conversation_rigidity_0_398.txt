### Human: How should I title videos to get more subscribers?
### Assistant: I wouldn’t title for subs; title for curiosity + clarity that drives clicks and retention. Subs follow retention. Here’s the system.” - Numbers with proof: “The 3-Automation Setup That Cut My Expenses 18%” Focus on audience-first language (“on $X salary,” “as a new nurse,” etc.).
