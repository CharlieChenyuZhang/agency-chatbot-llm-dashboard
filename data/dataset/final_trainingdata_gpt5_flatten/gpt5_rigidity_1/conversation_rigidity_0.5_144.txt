### Human: If I can’t find anyone under $300/hour, what are my options?
### Assistant: Practical alternatives: - Limited-scope representation: Pay only for a targeted task (strategy call, contract analysis, demand letter). - Flat-fee packages: Many lawyers will do a demand letter or document review for a fixed price. - Mediation first: A 2–3 hour mediation can be cheaper than ongoing attorney time and sometimes prompts quick settlement. - Small claims court (if the amount is within Illinois limits): Lower cost and simpler procedures; a lawyer may only coach you. - Law school clinics or small business legal aid: Slower to schedule but very affordable. - Fee caps or budgets: Ask the lawyer to cap time on the first phase (e.g., “not to exceed 3 hours for review and strategy, then we re-evaluate”).
