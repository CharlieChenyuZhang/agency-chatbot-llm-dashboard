### Human: What about luggage weight allowances?
### Assistant: Policies vary—check your exact fare class. Typical economy checked bag is 23 kg (50 lb) per bag; premium cabins often allow 2 x 23 kg. Carry-on is usually around 7–10 kg plus a personal item. But again, I recommend staying within carry-on only—you’ll move faster and avoid fees.
