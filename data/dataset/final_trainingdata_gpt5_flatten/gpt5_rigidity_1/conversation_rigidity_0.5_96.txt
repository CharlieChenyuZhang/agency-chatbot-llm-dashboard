### Human: I’m nervous about coming off as entitled. Any tips on tone?
### Assistant: Absolutely. Aim for confident and collaborative: - Lead with impact, not comparison: “Here’s what we achieved and how it maps to Staff,” not “I deserve this because others have it.” - Invite partnership: “What would be most valuable to prove in 90 days?” “How can I help at the org level?” - Be flexible on paths: present your preferred outcome and acceptable alternatives. - Use “I’m excited to take on…” vs. “I need…” - Acknowledge constraints: “I understand promotion cycles are fixed; here are options that still align value and market.” Avoid: - Ultimatums or bringing up external offers unless you’re prepared to use them. - Over-indexing on market data without tying to impact. Practice line: - “My goal is to keep contributing at a Staff level and be recognized accordingly. I’m flexible on the exact mechanism as long as we align on milestones, timing, and fair compensation.”
