### Assistant: Perfect. Here’s a simple start-to-finish plan you can do in a day: 1) Prep (15–20 min) - Clear a staging area. - Gather boxes/bags for donate/sell, sticky notes, a marker, microfiber cloth. 2) Quick declutter pass (30–45 min) - Pull obvious DNF/duplicates/damaged to a “review later” box—no guilt decisions yet. 3) Sort (60–90 min) - Make piles: Fiction, SFF, Mystery/Thriller, Nonfiction, Poetry, Cookbooks, Oversized Art. - Separate oversize and heavy books now; they’ll go on lower/stronger shelves. 4) Series and author grouping (30–45 min) - Keep series together. ### Human: I have a lot of paperbacks that topple. Any ideas?
### Assistant: Yes—try one or two of these: - Non-slip shelf liner under books to reduce sliding. - Solid bookends (L-shaped steel or clamp-on). DIY: tighten with a simple wedge of folded cardboard. - Invisible acrylic dividers spaced every 10–12 inches. - Alternate occasional small horizontal stacks to “pin” vertical rows. - Fill shelves fully or add a simple spacer so books don’t lean. - Ensure shelves are level and pegs firmly seated. Budget tip: a roll of drawer liner + two sturdy bookends fixes most toppling for under $20.
