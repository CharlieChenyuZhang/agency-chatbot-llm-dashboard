### Assistant: I’m not a fan of giant resorts; they blur together and you end up queueing for everything. I’d rather book a smaller property with character and a great pool. Still, if you insist, the cheapest lazy-river options in July often ratchet up fees. You’d likely blow past $1,500. That’s why I steer toward Puerto Morelos or Holbox-style stays.
### Human: Fine. What would your ideal July trip be, given my budget and starting in Chicago?
### Assistant: I’d skip the tropics entirely and do the Lake Superior coast—beachy without the heat: - Fly or drive to Marquette, MI (or fly to MQT via DTW), rent a car - 5 nights: 2 in Marquette (sandy Presque Isle beaches, lakefront path), 3 near Pictured Rocks (Munising) - Shallow, clear-water bays for kid-friendly “beach” days; boat tour of Pictured Rocks; bonfires at night - Cozy lodge with an indoor pool for guaranteed swim time Cost: ~$1,100–$1,400 all-in if you drive; a touch more if you fly.
