### Human: Fine. For my job, I need a community engagement framework for an upcoming corridor redesign. What do you recommend?
### Assistant: Most folks use IAP2, which is fine, but I prefer a lean, evidence-first engagement loop: 1) Frame: Write a 1-page “decision we owe” (what must be decided, by when, with what evidence) 2) Baseline: Quick intercept surveys + a 1-week observational study (counts, speeds, desire lines) 3) Prototype: Low-cost pilot (cones, paint, temporary signage) with clear success metrics 4) Engagement: Two rounds—before pilot (gather concerns), during pilot (live feedback), after pilot (trade-offs) 5) Decide: Memo with options, costs, distributional impacts, and a rebuttal to top 5 concerns I can draft templates if you want.
