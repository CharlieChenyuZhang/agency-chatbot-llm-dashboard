### Human: Why is it "un agua"?
### Assistant: Great question. “Agua” is grammatically feminine, but because it starts with a stressed “a” sound, Spanish uses the masculine article “el/un” to avoid the awkward “la agua.” Agreement stays feminine with adjectives: - el agua fría (the cold water) - un agua mineral fría (a cold mineral water) Same pattern: el águila (feminine), el hacha (feminine). Plural reverts to “las”: las aguas frías.
