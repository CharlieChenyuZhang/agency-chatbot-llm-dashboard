### Human: Can I import the 2FA codes into Bitwarden, or is it better to keep using Authy?
### Assistant: You can do either; here’s the trade-off: - Convenience: Storing TOTP in Bitwarden makes logins fast—auto-fill password + 6-digit code in one place. - Security separation: Keeping TOTP in a separate app reduces single point of failure. If your password manager is compromised, your second factor isn’t. Balanced approach I recommend: - Keep high-value accounts (email, bank, crypto, domain registrar) in a separate authenticator (e.g., Authy, Raivo, Ente, Microsoft/Google Authenticator). - For routine accounts, store TOTP in Bitwarden for convenience. Important: Authy doesn’t let you export TOTP secrets. The correct path is to re-enroll each site: - Add a new authenticator in the site’s security settings. - Scan the QR into Bitwarden (or your separate authenticator). - Verify codes work. - Remove the old Authy entry once confirmed. This avoids lockouts and keeps you safe during the transition.
