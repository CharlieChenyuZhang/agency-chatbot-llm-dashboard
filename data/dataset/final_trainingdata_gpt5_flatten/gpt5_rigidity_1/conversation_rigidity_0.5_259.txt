### Human: It’s a small home office corner. Desk is messy with cables and papers, two monitors, small budget, I rent so no drilling. I want it to look clean. I have ADHD and only have Saturday to fix it.
### Assistant: Got it. Here’s a renter-friendly, budget-conscious plan you can do in about 3 hours on Saturday, with ADHD-friendly structure. Prep (10 min) - Gather a laundry basket, trash bag, 3 boxes: Keep, Action, Toss/Recycle. - Put on a 25-minute timer; break for 5 minutes between rounds. Phase 1: Clear and sort (40 min) - Sweep everything off the desk into the basket. - Rapid sort: Keep on desk, Action (needs doing), Toss/Recycle. - Wipe desk; place only monitors/keyboard/mouse back. Phase 2: Cable control (35 min) - Use a clamp-on or adhesive under-desk cable tray; route all power strips and bricks into it. - Bundle each device’s cable with small Velcro ties; label ends. - Use a few adhesive clips to guide cables down a leg. Phase 3: Paper and tools (35 min) - Set a vertical file sorter with 3 slots: Inbox, Action, To-File. - One flat tray or desk mat for keyboard/mouse; one pen cup; one sticky note pad. - Everything else goes in a small drawer tray or a lidded box. Phase 4: Visual clarity (15 min) - Desk mat to define work zone and hide minor scuffs. - Hide extras in a small rolling cart or single bin under desk. Quick ADHD supports - One “launchpad” spot for keys/earbuds/wallet. - Timer + playlist for sprints. - End with a 10-minute “reset” checklist. If you can’t buy organizers before Saturday, I’ll give DIY alternatives too.

### Human: I also share the desk with my partner sometimes. Any ideas?
### Assistant: Aim for quick swap-outs and clear zones. - Two labeled grab-and-go kits: small bins each with your essentials (notebook, pens, headset, chargers). When one person starts, swap bins. - Shared but neutral area: center space for keyboard/mouse and desk mat; left side yours, right side theirs. - Cable sharing: one powered USB hub and a single accessible HDMI/USB-C dongle so each person plugs in one or two cables only. - If space is tight, a slim rolling cart can park next to whoever’s using the desk; when done, roll away.

### Assistant: Keep it ultra-simple so it sticks. - Top-level folders (5 max): Work, Personal, Finance, Projects, Archive. - Inside each, use YYYY-MM structure: 2025-11, or by project name. - File names: date_subject_version (2025-11-12_budget_v2). - Triage routine (10 minutes, 3x/week): Desktop to Inbox folder, then move to the right bucket. Automation if you want: - Create a download rule to send PDFs with “invoice” into Finance/Invoices. - Pin your 5 folders in Quick Access/Favorites.
### Human: I can’t keep routines. Can you make a realistic schedule?
### Assistant: Yes—keep it short and anchored to existing habits. Daily (5–10 minutes, after lunch or last email) - Reset desk: toss trash, return tools to bin, clear mat, put papers into Inbox. - One-minute cable check: coils off the floor. Weekly (20 minutes, Saturday morning) - Process Inbox: 2-minute timer per paper; file or add to Action. - Digital: empty Desktop/Downloads into the right folders. - Restock your kits (pens, sticky notes, wipes). Monthly (20 minutes, first Saturday) - Archive older files to the Archive folder. - Wipe desk, clean keyboard, check labels. If even that’s hard, do a “2-minute closeout” only: set a phone alarm and stop after two minutes. Something > nothing.
### Human: I rent and can’t drill; you said clamp-on tray, but my desk lip won’t take clamps.
### Assistant: No problem—adhesive and floor-based options will work. - Adhesive cable raceways under the desk for power bricks and lighter cables; use 3M VHB tape. - Mount the power strip on the desk underside with heavy-duty adhesive strip mounts. - Run the main cable down a desk leg using zip ties and adhesive tie mounts. - Floor cable sleeve or rug runner to the wall outlet. - Test adhesive on painter’s tape first if you’re worried about finish.
### Human: I have a Roomba that keeps eating cables.
### Assistant: Let’s make cables robot-proof. - Elevate: keep all cables off the floor using adhesive clips along the desk underside and leg. - Consolidate: fabric cable sleeve from desk leg to strip, then a low-profile floor cord cover to the wall. - Relocate power strip to the underside of desk or top rear edge. - Quick backup: schedule the Roomba during times when cables are docked, or place a small floor barrier (door draft stopper) around the cable area.
