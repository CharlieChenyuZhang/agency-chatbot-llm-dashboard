### Assistant: On macOS or Linux (requires jq; install with: brew install jq if needed): curl -s 'https://www.reddit.com/r/nyc/top.json?t=week&limit=1' | jq -r '.data.children[0].data | "\(.score) — \(.title) (by u/\(.author))\nhttps://reddit.com\(.permalink)"'
