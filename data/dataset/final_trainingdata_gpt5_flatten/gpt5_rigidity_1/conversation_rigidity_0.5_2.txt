### Human: Can you give me the exact Picard rename script?
### Assistant: Yes. In Picard, go to Options > File Naming, check “Rename files” and “Move files,” set your target music folder, and use this pattern: $if(%compilation%,Compilations/$if(%date%,$left(%date%,4) - ,)%album%, $if2(%albumartist%,%artist%)/$if(%date%,$left(%date%,4) - ,)%album%/ $if(%discnumber%,CD%discnumber%/,)$num(%tracknumber%,2) - %title%) Notes: - Compilations go to Compilations/Year - Album/… - Regular albums go to AlbumArtist/Year - Album/… - Multi-disc albums get CD1, CD2 subfolders. - Picard auto-handles file extensions; it also sanitizes illegal characters. If you’d like “Album Artist” always, even when blank, we can tweak to force %artist% fallback (already covered by $if2).

### Human: Duplicates: I want to keep FLAC over MP3 but sometimes the FLAC is 16/44 and the MP3 is 320 with a different master.
### Assistant: Good call. To balance quality with different masters: - In dupeGuru “Music mode,” scan on the cleaned folder. It shows bitrate, format, duration. - Default rule: prefer lossless (FLAC/ALAC) over lossy, and higher bitrate within each format. - Exception rule for different masters: if durations differ by more than ~2 seconds or album release differs, keep both. That usually indicates a different edit/master. You can also keep both if album tags/MBIDs differ. dupeGuru can’t analyze loudness to spot different masters, but duration and album IDs are practical proxies. Start with cautious thresholds and manually review “possible” dupes before moving them to Quarantine. If you later want deeper automation, beets’ duplicates plugin can prioritize by format/bitrate and compare MusicBrainz IDs automatically.
