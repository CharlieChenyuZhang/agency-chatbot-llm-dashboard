### Human: I heard the JR Pass might save money. Should I get it?
### Assistant: For your route, the 7-day national JR Pass is often close to break-even or more expensive now. Rough one-way reserved-seat fares: - Tokyo → Kyoto: ~14,000–15,000 JPY - Kyoto → Hiroshima: ~11,000 JPY - Hiroshima → Tokyo: ~19,000–20,000 JPY Total: roughly 44,000–46,000 JPY, plus a bit for local JR lines. The 7-day JR Pass is around the low–50,000 JPY range and doesn’t cover Nozomi trains (the fastest), so you’d ride Hikari/Sakura or pay extra workarounds. Given that, individual tickets are simpler and may be cheaper. If you were taking more long-distance rides, the pass could win. Alternative: a regional Kansai–Hiroshima pass can be good value, but it doesn’t cover Tokyo legs, so it’s awkward here. My recommendation: buy individual Shinkansen tickets and reserve seats via SmartEX or at ticket machines when you arrive.
