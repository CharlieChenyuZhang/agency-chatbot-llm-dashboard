### Human: Add one counterargument and response I can use if someone says cool pavements aren’t worth it.
### Assistant: Counterargument: - “Cool pavements don’t help people—they just reflect heat and cause glare.” Response: - Data shows mid-day surface temps drop meaningfully (~10–12°F), which can reduce re-radiated heat and tire/road wear; however, reflectivity can raise radiant heat for pedestrians in direct sun. Pilot, measure, and combine interventions rather than using cool coatings alone.
