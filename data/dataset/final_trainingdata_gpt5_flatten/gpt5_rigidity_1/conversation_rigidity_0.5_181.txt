### Human: I might rent a car for the Fuji day. Worth it?
### Assistant: It’s possible, but not optimal: - Pros: Flexibility, easy to reach multiple viewpoints if weather is clear. - Cons: Tolls and parking fees, potential traffic, mountain weather can change quickly, and you’ll need an International Driving Permit plus your home license. Weekend crowds near Kawaguchiko can mean delays. - Simple alternative: Highway bus from Shinjuku to Kawaguchiko is direct, cheap, and drops you near shuttles for the festival and sights. If you still prefer to drive: - Route: Shinjuku → Chuo Expressway → Kawaguchiko IC (~1.5–2.5 hours depending on traffic). Pay ETC tolls or cash. - Parking: Use official Shibazakura Festival parking, then shuttle in. - Backup plan: If Fuji is clouded over, pivot to Lake Saiko or Oshino Hakkai.
