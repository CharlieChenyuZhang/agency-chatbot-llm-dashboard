### Human: I also have anxiety. Should I try a psychiatrist instead of a PCP?
### Assistant: It depends on severity and what you want help with: - Many PCPs manage mild to moderate anxiety, do initial screening (like GAD-7), start first-line medications if appropriate, and refer for therapy. - If you’ve tried first-line options without relief, have complex medication needs, or severe symptoms (panic interfering with daily life, major impairment), a psychiatrist is a good next step. - Parallel option: therapy (CBT, ACT) can be very effective; many people do PCP + therapist, and add psychiatry if needed. - If symptoms feel urgent or unsafe (e.g., thoughts of self-harm), seek immediate help via urgent care, ER, or a crisis line.
