### Human: I’m intermediate. Goal is body recomposition with a bias toward hypertrophy. Full gym access. I can do 4 days lifting, 60–75 minutes per session. I run 2x/week, usually 5Ks. Mild knee irritation with deep squats. I also want to get better at pull-ups.
### Assistant: Great—here’s a 12-week block tailored to that: High-level structure - Split: 4-day Upper/Lower x2 + 2 runs (6 active days, 1 full rest) - Mesocycles: - Weeks 1–3: Volume build (6–12 reps, 2–3 RIR) - Week 4: Deload (~50–60% volume, 3–4 RIR) - Weeks 5–7: Intensity up slightly (5–10 reps, 1–2 RIR) - Week 8: Deload - Weeks 9–11: Heavier/peak hypertrophy-strength (4–8 reps, 0–1 RIR on last set of key lifts) - Week 12: Test/recover (back-off volume, optional rep PRs) - Progressive overload: Double progression (add reps within a range, then add load), last set “plus” set optional. - Run placement: Put runs after Upper days or on the deload weeks keep both easy. To protect your knee, avoid hard runs the day before Lower days. Weekly schedule example - Mon: Upper A - Tue: Lower A - Wed: 5K easy-moderate - Thu: Upper B - Fri: Lower B - Sat: 5K easy - Sun: Rest Lower-body knee-friendly swaps - Squat patterns: High box squat, safety bar squat, goblet box squat - Hip hinge: Trap-bar deadlift, RDL - Unilateral: Step-ups, split squats with shorter ROM - Sled drags/reverse sled for quad work Program details (60–75 min sessions) Upper A - Pull-ups: 4 sets in 6–10 rep range (add assistance or weight to stay in range); last set AMRAP leaving 1–2 reps in reserve (RIR) - Incline DB press: 3–4 x 8–12 - Chest-supported row: 3–4 x 8–12 - Cable lateral raise: 3 x 12–15 - Face pulls or rear-delt fly: 3 x 12–15 - Optional: EZ-bar curl superset rope pressdown: 2–3 x 10–15 each Lower A - Box squat (just above parallel): 3–4 x 6–10 - Trap-bar deadlift: 3–4 x 5–8 - Reverse sled drag (quad bias): 4 x 20–30 meters - Hamstring curl: 3 x 10–15 - Calf raise: 3 x 10–15 - Short knee-prep: Spanish squat isometric 3 x 30–45s Upper B - Flat barbell or DB bench: 3–4 x 6–10 - Lat-focused pull (wide-grip pulldown or pull-up variation): 3–4 x 8–12 - Seated cable row (neutral grip): 3–4 x 8–12 - DB overhead press: 3 x 6–10 - Rear delt + lateral raise combo: 2–3 x 12–15 each - Biceps + triceps of choice: 2–3 x 10–15 Lower B - RDL: 3–4 x 6–10 - Leg press (foot placement to tolerance): 3–4 x 8–12 - Step-ups (knee-friendly height): 3 x 8–12/leg - Hip thrust or glute bridge: 3 x 8–12 - Core: Pallof press or weighted plank: 3 sets Progression rules - Weeks 1–3: Start conservative (2–3 RIR). Each week, add 1–2 reps per set until the top of the range; then add 2.5–5% load next session and return to the low end of the rep range. - Week 4 deload: Halve sets or cut intensity to 3–4 RIR. Keep movement patterns, move fast, leave fresh. - Weeks 5–7: Same progression, tighter RIR (1–2). Slightly heavier loads than meso 1. - Week 8 deload: As above. - Weeks 9–11: Lower rep ranges (4–8) on big lifts; keep accessories 8–12. One key lift per day can approach 0–1 RIR on the final set; others stay 1–2 RIR. - Week 12: Pull back volume 40–50%. Running guidance - Keep both runs easy conversational most weeks to minimize interference with leg training and knee stress. - If you want one “quality” run, make it short intervals (e.g., 6 x 400m easy-moderate) on an Upper day, but this may slightly slow hypertrophy. If in doubt, keep both easy. Warm-up (10 minutes) - 3–5 minutes light bike/row - Dynamic prep for joint(s) of the day - 2–3 ramp-up sets before the first big lift - Knee prep: Spanish squat iso + slow terminal knee extensions if available This hits your goals and respects your knee and running. If you want a 5- or 6-day lifting split instead, we can do that, but with your runs and knee history, 4 days is the most practical balance.

### Human: What should my nutrition look like for recomposition?
### Assistant: Simple recomposition framework - Calories: Start around maintenance (bodyweight in lb x 14–15). If your waist shrinks and lifts progress, you’re good. If weight stalls and you want more fat loss, drop 150–250 kcal; if strength is dragging, add 100–150 kcal. - Protein: 0.7–1.0 g per lb bodyweight daily (1.6–2.2 g/kg), spread over 3–5 meals. - Carbs: Center around training (pre + post). Roughly 1.5–2.5 g/lb depending on appetite, steps, and run volume. - Fats: Fill the rest, typically 0.3–0.5 g/lb. - Pre-workout: 20–40g protein + 30–60g carbs 1–2 hours before. - Post-workout: 25–40g protein + 50–80g carbs within 3 hours. - Supplements: Creatine monohydrate 3–5g/day, vitamin D if deficient, fish oil if low omega-3 intake, caffeine as needed. - Hydration: 30–40 ml/kg/day plus 500–1000 ml around training. - Fiber/micros: 6–10g fiber per 1000 kcal, eat fruits/veg daily, add a pinch of salt pre-training.
### Human: How do I get from 6 to 12 strict pull-ups?
### Assistant: 8-week pull-up focus layered onto the plan - Frequency: 2 main sessions (Upper A/Pull day) + 1 micro “technique” session (2–3 submax sets after Push day) - Method: Rep-goal progression - Session A: 4 sets aiming for a total of 24–32 reps across sets. When you hit 32, add 2.5–5 lb next time. - Session B: 3–4 sets of 4–6 reps with 2–3 second eccentrics. Add a small load when all sets hit 6 at 1–2 RIR. - Micro session: 2 sets at half your max reps, perfect form, long rest. - Assistance/overload rotation (alternate weekly) - Week 1: Bodyweight strict; Week 2: 2-second pauses at top and halfway; Week 3: +5–10 lb if ready; Week 4: Deload (keep easy lat work); repeat with slightly higher loads Weeks 5–8. - Accessories that help: Chest-supported rows 3 x 8–12, straight-arm pulldowns 2–3 x 12–15, biceps 2–3 x 10–15. - Technique cues: Full hang, ribs down, drive elbows to hips, slight arch, chin clearly over bar. 2–3 min rest between sets.
### Human: If I miss week 3 for travel, how do I resume?
### Assistant: Do this: - While traveling: If possible, do two short sessions: 3–4 rounds of push-ups, inverted rows, split squats, RDLs with a suitcase, and planks. Keep runs easy. - When you’re back: Repeat the last successful week (week 2 loads/reps) for one session per lift, then continue as planned. If everything feels heavy, treat your first week back as a mini-deload (reduce volume by ~30%) and resume progression the following week. - Don’t “make up” missed volume in one week; just pick up the progression.
### Human: My knee flared up after a 5K with hills. Adjustments?
### Assistant: Short-term tweaks (7–10 days) - Runs: Switch to flat, easy pace or replace with 20–30 min incline walk/cycle. No downhill intervals for now. - Lower days: Replace squats with: - Spanish squat isometrics 3–4 x 30–45s - Reverse sled drags 5–6 x 20–30m - Leg press short ROM in tolerable zone 3–4 x 10–15 - Hip-dominant work (RDL/hip thrust) stays if pain-free - Keep RIR 2–3 on all lower work; avoid pain >3/10. If pain persists beyond 2–3 weeks or spikes, consult a clinician. Once symptoms settle, reintroduce box squats at a higher box, then gradually lower over 2–3 weeks.
