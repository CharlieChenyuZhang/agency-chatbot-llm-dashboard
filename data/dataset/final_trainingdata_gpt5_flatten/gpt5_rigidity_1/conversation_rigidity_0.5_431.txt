### Human: Our review cycle just ended. I don’t want to wait a year. How can I push for it sooner?
### Assistant: Aim for an off‑cycle path with a short, measurable runway: - Ask about policy: “What’s our process for off‑cycle promotions?” - Propose a 6–8 week Staff‑level project (cross‑org impact, ambiguity, mentoring others) with 2–3 clear success criteria. - Set milestones: mid‑point check‑in at week 3–4, final decision by week 8 with an effective date. - Tie to a timely business need: “If I lead X initiative now, we de‑risk Q1 goals, which justifies an off‑cycle promotion.” - If comp can’t move immediately, negotiate title/scope now with a dated comp adjustment or a retention bonus in the interim. You’ll be asking for: agreement on criteria, decision owner(s), exact review date, and effective date options under budget constraints.
### Human: What if my manager says there’s no budget?
### Assistant: Keep momentum by converting “no budget” into “which lever can we pull?” Options and sample responses: - Title now, comp at the next budget window: “If cash can’t move now, can we confirm Staff title and scope effective [date] with the salary adjustment on [pay period]?” - Partial now, remainder later: “Would 10% now and 10% on [date/milestone] work?” - One‑time retention bonus: “Could we do a $X retention bonus now with the Staff adjustment slated for [date]?” - Equity refresh or spot award: “Is an equity refresh feasible this quarter?” - Written criteria + date: “Can we document the Staff criteria and set a decision date of [date], so this is not open‑ended?” - Scope and visibility: “Let’s formalize a Staff‑level initiative I can lead now and have it be the centerpiece of the decision.” Close by asking: “Which of these fits within our constraints, and who else needs to sign off?”
