In practice, that means: - Decide your non‑negotiables outside work (sleep, family time, movement). Put those on the calendar first. - Shrink your work surface area (fewer projects, fewer meetings) before optimizing your schedule. - Add a hard daily shutdown ritual and one weekly reset hour. Those two habits do more than any elaborate system. If you want, I’ll help you design around that instead of chasing “balance.”

### Assistant: Time‑blocking looks tidy but tends to collapse under real life.
