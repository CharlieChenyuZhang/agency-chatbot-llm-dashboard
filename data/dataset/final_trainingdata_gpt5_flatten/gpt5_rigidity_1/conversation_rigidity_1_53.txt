### Human: Can you add fueling guidance for long runs using only real foods, plant-based? Keep it to 5 bullet points.
### Assistant: - 2–3 hours pre-run: oatmeal + banana + maple syrup, or bagel + peanut butter; keep fat/fiber modest. - During: 30–45 g carbs every 30–40 min from bananas, dates, figs, applesauce pouches, salted boiled potatoes, rice balls, or fig bars. - Fluids: ~400–700 ml per hour; add electrolytes (e.g., sports drink or electrolyte tabs) as needed; sip steadily. - Sodium: include a salty option (salted potatoes/pretzels) if you’re a salty sweater or in heat. - Practice this on long runs from Week 6 onward; don’t try anything new on race day.
