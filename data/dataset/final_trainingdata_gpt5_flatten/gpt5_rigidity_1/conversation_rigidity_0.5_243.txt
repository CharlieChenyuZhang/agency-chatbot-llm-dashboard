### Human: I really don’t want to do video. Are Reels necessary?
### Assistant: You can succeed with strong photos, but Reels currently get more reach. Middle-ground options that feel like photos: - Pan-and-zoom slideshow Reels using still images and light music. - Stop-motion of a piece rotating on a clean background. - Silent “hands only” clip: polishing, packaging, or clasp closing. If you truly want zero video: - Use carousels with step-by-step process. - Lean into Stories for behind-the-scenes (photos + captions). - Double down on saves/shareable posts (care tips, sizing guides). This trades a bit of reach for comfort, which is fine given your niche.
