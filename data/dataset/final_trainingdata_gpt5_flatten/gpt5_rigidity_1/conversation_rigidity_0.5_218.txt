### Assistant: No problem—let’s keep it simple and joint-friendly. Try one of these: - Shadow boxing rounds: 12 x 1 minute on, 30 seconds off. ### Human: How do I know if it's working without obsessing over the scale?
### Assistant: Use a small set of simple metrics: - Weight: 3x/week, morning after the bathroom; track the weekly average. - Waist: once per week at the navel. - Photos: front/side/back every 2 weeks, same lighting. - Performance: are reps/loads or work intervals improving? - How clothes fit, energy, sleep. Mini-cut adjustment rule: - If after 2 weeks your weekly average weight and waist haven’t budged, reduce calories by ~100–150 or add 10 minutes to two conditioning sessions. If you’re too hungry or drained, ease back by ~100 calories.
### Human: If I miss a workout, should I double up the next day?
### Assistant: Doubling up isn’t ideal—your knees and recovery will thank you if you don’t. Better options: - Shift the missed session to the next day and push the rest of the week back one day. - Or run a condensed full-body (30 minutes): 2 sets each of goblet box squat, RDL, row, floor press, and a 6–8 minute finisher. - If you must combine two days, cut total sets by ~30–40% and skip overlapping heavy leg work. Consistency over perfection wins here.
