### Human: Give me the conflict mediation steps.
### Assistant: Great. Here’s a lightweight approach. If your company has HR protocols, follow those; otherwise, this works well for peer conflicts. 1) Prep (10–15 min each) - Gather concrete examples from each dev: when, what happened, impact on work - Ask each to outline desired outcomes, not just grievances 2) Separate 1:1s - Use SBI: “In last Tuesday’s grooming (S), when you dismissed the estimate as ‘ridiculous’ (B), it stalled the session and the story missed the sprint (I).” - Ask: “What’s your best intent here?” and “What constraint are you optimizing for?” 3) Joint session (45–60 min) - Set the frame: shared goals, no interruptions, assume positive intent - Each states their perspective using facts and impacts; the other mirrors back - Identify where they agree on goals; name where constraints differ (quality vs speed, scope vs timeline) - Decide on two operating norms (e.g., propose alternatives, not just critiques; timebox debates to 10 minutes then escalate) - Document in a short note and share with the team 4) Follow-up (2 weeks) - Observe one meeting they both attend; reinforce when norms are followed - 15-minute check-in with both to adjust norms if needed If asynchronous is easier across time zones, I can adapt this to a written pre-read and a shorter live call.

### Human: I’d like to delegate more. What’s the delegation brief you mentioned?
### Assistant: A simple delegation brief helps you hand off ownership without micromanaging. - Purpose: What outcome is this owner driving? Why it matters. - Scope: What’s in/out. Link to any relevant context. - Decision rights: What they can decide vs. when to consult you. - Success measures: 2–3 measures (e.g., release predictability, fewer rollbacks). - Cadence: Check-in frequency and artifacts (e.g., weekly 10-minute update, dashboard link). - Timebox: Duration or milestone when you’ll review ownership. Example - Purpose: Own release coordination to reduce failed deploys by 50% in 6 weeks. - Scope: Plan cutoffs, coordinate testing, run go/no-go, postmortems. Not responsible for code quality. - Decision rights: Can change cutoff by ±1 day; consult EM for bigger changes. - Measures: Failed deploy rate, rollback count, on-time release percentage. - Cadence: 10-minute weekly update; dashboard link in Slack. - Timebox: Review ownership after two releases. If you prefer, I can turn that into a fill-in-the-blanks template.
