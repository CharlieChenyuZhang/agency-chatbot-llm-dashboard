### Human: How about work options after graduation? I want to stay in the US if possible.
### Assistant: Key points for international CS students: - OPT/STEM OPT: After a CS degree, you typically get 12 months of OPT plus a 24-month STEM extension (total up to 36 months) to work in the US. - CPT/Internships: Many programs allow paid internships during school (CPT). Co-op programs can be a big advantage for experience and employer sponsorship later. - H-1B: Longer-term work often relies on H-1B sponsorship (lottery). Schools with strong recruiting pipelines and career services can improve odds via multiple job options. - Employer hubs: Being near tech hubs (Seattle, Bay Area, Austin, Boston) can help with internships and networking. If staying in the US is a priority, favor schools with: - Active tech recruiting (career fairs, on-campus interviews) - Strong co-op/internship support (e.g., Northeastern, Georgia Tech, UCSD, UW–Seattle) - Large alumni networks in tech
