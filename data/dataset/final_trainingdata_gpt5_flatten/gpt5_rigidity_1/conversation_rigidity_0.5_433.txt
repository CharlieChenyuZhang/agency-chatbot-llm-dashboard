### Human: Should I run ads to get followers quickly?
### Assistant: You can, but pure “follower campaigns” usually cost more and attract low-intent followers. With $200/month, a better setup is: - Promote your best-performing reel to your niche for social proof and reach ($3–$5/day). - Run a simple lead ad or conversion ad to your free 7-day Jumpstart ($5–$7/day). Email subscribers convert to sales more reliably than followers. - Retarget people who watched 50%+ of your reels or visited your site with a short ad inviting them to the $99 program. If you really want follower growth, combine low spend with collaborations and shareable content—it’s more cost-effective than buying followers via ads.
