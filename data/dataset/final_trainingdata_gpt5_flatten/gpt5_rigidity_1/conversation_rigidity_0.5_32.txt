### Human: How do I get my iPhone contacts into Google Contacts?
### Assistant: Two main ways: Option A: One-time export from iCloud to Google 1) Backup first: iCloud.com > Contacts > gear icon > Export vCard (downloads .vcf). 2) Import: contacts.google.com > Import > Select file > upload the .vcf. 3) Deduplicate: “Merge & fix.” 4) On your iPhone: Settings > Contacts > Accounts > Add Account > Google > enable Contacts, and set Default Account to Gmail if you want new contacts saved to Google.
